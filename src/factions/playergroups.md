# **Groups: Purgatory's Player-Made Communities**

Purgatory by Night is built on two kinds of story: the wide, sim-spanning narratives carried by the Factions, and the smaller, personal stories players build with each other. Groups exist to support the second kind.

## **What Is a Group?**

A Group is a player-made collection of characters who share a common thread that isn't already represented by one of the five Factions. Maybe it's a family bloodline protecting its own interests, a street gang carving out a corner of the city, a clan holding to old traditions, or a crew bound by something entirely their own. If there's a shared idea a handful of players want to build roleplay around, and it isn't already covered by a Faction, that's a Group.

## **How Groups Relate to Factions**

Groups and Factions aren't in competition. A Group can choose to sync with one Faction, giving its members access to that Faction's benefits alongside their own. This lets a family of hunters formally back the Order of Vigiles, or a gang of opportunists throw their weight behind the Ashen Court, without folding the Group's own identity into the Faction itself. Syncing is entirely optional. Plenty of Groups will have no Faction ties at all, and that's just as valid.

## **Benefits of a Recognized Group**

Once a Group is Recognized, its members gain:

* **Faction syncing.** The option to align with one Faction and share in its benefits across the whole Group.  
* **Territorial roleplay.** The right to build roleplay within the Group's claimed territory, within the sim's rules of engagement.  
* **Claimed territory.** The right to claim roleplay territory, provided the Group's own members are the ones supervising and maintaining it.  
* **Claimed buildings.** The right to claim specific sim buildings within the Group's roleplay area.  
* **A dedicated Discord channel.** A Facilitator-maintained channel for the Group's own communications.

## **Boundaries and Expectations**

Recognition comes with responsibility, and Staff take that seriously.

Purgatory by Night is a dark-themed sim, and we expect dark, morally complicated stories. What we won't tolerate is any Group using its status to bully, abuse, or discriminate against other players, for any reason. Staff reserve the right to disband any Group that crosses that line.

Territory and building claims are in-character claims only, never out-of-character ones. If a claim can't be defended in character, Staff will not step in to defend it on the Group's behalf. Roleplay holds the territory, not a reservation.

Finally, no Group may lock an entire Faction out of a non-Faction area of the sim. Purgatory is built on inclusivity, and that standard applies to Groups just as much as it applies to everyone else.
