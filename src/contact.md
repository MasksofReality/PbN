*The people who keep Purgatory by Night running.*

Purgatory by Night runs on five staff roles, each responsible for a critical layer of the community. Below is a summary of every role and what it does, followed by the current staff list. To reach us, see [Join Us](/the-sim/join-us/) for our Discord and in-world Landing Point.

## Staff Structure Overview

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Sim Owners** | Fund and maintain the sim spaces. Hold final authority on major decisions and sim direction. Responsible for tier payments and ensuring the sim remains operational. |
| **Admins** | Handle all player-facing issues and conflict resolution. Make decisions on sim policies, rule enforcement, and community standards. Act as the primary leadership body for the sim. |
| **Architects** | Manage all Out-of-Character (OOC) operations including the sim website, event calendar, rental management, and group notices. Keep the community informed and the sim infrastructure running smoothly. |
| **Storytellers** | Run In-Character (IC) stories, plots, and events for the entire sim. Create engaging narrative arcs that all players can participate in, and guide the overall IC direction of the world. |
| **Mentors** | Onboard new players by guiding them through character creation and concept development. Help newcomers identify starting points that align with their interests and connect them to the IC community. |

## Role Breakdowns

### Sim Owners

The two Sim Owners are the financial backbone of the community. Their responsibilities include:

- Paying for and maintaining the sim tier(s)
- Final approval authority on major community decisions
- Setting the overall vision and direction for the sim
- Serving as a resource and safety net for Admin-level decisions

### Admins

Admins are the governing body of the sim. They are responsible for:

- Handling all player issues, disputes, and violations
- Creating, updating, and enforcing sim rules and policies
- Making binding decisions about sim direction with Owner oversight
- Coordinating across all other staff roles

### Architects

Architects keep the Out-of-Character side of the sim running. Their domain includes:

- Managing and updating the sim website
- Maintaining and publishing the event calendar
- Handling rental agreements and sim land management
- Sending out group notices and community announcements
- Coordinating with Admins on OOC communications

### Storytellers

Storytellers are the creative engine of the sim's In-Character life. They are responsible for:

- Writing and running IC storylines, plots, and events for all players
- Coordinating narrative arcs across factions and groups
- Creating atmosphere and world events that reflect the homebrew setting
- Collaborating with Mentors to ensure new players have story entry points
- Maintaining consistency with the sim's lore and world bible

### Mentors

Mentors are the first point of contact for new players joining the sim. Their responsibilities include:

- Guiding new players through the character creation process
- Reviewing and approving character concepts within sim guidelines
- Identifying IC starting points based on each player's interests
- Connecting new players with existing community members and factions
- Answering questions about the setting, rules, and sim culture
