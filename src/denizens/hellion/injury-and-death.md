You use the ordinary seven-level Health track and the ordinary wound penalties.

**Soak.** Stamina plus armour against bashing and lethal. Aggravated is unsoakable unless an Art says otherwise, and Iron Mountain is the only Art in the roster that says otherwise.

**Sunlight.** Unlike the Lilum, your weakness to the sun has not lessened. Every turn directly exposed to sunlight deals one point of aggravated damage as you wither into a mummified husk. Sunblock charms which reduce this to only bashing damage are highly advised purchases.

**Fire deals aggravated damage.** So do the claws and Arts of things that unmake rather than injure. Everything else behaves as it would against a living body, and nothing in this document makes bullets less effective on you.

**Healing.** Spend 1 Essence to close a level of bashing or lethal, one level per turn, no roll. Aggravated closes at 1 Essence plus 1 Willpower per level and takes a full day of undisturbed rest for each. Injury taken past an empty pool has to start healing before the pool begins to refill, which is the trap that kills newcomers: you are hurt, so you spend to heal, so you are empty, so you go into Attrition, so you hunt badly, so you get hurt.

**The weak point.** The four aspects are anchored together in a pearl seated behind the sternum. A called shot to it runs two difficulties harder as usual, and on a hit the damage is aggravated whatever the weapon. People who hunt your kind for a living are taught where it is.

**Past Incapacitated.** Further lethal drops you into **torpor**: an unresponsive comatose state. You wake when someone feeds you or when you have healed back to Crippled, whichever comes first. A Hungry Dead left at zero Essence for a full night and the day after it goes into torpor the same way.

Further damage destroys your corpse, and it rapidly crumbles to dust and bones. Your soul remains anchored to its pearl and so long as it survives your body will regenerate around it after (4 + 1d10 − Dharma rating) days in torpor. Destroying the pearl by exposing it to sunlight destroys them for good. The lucky ones have their soul aspects come apart and their soul unravels into nothingness. The rest are dragged back to the hells. Nobody has ever come back a second time.
