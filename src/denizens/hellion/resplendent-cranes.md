## Who They Are

Magistrates. Resplendent Cranes believe all things have a proper order, that the world has fallen out of alignment with it, and that the hungry dead, who have been cheated of their true afterlife, are uniquely obliged to put it back. They believe in law, precedent, correct speech, correct action and swift correction of those in violation of any of those. They will impose all of it on you at length, and they are frequently right, which is the most irritating part.

Play a Resplendent Crane to be the one with standing, the one who convenes things, and the one whose worst moments come from certainty.

**Attributes:** Social 7, Mental 5, Physical 3.

**Feeding.** By right and by title. This path is the reason the clean options are political at all, and Cranes treat unlicensed hunting in another Hungry Dead's territory as an offence, not a faux pas. A Crane who has lost their standing and has to hunt like a newcomer is a Crane one bad night from Denial imbalance.

<h2 id="the-mandate">Affinity: The Mandate</h2>

The Righteous Aspect turned outward: judgement, authority, and the sanctioned use of both. Cranes who take the Mandate to its end become something the Fellowships have to reckon with. Cranes who take it there for the wrong reasons find out what the last dot costs.

**● Observe Karma** — *Free, passive, one turn of concentration.* Look at a mortal and know their name, their true Nature, and what is weighing on them spiritually. It works on other nightfolk of lower art rating too, but nothing is given up freely and you will have to contest for it.

**●● Heavenly Ward** — *1 Essence.* Everyone but you and whoever you are touching is thrown outside a three-meter radius for the scene, and has to beat you in a contest to re-enter. Possessing spirits and demons are pushed out temporarily, though it will not clean out anything entrenched.

**●●● Mandate of Authority** — *2 Essence.* Your Righteous Aspect goes ablaze. Your words carry supernatural authority, and are received as if said by someone the listener implicitly trusts. Mortals are helpless before this, so mark the Stability trigger. Using it for unrighteous or cowardly ends costs an extra Essence and forces a Righteousness imbalance check when the scene closes.

**●●●● Divine Punishment** — *3 Essence, contested.* Look on someone who has broken their word or committed a crime and drain a point of Willpower per net success. One success stings. Two or three leave them visibly unable to meet anyone's eye for the rest of the scene. Four or more break them into near-catatonic state and can leave a lasting derangement. A target of higher willpower has to be defeated and subdued before this reaches them at all.

**●●●●● Sentence of Heaven** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower, contested.* Name aloud an offence the target actually committed and pass sentence on them: a single prohibition, stated plainly and narrowly. Do not enter this district. Do not speak to her. Do not lift a weapon. It holds for 28 days and no distance or realm escapes it. Every time they defy the sentence they take a level of aggravated damage and a golden brand surfaces on them for the rest of that scene, legible to anyone looking, naming exactly what they were sentenced for. Name a crime they did not commit and the sentence recoils: it fails immediately, everyone present feels it fail, and you lose a point of Dharma permanently.

## Strength and Weakness

**Strength.** The best social and political toolkit in the splat, and the only Affinity Art that can end an argument without a fight.

**Weakness.** Every trigger for Righteousness imbalance is something a Crane does on purpose. Being challenged by the corrupt, punishing a wrongdoer successfully, being begged for mercy. Your own competence is your failure condition.
