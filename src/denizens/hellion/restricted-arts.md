## The Demon's Eye

*Soul Art · Righteousness and Wickedness · Restricted*

Every Hungry Dead can see that souls exist. The Demon's Eye is the study of what they are made of, what holds them together, and what happens when you take one apart. It requires a teacher willing to break their own oath to give it to you, and staff permission before the first dot.

<div class="callout">
<strong>A Stigma, not a cost.</strong> A third eye grows in your forehead the moment you take the first dot, and it worsens with every dot after: weeping at two, lidless at three, reflecting nothing at four, and at five it is not an eye. It cannot be hidden by any Art in this document, it cannot be bought off, and every Hungry Dead who sees it knows exactly what you have been learning.
</div>

**● The Eye Opens** — *Free, passive.* See the shape of any soul in front of you: how many pieces it is in, what is holding it together, and where the seams run.

**●● Weigh the Soul** — *1 Essence.* Learn what a soul is owed and what it owes, including anything the Hells still hold against it and anything a Gentry's Title has fixed to it.

**●●● Sever** — *2 Essence, contested.* Cut one bond a soul is carrying. A pact, a Geas, a Tether, an inherited debt, a Blood Bond somebody else made. It is the most valuable service any Hungry Dead can sell and the fastest way to make a permanent enemy of whoever tied the knot.

**●●●● Hold the Knot** — *3 Essence.* Take a soul that is coming apart and hold it together by hand for 28 days. Enough for a dying mortal to finish saying something, a shade to survive an obsidian mark, or a Hungry Dead at their last death to be somewhere else when it arrives.

**●●●●● Amaranth** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Consume another Hungry Dead's soul entirely at the moment of their last death. You take one Dharmic Art they held, at one dot lower than they had it, and it advances thereafter on the Discordant track. You lose a point of Dharma, permanently, and no play recovers it. It is the only route to another Fellowship's Affinity Art that does not require a teacher, and every Fellowship in the world executes for it.

<div class="callout">
Amaranth reaches Dharmic Arts and nothing else. A Lilum's Blood Arts, a Sorcerer's Occult Arts, a Wraith's Shadow Arts and everything else outside your own splat stay outside it, no matter whose soul you eat.
</div>

## Hellweaving

*Soul Art · Wickedness and Denial · Restricted*

The craft of dealing directly with the domains that held you. Hellweavers argue that the only people who understand the chain are the ones willing to study it, that somebody has to negotiate, and that pretending the Hells are not there has never once made them go away. The counter-argument is that every Hellweaver has said this, and the Hells have never once come out of a negotiation worse off.

Needs a teacher and staff permission. Nobody will teach you without wanting something.

**● The Chain You Wear** — *Free, passive.* See the tether the Hells left on any Hungry Dead, including your own: which domain issued it, and how strong it still is. You also read a demon's true nature and standing on sight, which is the only reason anyone tolerates this Art at all.

**●● Miasma** — *1 Essence.* You consume what is born of all suffering in your presence. Every Health level anyone takes within ten meters, including your own, gives you a point of Essence to your maximum. It is not feeding, it leaves no tally, and it does not accrue Tether. Absorbing Miasma also comes with a pleasurable rush that is highly addicting.

**●●● The Debt Named** — *2 Essence, contested.* Speak the terms of a soul's outstanding debt aloud. The target must beat you or act on it for the scene, and anything the Hells hold a claim on acts on it whether or not it wants to. Say the wrong name and something on the far end hears you say it.

**●●●● The Open Door** — *3 Essence.* Cut a passage to a hell domain you have visited, holding for a scene. Things come through it. You do not control which. The leadership of every Basilica treats this as sufficient grounds for a writ, and the Order of Vigiles has never once had to argue the point.

**●●●●● The King's Attention** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* A Demon King bound to a throne they cannot rise from turns and looks at you. Ask one question and get a true answer, or ask one favour and receive it. The price is set by him, it is set afterwards, and it is never what you expected. Mark a Stability trigger, take the loss, and start writing your character's exit.
