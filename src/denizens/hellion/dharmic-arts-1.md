<h2 id="the-ghost-mantle">The Ghost Mantle</h2>

*Soul Art · Denial · Open to every Fellowship*

The Spirit Mantle reaches the Fellowships of the living pattern. This one reaches downstream, to the Shadowlands and the cold enclaves the Restless Dead have built into the ectonite.

**● Lesser Ghost Mantle** — *Free, passive, one turn of concentration.* For the scene you perceive anything that resonates with death in the Pale lands and they perceive you. You can speak with shades, detect ongoing hauntings, and sense if death has touched a location recently. Everything physical runs two difficulties harder while it is up and your vision lacks color.

**●● Call the Departed** — *1 Essence, contested.* Summon a specific shade by name. A relic of theirs lowers the difficulty by one; something they died holding lowers it by two. Not all of them come pleased and nothing in this ability constrains what they do when they arrive.

**●●● Greater Ghost Mantle** — *2 Essence.* Cross directly into the shadowlands at any death saturated location. Entering this way avoids the aggravated damage toll non-wraiths usually must pay to open the door. Otherwise, this ability grants no protection against Shades or other denizens of the underworld.

**●●●● The Ferry** — *3 Essence.* Eject the unanchored dead haunting a natural mooring into the underworld. Contested where the shade objects.

**●●●●● The Quiet Harbour** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Anchor a stretch of ground on both sides at once for 28 days. Shades within it cannot be compelled, dragged or consumed, the rivers of eternity will not reroute through it, and nothing wearing an obsidian mark can enter.

<h2 id="the-spirit-mantle">The Spirit Mantle</h2>

*Soul Art · Vitality · Open to every Fellowship*

Not mastery of the corpse but fluency across the barrier. The Pale Between is riddled with doors, and a Veil learns to find them the way other people learn a street grid. Note the Denial-keyed mirror of this ladder, the Ghost Mantle, which reaches the Shadowlands rather than the spirit Fellowships. Every Hungry Dead can learn that one on the Aligned track, and most Veils do.

**● Lesser Spirit Mantle** — *Free, passive, one turn of concentration to raise.* You look across the veil for the scene. You see and are seen, you can speak, you can touch lightly. Everything you attempt in the physical world runs two difficulties harder while it is up. No combat and no feeding at this level.

**●● Spirit Summons** — *1 Essence, contested.* Call a specific named spirit to you. Holding a meaningful symbol of it lowers the difficulty by one; a symbol that is one of a kind lowers it by two. A hostile spirit may resist, though most are curious enough to come anyway.

**●●● Greater Spirit Mantle** — *2 Essence.* Cross fully for the scene, into the Pale and onward to the spirit Fellowships beyond it. You can fight, feed and travel, with no protection whatsoever. You are effectively a spirit, with everything that implies.

**●●●● Spirit Ride** — *3 Essence.* Open a gateway that holds for a minute with a vessel waiting on the far side: a boat, a beast, a cart that should not run. You and your companions travel for one scene. One more Essence holds it open, or opens the way back.

**●●●●● Both Sides at Once** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* For the scene you stand wholly in the material world and wholly in the Pale, not half in either. Nothing runs at a penalty, you act, fight and feed in both, and you may take an action on one side and your next on the other without crossing. Everything else works the same way in reverse: whatever is over there can reach you here, damage taken on either side lands on the one body, and there is no side to retreat to. Being in two places at once makes you loud. Every spirit within a mile knows where you are standing and some of them will come to see what could do that.

<h2 id="godbody-of-flesh">Godbody of Flesh</h2>

*Godbody Art · Vitality · Mandatory at Creation*

Bone insists on permanence. Flesh insists on the opposite: that a body is a process, that a process can be restarted, and that the distance between a corpse and a person is a matter of degree rather than kind.

**● Warm to the Touch** — *Free, passive.* You pass for living. Breath, colour, pulse, body heat, the ability to eat and drink and bleed convincingly. Add your rating in dice to any mundane roll to be taken for a mortal. Registration paperwork does not care, and neither does a Vigiles investigator with your file open.

**●● Knitting** — *1 Essence.* Close a level of bashing or lethal, one level per turn, for as long as you can pay. Faster than ordinary healing and vastly cheaper in dignity than the alternative.

**●●● Borrowed Shape** — *2 Essence.* Reshape your face, frame, height and voice for the scene. You cannot copy a specific person's exact appearance well enough to fool someone who knows them, though you can get close enough for a bad light and a hurried glance.

**●●●● The Second Heart** — *3 Essence.* For the scene the seat behind your sternum is not there. Called shots against your weak point simply fail, and aggravated from fire and sunlight is halved, rounding up.

**●●●●● Phoenix Rising** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Instantly regain 3 health boxes, restoring the least damaged health boxes first. If in torpor this revives you. Additionally, the energy released manifests as a rush of flame which burns anyone within 5 meters for a point of aggravated damage.

<h2 id="godbody-of-ghost-flame">Godbody of Ghost-Flame</h2>

*Godbody Art · Denial · Open to every Fellowship*

The cold fire that sits in a body nobody is using. Ghost-Flame is the discipline of being less present, degree by degree, until presence is optional.

**● Corpse-Cold** — *Free, passive.* No heat, no breath, no pulse, nothing for a dog or a ward to catch. You ignore penalties from cold and from lack of air entirely. Spirits and shades read you as a dead thing and act accordingly, which cuts both ways.

**●● Grave Light** — *1 Essence.* Pale cold flame gathers on your hands for the scene. Natural weapon at Strength +1, and against shades, spirits and anything made of plasm it burns aggravated with no Essence surcharge.

**●●● Etherealness** — *2 Essence.* Become half-there for the scene. Walk through a wall at a slow pace, take nothing from a fall, and mundane weapons find you two difficulties harder. You cannot carry anything heavier than your own clothes through solid matter.

**●●●● The Whisper Body** — *3 Essence.* Fully insubstantial for the scene. You cannot touch or be touched by anything physical, you pass through the veil into the Pale Between unaided, and you cannot feed or attack while it holds.

**●●●●● The Hollow Lantern** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Burn your own presence. For 28 days, nobody who saw you in this scene can hold on to your face, your name or what you did; records survive, memories do not. It costs you a level of aggravated and it will not heal until the effect ends.
