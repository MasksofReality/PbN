*Wickedness · the perfect demon*

> *"You want the version of me that plays nice. I don't have one. What I have is a list of people I'd rather ruin than you."*

## Who They Are

The monster that hunts monsters. Devil-Tigers hold that the Hells did not corrupt them, they revealed them, and that pretending otherwise is the cowardice underneath every other Dharma. The discipline is not restraint, it is aim. Become perfectly terrible, then point it at the corrupt, the predatory and the smug. Done well this is a spiritual practice with a body count. Done badly it is exactly what everyone assumes it is, and the Fellowships are rarely wrong about which one they are looking at.

Play a Devil-Tiger to be frightening without apologising for it, and if you can carry a character whose failure state is becoming what they hunt.

**Attributes:** Physical 7, Social 5, Mental 3.

**Feeding.** On the deserving, and they are the one path that will say so out loud. Predators, traffickers, slavers, ichor-brokers who cut their product with something worse. A Tiger's hunting is framed as sentence, not appetite, which conveniently makes the ugliest method the most defensible one. Some go further and eat flesh, which is taboo everywhere and the fastest way off any path. Tigers who make a habit of it do not keep their Dharma long.

## Affinity: The Red Panoply

Raw capacity dragged out of hell. Each dot grants one permanent monstrous **Feature** you can call up at will. Remember the [infernal tell](/denizens/hellion/chi-and-feeding/#the-infernal-tell): Demon Arts leave everyone nearby a little worse for hours.

**Features.** Choose one per dot. The damage your demonic features cause is considered aggravated damage for purposes of soaking.

- **Black Iron Claws.** Natural weapon, Strength +1.
- **Demon Armour.** Two extra soak dice against lethal, plus dice equal to your rating that soak aggravated.
- **Demon Weapon.** Natural weapon, Strength +2, and it can be disarmed for the rest of the scene.
- **Fanged Maw.** Natural weapon, Strength +2, requires a successful grapple first.
- **Rope-Dart or Spiked Whiptail.** Five meters of reach, one bonus die to attack. A second purchase doubles the reach and adds venom.
- **Unbearable miasmic aura.** Two-die penalty to hit you, and anyone facing you rolls Grit or flees.
- **Wings.** Aerial manoeuvres to five meters. A second purchase grants true flight.

**● First Fruit** — *Free, passive.* One Feature, permanently yours, raised or hidden at will with a turn's concentration.

**●● Open the Armory** — *1 Essence.* A second Feature, and your rating in bonus dice on Strength rolls and hand-to-hand damage for the scene.

**●●● Third Gift** — *2 Essence.* A third Feature. Two Features may now be up at once with no turn of delay between them.

**●●●● The Whole Beast** — *3 Essence.* A fourth Feature, and every Feature you own comes up in a single turn.

**●●●●● Brutal Truth** — *5 Essence.* A fifth Feature and the full panoply at once, for the scene. Mortals who witness it roll Grit or break, and none of them are the same afterwards. Neither is your standing at Fellowship.

## Strength and Weakness

**Strength.** The best combat kit in the splat at low XP, and the only path where being visibly a monster is an asset.

**Weakness.** Wickedness can never sit Recessive for anyone, and Tigers rank it Dominant, so Wickedness imbalance is a standing condition and not an event. Politically you are the first suspect for everything, and once the Panoply is up it cannot be walked back.
