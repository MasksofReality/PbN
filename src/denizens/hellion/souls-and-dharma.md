## The Four Aspects of the Soul

You do not have one intact soul. You have four aspects, each a presence with its own appetite, and they argue.

| Aspect | It Wants | It Sounds Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Righteousness** | Order, justice, correct behaviour, respect | *"This is beneath you. Stand up. Punish him."* |
| **Vitality** | Sensation, appetite, movement, life | *"You see people. I see tender flesh. Feed."* |
| **Denial** | Stillness, cold, distance, the grave | *"Nothing matters. Sit down. Let it end."* |
| **Wickedness** | Spite, audacity, revelation, cruelty | *"Wouldn't it be funny if you ruined her life?"* |

The soul aspects are not rated and they cost nothing. At creation you rank them: **Dominant, Ascendant, Quiet, Recessive.** Your Fellowship's keyed soul must sit Dominant or Ascendant. Wickedness can never be Recessive, because the Hells put it there and it does not shrink. A Fellowship keyed to Balance places its requirement anywhere.

That ranking does three things. It sets your [soul gate](/denizens/hellion/chi-and-feeding/#the-soul-gate). It tells other Hungry Dead what you are before you open your mouth, because they can feel the order on you. And it decides which way you come apart.

## Soul Imbalance

When a trigger fires, roll the **opposing** aspect's Psyche trait at difficulty 6, or difficulty 8 for a severe trigger. You hold yourself together with the part of you that wants the opposite thing, and your devil is what keeps your saint honest.

| Aspect Taking Over | You Become | Resist On | Set Off By |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Righteousness** | Authoritarian, punishing anyone you judge unworthy | Self-Control | Your authority challenged by someone corrupt; successfully punishing a wrongdoer; being begged for mercy |
| **Vitality** | Gluttonous, savage, or fleeing at any cost | Conscience | Eating human flesh; feeding at 3 Essence or less; heavy injury, above all fire or sunlight |
| **Denial** | Cold, clinical, morbid, or catatonic | Grit | Too long among graves and shrines; being refused by the living for what you are; killing someone from your old life |
| **Wickedness** | Cruel, openly, and smiling about it | Conscience | Using a Demon Art on an innocent; watching something awful succeed beautifully; being humiliated by your own principles |

Failure costs you 1 current Stability and you lose control for the scene, in whatever direction the table above points. Failure while an aspect matching the trigger sits Dominant costs 2 instead, since the thing taking hold of you had the run of the house already.

At current Stability 7 or below, add a difficulty to every imbalance roll. At 3 or below an imbalance can seize you at any Essence level, whether or not you were anywhere near empty.

Three of the four are not primarily violent, and Denial imbalance is usually a character going quiet and cold mid-conversation, which unsettles a table considerably more than a fight does. **Warn people out of character before you go off**, and always before you point it at their character.

## What the Fellowships Add to Stability

Beyond the standard triggers, mark a Stability trigger for the scene if your character did any of the following.

- Ate the flesh of a sapient being
- Fed inside another Hungry Dead's held territory without leave
- Let a soul be taken by the Hells, or delivered one there

You do not begin play with Eldritch, and no Hungry Dead may take it at creation. To remain sane your memories of the Hells are locked under a haze of disassociative amnesia. Staff award Eldritch in play or you do not have it.

**Catharsis works normally.** Your Vice restores 1 current Stability once per cycle, played out in a real scene with witnesses and consequences. Recovery is far slower than loss and always will be. Stability is a condition you are in, not a resource you manage.

## Dharma

A Dharma is your character's answer to *"what am I for, now that I'm dead?"* Part religion, part philosophy, part training regimen, and a theory about how a dead monster becomes something else. Every one of them is ultimately a method for cutting the anchor and reaching the rivers. They disagree completely about how, which is exactly why the Fellowships argue.

Your Dharma rating runs 1 to 5. **Everyone starts at 1**, however ancient the concept, and other Hungry Dead can feel the number on you the way you can feel theirs. It gates the clean feeding methods. It is not a morality track and it does not move because you were good this week.

To raise it, you must wait a number of months equal to the rating you wish to reach, and submit at least one role play log per month in the interim demonstrating your walking of your Dharma path. When you reach the requirement for Dharma 5, you must undergo a ST-run hellish tribulation in which the hells try one last time to drag you back into the hells by whatever means necessary. **In a perfect world this means it will take at least 14 months to reach Dharma 5.**

**Dharma catches you once a month.** The first time in a lunar month that you would be forced into a Breaking roll at zero Stability, roll Dharma at difficulty 6 in place of Conscience. That is the path holding you up, and it does not stack with anything else that stands in for the same roll. Succeed and you have bought yourself the month. Fail and you lose a point of permanent Stability as normal, and a point of Dharma with it, because the path did not hold.

At Dharma 5 the chain slackens enough to cut, and the road that has been closed to you since the day you died opens. What that looks like is between you and staff. It is where the character's story ends, not a build target, and it is the only clean ending any Hungry Dead gets.

At Dharma 0 you are a mindless thing lurching from one imbalance to the next. It is recoverable with help. Barely.
