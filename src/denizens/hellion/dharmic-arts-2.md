## Jeweled Eyes

*Demon Art · Wickedness · Open to every Fellowship*

Hell is efficient. It learns what you want, it learns what you fear, and it uses both with cruel efficiency. Everyone who came back brought some of that home. Jeweled Eyes is the discipline of admitting it.

**● Read the Wanting** — *Free, passive.* Look at someone and know the strongest thing they want and the strongest thing they fear, right now. Mortals give it up without noticing. Anything supernatural and resisting will contest.

**●● Demon's Whisper** — *1 Essence.* One suggestion, which the target will rationalise afterwards as their own idea. One success buys a small thing done without thinking. Two or three buys something they would ordinarily have argued about. Four or more buys something they will spend a long time explaining to themselves. Nothing here makes anyone act against their true Nature.

**●●● Borrowed Face** — *2 Essence.* For the scene every viewer sees somebody they trust, and no two of them see the same person. It does not survive a conversation of any length with anyone who knows the borrowed face well.

**●●●● The Long Look** — *3 Essence, contested.* Set a single standing compulsion in someone that holds for 28 days: an aversion, a loyalty, a habit they did not have. It fades if they are shown plain evidence of what was done to them.

**●●●●● The Whole Wanting** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* For the scene everyone who can perceive you sees whoever they want most, standing where you are standing, and understands without being told that you are the only road to it. Mortals are helpless and will hand over almost anything short of their own lives to keep looking. Anything supernatural contests, and even the ones who win spend the scene two difficulties worse on anything aimed at you, because winning does not mean not seeing it. When it drops they know precisely what was done and who did it, and for 28 days afterwards they go looking for that feeling again in whatever will approximate it. This is how Jeweled Eyes makes its long-term enemies, and hell taught you the trick for the same reason.

## Tears of Hell

*Demon Art · Wickedness · Open to every Fellowship*

Miasma is the currency of the Ten-Thousand Hells and suffering is what mints it. You spent a long time as raw material. Some of the process came back with you, and it comes out through a wet vertical eye that opens in your forehead and does not blink.

**● The Eye Opens** — *Free, passive.* The eye opens on command and you see what is actually there: spirits, wards, glyph-work, the seams where the Pale runs close, and straight through darkness and illusion alike. It is unmistakable and nobody who sees it forgets it.

**●● Miasmic Tears** — *1 Essence.* Draw a stream of burning ichor from your third eye and make a whip-like strike. Deals lethal damage which cannot be soaked but is dodgeable.

**●●● Cauterise** — *2 Essence.* Pour the fire of the Hell of Black Flames into an open wound in something you are touching. Deals bashing damage. It unmakes rather than injures and nothing soaks it.

**●●●● Molten Gaze** — *3 Essence.* A beam of hellfire erupts from your third eye at a single target. Deals 1 point of aggravated damage and is dodgeable by anyone who can move. Flammable materials focused upon combust as normal. The scorch it leaves is legible to any Hungry Dead for a month, and it tells them exactly who did it.

**●●●●● The Scalding Word** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Everything you say for the scene manifests as hellish torment. Anyone who hears you rolls Grit or takes 1 aggravated damage and gives ground. Allies are not exempt and you cannot make them exempt.

## Godbody of Blood

*Godbody Art · Vitality · Open to every Fellowship*

The Art that makes the resemblance to the Lilum literal, and the reason a certain kind of Hungry Dead can clear a room in Purgatory simply by ordering a drink. It does not make you one of them. It cannot ghoul, it cannot bond, and no dot on this ladder ever will.

**● The Red Thread** — *Free, passive.* A drop of someone's blood gives you their general direction and their broad mood for a night, at any distance short of another realm. It does not cross into the Pale and it does not follow anyone through a waygate.

**●● Corrosive Ichor** — *1 Essence.* A mouthful of your own ichor thrown as a corrosive. Deals 1 lethal damage, but as an ordinary physical effect, it can be both soaked and dodged.

**●●● Blood Reckoning** — *2 Essence.* Taste a mouthful of blood and know what the owner is, how badly hurt they are, when they last fed or were fed on, and whether anything has a claim on them, but not who that is. It is a knowledge of splat. Nightfolk can contest this if they are aware it is happening to them.

**●●●● Draw the Tide** — *3 Essence.* Someone within sight bleeds toward you and you drink it out of the air. Deals 1 point lethal damage, and every level you inflict transfers to you as Essence at the standard rate. It counts as feeding in every respect, including the tally and the Tether. Unable to be contested except for by Lilum; losing the contest by 3 or more means they *reverse* the flow and instead damage you.

**●●●●● Blood Curse** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Cause the blood of a target whose blood you have tasted this scene to begin to boil, dealing 1 point of aggravated damage. You can pay an additional essence each turn thereafter you do nothing else and continue the same effect for as long as you have the essence to fuel it.

## Godbody of Jade

*Godbody Art · Denial · Open to every Fellowship*

Stone does not want anything. That is the lesson. Jade is the least popular Godbody among newcomers and the most common among elders, which tells you something about how the centuries go.

**● Set** — *Free, passive.* While you do not move from where you stand, you cannot be moved by anything short of a gentry-class being, and you add your rating in dice to soak against bashing.

**●● Jadeskin** — *1 Essence.* Your skin greens and hardens for the scene. Two extra dice of armour against bashing and lethal, and no Dexterity penalty for it.

**●●● The Stone Wants Nothing** — *2 Essence.* For the scene your appetites simply stop. No Attrition roll can be forced on you, no soul imbalance can seize you, and fear, pain and temptation find no purchase because there is nothing in you for them to pull on. You also cannot feed, you cannot regain Willpower, and every Social roll you make runs two difficulties harder, because the thing people are talking to is not answering them as a person.

**●●●● Root and Rise** — *3 Essence.* Reshape stone and earth within reach for the scene: open a wall, close a doorway, raise cover, sink a foundation. Slow, obvious, and impossible to argue with.

**●●●●● Avatar of the Mountain King** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* Become a living statue. Sunlight and fire do nothing to you, ordinary weapons do nothing to you, and you remain this way for a full day. You see and hear everything. You cannot speak nor end it early, which can be as awkward to explain as it sounds.
