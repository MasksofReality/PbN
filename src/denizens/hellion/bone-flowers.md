## Who They Are

The ones who tend the dead. The Gravekeepers hold that the way out is through attention, that the dead are owed care, memory and accurate records, and that a monster who provides those things is earning a place among them. They are morticians, archivists, historians, keepers of graves and grave-goods. Their patience unsettles other Hungry Dead. Their memory unsettles them more.

Play a Bone Flower to be the one everybody comes to for information, and the one nobody wants to owe a favour.

**Attributes:** Mental 7, Physical 5, Social 3.

**Feeding.** From place, and from the properly mourned. A tended grave-field, a shrine room behind a mortuary, a family plot nobody visits any more. Those who do hunt feed from the dying rather than the healthy, and say the *Prayer for the Eaten* afterwards whether or not anyone is watching.

## Affinity: The Godbody of Bone

Bone and Flesh are called the straightforward Godbody Arts, which is a misreading. Bone is not about literal bones. It is about the connection between the corpse-body and death itself. Even a cremated body leaves an enduring skeleton of its life somewhere.

**● Boneyard Shroud** — *Free, passive.* While you stay in shadow and move no faster than a walk, you are undetected, and you stay undetected stepping through light into another shadow. In death-resonant ground (grave-fields, ossuaries, execution sites) you may move as fast as you like and it holds for an hour. While it is up you look like a dried-out skeletal corpse.

**●● Malleable Bone** — *1 Essence.* Soften or harden your skeleton for the scene. Softened, you slip bonds and squeeze through gaps a body should not fit. Hardened, your unarmed strikes become natural weapons at Strength +1 lethal, and you add your rating in dice to soak against bashing and lethal.

**●●● Ossuary Toolbox** — *2 Essence.* For the scene, spend a turn to draw a bone out and shape it: a key, a crowbar edge, razor claws, a forearm sword. Weapons made this way are natural weapons at Strength +1. Each new shape costs another turn.

**●●●● Bone Needle Cloud** — *3 Essence.* You burst apart into a cloud of razor needles. Damaging, lethal, unsoaked, out to ten meters. Anyone who can move may dodge; anyone behind cover is untouched. You reform on your next turn, anywhere inside the blast.

**●●●●● Grave-Beast** — *5 Essence and 1 Willpower.* For one hour you are a bone-plated horror running with black ichor. You can devour living souls and shades alike for Chi. However everyone, including Mundane Humans, will see that you are a bone-plated horror running with black ichor and will act accordingly to such a sight. This is not discreet nor is it publicly welcomed.

## Strength and Weakness

**Strength.** The most self-sufficient feeder on any path, which frees you to play politics from a position nobody else can afford.

**Weakness.** Denial-heavy by construction, and Denial imbalance is triggered by exactly the life you have chosen. Your Nest is a leash. Everyone who matters knows where you sleep and what you cannot afford to lose.
