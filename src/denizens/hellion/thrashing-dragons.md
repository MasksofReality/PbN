## Who They Are

Alive in a way only the dead manage. Thrashing Dragons hold that the Hells taught them one true thing, that existence is sensation and the crime is not appetite but numbness. So they eat, fight, dance, paint, bed anyone that catches their eye and constantly move from one bad romance to the next, on the stated theory that this is a spiritual discipline. It is one. It also looks identical to hedonism from outside, and its followers find it hilarious you can't see that is the point.

Play a Thrashing Dragon to be in the middle of everything, moving faster than everyone, with the most social freedom and the least dignity.

**Attributes:** Physical 7, Social 5, Mental 3.

**Feeding.** From the living, up close, and often. This is the path most likely to keep a herd of willing mortals, most likely to fold feeding into whatever else it is doing, and most likely to burn a whole pool in one night for the pleasure of a warm body that breathes and tastes. Also the path most likely to be sitting at three Essence when something goes wrong, which is a Vitality imbalance trigger by itself.

## Affinity: Black Wind

In hell the universe is your enemy. It lashes out and it never lets you catch your breath. A Hungry Dead who takes up the Demon Art of Motion learns that the only way to endure is to be faster, and that if you are aiming to hit you should be aiming to kill.

**● Quickened** — *Free, passive.* Add your rating to initiative and to any Dexterity-linked reflex pool.

**●● Storm Step** — *1 Essence.* Your rating in bonus dice on movement, dodge and Dexterity actions for the scene.

**●●● Two Places** — *2 Essence.* One extra action per turn for the scene, and it does not split your pool.

**●●●● Unseen Passage** — *3 Essence.* For the scene you dodge every incoming attack with your full pool, on top of your regular action, and once during it you may cross any single scene-sized space before anyone present can react.

**●●●●● The Wind Itself** — *5 Essence.* Two extra actions per turn for the scene, at most one of them an attack, and none of them splitting your pool. She was across the alley before the sound of the door reached him.

## Strength and Weakness

**Strength.** The fastest thing in the city, the hardest to pin down, and the easiest path to make friends on. Nobody passes for living better than a Dragon.

**Weakness.** Vitality high and Denial low means Vitality imbalance is your default failure, and it is the loudest, most public and least forgivable kind. Your feeding habits leave witnesses who remember.
