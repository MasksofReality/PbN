## Gnosis

Gnosis is Essence. Every ordinary rule about Essence applies to you unchanged.

**Ambient recovery.** At midnight server time you regain 5 Gnosis and your current Willpower refreshes fully. On a pool of 7 that is most of your capacity back each night. A Gnosis 5 Garou gets half, which is why higher-tier characters live so much closer to their pacts than you currently need to.

**Replenishment** comes from fulfilling a pact with a spirit, or from acting meaningfully in line with your Patron's ideals. That earns 1 Gnosis per two post rounds, and you have to roleplay it to earn it.

**The nightly refresh can fail.** A Garou cut off from every spirit and unable to serve their Patron gets nothing at midnight. Staff will call it whenever the story has put you somewhere that makes the refresh a lie: a sealed paratech cell, deep Bitterthorn, inside a ward spirits cannot cross. There is no Garou exception to this. If you are cut off, you are cut off.

**Attrition and going feral.** You cannot activate a costed Art at 0 Gnosis, though free tier-1 Arts keep working. At the end of every scene you spend at zero, roll Willpower at difficulty 6. Fail and you go feral for the rest of the scene, hyper-aggressive and territorial as the balance between beast and person comes apart. Botch and control does not return until the next midnight reset. Once your Stability drops to Unmoored, it can happen at any Gnosis level at all.

**Pushing is what Rage used to be.** Pay 1 Willpower and 1 aggravated to cover an Art's entire base cost, or to buy a single die at one aggravated apiece. Buying dice at that price is a bad trade and is meant to be. Covering the cost is how a spent Garou still acts when it matters. Aggravated is the worst currency on your sheet, and that is the whole point of the exchange rate.

## Forms, Regeneration, and the Bane

### The Five Forms

Shifting is free. No Gnosis, no roll, one change per round. It is part of what you are, and you never spend anything to do it. Form bonuses are mundane only and never touch an Art roll.

| Form | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | No modifiers. Passes as human, completely. |
| **Glabro** | +1 die to mundane Strength- and Stamina-based pools. Visibly wrong, so +1 difficulty on Social rolls with strangers, though this century most people who notice read it as wyrd-taint and leave it there. |
| **Crinos** | +3 dice to mundane Strength-based pools including damage, +2 dice to soak bashing and lethal, and unarmed attacks deal lethal. Cannot speak any human language. See below. |
| **Hispo** | +2 dice to mundane Strength-based pools and to Survival, tracking and pursuit. Bite deals lethal. No tools, no speech. |
| **Lupus** | +2 dice to mundane Stealth, Survival and tracking pools. No tools, no speech. Passes as a large wolf, which inside a Basilica is a problem of its own, except in Calgary, where nobody blinks at anything with fur. |

You have one health track of seven levels, with wound penalties as written, in every form. There is no second track.

### Crinos in Public

The war form is enormous, unmistakable, and impossible to explain away. It carries two separate consequences and you want both of them in your head before you shift.

**It frightens people.** Any mortal who sees Crinos resists on Grit alone, at difficulty 6 modified for circumstance, or flees, freezes, or comes apart. Nightfolk resist on Grit plus their highest Art. This is not a Gift and it costs you nothing. It is what the shape does to a mind.

**It is a registration incident.** The Masquerade is over. The Pax is not. Being seen in Crinos by people who did not consent to see it is exactly the perception of using inhuman advantage over a human that revokes BRD status and issues a writ to the Aberrant Hunters, who do not investigate twice. In Salamanca the paperwork will be in order before anyone asks what happened to you.

Inside the walls, treat Crinos as a last resort. Outside them, use it freely. That is what it is for.

### Regeneration, and Where It Stops

| Damage | Rate |
|---|---|
| Bashing | 1 level per round, free, automatic, even while acting |
| Lethal | 1 level per minute of rest with no acting, free, or **1 Gnosis per level** to close it instantly |
| Aggravated | The mortal lethal rate, and **nothing accelerates it**: not Gnosis, not a Rite, not a Gift, not a fetish |

That last row is the shape of the whole splat. Aggravated heals at the lethal rate and cannot be hurried unless an effect names aggravated explicitly, and nothing in this system does. A Garou sitting at Crippled from an obsidian wound is looking at a month per level. At Incapacitated, two.

### The Bane: Obsidian, Fire, and Your Court's Material

Obsidian bypasses regeneration entirely and deals aggravated to every Garou. So does fire. So does your Patron court's material. Aggravated cannot be soaked, and wounds from these three scar permanently, closing early only through a Rite staff have specifically approved.

Obsidian does not send you into frenzy on contact the way the old silver did. What it does is worse and slower. It leaves a wound that will not close, on a body built around the certainty that wounds close. Take obsidian, fire, or court-material damage and roll Self-Control at difficulty 6 or go feral from the shock, resolving the same way Attrition frenzy does.

### Eshtarra's Immunity

No cybernetic or paratech implant takes in a Garou body. Grafts reject, always, with no roll. Put this in both columns of your ledger, because it cuts both ways: nothing can be done to you that way, and nothing can be done for you that way either. You cannot take the Grafted Merit, and no Artificer, Ghostwright, or Institute surgeon has ever found a workaround worth the name.

## Merits

Merits cost 1 freebie per point at creation and 2 XP per point afterwards, they are story-gated once play begins, and you do not get a refund if you lose one. There are no Flaws in this system. Tribal Costs are Stigma-class and nobody pays for them.

| Merit | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Pactbound** | 2 | A standing pact with a named spirit. Replenishment is reliably available, and you take −1 difficulty on mundane rolls to negotiate with that brood. |
| **Territory Sense** | 1 | Name a territory. −1 difficulty to notice what has changed in it since you were last there. |
| **Passes for Taint** | 2 | Your Glabro reads as wyrd-taint to a casual eye. +2 difficulty for others to identify you as Garou by sight alone. |
| **Steady Under the Moon** | 2 | −1 difficulty on Self-Control rolls made against your own Tribal Cost or against bane-shock. |
| **Kinfolk Household** | 2 | A family who know what you are and shelter you anyway. |
| **Waystone-Keeper** | 2 | You hold and maintain a waystone. |
| **Rite-Learned** | 2 | One more Rite beyond your starting Minor Rite, at a tier staff approve. |
| **Iron Belly** | 1 | You can eat, and keep down, anything that was ever alive. Worth more outside the shimmer than you would think. |
| **Hard to Rattle** | 2 | −1 difficulty on Grit rolls against one named category of horror you have faced and survived. Naming *fire* is legal, obvious, and cheap at 2 for a reason. |
| **Unremarkable** | 2 | +2 difficulty for anyone trying to recall, describe, or trace you through witnesses. |

## Harano

Harano is your Stability. The black grief is not atmosphere and it is not a mood your Storyteller narrates at you. It is a number, it sits on your sheet, and it moves.

The Garou words for the states are **Composed** at 8 to 10, **Fraying** at 6 to 7, **Slipping** at 4 to 5, **Unmoored** at 2 to 3, where the feral turn can come at any Gnosis level, and **Breaking** at 0 to 1, where every trigger threatens permanent Stability.

At permanent Stability 1 a Garou is no longer reliably themselves. That means a conversation with staff, and then retirement, conversion to an NPC, or a final scene of your choosing.

### What Sets It Off For Us

The ordinary list of triggers applies in full and nothing about being Garou softens it. On top of that, mark one if your character does any of the following in a scene.

**Let corruption stand.** Knowingly left a Bane, a taint, or a corrupted place in position when they could have confronted it. This is the Garou trigger, and it will fire more often than any other, because the mandate is infinite and you are not.

**Turned on their own.** Struck, betrayed, or abandoned a packmate, a Kinfolk, or a Garou who had reason to trust them.

**Fed the fear.** A mortal who had no idea what you were learned it from your body, in terror, and cannot unlearn it.

**Broke the Litany.** Each of the nine sung songs is a trigger for the Garou who breaks it, judged against what it plainly says. The tenth song is deliberately unwritten and is never a trigger.

Where one of these and an ordinary trigger describe the same act, mark it once.

### Getting Stability Back: The Kept Pact

In a staff-witnessed scene where you complete a pact with a spirit or perform a Rite of Renown in front of your community, you recover 1 current Stability. Once per lunar month, not once per cycle. It needs another character present, a spirit or a community satisfied, and a scene log.

It is deliberately slow, and it costs you something you cannot manufacture on your own, which is other people's participation. That is the point of it. Whatever holds a Garou together, it is not solitude.

### Eldritch, for Garou

Every dot of Eldritch permanently reduces both your maximum and your current Stability, forever. No Garou takes it at creation, and it moves only when staff award it.

It opens exactly one door. A character with Eldritch may roll Intelligence plus Eldritch to understand what a working they are watching actually is and does. Arcane never substitutes for it, and Arcane costs you nothing, so you can hold Arcane 5 on spirit taxonomy and sleep perfectly well.

You will never pick up Eldritch from exposure to the ordinary supernatural, because you *are* the ordinary supernatural. The roads that lead there are specific, and all of them hurt. What Eshtarra actually did to climb from Incarna to Celestine, and what it cost her. What happened to Gaia the Elder's children, who did not die so much as stop having ever existed. The Interstice, and what the last of the first generation will not say about it. The Mistcrown, and the attention that notices expeditions. The courts that refused, and why. And for a Glass Walker, the quintessence reactors, which everyone knows exist and almost nobody understands.

Worth doing the arithmetic before the first dot lands: a Garou at Eldritch 2 with Conscience 3 and Self-Control 3 holds permanent Stability 4, which is one bad month from Slipping.

## Rites, Renown, and Rank

**Rites are not Arts.** They are communal ceremonies that invoke pacts already standing between the Garou and Eshtarra's spirit network, and they need precise methods, witnesses, and time. You roll Intelligence plus Arcane at difficulty 6, pay a Gnosis cost set by the Rite's tier, and measure the duration in scenes rather than rounds. Rites never roll Art plus Gnosis, they never add dice to an Art roll, and you cannot perform one in the middle of a fight.

You begin with one Minor Rite, chosen with your Mentor. Everything after that comes from play, from Renown milestones, and from instruction by Theurges and elders.

| Category | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Rites of Accord** | Forming, honouring and renewing agreements with spirits, and marking truces |
| **Rites of Death** | Honouring the fallen and sending spirits to rest. In this world, the busiest category we have. |
| **Mystic Rites** | Talisman Dedication, the Rite of the Opened Caern (extraordinarily rare), protective workings |
| **Minor Rites** | Cleansing, meditation, and the daily acknowledgements that keep a Garou spiritually intact |
| **Rites of Punishment** | Ostracism, the Stone of Scorn, and whatever else a community does when it has to act against its own |
| **Rites of Renown** | Rites of passage and Rank advancement |
| **Seasonal Rites** | The turning of the year, attended by everyone |
| **Tribal Rites** | Specific to each tribe and its totem |

**Renown and Rank are social.** Glory, Honor, and Wisdom get tracked, awarded, and argued over, and they gate Rites, mentorship, standing, and who gets listened to at a moot. They are not dice, they do not modify rolls, and they are not Backgrounds. Everyone starts at Rank 1, Cliath.

Reaching tiers 4 and 5 in any path is expected to come with an in-fiction teacher of appropriate Rank and a sign-off from staff. That is a house process, not a rule of the system, and the details live in the application handbook.
