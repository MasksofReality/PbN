*Peacekeepers of the tribes*

## Who They Are

They were the Children of Gaia, and the renaming at the moment of their rebirth was deliberate. They are not the Elder's children recovered, they are the Younger's children made new. Where the old tribe sought peace because the world was worth preserving, these seek it because the world has to be rebuilt, and nothing gets built while everyone is swinging. They are the diplomats and the healers. They keep the conversation open long after the other tribes have stopped listening, and they remember more clearly than anyone that Eshtarra herself built a world on cooperation between powers who should have been enemies.

## Gift of Eshtarra: The Healer's Grace

+2 dice on mundane Medicine rolls, and on mundane Social rolls made to de-escalate a conflict.

## Tribal Cost: The Peacekeeper's Burden

To enter violence before a non-violent option has been honestly attempted, roll Self-Control at difficulty 6. This is not pacifism. They will fight, and they are good at it. It is the weight of actually believing another way exists, right up until it clearly does not.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● Sense the Wyrd** *(passive, free)*. Corruption and Bane presence register as physical wrongness: a sickness in the ground, a taste in the air. No roll, no cost, no off switch.

**●● Mother's Touch** *(1 Gnosis)*. Lay hands on another living being and close 1 lethal or 2 bashing, plus 1 more level per net success past the first. Never on yourself. Even beings who distrust Garou instinctively trust this.

**●●● Calm** *(2 Gnosis)*. Your voice carries Eshtarra's peace into the room. **1:** one being of your choice currently in frenzy or any other loss of control immediately receives a fresh Willpower roll at difficulty 6 to shake it. **2–3:** everyone in earshot receives that roll, and for the scene the *first* attack anyone initiates in your presence takes +1 difficulty. **4+:** as above, and for the scene everyone in your presence rolls to resist frenzy or compulsion at −1 difficulty. Calm never compels peace. It makes peace the easier road, which is the whole tribe in one sentence.

**●●●● The Living Earth** *(3 Gnosis)*. Sanctify a space. Inside it, natural healing rates double, healing Arts roll at −1 difficulty, corruption cannot take root, and a hostile spirit has to beat you in a contest to enter at all. **1:** a room, until midnight. **2–3:** a hall or a grove, for a week. **4+:** a compound, for a lunar month, tended weekly at 1 Gnosis. Aggravated wounds heal at their own miserable rate even here.

**●●●●● Eshtarra's Embrace** *(5 Gnosis)*. Become a conduit for the Younger herself for one scene. Every allied being present regenerates 1 bashing or lethal per round, their choice. Wyrd corruption in the area is suppressed and stays suppressed for a week afterwards. Every Bane in your presence activates its powers at +2 difficulty. Aggravated damage is untouched, during the scene and after. When it ends the channel closes hard: you cannot spend Gnosis for the rest of the cycle, and all your mundane rolls take +1 difficulty until midnight.
