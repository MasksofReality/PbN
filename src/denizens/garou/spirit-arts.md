## How Your Kit Is Put Together

You have access to four paths, which is the limit for anyone, so a Garou's kit is complete and closed from the day you make the character:

1. **Your Lineage path.** Cityborn, Wildborn or Traveler. Aligned.
2. **Your Tribe path.** Aligned. See each [tribe's page](/denizens/garou/) for its Spirit Art.
3. **Your Patron path**, granted at the Rite of Weighing. Aligned.
4. **One Pact path.** Stone, Wind, Flame or Water. Nonaligned.

At creation you take two of your three Aligned paths, and one of them must be your Lineage. One starts at two dots and the other at one.

**Discordant paths** are another group's Aligned path from inside our own splat: a second Lineage, another tribe's, another court's. Thirty-nine XP to reach 5, never at creation, never with freebies, and always requiring a route established in play that staff sign off on. A teacher breaking their own tradition. An unusual pact. A debt called in. At roughly thirteen months of gross income for the capstone, these are scenery.

**Restricted paths** need both a teacher and staff permission before you may learn them at all, never at creation, and they advance on the Nonaligned track once you have them. These are the paths of the Celestine courts that refused Eshtarra's Garou at the Conclave. They exist. They did not choose you, because they chose nobody. A Garou who finds one of them willing to teach has a story that starts with *why*.

Spirit Arts belong to the Garou and to nobody else. No Lilum, Lost, Sorcerer, or mortal holds one at any price.

## How to Read an Entry

Roll Art plus Gnosis at difficulty 6. Tier 1 is free. Tier 2 costs 1 Gnosis. Tiers 3, 4 and 5 cost 2, 3 and 5.

Effects grade in bands of 1, then 2 to 3, then 4 or more net successes, because no acting pool goes past ten dice and anything keyed to 5 or more would simply never happen against a peer.

Anything marked **Contested** stays contested even against a helpless or unaware target. Everything unmarked works the normal way, which means a mortal who is unaware or not actively resisting is not contested at all, and then you mark a Stability trigger, because mortals always count as helpless in front of an Art.

Art damage is 1 level on any success, plus 1 more per net success past the first. It goes unsoaked unless the effect is an ordinary physical one, and it is aggravated only where the Art unmakes rather than injures.

## Lineage Paths

### Cityborn

*Born inside the walls, raised in a community with a divine mandate and a great many neighbours.*

**● Two Worlds** *(passive, free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane Social rolls with humans, and on rolls to get through Basilica institutions: permits, records, hearings, queues. You were raised here and it shows.

**●● Jam the Works** *(1 Gnosis)*. One paratech or salvaged scitech device within sight fails for the scene. **1:** a hand-held or household device. **2–3:** a vehicle system, a ward-lock, a surveillance cluster. **4+:** a warded or Institute-hardened system, which somebody will notice when it comes back up.

**●●● Reshape Object** *(2 Gnosis)*. Reshape a natural or crafted object you are holding without changing what it is made of. A steel bar flows into a blade, a plank into a shelter frame, a chain into a hook. The change lasts until something reshapes it again. **1:** a simple change to something hand-held. **2–3:** a complex change, or anything up to door-sized. **4+:** a fine mechanism such as a working lock or a true edge, or a man-sized mass. A weapon you make this way is an ordinary weapon: mundane pool, soakable, a blade at Strength +2 lethal.

**●●●● Disquiet** *(3 Gnosis, Contested)*. Project a deep wrongness at one target, the animal certainty that they should not be anywhere near you. **1:** deeply uneasy, and +1 difficulty on anything they attempt against you this scene. **2–3:** they leave your presence if any exit exists, and will not act against you or anyone with you unless attacked. **4+:** as above, and they will not come back to this place before the next midnight.

**●●●●● Assimilation** *(5 Gnosis)*. For up to a full cycle you are supernaturally indistinguishable from a mundane human. **1:** you pass every passive scrutiny. **2–3:** active mundane examination and passive supernatural senses read you as human. **4+:** deliberate supernatural probes, Kinship Sense included, have to beat your figure in a contest to find anything at all. While it runs you cannot spend Gnosis or activate any other Art.

### Traveler

*Descended from the nomadic packs who kept the earliest traditions before anyone had dreamed up a Basilica. Scouts, messengers, traders, storytellers, and the bridge between the isolated Wildborn and the settled Cityborn.*

**● Spiritual Intuition** *(passive, free)*. You see spirits at full resolution without stepping sideways, and you know at a glance whether one is healthy, damaged, hostile, or bound.

**●● The Road Knows** *(1 Gnosis)*. The safe route to anywhere you have been, and whether it has changed. **1:** the way, as you left it. **2–3:** the way, plus what has changed on it since you last came through. **4+:** the way, the changes, and the safest detour around each one.

**●●● Sense Corruption** *(2 Gnosis)*. An active scan of a large area for every trace of Wyrd corruption: type, severity, and how fresh. **1:** presence and rough location. **2–3:** a full map of the area's corruption with age and kind. **4+:** as above, plus the direction each trace's source lies in.

**●●●● See into the Pale** *(3 Gnosis)*. Perceive the Pale Between, the deeper layer behind the Penumbra, without stepping sideways, in full detail, for a scene. **1:** see and hear it clearly. **2–3:** converse freely and be clearly understood. **4+:** things hiding in the Pale must contest your successes to stay hidden from you.

**●●●●● Sacred Wound** *(5 Gnosis, Contested)*. Turn the weight of the old Garou's total failure onto one target within sight. **1:** staggered, +2 difficulty on everything they attempt this scene. **2–3:** incapacitated by guilt and shame for the scene, able to defend themselves and nothing more. **4+:** as above, and no Replenishment of any kind restores their Essence before the next midnight. It works on anything capable of guilt.

### Wildborn *(Restricted)*

*Natural wolves chosen by the spirits and uplifted by the worldsoul herself. The rarest lineage and the one most burdened by other people's expectations.*

First shift comes at two to four years old, and on first taking Homid a Wildborn looks about nineteen to twenty-three.

**● Prey Mind** *(passive, free)*. −1 difficulty to find cover, escape routes, safe refuges, and the exact moment a pursuer overcommits.

**●● Sense the Unnatural** *(1 Gnosis)*. Supernatural presence and Wyrd corruption within line of sight. **1:** something is wrong, and roughly where. **2–3:** what kind of wrong, and how strong. **4+:** a clean read of every unnatural presence in sight, including ones actively hiding.

**●●● Wolf's Song** *(2 Gnosis)*. Howl, in any form, and the natural animals answer. **1:** the animals nearby, and their current impressions. **2–3:** everything within a mile, actively watching, for the scene. **4+:** the countryside rises into a coordinated screen of eyes and ears that holds until the next midnight.

**●●●● Elemental Favor** *(3 Gnosis)*. In a natural setting, ask the spirits of earth, water, wind, or fire for one specific service that natural forces could achieve. **1:** small and slow. **2–3:** significant, arriving within minutes. **4+:** dramatic and immediate.

**●●●●● Earth Mother's Embrace** *(5 Gnosis)*. For one scene you are a pure expression of wild nature: lethal damage regenerates at 1 level per round even while you act, every natural animal present serves you without question, and any Art or Bane effect that would compel, corrupt, or terrify you simply fails. Aggravated is untouched. When the scene ends you cannot spend Gnosis for the rest of the cycle, and until the next midnight every mundane roll takes +1 difficulty.

## Patron Paths

Auspice used to come from the moon. Luna has been missing for centuries.

Eshtarra's first children started with only what she and her own court could give them, and after fifty years of service the Conclave concluded that it was not enough. The Celestines agreed the case had merit, then refused to bless a whole tribe or lineage ever again. Now Judges of each court attend the **Rite of Weighing** and consider one soul at a time.

Five courts were willing. Their names are the old ones. Their meanings are not.

Your patronage is a calling rather than a job description. A Philodox who becomes a soldier has not abandoned Zarok, they have carried the Philodox's question into a fight. Your court shapes how you approach a role. It never tells you which roles you may hold.

| Court | Patron | Role | Bane Material | Attribute Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ragabash** | Mitanu, the Clever Rogue (Mercury) | Questioner, scout, the necessary discomfort | Mercury or emerald | Mental / Physical / Social |
| **Theurge** | Lu-Bat, the Great Conciliator (Saturn) | Spirit-speaker, healer, rite-keeper | Lead or blue sapphire | Mental / Social / Physical |
| **Philodox** | Zarok, the Lawgiver (Jupiter) | Judge, mediator, keeper of the Litany | Tin or yellow sapphire | Social / Mental / Physical |
| **Galliard** | Tambiyah, the Veiled Sage (Venus) | Lorekeeper, voice of the pack | Copper or diamond | Social / Mental / Physical |
| **Ahroun** | Nerigal, the Ice Warrior (Mars) | Warrior, protector, the edge of the blade | Pure iron or jasper | Physical / Social / Mental |
| **Unclaimed** | — | Not yet chosen | None. Obsidian and fire only. | Player's choice, with staff approval |

### Ragabash

*Mitanu's Path. The Clever Rogue, Mercury.*

**● Open Seal** *(free)*. Any mundane lock, latch, or seal opens at your touch, with no tools. **1:** simple mechanical. **2–3:** complex mechanical, or unmonitored paratech. **4+:** monitored paratech, though the log will show that *something* opened it.

**●● Blur of the Milky Eye** *(1 Gnosis)*. While you stay still and quiet, observers take +2 difficulty to notice you or to recall you afterwards. Movement or sound ends it instantly.

**●●● Taking the Forgotten** *(2 Gnosis)*. Take one small object from a target without them noticing. **1:** something pocketed or set down. **2–3:** something carried or worn. **4+:** something in their grip, and the memory of the object blurs for the scene.

**●●●● Mitanu's Blessing** *(3 Gnosis)*. The Clever Rogue lends you his tongue for a scene. **1:** one specific lie is sealed. **2–3:** every lie you tell this scene is sealed. **4+:** your figure counts as 2 higher against passive senses.

**●●●●● The Thousand Forms** *(5 Gnosis)*. Beyond the five forms: take any animal or humanoid shape you have closely studied, physically accurate and fully functional. **1:** passes casual scrutiny. **2–3:** passes active mundane examination. **4+:** supernatural probes must beat your figure. You can never wear a specific named individual.

### Theurge

*Lu-Bat's Path. The Great Conciliator, Saturn.*

**● Spirit Speech** *(passive, free)*. No language barrier exists between you and any spirit.

**●● Sense Corruption** *(1 Gnosis)*. Read the spiritual state of wherever you are standing. **1:** presence and severity. **2–3:** kind, age, and which spirits are suffering for it. **4+:** the shape of the cause, clearly enough to plan against.

**●●● Exorcism** *(2 Gnosis, contested against the spirit)*. Force a spirit or Bane out of a possessed person, place, or object. **1:** ejected from the host, but lingering nearby. **2–3:** ejected and barred from that host for a week. **4+:** banished from the whole area for a lunar month.

**●●●● Pulse of the Invisible** *(3 Gnosis)*. A full spiritual census of where you are. **1:** a building. **2–3:** a block, with natures and allegiances. **4+:** a full ward, plus each significant spirit's current purpose.

**●●●●● Bind Bane** *(5 Gnosis, contested against the spirit)*. Bind a Bane or hostile spirit to a location or object so that it cannot act and cannot move. **1:** bound until the next midnight. **2–3:** bound for a lunar month. **4+:** bound indefinitely, so long as you reinforce the working monthly at 1 Gnosis.

### Philodox

*Zarok's Path. The Lawgiver, Jupiter.*

**● Truths of the Lawgiver** *(passive, free)*. A direct lie spoken in your presence registers as a wrongness you cannot ignore.

**●● Weigh the Claim** *(1 Gnosis)*. Learn whether a stated agreement, oath, or pact has actually been broken, and by which party.

**●●● Weak Arm** *(2 Gnosis)*. Read the structural flaw in one opponent. **1:** your mundane damage pools against that target gain +2 dice for the scene. **2–3:** yours and your immediate pack's. **4+:** as above, and mundane attacks against that target take −1 difficulty.

**●●●● Call to Duty** *(3 Gnosis, Contested)*. Speak a command in Zarok's name. One reasonable directive binds any Garou who hears it. **1:** the target spends 1 Willpower to disobey this round. **2–3:** they comply for the scene unless attacked. **4+:** they comply, and cannot raise a claw against you for the scene.

**●●●●● Zarok's Verdict** *(5 Gnosis, Contested)*. Deliver a formal judgment before at least one witness. Choose the consequence at activation: the target cannot speak a lie, or cannot act against your community, or feels real remorse for one named transgression. **1:** holds until the next midnight. **2–3:** a week. **4+:** a lunar month.

### Galliard

*Tambiyah's Path. The Veiled Sage, Venus.*

**● Beast Speech** *(passive, free)*. You speak and understand the languages of animals as your own.

**●● The Howl That Carries** *(1 Gnosis)*. A howl that steadies the pack. **1:** your pack. **2–3:** every ally in earshot. **4+:** the steadiness holds for each ally's next two such rolls.

**●●● Mindspeak** *(2 Gnosis)*. A two-way mental link with any Garou you have personally met, at unlimited range, for a scene. **1:** one partner. **2–3:** up to five, each linked to you. **4+:** a true conference, where everyone linked hears everyone.

**●●●● Lore Recall** *(3 Gnosis)*. Put one specific question to the ancestor-memory of the people. **1:** a fragment, symbolic. **2–3:** a clear account from a single witness-spirit. **4+:** multiple witnesses, and you can feel exactly where their accounts disagree.

**●●●●● The Living Story** *(5 Gnosis, Contested against the unwilling)*. Tell a story so true it briefly lives, for up to ten minutes, as a vivid shared vision. **1:** they see it. **2–3:** they feel it as though they lived it. **4+:** the story is carried, and anyone who heard it can retell it once within the week with the middle band's effect intact.

### Ahroun

*Nerigal's Path. The Ice Warrior, Mars.*

**● Bane-Sense** *(passive, free)*. You feel worked obsidian, or your court's iron and jasper, within about thirty feet.

**●● The Falling Touch** *(1 Gnosis)*. A touch delivers a concussive shock of spirit-force. The target goes down and loses their next action. No damage.

**●●● Ice Warrior's Claws** *(2 Gnosis)*. Your natural weapons turn spirit-keen for the scene: +2 dice, and against spirits, Banes, and the animate dead the damage becomes aggravated. Against the living it stays lethal.

**●●●● Inspiration** *(3 Gnosis)*. Your presence in a fight becomes a fact people rally to. **1:** your pack. **2–3:** every ally in the scene. **4+:** the effect survives you, holding to the end of the scene even if you fall.

**●●●●● Hero's Stand** *(5 Gnosis)*. Declare a piece of ground yours. For one scene you cannot be moved, knocked down, or forced to retreat by any physical force, and every attack against you suffers +2 difficulty. The moment you voluntarily leave the ground, it ends.

### The Unclaimed

Some Garou complete the Rite of Weighing and no court claims them. It carries no shame. It is a *not yet*, and it happens rarely enough to make the Unclaimed objects of curiosity and quiet sympathy.

Mechanically they hold two Aligned paths instead of three, and in exchange they take a second Pact path as their fourth Art and carry no court bane material at all, leaving only obsidian and fire. Over a long life, that is a real advantage.

If a court claims one of them during play, the new Patron path enters at dot 1 on the Aligned track and they lose nothing they already hold.

## Pact Paths

These are the Gifts taught by spirit broods who answer to no tribe, court, or lineage.

### Pact of Stone

**● Sure Footing** *(free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane Athletics and Survival rolls on unstable ground. You always know true north and how deep you are.

**●● Skin of Stone** *(1 Gnosis)*. +2 dice to soak bashing and lethal for the scene. Not aggravated. Never aggravated.

**●●● Voice of the Deep Rock** *(2 Gnosis)*. Commune with the earth of this place: what has passed over it, what is buried in it, where its hollows and tunnels run.

**●●●● Shape the Stone** *(3 Gnosis)*. Reshape earth and stone by touch: open a door in a wall, seal a tunnel, raise a barricade worth +2 cover.

**●●●●● The Unmoved** *(5 Gnosis)*. Step into living rock. **1:** seal yourself inside it, unreachable, until the next midnight. Ambient recovery still applies. **2–3:** travel through stone at walking pace for the scene. **4+:** carry one companion with you.

### Pact of Wind

**● Whisper on the Wind** *(free)*. A short spoken message reaches one person you know within about a mile, heard by nobody else.

**●● Swift Feet** *(1 Gnosis)*. Movement doubled, and −1 difficulty on pursuit and evasion rolls for the scene.

**●●● Ride the Gale** *(2 Gnosis)*. For the scene your leaps become soaring bounds: rooftop travel, chasm crossings, and no fall from any height harms you.

**●●●● The Listening Wind** *(3 Gnosis)*. The wind brings sound to you. Eavesdrop on a conversation happening anywhere along connected open air within about a mile.

**●●●●● Wings of the Storm** *(5 Gnosis)*. True flight for a scene at the speed of a diving hawk, carrying one companion if you choose, and mundane ranged attacks against you take +2 difficulty while you fly.

### Pact of Flame

**● Ember Sense** *(free)*. Sense heat and fire through walls, and know at a glance whether a fire was set on purpose.

**●● Cleansing Flame** *(1 Gnosis)*. A touch that burns what should not be here. Art damage, aggravated against spirits, Banes, and the animate dead, lethal against anything alive.

**●●● Hearthguard** *(2 Gnosis)*. Command mundane flame within sight for the scene: quench it, kindle it, hold it from spreading, or shape it into a line, ring, or wall. It grants you no protection whatsoever from fire, including the fire you are commanding.

**●●●● Brand the Unclean** *(3 Gnosis)*. Sear a spirit-mark into a Bane or corrupted being. For one week the branded thing cannot hide from Garou senses.

**●●●●● The Cleansing Pyre** *(5 Gnosis)*. Raise a pyre of spirit-fire around yourself. Every Bane and hostile spirit within its reach takes aggravated Art damage, all at once. A Bane reduced to nothing is destroyed rather than discorporated. The living caught in it take lethal, resolved as an environmental hazard.

### Pact of Water

**● Still the Blood** *(free)*. Stop a bleeding wound by touch. A dying mortal stabilises.

**●● Mother's Flow** *(1 Gnosis)*. Close 1 level of another's bashing or lethal, plus 1 more level per net success past the first. Aggravated is untouchable.

**●●● The River Remembers** *(2 Gnosis)*. Read water: scry what happened at a body of water within the last cycle, or set it to tracking and learn where anything that crossed it went.

**●●●● Breath of the Drowned** *(3 Gnosis)*. You breathe water, move through it as easily as air, and speak clearly beneath it, for a scene.

**●●●●● Tide's Mercy** *(5 Gnosis)*. Lay hands on one being and let the tide take what it can carry. **1:** close up to 3 lethal. **2–3:** close all bashing and lethal. **4+:** as above, and purge poison, disease, and ichor-taint, stabilising even a mortal past Incapacitated. Aggravated is untouched. No being benefits from Tide's Mercy more than once per lunar month.
