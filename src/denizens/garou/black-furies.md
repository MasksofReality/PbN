## Who They Are

Before the End, the Furies' justice was for women and children. After watching the entire world come to ruin they widened the mandate instead of narrowing it, on the reasoning that everything alive has now been on the receiving end of unearned power, so everything is worth defending. Gender is not a prerequisite for deserving justice. Every being harmed by someone wielding power they did not earn has a Fury who will hear them out.

## Gift of Eshtarra: The Avenging Claw

+2 dice on mundane Brawl and Melee rolls while defending or avenging harm done to someone who could not defend themselves.

## Tribal Cost: The Binding Fury

On witnessing a direct act of injustice against someone vulnerable, roll Self-Control at difficulty 6 to choose restraint over acting right now. A Fury who keeps restraining without ever addressing the injustice loses 1 current Stability per week until she confronts it. This touches current Stability only, never permanent.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● Sense Injustice** *(passive, free)*. Touch a place or a person and feel the residue of harm, exploitation, or wrongful treatment, as a direction rather than a detail. No mundane means can fake it.

**●● Avenging Focus** *(1 Gnosis)*. While you are honestly defending or avenging someone, and Eshtarra knows the difference between that and settling a personal score: −1 difficulty on all mundane attack rolls for the scene.

**●●● Avenging Strike** *(2 Gnosis)*. Name the harm and its author aloud. For the scene, against that author, your mundane damage pools gain +2 dice and armour adds nothing against you. Stamina still soaks, because flesh is not the thing being judged. Where the author is a Bane, a spirit, or one of the animate dead, your strikes deal aggravated instead. The cause has to be sound. A Fury who invokes this over a grudge finds that the Gift simply does not come.

**●●●● Gorgon's Gaze** *(3 Gnosis, Contested)*. Meet the eyes of a being who has committed a real injustice. **1:** they cannot approach you or those you protect this scene. **2–3:** incapable of acting against you or your protected for the scene, able to defend themselves and nothing more. **4+:** while you hold the gaze they cannot act at all, and holding it is the only thing you may do besides speak. It works on anything capable of conscience.

**●●●●● Eshtarra's Justice** *(5 Gnosis, Contested)*. Formal judgment on a documented, witnessed injustice, witnessed by another or established before a Philodox. The transgression becomes a spiritual brand, visible to all Garou and most supernaturals, and the branded cannot deny it. **1:** a week. **2–3:** a lunar month. **4+:** until they make genuine amends, as judged by a moot. If the claim was false the brand does not take, and every spirit present watched you try.
