*The Wanderers*

<p class="eyebrow">← Back to <a href="/denizens/garou/">Garou overview</a></p>

## Who They Are

The Striders have always moved between communities, carrying news and keeping the whole from fragmenting into isolated pockets. In a world of eighteen Basilicas separated by hundreds of miles of lethal wilderness, that work is infrastructure. They know which stretches carry the worst Wyrd corruption, where the spirit paths still run, and which caravans out of the Great Caravanserai are safe to travel with. They carry letters, intelligence, goods, and occasionally people, and they ask for passage and a meal in return.

In Purgatory they are your connection to everywhere else: what New Columbia is arming, what the Striders of Chino are worried about this season, what the outer territories are reporting. The waygates changed their trade about ten years ago and they have complicated feelings about it.

## Gift of Eshtarra: The Road's Gift

You are immune to the disorientation and exhaustion of extended wilderness travel. Staff do not roll fatigue against a Strider on the road.

## Tribal Cost: The Dead's Attention

Wherever a Strider goes, the dead notice. Not aggressively, just persistently. Stay anywhere more than a week and ghostly hangers-on accumulate, visibly enough that other characters start asking about it. You cannot suppress it without active effort, and the Restless Dead have reasons of their own to be interested in someone who walks between Basilicas.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● Ground Eaten** *(passive, free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane travel, navigation, and endurance rolls. You never get lost by mundane means, and you always know your bearing and rough distance to the nearest Hearthstone.

**●● Silence** *(1 Gnosis)*. You and up to three chosen companions move in complete silence for the scene: no footfall, no breath, no gear, and nothing you say to each other can be overheard by paratech listening. The tribe's name lives here.

**●●● Sense the Dead** *(2 Gnosis)*. Attune to death for a scene. Perceive ghosts, Wraiths, and the residue the dead leave behind, and know where the dead concentrate on the route ahead. **1:** presence and direction. **2–3:** clear perception, and conversation with any willing dead. **4+:** read a residue's last moment and learn how they died, in a single image. Every use brightens you to them, because your Tribal Cost is not suspended for being useful.

**●●●● The Unseen Pass** *(3 Gnosis)*. One passage through a guarded or restricted place, entry, traversal, and exit, leaving nothing behind: no records, no spirit-impressions, no scent, no paratech footage. **1:** you alone, against mundane security. **2–3:** you and up to two companions, paratech included. **4+:** even spirits and wards retain no memory of you, and any later supernatural attempt to establish that you were there has to beat your figure.

**●●●●● Reach the Umbra** *(5 Gnosis)*. Step sideways where the damaged Gauntlet would refuse you, or faster than it should allow, taking up to two companions with you. **1:** you alone, through a breach that seals behind you. **2–3:** you and two companions. **4+:** the crossing holds open a path your pack can use for the rest of the scene, a temporary way, which is about the most Strider thing a Strider can make. Bear in mind that the Circle of Bone & Ivy considers an unregistered way through the Gauntlet to be *exactly* their business.
