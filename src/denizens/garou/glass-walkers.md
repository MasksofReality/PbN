*The Clever Wolves*

## Who They Are

The Glass Walkers always understood the city, and in the old world that was held against them. Then the world ended, the city became the only place anybody could live, and their expertise stopped being a failing. They know Purgatory's infrastructure: which conduits the Institute actually monitors, where the Vigiles' ward-locks are theatre, what a Glyphcode formula does when it was etched by someone in a hurry. They talk to the spirits living in metal and glass and etched leystone, which most tribes never learned to look for. They are pragmatists at peace with a pragmatic world, serving the mandate through the city that is now the only wilderness most humans will ever see.

## Gift of Eshtarra: Urban Instinct

+2 dice on mundane Technology rolls, and on mundane Stealth rolls inside a built environment.

## Tribal Cost: Wilderness Disconnect

Outside a settlement's barrier you take +2 difficulty on mundane Survival rolls, and on any Art roll drawing on wild places or wild spirits at staff call. The spirit networks they rely on are not out there. They are not helpless, but they are worse at it than any other Garou, and everyone knows it.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● Ask the Machine** *(free)*. The spirit in a simple mechanism performs or ceases its function at your request, whether a lock, a valve, or a lever, with no contact needed. Any device you handle for a minute confesses its faults, its tampering, and its modifications.

**●● Dashing Entrance** *(1 Gnosis)*. The city parts for you. Doors open, lifts arrive, gates cooperate, and paratech surveillance loses you for the scene.

**●●● Attunement** *(2 Gnosis)*. Attune to the spirit of a building or a block for a full sensory map: every room, every person, every active device, every spiritual presence. Built environments only. **1:** a snapshot. **2–3:** live awareness for the scene. **4+:** the building quietly favours you, giving −1 difficulty on mundane Stealth and Larceny rolls inside it for the scene.

**●●●● Grid Communion** *(3 Gnosis)*. Interface directly with the paratech grid, with no terminal and no credentials. Read system status, trace Glyphcode flows, spot anomalies in the reactor conduits, and make limited adjustments to non-critical systems. **1:** read-only, the local node. **2–3:** ward-scale reads plus one non-critical adjustment. **4+:** district-scale, and a handful of adjustments. On any botch, or on any top-band reach into Institute-monitored systems, the Promethean Institute notices. They are not automatically hostile. They are *interested*, and their interest has a way of making things complicated.

**●●●●● The Waking City** *(5 Gnosis)*. Rouse the spirit of a ward for one scene and the built world takes your side. Every door, gate, and lift serves you and your allies. Any attempt to find or track your group by mundane or paratech means takes +2 difficulty. Your enemies' movement through the ward is halved, and its locks, doors, and systems actively oppose them. Meanwhile the ward's small spirits report every movement inside it to you, continuously. Out in the wilderness this Gift is a howl into dead air, because the spirit networks it calls on are not there.
