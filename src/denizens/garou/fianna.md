*Heralds and protectors of beauty in all its forms*

## Who They Are

The Fianna remember. Their ancestor spirits held more than anyone else's through the decade when the Garou did not exist, not memories exactly, more the emotional shape of what had mattered. When Eshtarra restored them, their first act was to start talking, and they made it into stories before any of it could be lost. They take that work seriously enough to surprise people who mistake celebration for shallowness. They love beauty because they know what it costs, and they have seen what a world without passion looks like.

## Gift of Eshtarra: Voice of the Ancestors

+2 dice on mundane Expression rolls telling a true story, and on Intelligence plus Arcane rolls to perform a Rite.

## Tribal Cost: The Weight of Memory

On witnessing something beautiful destroyed, whether art, a community, a relationship, or a wild place, roll Self-Control at difficulty 6 or react visibly and lose your next action. They will not go feral from it. They will weep, or freeze, or say the wrong true thing at the worst possible moment. They feel it more than they want to, every time.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● The Told Thing** *(passive, free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane Expression and Performance rolls when what you are telling is true.

**●● Faerie Light** *(1 Gnosis)*. A warm light rises off you, less brightness than a quality. −1 difficulty on Social rolls within it, and anyone inside it carrying hostile intent towards those it touches feels unwelcome without knowing why.

**●●● Balm of the Goddess** *(2 Gnosis)*. Perform for at least a full minute, singing or telling or playing, and the audience recovers. Each willing hearer regains 1 current Willpower, never above their permanent rating, and no being benefits from any Balm more than once per cycle. They also take −1 difficulty on their next roll this scene to resist fear or loss of control. **1:** a pack-sized audience. **2–3:** a hall. **4+:** the steadiness lingers until midnight. The Balm mends nerve. It does not mend flesh, and it does not mend Stability, because grief has an economy of its own and the Fianna respect it.

**●●●● Howl of the Banshee** *(3 Gnosis, fear)*. A howl carrying the grief and fury of everything the people survived. Every hostile being in earshot defends on Grit, and mortals defend on Grit alone. **1:** shaken, +1 difficulty on their next action. **2–3:** +1 difficulty on everything for the scene. **4+:** they rout, withdrawing from the fight unless they spend 1 Willpower to hold, and spending again each round they stay. The name is older than the tribe, and so is the grief in it.

**●●●●● Song of the Heroes** *(5 Gnosis)*. Sing a deed you personally witnessed or performed, with enough truth in it that it briefly lives again for everyone who hears. Every allied Garou in the scene instantly heals 1 lethal and takes −1 difficulty for the rest of the scene on all mundane rolls made in defence of the community. The first time each ally would break this scene, whether through a failed Grit roll, a failed frenzy resistance, or a rout, they may reroll it once. The deed has to be real and it has to be yours to tell. A Galliard's embellishment, declared as embellishment, is art. A Song built on a lie breaks the song about singing your shame, and it does so with an audience.
