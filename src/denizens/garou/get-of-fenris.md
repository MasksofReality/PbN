## Who They Are

The old Get were fierce and sometimes brutal about what counted as strength. The End changed them the way it changed everything. The last Get standing at the final moment were not the most dominant, they were the most committed, the ones still fighting when there was no rational reason to think fighting would help. The new Get carry that lesson forward. Strength is real and they will not apologise for valuing it, but strength without purpose is vanity in the making. They are the ones on the barrier when a wyrdstorm turns, the first between the community and something that needs killing, and more and more often the ones who stay afterwards to rebuild whatever the fight broke.

## Gift of Eshtarra: Heroic Spirit

+2 dice on mundane Strength-based damage pools while standing between a threat and someone who cannot face it themselves.

## Tribal Cost: The Hero's Compulsion

On witnessing a direct threat to the innocent or to their community, roll Self-Control at difficulty 6 to choose strategy over stepping in right now with everything they have. They can be strategic. It is never the first instinct. A Get who keeps restraining without acting loses 1 current Stability per week until they fight something worth fighting.

## Spirit Art of the Tribe

**● Endure** *(passive, free)*. Ignore 1 point of wound penalty on mundane Physical rolls only. Never Art rolls, never contested defence, never soak. The commitment is real, and so is the lever it leaves you.

**●● Snarl of the Predator** *(1 Gnosis, fear)*. A challenge with enough physical presence behind it that the target hesitates a full round, unable to take an aggressive action. Defended on Grit, and on Grit alone for a mortal.

**●●● Raging Strength** *(2 Gnosis)*. +2 dice to all mundane Strength-based pools, damage included, for the scene. It stacks with form, so a Crinos Get swings a frightening pool, all of it soaked as normal. **1:** the strength. **2–3:** also −1 difficulty on feats of lifting and breaking. **4+:** also, anyone contesting your grapple does so at +1 difficulty.

**●●●● Victor's Howl** *(3 Gnosis)*. On defeating a foe who was a real threat, howl. Every allied Garou in earshot rallies: all ongoing fear penalties on them end at once, and each regains 1 current Willpower, never above permanent, and no ally benefits more than once per cycle. The victory has to be real. The Gift knows the way Eshtarra knows, and a staged triumph earns you silence.

**●●●●● Unstoppable** *(5 Gnosis)*. For one scene, nothing short of the worst can stop you. You cannot be grappled, knocked down, moved, or forced to retreat by anything that does not deal aggravated damage, and wound penalties from bashing and lethal do not apply to your mundane Physical rolls. They apply in full to your Art rolls and everything else, because a body refusing to fall is not the same as a spirit going unhurt. When the scene ends, everything you postponed arrives at once, so plan to be somewhere you can fall down.
