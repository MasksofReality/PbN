A Motif is you enforcing the narrative law of your story on the physical world. Every tree states its law at the head, and every tree follows the universal Art rules without exception.

## How They Work

- **Cost:** 1 dot free, 2 dots 1 Glamour, 3 dots 2 Glamour, 4 dots 3 Glamour, 5 dots 5 Glamour.
- **Roll:** Motif + Glamour, difficulty 6. Attributes never contribute. No Boon, Aspect, Ban or Merit ever adds dice to a Motif roll. Such things move difficulty, or they do not exist.
- Wound penalties apply to Motif activations.
- **Contests:** a defender rolls permanent Willpower + highest Art, or Grit + highest Art where the effect is fear, horror, or a compulsion to flee or yield. Mortals defend on Willpower alone, or Grit alone. Against NPCs, use the core tier subtraction.
- **Helpless targets** are not contested unless the entry says Contested, in which case it is contested regardless. Using an Art on the helpless or unaware is a Stability trigger, and mortals always count as helpless.
- **Banked effects** (wards, curses, broken rules) set a static threshold. The banked figure is never rerolled. Whoever tests it must equal or exceed it.
- **Damage:** 1 level on a successful roll, +1 per net success beyond the first, unsoaked unless the Motif produced an ordinary physical thing. Each damaging entry says whether it crosses space, in which case Dodge on Dexterity + Athletics applies, or originates at the target, in which case it does not.

*\* Affinity Motif of that Fable*

**The Mask** is the shared inheritance. Every Dreamborn wears one, so every Dreamborn advances it on the Aligned track, and you may take it at rating 1 at creation as your second Aligned Art. You do not get it free. It is simply an option open to everyone.

Every other tree outside your Fable's two, plus the Mask, is Discordant: 39 XP to 5, never at creation, requiring an in-fiction route and staff approval. That gate is deliberate. A Dreamborn learning another story's law is a Dreamborn being taught by somebody, and that somebody is a plot.

**What this means for your sheet.** Four trees is the ceiling, forever. Two come free with the Fable, the Mask sits on the Aligned track for everyone, and Gossamer is the fourth slot most Dreamborn end up filling, because it is the only breadth available without a pact. Spend the Discordant price instead and you have told the table something about who taught you.

## Motif of the Mask

*Nothing is only what it appears to be.*

**● The Polished Mask** *(free)*: alter mundane details of your own Mask for one scene, covering features, hair, vocal pitch, apparent height within a foot, and the cut and cleanliness of your clothes. Sensory paratech and mortal eyes accept it completely. It does not conceal your Mien from anything that sees Miens, and it will not make you someone specific enough to fool their own mother.

**●● Ghost on the Glass** *(1 Glamour)*: conjure a static illusion of sight and sound, up to the size of a room, for the scene or until touched. Bank your net successes. Anyone actively examining the illusion rolls their defence pool at difficulty 6 and must equal or exceed the banked figure to see through it. Staff should lower that difficulty by 1 or 2 for close inspection, good light, or a professional's eye.

**●●● Phantasmal Armour** *(2 Glamour)*: illusory duplicates blur your silhouette. Incoming mundane physical and ranged attacks suffer +2 difficulty for the scene, or until you take a Health level from any source, since a blow that lands proves which one is real.

**●●●● Dream-Walking** *(3 Glamour)*: step bodily into a sleeping target's dreamscape, at arm's reach. Inside it you may converse with their subconscious, Harvest, or plant a suggestion. The suggestion is Contested. 1 net: an idea they wake up already half-holding. 2 to 3: a conviction they will defend as their own. 4+: a course of action they will begin taking today, and will rationalise for a cycle.

**●●●●● The Waking Fable** *(5 Glamour)*: manifest a full hallucinatory reality over roughly 50 yards for the scene, whether a burning forest, a drowned cathedral, or the street as it was two hundred years ago. Contested against every unwilling being present. Those who fail believe it completely and act accordingly, and hazards within the fable inflict bashing psychosomatic damage, originating at the target and therefore undodgeable, read once from your net successes. Those who succeed see their allies fighting nothing.

## Motif of the Hearth

*The monster cannot cross the threshold uninvited.*

**● The Warm Threshold** *(free)*: touch a doorway, campsite or vehicle and claim it for the scene. Temperature stays comfortable, weather stays out, and nobody outside can eavesdrop by mundane means. While you remain within, you know each time someone crosses the boundary.

**●● Calming the Beast** *(1 Glamour)*: radiate peace at one subject. Contested on Grit + highest Art where unwilling. 1 net: their panic, frenzy or Attrition episode is suppressed until fresh provocation. 2 to 3: suppressed for the scene. 4+: the calm holds for the scene even through fresh provocation. An episode caused by a botched Attrition roll cannot be ended this way, only suspended for the current scene.

**●●● Ward of the Boundary** *(2 Glamour)*: trace a boundary up to 10 yards across and bank your net successes as a static threshold. It holds for the scene, or until you leave it. Any being intending harm to those within must equal or exceed the banked figure on its defence pool to cross. A being that fails may force the line by spending 1 Willpower, which admits it for one round only.

**●●●● The Guest-Right** *(3 Glamour)*: offer food, drink or shelter and bind it. Contested, though an offer accepted knowingly is uncontested. For one cycle, neither you nor the guest can act to harm the other, directly or by arrangement. Whoever breaks it takes 1 unsoakable aggravated and marks the Stability trigger. This works on Gentry. It has been the last surviving move of a great many small people.

**●●●●● Sanctuary of the Old Gods** *(5 Glamour)*: anchor a temporary zone equivalent to a Minor Hearthstone for one cycle. Wyrdstorms are deflected, aberrants will not willingly enter, and hostile Arts used inside suffer +2 difficulty. It cannot be moved, it cannot be renewed in the same place twice in a lunar month, and the paratech grid cannot draw from it, which has not stopped people trying.

## Motif of the Crown

*The sovereign's word is absolute.*

**● The Weight of Bearing** *(free)*: project gravitas. Mundane mortals do not interrupt you, defer instinctively, and treat you as the person in charge until somebody with actual standing contradicts you.

**●● Dreadful Gaze** *(1 Glamour)*: terror, bare and personal. Contested on Grit + highest Art. The target flees or cowers for a number of rounds equal to your net successes, and will not willingly approach you again this scene.

**●●● Royal Decree** *(2 Glamour)*: issue one sentence of command. Contested on Willpower + highest Art. 1 net: obeyed for a single action. 2 to 3: obeyed until the sentence is fulfilled or the scene ends. 4+: obeyed to the letter, with initiative, for the scene. A command that is obviously suicidal, or that requires the target to betray their stated Nature, fails automatically, and they know exactly what you tried to do.

**●●●● Mantle of the Monarch** *(3 Glamour)*: your Mien flares with abyssal majesty. Bank your net successes. For the scene, anyone who wishes to attack you must first equal or exceed the banked figure on Grit + highest Art. Success frees them for the rest of the scene. Failure costs them their action.

**●●●●● The Sovereign Geas** *(5 Glamour)*: bind a willing or defeated subject to a named task or prohibition. Contested. While the geas is broken or the task neglected, they suffer 1 unsoakable aggravated per cycle, damage that heals at the lethal rate and cannot be accelerated. It ends when the task is completed, when you release them, or after one lunar month. You may hold only one Geas at a time.

## Motif of the Road

*The road provides for the one who walks it.*

**● Beast-Tongue** *(free)*: speak with and understand mundane animals, and wyrdtamed beasts such as the Calgary prairie drakes, for the scene. They answer as animals do: honestly, and beside the point.

**●● Know the Way** *(1 Glamour)*: learn the physical hazards, aberrant nests, anomalies and wyrdstorm paths within 10 miles, and the shortest safe route between two points you can name.

**●●● Tangling Briars** *(2 Glamour)*: terrain twists to seize everything in a 10-yard radius. Bank your net successes. Escaping is a mundane action, Strength or Dexterity + Athletics at difficulty 6, that must equal or exceed the banked figure. The briars are real, physical, and remain after you leave.

**●●●● Gale and Deluge** *(3 Glamour)*: summon a sudden localised storm for the scene. Visibility collapses, giving +2 difficulty on ranged attacks and Perception, open flames are snuffed, and mundane movement across the affected ground is at +1 difficulty.

**●●●●● Wrath of the Elements** *(5 Glamour)*: call down storm-energy or seismic upheaval. Lethal Art damage to everything within 10 yards, unsoaked. The effect originates at the target, so no Dodge is permitted. It leaves the ground scorched or broken, which the Vigiles photograph.
