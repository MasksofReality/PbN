## Glamour, Harvest and Attrition

You run on Glamour, which obeys every universal Resource Trait rule in the core book.

- **Starting Glamour:** 2, for a starting pool of 7.
- **Pool maximum:** Glamour rating + 5. Glamour is derived, rises automatically with your highest Motif, and is never purchased.
- **Motif roll:** Motif rating + Glamour, difficulty 6.

### Ambient Recovery

At the midnight reset you regain 5 Glamour, provided that somewhere in the cycle you were in contact with an environment touched by human life, story or emotional expression. An inhabited street counts. So does a caravan camp, or a lit room with people arguing in it.

Locked in a sterile paratech cell, sealed in cold iron, or alone in the dead wastes, the core presumption fails and ambient recovery yields nothing.

There is one narrow exception. Spend the full cycle inside a place where your own story is actively kept, meaning a hearth where your tale is recited, a shrine tended in your name, a household that still leaves out the bowl, and you receive ambient recovery even where the ordinary presumption would fail. It exempts you from nothing else. Somebody can still drain you to 0, and you still enter Attrition when they do.

### Replenishment: The Harvest

You replenish by feeding on emotional resonance. Which rule governs depends on whether a pool is being drained.

| Mode | What It Is | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **The Epiphany** | Willing inspiration: stoking artistic passion, wonder, laughter, love. Nothing is taken from the mortal but time. | Ordinary replenishment, 1 Glamour per two post rounds, roleplayed and visible. |
| **The Reap** | Predation. Seducing, terrifying or breaking a subject and drinking what comes out. Drains Vitality from mortals, Essence from Nightfolk. | Full feeding: escalating rate table, 1 lethal per 2 points, victim's 24-hour tally, Tether on Nightfolk victims. |
| **The Dream-Weave** | Harvesting from a sleeping mortal at close proximity. The sleeper counts as helpless and wakes exhausted. | Full feeding, as above, and the sleeper had no way to refuse. |

The Epiphany is not feeding. It accrues no tally, no damage and no Tether, and it is the reason most Dreamborn are pleasant company. It is also slow, public, and dependent on somebody actually feeling something, which is harder work than it sounds.

The Reap and the Dream-Weave are feeding in full, with everything the core rules attach to that word. Willing donors bleed at 1 lethal per 3 points, points past empty come out of Health, and three feeds on the same Nightfolk inside a lunar month buys you a leash you did not ask for.

### Attrition

At 0 Glamour you cannot activate any Motif that has a cost, though 1-dot Motifs keep working. At the end of each scene spent at 0, roll Willpower at difficulty 6. On a failure:

- you become an open conduit to the raw, unshaped Dreaming;
- your Mask dissolves entirely, in front of whoever is present;
- the suppressed anxieties and terrors of everyone in the scene bleed into the physical world as waking nightmares for the rest of the scene, or, on a botch, until the next midnight reset.

An Attrition episode in a public place is a BRD-status event, and everyone knows it. Let your pool hit zero in the Basilicas and you are gambling your legal personhood on one Willpower roll.

## Stability and the Narrative Unweaving

You use the universal track. Permanent Stability is Conscience + Self-Control minus Eldritch, and Eldritch is barred at creation.

### What Marks a Trigger for Dreamborn

On top of the universal list in the core book, you mark a trigger if, in this scene, you:

- defied your Dramatic Law in a moment of real consequence;
- broke guest-right, as host or as guest;
- permanently destroyed a unique piece of human folklore, art or wonder;
- were bound, commanded or forced to act by cold iron;
- acted out your Fable's monstrous extreme while in Attrition or any other loss of control.

These extend the core list. They soften no part of it.

### The Telling

The core rules allow splats to define their own movement of current Stability, and here is theirs. When a mortal who is under no compulsion tells your story to another person as a story, unprompted, in a played scene, you regain 1 current Stability.

Once per lunar month. The price is what it costs to earn: you have to have shown a mortal enough of yourself to be worth retelling, which is the precise behaviour the Pax Nocturnorum punishes. Catharsis returns 1 per cycle for free. The Telling returns 1 per month and risks your BRD status, which makes it rarer rather than better. Permanent Stability is untouched either way.

### Breaking, and the Sanctioned Stand-In

Breaking works as the core book states. Once per lunar month, in place of the first Breaking roll, you may instead let the world forget one of your connections: permanently lose one dot of a Background of your choice, and skip the Conscience roll. The dot is not credited back, because it was the player's choice rather than the plot's.

This is what a fading story does. It sheds people to stay itself.

### The Unraveling

At 0 to 1 Stability your narrative integrity is coming apart, and you risk becoming a Hollow Fable: a thing that has forgotten its human connections and become a caricature of its own archetype, acting out fairy-tale logic with lethal, amoral detachment. The Champion who only duels. The Hearthkeeper who will not let anyone leave.

Permanent Stability 1 is the floor, and reaching it means a staff conversation: retirement, NPC conversion, or a final scene of the player's choosing.

## Body, Healing and Death

### Soak and Defences

- Bashing and lethal: soaked on Stamina + armour at difficulty 6.
- Aggravated: unsoakable.
- Art damage: unsoaked, unless the Motif produced an ordinary physical thing.

### Healing

You heal mundane injury naturally at the mortal rates, and you may spend Glamour to close flesh.

- **Bashing and lethal:** 1 Glamour per Health level, instantly, one action in combat.
- **Aggravated:** no expenditure accelerates it. It heals at the lethal rate, and nothing here touches that.

Mind the healing trap the core book warns about: the resource that closes your wounds is the resource a predator just took.

### The Cold Iron Bane

Cold iron, meaning telluric or meteoric iron worked by hand without a forge, is the mythic poison of the Fae. In 200 TE it is rare, expensive, and almost entirely in the hands of Vigiles, Hunters and the Society of Leopold, which is the only reason Dreamborn walk around at all.

- Cold iron weapons inflict unsoakable aggravated damage. This is a stated exception to the core rule that nothing makes weapons less effective against Nightfolk. Here, something makes one more so.
- Sustained contact burns: 1 bashing per round of continuous physical contact, unsoakable.
- Being bound or worn in cold iron suppresses your ability to spend Glamour or activate any Motif at all.

### The Named Flaw

Every Mien carries one visible flaw of the story: the ribbon that keeps the head on, the knot behind the knee, the reversed cloak, the seam in the clay. Name yours at creation. A called shot at +2 difficulty may target it, and an attack that hits it and deals any damage also prevents you from spending Glamour until the end of the following round.

Nightfolk can see a Mien, and therefore its flaw. Mortals need to be told, or to be very observant, or to have read the right story as a child.

### Past Incapacitated

Further lethal damage does not end you immediately. You collapse into Guttering: gossamer-thin, unable to act, Mask gone, half-present. A Guttering Dreamborn is destroyed at the next midnight reset unless, before then, someone spends 3 Glamour on them, or carries them inside a Hearthstone zone or a claimed refuge where mortals keep company with them until morning. Any further damage while Guttering destroys them outright.

Aggravated damage past Incapacitated destroys you immediately. There is no Guttering and no saving you.

### Destruction

A destroyed Dreamborn leaves no mundane corpse. The body goes to gossamer dream-mist, fading autumn leaves, seafoam or leystone ash, according to the Mien, which has caused legal problems for Vigiles investigators who need a body to close a file.

The character is permanently dead. The foundational essence returns to the Dreaming, and provided somebody keeps recounting the central myth in any fashion, it will eventually coalesce into a new iteration. Many Dreamborn have lived through numerous incarnations this way. None of them remember any of it.

### Losing the Story

You are anchored in human telling, and that anchor can be cut. After three consecutive cycles with no contact whatsoever with human life, story or emotional expression:

- ambient recovery fails;
- no Harvest of any mode is possible, because there is nothing to draw from;
- each further cycle of isolation costs 1 current Stability directly.

This is why exile is a sentence rather than an inconvenience, why the wastes are worse for Dreamborn than for anything else that walks them, and why a Dreamborn locked in a quiet white room by people who know what they are will tell them absolutely anything by the fourth day.

## Character Creation

You follow the universal creation standards in the core book, from which no splat may deviate.

| Category | Value |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3, priority set by your Fable |
| Abilities | 13 Talents / 9 Skills / 5 Knowledges |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control and Grit, minimum 1 each |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots |
| Freebie points | 21 |
| Starting Motifs | One Aligned at 2, one Aligned at 1; one of them must be your Fable's Affinity Motif |
| Starting Glamour | 2, for a pool of 7 |
| Eldritch | 0, strictly barred at creation |

**Freebie costs.** Attribute 5, Ability 2, Willpower 2, Psyche trait 2, Background 1, Merit 1 per point, Motif dot 11 with a maximum of one dot bought this way. Discordant Motifs cannot be bought with freebies under any circumstance.

That freebie dot may be spent on the Gossamer. The core rules bar only Discordant and Restricted Arts from the freebie budget, and Gossamer is Nonaligned, so you may open play with it at rating 1: a third tree bought at eleven of your twenty-one points, which is a poor exchange and occasionally exactly the character. It cannot go above 1 at creation, and your two Aligned starting Motifs are still mandatory.

**Also chosen at creation:** Nature and Demeanour, both mandatory; a Cathartic Vice; your Fable; two Aspects; one Ban; your Mien's named flaw; and, if you are a Warden, your charge.

### Order of Play at the Table

1. Pick the Fable. This is the story you are, and it sets your Aligned Motifs, Boon, Law and attribute priority.
2. Describe the Mien. Anything from any mythology, or nothing from any of them.
3. Choose two Aspects to make that Mien mechanically true.
4. Choose a Ban and name your Mien's flaw.
5. Spend the standard budget above.

### Motif Advancement

- **Aligned and Affinity Motifs:** 20 XP to 5 (0 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8). This includes the Motif of the Mask, for every Dreamborn.
- **Discordant Motifs:** 39 XP to 5 (2 / 4 / 7 / 11 / 15). Requires an in-fiction mentor or pact, plus staff approval. Never at creation.
- **Nonaligned:** 31 XP to 5 (1 / 3 / 5 / 9 / 13). The Motif of the Gossamer only, for every Dreamborn. No mentor, no gate.
- **Tree limit:** four Arts, total, forever.
- Pact Arts belong to the Lost and advance as Discordant, on the same gate.
