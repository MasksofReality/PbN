Exactly one Ban binds every Dreamborn: the folkloric rule that governs your kind of story. It is Stigmata-class, chosen at creation with staff approval, and absolute inside its stated circumstance. There is no roll to resist a Ban.

A Ban is not a secret weakness you are unaware of. You know it perfectly well, you have arranged your life around it, and you will lie about it to anyone who asks.

| Ban | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Invitation** | You cannot enter a private dwelling unless someone with a right to be there invites you in. Businesses, public rooms and abandoned buildings are not dwellings. |
| **The Count** | Small scattered things compel you. Spilled grain, beads, nails, seeds: you must stop and count them, and you may not act on anything else until you have, or until someone physically removes you. |
| **The Threshold Nail** | You cannot cross a threshold marked with iron, whether a nail, a horseshoe or a driven spike, while the mark remains. |
| **Running Water** | You cannot cross flowing water under your own power. Carried, bridged or shipped is fine; wading or swimming is not. |
| **The Salt Line** | An unbroken line of salt stops you as a wall would. Breaking it yourself is impossible. Wind, feet and rain are not your problem. |
| **The Given Word** | You cannot tell a direct, deliberate lie about your own nature to someone who has asked you plainly and to your face. You may evade, riddle and refuse for as long as your wit holds out. |
| **Bread and Salt** | You cannot harm, nor act to bring harm upon, anyone who has shared food with you, until the next sunrise. |
| **The True Name** | Anyone who knows and speaks your true name aloud gains +3 dice on their defence pool against your Motifs for the rest of the scene. |
| **The Repaid Gift** | A gift freely given to you must be answered with a gift of comparable worth before the next midnight reset. Until you have, all your rolls suffer +1 difficulty. |
| **The Reflection** | You must stop and look at your own reflection whenever you unexpectedly encounter one, for at least a moment, in front of whoever is present. |
| **The Core** | The linchpin of your existence sits outside your body: a pearl, a gem, something small enough to steal. Anyone who takes it can extort you for its return, and the magic of your story enforces the bargain. You must perform a favour that does not defy your Nature, after which the Core returns to you as if teleported. If the thief destroys it instead, you lose access to your Motifs for one lunar month, at which point it regenerates. |
| **The Avaricious One** | You have a specific thing you compulsively collect: gold, bottle caps, clocks. Resisting the urge to add something new to the hoard, even where its current owner is unobliging about the notion, costs a point of temporary Willpower. Being stolen from reduces your current Stability by 1 until you recover what was taken, by any means necessary. |

An Innocent carrying The Avaricious One resolves one thing rather than two: pay the Willpower and skip the Self-Control roll.

## Bans and the Pax

The Vigiles keep a Ban column on the BRD register, and registering yours truthfully is a legal requirement. Registering a false one is a fraud that has cost several very old Dreamborn their heads. Registering a true one has cost several more. Most Dreamborn file something true-ish and inconvenient, and keep the real one in the family.
