A tree is a cohesive package. Some concepts do not need a package. They need the one thing their myth is famous for, and they need it at creation, when Discordant trees are barred and the tree that would carry it belongs to somebody else. A dragon's breath is the standing example, and a Signature is how it reaches the sheet.

A Signature is a single fixed supernatural effect, bought as a Merit at 5 points: 5 freebies at creation, or 10 XP after it, story-gated the way the core rules gate every Merit. One per character, ever.

**What it can be.** One effect of roughly 2-dot Motif power, meaning the tier that costs 1 Glamour. It has to be something the myth in question is actually known for, whether the breath, the cry, the stare or the venom, rather than a gap a player noticed in their build.

## How It Works, and the Three Ways It Is Worse Than a Motif

1. It rolls Glamour alone at difficulty 6, where a Motif rolls Motif + Glamour. For every other purpose it is an Art roll: wound penalties apply, nothing adds dice to it, and a contested Signature is defended on the ordinary defence pool.
2. It never grows. No rating, no bands beyond the one written down, no second application, no re-specification later. What you wrote at creation is what you have in your final scene.
3. It produces an ordinary physical thing wherever it plausibly can, so the damage it deals is soaked normally, unlike Art damage. Real fire is real fire.

Cost in play is 1 Glamour per use, and you cannot use it at 0 Glamour.

## Five Worked Signatures, for Calibration

| Signature | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Dragon's Breath** | A cone of fire out to 10 yards. Lethal, 1 level +1 per net success, soaked normally, and it crosses space, so Dodge on Dexterity + Athletics applies. It sets fire to whatever would ordinarily burn. |
| **The Keening** | One target who can hear you. Fear: Contested on Grit + highest Art. They flee or cower for net successes in rounds. Everyone else in earshot knows something died. |
| **The Stone Glance** | One target who meets your eyes. Contested. They cannot move or act for net successes in rounds, though they see and hear everything. Their skin greys and does not entirely recover for a cycle. |
| **The Trickster's Props** | Draw a dream object up to the size of a longsword out of the Dreaming. It cannot be mechanically complex, and it vanishes at the end of the scene, on exposure to sunlight, or on contact with cold iron, whichever comes first. |
| **The Envenomed Bite** | An unarmed strike that lands also inflicts 1 level of lethal, soaked normally, plus +1 difficulty on all the target's mundane rolls for the scene as the venom works. |

## Two Dials for Staff

The 5-point price assumes a 2-dot ceiling. A table that wants Signatures rarer should raise the price rather than lower the ceiling, since a weak-but-cheap version invites everyone to take one. And the post-creation route can be closed entirely if a table would rather Signatures were strictly something characters arrive with: either the myth was always famous for it, or it never was.
