## Motif of the Forge

*The made thing outlasts its maker, and a gift is never free.*

**● Maker's Eye** *(free)*: learn at a touch how a thing was made, what is wrong with it, roughly who made it and what it is worth. +2 dice on mundane rolls to diagnose a fault.

**●● Overnight Works** *(1 Glamour)*: compress craft, repair or construction worked under cover of darkness, so that a night's labour accomplishes roughly a week's. Requires materials and a place to work, and produces nothing from nothing.

**●●● Mending the Prop** *(2 Glamour)*: over one hour, a damaged structure or paratech item knits back together flawlessly. 1 net: a door, a rifle, a shattered glyph-plate. 2 to 3: a wall, a generator, a cart. 4+: a room, a vehicle, a section of grid. It cannot restore what has been utterly destroyed, and it cannot create.

**●●●● The Given Thing** *(3 Glamour)*: work an object into a gift lasting one cycle. Its bearer gains +1 die on one named mundane action type, or −1 difficulty on one named mundane action, never below 3. While the gift is carried willingly, you always know the direction and rough distance to its bearer. You need not mention that part.

**●●●●● The Wonder** *(5 Glamour)*: over a full night's work, and with staff approval, forge a true wonder out of the old stories: the sword that will not be dropped, the cloak nobody looks at, the lantern that burns in the wastes. Its bearer may trigger its stored effect, roughly the equivalent of a 2-dot Motif and stated at forging, once per cycle. While the Wonder exists your Glamour pool maximum drops by 1, because you left something of yourself in it. One Wonder at a time. Destroying it, or reclaiming it into your own hands, returns what you gave.

## Motif of the Hunt

*The quarry is always run down.*

**● The Cut Trail** *(free)*: read a trail up to a cycle old by scent, sign or aetheric residue. +2 dice on mundane tracking rolls.

**●● Naming the Quarry** *(1 Glamour)*: name one target you have seen and mark them for a cycle. Contested. You know their direction and rough distance, cannot lose their trail, and always know when they come within a hundred yards. You may hold one mark at a time.

**●●● The Closing Distance** *(2 Glamour)*: for the scene, nothing mundane outpaces you. Terrain costs you nothing, and mundane attempts to break away from you suffer +2 difficulty.

**●●●● The Baying** *(3 Glamour)*: against your marked quarry only. Contested on Grit + highest Art. While it holds, meaning the scene, they cannot rest, hide or regain Willpower, they hear you coming whether you are there or not, and every roll to escape you is at +1 difficulty.

**●●●●● The Inevitable Stroke** *(5 Glamour)*: the hunt ends. Against your marked quarry, your next mundane attack this scene cannot be dodged, ignores cover, and ignores armour dice in their soak, and you take 3 bonus dice on the attack roll. It deals ordinary weapon damage. The Motif does not kill for you. It simply makes sure you were always going to reach them.

## Motif of the Longing

*No one refuses what they truly want.*

**● The Sweet Air** *(free)*: company with you is a pleasure people cannot quite account for. +2 dice on mundane Social rolls to charm or put at ease over a scene of sustained contact.

**●● The Song That Pulls** *(1 Glamour)*: Contested on Willpower + highest Art. 1 net: the target cannot attend to anything but you while you hold their eye. 2 to 3: they will follow you somewhere quieter. 4+: for the scene they will not act against you unless you harm them or someone they love.

**●●● Revel's Contagion** *(2 Glamour)*: a gathering tips into revelry for the scene. Everyone present suffers +1 difficulty on rolls to keep their own counsel or refuse indulgence, secrets surface as gossip, and you may Harvest from the room as a whole rather than any one person, uninterrupted. The ordinary Harvest rate is unchanged. It simply cannot be broken off by one partner walking away.

**●●●● The Aching Absence** *(3 Glamour)*: plant a longing for something you name, whether a person, a place, a taste, or a life they did not choose. Contested. For one cycle, any roll to act against that desire is at +2 difficulty, and on 4+ net successes they will rearrange their day around it and be grateful.

**●●●●● The Drowning Chord** *(5 Glamour)*: sing, and the crowd empties toward you. Contested against everyone within roughly 50 yards. Those who fail walk to you through whatever lies between, whether water, traffic, a drop or a cordon, and the world does to them what the world does. The Motif itself deals no damage. Those who fail by 4+ do not stop while conscious. Wax, deafness and being somewhere else are the recognised defences, which is why Amaia's court is discussed so carefully in Purgatory.

## Motif of the Hollow

*What is buried always comes up.*

**● The Listening Dark** *(free)*: speak with any unrested dead present, and read a place's recent grief and violence, meaning where someone died, roughly when, and how frightened they were.

**●● The Traded Whisper** *(1 Glamour)*: ask a place, an object or a corpse one question about what it witnessed. 1 net: a fragmentary flash. 2 to 3: a clear silent scene. 4+: the scene with sound, including names spoken aloud in it. You receive images, never explanations.

**●●● The Buried Name** *(2 Glamour)*: Contested on Willpower + highest Art. You learn one secret the target would never have told you. You do not get to choose which one, and it is often not the one that would have been useful.

**●●●● The Curse Set** *(3 Glamour)*: lay a curse in the proper folkloric shape, bound to a named condition of release: until they apologise, until the stolen thing is returned, until they say your name kindly, or for one lunar month. Contested. While it holds, one named category of the target's mundane rolls suffers +2 difficulty. They learn the condition only by working out the logic of the story they are now in.

**●●●●● Open the Ground** *(5 Glamour)*: choose one, for the scene. Either every secret held by those within 20 yards claws its way into the open, Contested against each, with those who fail speaking their worst truth aloud, in company, in their own voice. Or the unrested dead of the site rise and act for the scene, on their own grievances rather than your orders, and quite possibly against you.
