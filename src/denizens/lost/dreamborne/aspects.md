Choose two Aspects at creation, with staff approval. Both are Stigmata-class: never bought, never bought off, never changed. Each one hands you a Gift and a Frailty together, and you do not get to keep half of a myth.

Gifts add dice to mundane rolls only. Where a Frailty touches Arts at all, it does so from the difficulty side. Gift dice do not stack with Boon dice or Motif dice, so take the highest single bonus as usual. A Fable and an Aspect covering the same ground makes a character thematically solid and mechanically no better, which is the intended shape: pick your Aspects for the Mien you want, not for the arithmetic.

| # | Aspect | Gift | Frailty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Titan-Framed** | +2 dice on mundane Strength rolls and feats of raw force; wield two-handed heavy weapons in one hand with no penalty. | +2 difficulty on mundane Stealth and on delicate work (fine paratech, surgery, pickpocketing). You cannot pass unremarked where ordinary people can. |
| 2 | **Slight** | Squeeze through any head-sized opening; +2 dice on mundane Stealth in clutter or crowds. | +2 difficulty on mundane rolls relying on physical force, and on being taken seriously by strangers who have not yet seen what you can do. |
| 3 | **Pelagic** | Breathe water; no penalties submerged; swim as fast as you run. | You must immerse yourself fully once per cycle. Each consecutive cycle without it inflicts 1 unsoakable bashing that will not heal until you are submerged. |
| 4 | **Bestial** | Assume one chosen non-human form for 1 Glamour, no roll (equipment does not travel); +1 die on mundane Perception rolls using scent or hearing in either shape. | Name one animal instinct: flight from fire, territorial display, hoarding, the freeze. When it is triggered, spend 1 Willpower or act on it. |
| 5 | **Verdant** | +2 dice on mundane Survival rolls and on moving or hiding in vegetation; you need no food for a cycle given sun and water. | +1 difficulty on all rolls inside sterile industrial paratech environments: deep Basilica bunkers, foundry floors, sealed laboratories. |
| 6 | **Sepulchral** | Converse with unrested ghosts natively, no Art required; +2 dice on mundane Stealth in dim light. | You physically cannot speak above a raspy whisper, and suffer +1 difficulty on all rolls in bright illumination or deafening noise. |
| 7 | **Enduring** | +2 dice on mundane Stamina rolls to resist environment, poison and exhaustion; passively regenerate one point of Bruising damage per post round. | You are slow. You may never take more than one action in a round (no pool-splitting), and you lose all initiative ties. |
| 8 | **Fleet** | +2 dice on mundane Athletics rolls for speed, leaping and balance; falls are bashing, at half the levels. | Confinement unmoors you. Grappled, bound, or in a sealed room, you take +2 difficulty on all mundane rolls and must spend 1 Willpower per scene to stay willingly. |
| 9 | **Radiant** | +2 dice on mundane Social rolls for first impressions. Your Mien is glorious, and those who see it do not forget it. | You cannot be inconspicuous: +2 difficulty on mundane rolls to blend in, be forgotten, or deny having been somewhere. Witnesses always remember you. |
| 10 | **Wrought** | You are made of something tougher than flesh and bone: +1 die of soak against bashing and lethal, and you do not breathe, tire or bleed. | You heal no bashing or lethal damage naturally, ever. Every level must be closed with Glamour or repaired by a Maker's craft. |
| 11 | **Sunless** | See perfectly in full darkness; +2 dice on mundane Perception rolls at night. | +1 difficulty on all rolls in direct sunlight, and you cannot Harvest beneath it. |
| 12 | **Elemental** | You are elemental in nature, immune to all damage from one elemental force declared at character creation; immersing yourself in your native element lets you passively absorb Glamour even where that would otherwise be impossible. | Attacks made with your elemental bane (Fire ⇄ Water, Earth ⇄ Air) cause double damage and cannot be soaked. |
| 13 | **Aerial** | You fly under your own power at roughly your running speed, and can carry one person of ordinary size for short distances. | You cannot fly in any enclosed space no matter its size, and you suffer a crippling claustrophobia. |

**Writing a new one.** Staff may approve an Aspect that is not on this list, built to the same shape: one Gift worth roughly two mundane dice or one clear capability, and one Frailty of comparable weight that will inconvenience you in ordinary scenes rather than only in exotic ones. Write both, or write neither.
