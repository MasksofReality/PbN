## Motif of Misrule

*The great are overturned by the small.*

**● The Slipped Cog** *(free)*: as an action, cause a small mundane mishap within sight: a jam, a spill, a dropped key, a misheard order. The target's next mundane action suffers +1 difficulty. Nobody ever quite blames you, which is not the same as nobody suspecting you.

**●● Turnabout** *(1 Glamour)*: for the scene, the first time each round that someone in sight brings force, rank or a rule to bear against you, their roll suffers +2 difficulty. The chair is in the wrong place, the badge is upside down, the sentence comes out wrong.

**●●● The Broken Rule** *(2 Glamour)*: suspend one small mundane rule of a place for the scene, and bank your net successes as a static threshold. A lock does not lock, a ledger will not add, a paratech door reads everyone as authorised, a queue forgets whose turn it is. Anyone attempting to force the rule back into place must equal or exceed the banked figure.

**●●●● The Jester's Crown** *(3 Glamour)*: Contested. For the scene, a chosen figure of authority is not taken seriously by anyone present. Orders draw laughter, a uniform reads as costume, and their mundane rolls relying on rank, reputation or command suffer +2 difficulty. Where the room needs someone to look to, it looks to you.

**●●●●● The World Turned** *(5 Glamour)*: for the scene, within roughly 50 yards, misrule reigns. Every attempt to enforce order, hierarchy or a plan suffers +2 difficulty. Every attempt to escape, improvise, hide or overturn is at −1 difficulty, never below 3. One mundane rule of the place breaks outright, and any crowd present acts on its worst impulse. This applies to your friends, your plan, and you.

## Motif of the Elements

*Nature is the root of the oldest stories, told at the first firesides.*

Taking this Motif requires a declared native element: the one named by your Elemental Aspect if you hold it, and otherwise declared when the first dot is bought and never changed afterward. Every entry below reads through that element.

This tree is Discordant unless you hold the Elemental Aspect, in which case it advances on the Aligned track in your declared element only, and needs no mentor. The Aspect is the qualification: you cannot command a substance you are not made of, and if you are made of it, nobody needs to teach you.

Note the division of labour with the Road. The Road's weather is environmental, happening to the journey at the scale of the terrain. This tree is embodied. The element is your own substance, and these are the things a story does with the stuff it is made of.

**● The Native Sign** *(free)*: the element answers you in small ways. Flames lean toward you, water parts an inch, dust settles, the air stills. You may produce or quench a double handful of it at will, you are untroubled by mundane ambient extremes of it (heat, damp, dust, wind), and you know the nearest substantial source within a hundred yards. This is not the Elemental Aspect's immunity and does not become it.

**●● Elemental Cloak** *(1 Glamour)*: wreathe yourself for the scene. Anyone in sustained physical contact with you, whether grappling, holding or striking unarmed, takes 1 level per round, lethal for fire and lightning, bashing for water, earth and air. This is an ordinary physical effect and is soaked normally. Mundane attempts to grapple or hold you suffer +2 difficulty.

**●●● Shape the Element** *(2 Glamour)*: conjure and hold a mass of your element within 20 yards for the scene. Bank your net successes. 1 net: a doorway's worth. 2 to 3: a wall across a room. 4+: across a street. It blocks line of sight, giving +2 difficulty on ranged attacks and Perception through it, and forcing a way through costs 1 level of ordinary physical damage, soakable.

**●●●● Elemental Body** *(3 Glamour)*: become your element for the scene. You go wherever it goes, through grates, cracks, under doors, up a chimney, out with the tide, and mundane bashing and lethal damage do nothing to you at all. In exchange you cannot use equipment, cannot speak except as the element speaks, and cannot Harvest. Aggravated damage, Art damage, cold iron and Unweaving reach you exactly as they always did.

**●●●●● The Element Unbound** *(5 Glamour)*: everything within 20 yards takes lethal Art damage, unsoaked, originating at the target and therefore undodgeable. You alone are untouched. The ground is then transformed for the scene into a burning street, a flooded floor, a cracked road, or a wind that keeps everything down, and all mundane action within it suffers +2 difficulty. This is the loudest thing in the book, and there is no version of using it that the Vigiles do not hear about.

## Motif of the Gossamer *(Nonaligned)*

*No law at all. The waking world is the thinner of the two, and this is the seam.*

Every other Motif is a story insisting on itself. This one is a Dreamborn handling the substance they are made of: the mist, the seam, the unshaped stuff of the Shallows. It belongs to no Fable because it belongs to no story, which is precisely what makes it available to all of them and beloved of none. Working Gossamer is widely held to be undignified, faintly self-destructive, and extremely useful.

The Gossamer is Nonaligned to everyone. It advances on the Nonaligned track, 31 XP to 5, for every Dreamborn, needs no mentor and no staff approval, and it is the one tree any of them can simply reach for. Which is exactly why the old ones consider studying it a vulgar habit, like a person picking at their own stitching.

**● Gossamer Sight** *(free)*: see the dreamstuff. You perceive thin places within a hundred yards, the residue of Glamour spent nearby within the last cycle, whether an object is fae-worked or fae-grown (goblin fruit, Bitterthorn briar, a Wonder), and who within sight is currently dreaming. You do not learn what a working does. That is Eldritch's business.

**●● Mist and Murmur** *(1 Glamour)*: draw a haze of half-shaped dream over roughly 20 yards for the scene: cold mist, whispers at the edge of hearing, shapes that resolve into nothing. Mundane Perception rolls and ranged attacks made within or into it suffer +1 difficulty, and mundane attempts to track or follow anyone inside it suffer +2. Your own mist does not impede you.

**●●● The Thin Place** *(2 Glamour)*: open a few steps of passage through the Shallows and come out somewhere else. Bank your net successes. 1 net: yourself alone, one step, around a locked door, over a cordon, past a wall you are touching. 2 to 3: yourself and one companion per net success, between thin places within a mile. 4+: as above, and the passage holds open for the scene. The Shallows are not empty, and time inside them does not keep faith with the clock. Staff may rule that something was waiting, and should say so before the party habituates.

**●●●● Unweaving** *(3 Glamour)*: pull at the stitching. Choose one on activation.

- *Unmake a working.* Against a banked fae effect, equal or exceed its threshold to dissolve it entirely. Against one being actively maintained, this is Contested on the caster's Willpower + highest Art. A Dreamborn's Mask counts as a working, and on a success it fails for one round, in front of everyone.
- *Unmake the substance.* Against a being whose body is dream, meaning a Dreamborn, a hobgoblin, a Wyrdborn thing or a manifested nightmare, this deals aggravated damage, unsoaked, originating at the target and therefore undodgeable. It does nothing whatsoever to a mortal, a vampire, or a wall.

This is why the tree has no owner and no admirers. Cold iron is a Hunter's tool. Unweaving is a Dreamborn doing to another Dreamborn what cold iron does, with their bare hands, for three Glamour. Staff should expect its use in a Basilica to become a political matter within the day.

**●●●●● Into the Shallows** *(5 Glamour)*: step bodily out of the waking world, taking anyone holding onto you. 1 net: minutes, and you come back where you left. 2 to 3: up to a scene, and you may surface at any thin place you have personally visited. 4+: up to a full cycle, and you may surface at any thin place you can describe. Nothing in the waking world can reach you there, which is the entire point.

Three costs come with it, all stated rather than rolled. Time inside runs at the Shallows' pleasure, and staff set the hour of return. Any traveller who is not Dreamborn marks having witnessed something that should not exist, and staff may award Eldritch for what they saw. And you may carry out one impossible thing, a fruit, a coin, a knife, an answer, which is entirely real until the next midnight reset and then is not.
