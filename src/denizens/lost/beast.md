## Who They Are

In the old stories, someone wicked was transformed into a hideous beast, and someone pure of heart kissed a frog and found a royal. Both truths live in every Beast. They are the animals they represent, shaped by a Keeper who saw them as a pet: a creature to be owned and made useful. The Dreaming carved the animal that lived in their heart into their flesh and told them *you were always this, you were never more*.

It was wrong. The Beast proved it by surviving. By planning. By thriving in the wilds of the Dreaming in ways their Keepers never anticipated. Of all the Lost, Beasts are the most adaptable, the ones who turned the loss of their humanity into a weapon instead of a wound. The story of the Beast is a story of perseverance, and perseverance is winning by refusing to stop.

A Beast without a mind is a thing a Keeper owns, and no Beast will accept that. Mental primary is not an oversight. It is the whole argument of the Seeming.

**Blessing: Animal Affinity.** The Beast speaks with animals as though they were people. The animal's voice registers as intelligent and comprehensible to the Beast alone; everyone else hears normal animal noises. The Beast gains +1 die on Animal Ken rolls and a free Animal Ken specialty for the animal closest to their Kith. Mundane rolls only; it adds nothing to a Contract.

**Curse: Mental Block.** In a moment of genuine crisis, the Beast drops into pure animal instinct. The trigger is one of three things: being ambushed or surprised, being cornered with no visible way out, or taking damage that puts them at Wounded or worse. Once per scene, on the first such moment, roll Self-Control at difficulty 6. On a failure the Beast goes non-verbal and instinct-driven until the crisis ends or another character spends an action calming them (Charisma or Manipulation + Animal Ken, difficulty 6).

## Contracts of Fang and Talon

**● Beast's Keen Senses** — *Free · passive, no roll*

Animal senses sharpened past anything mundane. +2 dice on all Perception rolls involving scent, hearing or motion. Always on; suppressing it for a scene costs 1 Willpower. Mundane rolls only.

*Catch:* Flavour only. The senses come alive when the Beast is hunting something they need rather than something they want. Nothing to waive, nothing to roll.

**●● Claws of the Wild** — *1 Essence*

The Beast's hands become natural weapons: claws, talons, fangs, whatever suits their animal. Damage pool Strength + 2, lethal, resolved as an ordinary physical attack and soaked normally. Lasts one scene.

*Catch:* Defending someone weaker than yourself from direct physical harm.

**●●● Animal Communication** — *2 Essence*

Beyond the passive Blessing, the Beast conveys complex ideas to animals: nuanced instruction, detailed report, tasks requiring genuine thought. One scene per activation. An animal asked to do something suicidal still refuses. This is conversation, not command.

*Catch:* Speaking to an animal that is clearly suffering or in danger.

**●●●● Pack Mind** — *3 Essence*

The Beast builds a shared sensory network with every willing Lost or ally within roughly a city block. For the scene they receive each bonded being's complete sensory feed — sight, sound, scent, touch — and process it without confusion. The bonded know the Beast's position and emotional state in return. Nothing carried on the network can be intercepted by mundane means or by paratech surveillance; a supernatural attempt to break in is contested.

One success bonds three beings. Each net success past the first bonds three more.

*Catch:* In the last 24 hours you did something for one of the bonded that cost you more than it cost them. A real sacrifice of resource, time or safety, with nothing asked in return.

**●●●●● Apex Predator** — *5 Essence*

For one scene the Beast becomes the apex of their bonded animal type in every sense that matters. All animals of that type serve without hesitation. Every being, mundane or supernatural, that stands in a prey relationship to that animal feels an unshakeable instinct to be elsewhere; this is fear, so a resisting target defends on Grit + highest Art. Damage from the Beast's natural weapons becomes aggravated. The Beast cannot be ambushed, flanked or surprised by any living thing for the duration.

Afterward the Beast must eat a full, substantial meal within the hour or take 2 lethal from the exertion.

*Catch:* You have gone without food for at least 24 hours immediately beforehand. Not by choice — because there was none, or because you gave yours to someone who needed it more.

## Contracts of the Vanishing

*Concealment as primal urgency. Running, hiding, surviving.*

**● Smoke Step** — *Free*

The Beast moves through a space without leaving the usual traces. Scent, sound and visual impression fade within moments of their passing. Mundane tracking is impossible; supernatural tracking is at +2 difficulty. One scene.

*Catch:* Fleeing genuine danger rather than moving tactically. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Muddled Scent** — *1 Essence*

The Beast rewrites their scent signature to mimic any animal or person encountered in the last 24 hours. Scent-trackers are fooled outright; supernatural tracking is at +2 difficulty. Lasts until the Beast ends it or until dawn.

*Catch:* The person being mimicked has done you a genuine favour recently.

**●●● False Trail** — *2 Essence*

A convincing false trail in a direction of the Beast's choosing: scent, sound, disturbance, all of it. Anyone following the real trail follows the false one instead. Lasts 24 hours, or until heavy rain takes it.

*Catch:* Laying the trail in territory where you spent your durance.

**●●●● The Unseen Road** — *3 Essence*

The Beast and up to three chosen companions become imperceptible to all pursuit. Mundane tracking, supernatural sensing, paratech surveillance and spirit-world observation all fail to register their passage. They leave no trail of any kind and the distance covered is unlimited. It ends when they stop moving or take a hostile action.

Afterward the Beast cannot activate any Vanishing Contract for 24 hours. The road has been used up.

*Catch:* You are genuinely fleeing. Not retreating tactically, not testing the power. Running from something that has already hurt you or someone with you. The fear has to be real.

**●●●●● Unmake the Memory** — *5 Essence, Contested*

The Beast erases up to one hour from the memory of everyone who witnessed a specific event. Not just their own involvement — the entire event. Witnesses know that time passed and cannot reach what filled it. Physical evidence remains; the memory of it does not.

Each witness contests separately on permanent Willpower + highest Art, and against a supernatural witness the Beast rolls at +2 difficulty. This cannot touch anything a witness has already reported to somebody else. A filed statement is out of reach.

The Beast loses the emotional weight of the erased event along with everyone else. They remember it as fact and can no longer feel it. **Cost: 1 current Stability, direct, no roll.**

*Catch:* The event genuinely hurt you to witness or experience. You are erasing your own pain along with the evidence, and you do it without hesitating.

## Contracts of the Long Chase

*Endurance in service of others.*

**● Speed Burst** — *Free*

For one turn the Beast moves at double speed. Not a supernatural blur; genuine explosive animal movement, for closing distance, breaking away, or reaching something first.

*Catch:* Running toward something or someone you genuinely love. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Endurance of the Wild** — *1 Essence*

The Beast ignores all wound penalties for one scene. They feel every injury and simply refuse to be stopped by it. At the end of the scene every suppressed penalty applies at once and the Beast rolls Stamina at difficulty 7 to stay standing.

*Catch:* You are the last one standing between your group and harm.

**●●● The Long Run** — *2 Essence*

Extraordinary sustained effort — running, swimming, climbing, fighting — for up to 24 hours without fatigue penalties and without sleep. Food and water are still required. Afterward, eight hours of rest.

*Catch:* Pursuing someone who has taken something from you by force.

**●●●● The Untiring Body** — *3 Essence*

For 24 hours the Beast's body does not fatigue at all. Sleep deprivation, hunger and exertion produce no mechanical consequence. At the end of the period it all lands at once and the Beast drops into a 12-hour sleep they cannot be woken from without being dealt damage.

*Catch:* Someone who cannot keep up with you needs you to carry them through, literally or otherwise. There must be someone depending on your endurance, not just yourself.

**●●●●● One Perfect Moment** — *5 Essence*

Time does not stop. The Beast simply moves through it fast enough that it looks that way. For one turn the Beast takes additional actions equal to their Glamour rating, each at their full undivided pool — no splitting. Nothing reacts to any of them until all are complete.

For the whole of the following turn the Beast takes no action of any kind. They are simply still. That is the price of the acceleration, paid in full and in public.

*Catch:* You have been completely motionless — genuinely still, not even fidgeting — for a full minute immediately before activation, and the stillness was chosen rather than forced.
