## Who They Are

While human children were told stories about the boogeyman and the thing under the bed, these Lost met the darkness and the darkness smiled back. There is no screaming in the dark. It is a cold, quiet place where fear reigns and the silence is full of untold things. That is the fate of many Darklings. They collect what humanity wants hidden, and what can anyone truly hide from the darkness, in its own house?

Darklings are not malicious by nature. They simply see the ugly, cold truth that lives in the darkest dreams. They find happiness and experience joy with a streak of realism that unsettles other Lost. A Darkling knows more than they will ever tell, and if they are smiling it may be for the happiness of the moment or because they know exactly how it ends. They will never say which.

Darklings survive by knowing first and moving second. Mental leads. Physical follows, because a Darkling who has to run had already made a mistake.

**Blessing: Shadow Walker.** While in shadow, a Darkling goes undetected by mundane means. No camera, motion sensor, paratech sweep or ordinary human perception registers them. Supernatural detection is possible at +2 difficulty, to a maximum of 9. As a side effect, Darklings see normally in all natural darkness.

**Curse: Light Sensitive.** In strong natural or paratech lighting, a Darkling takes −2 dice on all attack rolls. Magically created light does not trigger it; only mundane and paratech sources do. Darklings seek shadow instinctively and are genuinely uncomfortable in bright rooms.

## Contracts of Darkness

**● Eyes in the Dark** — *Free · passive, no roll*

The Darkling sees perfectly in natural darkness, as though in daylight. Magical or supernatural darkness does not blind them either; it grants +2 dice on Perception rolls instead. Always active.

*Catch:* Flavour only. Nothing to waive.

**●● Shadow Meld** — *1 Essence*

The Darkling merges with a shadow and becomes part of it. While melded they cannot act and cannot be detected by mundane means or by paratech; a supernatural search is contested. They can drift slowly from shadow to shadow without breaking the meld. Up to one hour, or until they surface.

*Catch:* Hiding from someone who has genuinely threatened your life in the last 24 hours.

**●●● Shadow Veil** — *2 Essence*

The Darkling stretches their shadow-nature over one other person or a room-sized area. For the scene, everyone inside gains Shadow Walker: undetected by mundane means while in shadow, and invisible to recording paratech.

*Catch:* Sheltering someone who came to you for protection.

**●●●● Living Shadow** — *3 Essence*

The Darkling becomes a shadow, literally. Two-dimensional, moving across any surface light can reach, through gaps no wider than a knife blade, up walls and across ceilings without effort. In this form they are immune to physical damage and invisible to mundane sight. They cannot speak, carry objects, or take any physical action. They observe everything perfectly.

The form holds indefinitely, but every hour past the first costs 1 additional Essence, and returning to solid form costs 1 Essence. A Darkling at 0 Essence in shadow form is stuck there, which is the sort of thing that happens to a Darkling once and never again.

*Catch:* You slip into shadow at the instant a blow lands that would have taken you to Wounded or worse. The dark takes you before the damage registers. Once per scene only.

**●●●●● The Dark That Thinks** — *5 Essence*

The Darkling commands darkness as a physical force across a large area: a whole building, or a city block. Darkness fills every space, penetrating even magically lit ones. Within it the Darkling sees as if in daylight and every other being is effectively blind, unless they hold Glamour 5, an Art at 5 granting supernatural sight, or an equivalent at staff's call. The dark can restrain: roll Darkness + Glamour, contested, to grapple a target in shadow tendrils. One full scene.

When it ends the Darkling is thrown back into restored light and is blinded for one turn per scene the effect ran.

*Catch:* You spent at least an hour completely alone in genuine darkness immediately beforehand. No light, no company, no distraction. You sat with the dark and made peace with it before you told it what to do.

## Contracts of Smoke

*Concealment as deliberate choice. Protecting, erasing, choosing the dark.*

**● Whisper Step** — *Free*

The Darkling moves in complete silence regardless of surface or footwear. No Stealth roll is needed for movement noise specifically; other detection methods still apply normally.

*Catch:* Moving through a space you have been in before. (−1 difficulty; on a success the movement is automatic for the rest of the scene.)

**●● Traceless Passage** — *1 Essence*

No physical evidence of having been somewhere. No prints, no scent, no disturbed dust, nothing on the paratech record. One scene.

*Catch:* Entering a space you were specifically told not to enter.

**●●● Borrowed Face** — *2 Essence*

The Darkling's Mask reshapes to resemble anyone they have observed for at least a minute. Visual only: voice, scent and mannerism are unchanged. Lasts one scene, or until the Darkling says more than a few words to someone who knows the original well.

*Catch:* Impersonating someone in order to protect them from harm they do not know about.

**●●●● Ghost Walk** — *3 Essence*

The Darkling passes through a location and retroactively was not there for the past hour. Paratech recordings resolve to what was there before them. The memories of anyone they spoke to shift to a different face and a different voice. Physical traces specific to them are replaced by neutral background.

This does not undo the consequences of anything they did. Only the evidence connecting them to it. A locked door is still open; nobody can say who opened it.

*Catch:* You are using this to protect someone else from the consequences of your shared presence, not yourself. The erasure covers them completely and leaves your own traces intact. You take the evidence; they go free.

**●●●●● The Perfect Stranger** — *5 Essence*

The Darkling assumes a complete identity — appearance, voice, scent, supernatural signature, emotional aura — of a specific person they have observed for at least one scene. The resemblance is absolute. Beings who know the original intimately may attempt Perception + Empathy at difficulty 9 to notice the wrongness, and supernatural truth-detection faces the same difficulty. Holds for one full day.

When it ends the Darkling suffers identity bleed: for the following scene, each time they are asked to confirm their own name or nature they must roll Willpower at difficulty 7 to answer correctly. **Cost: 1 current Stability, direct, no roll.**

*Catch:* You take the identity of someone who has wronged you significantly, and you inhabit them without anger. Genuine inhabitation, not performance.

## Contracts of Cursing

*Darkling misfortune: subtle, structural, patient.*

**● Ill Feeling** — *Free · requires physical contact*

A Wyrd smudge of misfortune left on a target the Darkling touches. Their next roll of the day is at +1 difficulty. They feel vaguely uneasy and cannot say why.

*Catch:* Against someone who lied to you in the last scene. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Shadow of Doubt** — *1 Essence, Contested*

The Darkling plants creeping uncertainty about a specific belief, relationship or decision. The target does not lose the belief; they begin genuinely to question it. This is not mind control. It introduces doubt where there was certainty, and a target who works through the doubt honestly may come out more certain than before.

*Catch:* Against someone absolutely certain of something you know to be false.

**●●● The Lingering Dark** — *2 Essence, Contested, Conditional*

A structured misfortune in one domain — social, physical or mental — named at activation. For the rest of the scene, and in every scene until the next midnight in which the target attempts something that matters in that domain, they roll at −1 die. It does nothing in scenes where the domain does not come up, which is most of them.

It is visible to a Lost or an equivalent supernatural, and can be lifted by one. No mundane means touch it.

*Catch:* Against someone who harmed an innocent in your presence and showed no remorse.

**●●●● The Mark That Follows** — *3 Essence, Contested, Charged*

Bad luck, saved up and spent on purpose.

The Mark carries charges equal to the Darkling's net successes, to a maximum of five. It sits on the target until spent, or until thirty days pass, whichever comes first.

At any point, in any scene, the Darkling's player may spend one charge to impose +2 difficulty on a single named roll the target is about to make. It must be a roll the target genuinely cares about, not a formality. The Darkling does not need to be present, does not need to know what the roll is for, and does not roll again. They spend the charge and the world declines to cooperate.

Nothing needs tracking between charges. The target notes a number on their sheet and it goes down.

The target knows they are Marked. To supernatural sight it reads as a faint shadow lying over them, and the number of charges left is visible to anyone who can see it at all. It can be removed by a Lost with Glamour 4+ or an equivalent intervention, and whoever removes it takes 1 Essence of drain for each charge still unspent.

*Catch:* You have been carrying a genuine, unfair burden caused by this same person for at least a week. You are not inventing the injustice; you are reflecting it. The Catch does not fire if the wrong is not real.

**●●●●● Ruin** — *5 Essence, Contested, Resolved*

Not nuisance. Structural harm, delivered once, and permanent.

The Darkling names one Background the target holds: an Ally, a Contact, a Mentor, Resources, a Safe Place, a Herd, a title. On a success the Wyrd goes to work on it, and in the next scene where that Background is relevant it fails catastrophically and irreversibly. The ally is dead, ruined, or turned. The resources are seized. The safe place burns, or is found. Facts shift to make it true, and the shift is visible to everyone.

The Background is struck from the sheet. Because it was lost to plot rather than to the player's choice, its XP is credited back — so the target loses the thing, keeps the points, and gets to spend them on whatever they become afterward. That is the entire mechanical footprint: one line struck out, one XP refund, and a scene worth playing.

Where the target holds no Background the Darkling can reach, Ruin destroys a narrative asset of comparable weight, named by staff before the roll rather than after it.

The Darkling cannot target the same person with Ruin twice, ever, and cannot use any Cursing Contract for a week afterward.

**Cost: 1 current Stability, direct, no roll.**

*Catch:* The target has caused equivalent ruin to you or to someone you love. You witnessed it, grieved it, and chose this rather than violence. The response has to be measured. You are returning what was given, not escalating.
