*They Who Live for the Task · Fix-its*

<p class="eyebrow">← Back to <a href="/denizens/lost/">Lost overview</a></p>

## Who They Are

Where Elementals are chosen for what they are, Wizened are taken for what they can do. Apothecaries, healers, miners, crafters, pulled out of their mortal lives to serve at their tasks and made small and unobtrusive and dedicated so they could work without distraction. The Dreaming made them into something that lives for work. Not because the work is joyful. Because stopping would mean finding out who they are without it.

Do not mistake this for subservience. The small and the out-of-the-way see everything. They are smarter and craftier than their Keepers gave them credit for, which is precisely why so many of them escaped. They build the homes, the weapons, the foundations. They set the stage the rest of the Lost stand on. They are not heroes in the visible sense. They are the reason the heroes have somewhere to stand.

**Blessing: Smart and Nimble.** The Wizened have two things going for them in the Dreaming: their minds and their hands. +1 die on all Dexterity-based rolls and +1 die on all Intelligence-based rolls. Mundane rolls only. Neither scales.

**Curse: Spiteful.** Wizened are brilliant at what they do and genuinely unpleasant about everything else. −2 dice on all Appearance-based rolls. This is not cosmetic eccentricity. It is years of being small, overlooked and underestimated, worn on the face. They know. They stopped caring some time ago.

## Contracts of Artifice

**● Quick Hands** — *Free*

The Wizened completes any manual task at double speed with no loss of quality: picking a lock, finishing a repair, assembling a device. Cannot be used inside combat rounds.

*Catch:* Completing a task somebody is urgently depending on. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Read the Craft** — *1 Essence*

The Wizened examines a crafted object and learns how it was made, what it was made for, an impression (never a name) of who made it, and whether it has been altered from its original design. This works on supernatural objects — Tokens, Fetishes, paratech Devices, glyph-woven work — as readily as on mundane ones.

*Catch:* Examining something you made yourself.

**●●● Impossible Fix** — *2 Essence*

The Wizened repairs any broken object, however damaged, over roughly one scene of extended work, including objects shattered, burned, or dissolved beyond mundane repair. The repair is genuine and permanent. It cannot restore something destroyed by supernatural means at this rating.

*Catch:* The object belonged to someone who died and left it behind.

**●●●● The Masterwork** — *3 Essence*

The Wizened creates a single object of extraordinary quality. Not magical in the Token sense, but supernaturally excellent at its mundane function. A blade that never dulls and always finds the weak point. A garment that is never damaged and always fits. A tool that outperforms every other tool of its type in the world. A lock that cannot be picked.

Mechanically, a Masterwork grants +2 dice on any roll using it for its intended function and never fails, breaks, or degrades. It is permanent. The Wizened may hold only one at a time and cannot make another until the first is destroyed or given away.

*Catch:* You give it away the moment it is finished. You do not keep it. It goes to someone who needs it more than you do, freely, with nothing expected back. The excellence was never meant to be yours.

**●●●●● Unmake and Remake** — *5 Essence*

The Wizened deconstructs a crafted object entirely and rebuilds it as something fundamentally different in function, keeping its materials and its spiritual signature. A weapon becomes a tool. A lock becomes a key. A device becomes its own counter-device. A building's security system becomes its vulnerability map.

The work takes one scene of focused effort. The result keeps any supernatural properties the original had, transformed into the new context. This creates nothing from nothing. It only transforms what exists.

Afterward the Wizened cannot use any Artifice Contract for 48 hours.

*Catch:* The object was made to harm you or someone you care for, and you are converting the instrument of your oppression into something that serves your freedom. The transformation must fully reverse the object's original purpose.

## Contracts of the Workshop

*Labour and love. The space matters because they worked it.*

**● Warm Presence** — *Free · passive, no roll*

The Wizened's dedication to craft steadies everyone working alongside them. Allies in the same scene gain +1 die on extended task rolls: crafting, research, repair. Always on.

*Catch:* Flavour only.

*(This is a different power from the Hearth entry of the same name. Power names repeat across trees. The trees themselves do not.)*

**●● Safe Harbor** — *1 Essence*

The Wizened consecrates a workspace or shelter, making it feel genuinely safe to anyone they invite in. For one scene, everyone inside is at −1 difficulty on Social rolls involving honesty, cooperation and trust.

*Catch:* The space is genuinely your own. Your lab, your home, your workshop.

**●●● Crafted Ward** — *2 Essence*

The Wizened works a protection into an object they personally made. Its owner gains 1 point of soak against the next Art that would harm them. This is the only place in this document where damage from an Art can be soaked at all. Single use; the ward discharges and is gone.

*Catch:* Making the object for someone who cannot protect themselves.

**●●●● Sanctuary** — *3 Essence, Held*

As the Hearth Contract of the same name and rating, with one difference: presence is not enough. The Wizened must tend it. At least one scene of actual work inside it per cycle, mending something, cleaning something, making something. A Wizened who merely visits their sanctuary loses it on the same schedule as an Elemental who never came at all.

*Catch:* You establish it when you are the one who most needs it. The exhausted one, the hurt one, the depleted one. You make the space safe for others first and let yourself rest in it last. The care given before receiving is what consecrates it.

**●●●●● The Living Home** — *5 Essence, Held*

The Wizened infuses a space they have worked and maintained with genuine life. The space becomes aware of who is inside it and what they need. It repairs its own damage overnight, keeps itself clean and ordered, holds a comfortable temperature and atmosphere regardless of what is happening outside, and quietly alerts the Wizened when someone within it is in distress. The Wizened can perceive anything happening inside at will.

It stands as long as it is lived in. The Wizened must spend at least one scene per cycle inside it, doing something — working, resting, arguing with it. Miss a cycle and the space settles back toward inanimate over three days. Return within those three days and it wakes again for free. Return after, and it costs the full 5 Essence.

Living Homes that are allowed to lapse are said to sulk. Wizened who say this are not being entirely figurative.

*Catch:* The space has been your home for at least three months. You have genuinely lived in it, worked it, loved it. A place you merely occupied cannot receive this.

## Contracts of Cleansing

*Craft and consent: precise, intentional, asked for.*

**● Detect Impurity** — *Free*

The Wizened detects whether any substance, object or material has been tampered with, poisoned, corrupted or supernaturally altered. They know something is wrong before they know what.

*Catch:* Examining something you are about to give to somebody else. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Purify Substance** — *1 Essence*

The Wizened removes poison, corruption or supernatural taint from any substance they can hold in both hands: food, water, a poultice, a compound. Complete and immediate. It cannot purify a living body, only substances.

*Catch:* Purifying something meant for a child, or for someone who cannot detect the danger themselves.

**●●● Cleanse the Body** — *2 Essence*

Over one scene of physical contact and focused work, the Wizened removes poison, disease, or minor supernatural contamination from a living body. This does not heal injury; it removes introduced foreign agents. Against genuine possession or a deep curse it only reduces the effect rather than ending it.

*Catch:* Treating someone who came to you for help rather than being brought by force.

**●●●● Purge the Deep Taint** — *3 Essence*

As the Clean Flame Contract of the same name and rating. The Wizened's body processes the taint through the hands. Their work is disrupted, their grip shakes, they endure it. For one full day they suffer the taint's mechanical effect and cannot use any Cleansing Contract.

*Catch:* The affected person asked directly, named what they needed, and accepted the cost. Unconsented purging at this depth collapses immediately and the Essence is spent.

**●●●●● The Perfect Instrument** — *5 Essence · one hour of ritual*

The Wizened purifies themselves, removing every supernatural influence currently on them in a single extended ritual. Blood Bonds are severed. Dominate instructions clear. Dementation effects resolve. Ongoing Art effects end. Cursed conditions lift.

What it does not touch, and this list is exhaustive:

- **Tether.** Tether is a property of your own aspected essence. A palate, not an influence. It fades only by abstinence.
- **Stability.** No Stability is restored, permanent or current.
- **Eldritch.** Knowledge does not wash off.
- **Blessings, Curses, and every universal Lost trait.** These are not influences. They are what you are.

Afterward the Wizened is depleted: −3 dice on all pools for one full day, and they must eat and sleep within four hours or take 2 lethal.

*Catch:* You are purifying yourself in order to be clean enough to help someone else. There is a task that needs your uncorrupted self, or a person who needs you free of what was put on you, and that need is the reason. The purification serves them, not you.
