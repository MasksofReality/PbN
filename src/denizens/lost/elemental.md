## Who They Are

Elementals are the most plentiful of the Lost. They are the golems of legend, the living flame forever dancing in the pyre, the child who longed to be real. Whatever the reason, the human who became an Elemental was chosen for a single characteristic, and that one part of them became their entire identity to the Keeper. It was the one element of them that mattered.

In the Dreaming, that element became everything. A singer sings until the song loses all joy. A gardener tends the earth until the smell of it turns their stomach. They are driven so far into their own task that they form love-hate relationships with the thing that defined them, and still they cannot stop. To stop feels like disappearing. The greatest fear of an Elemental is not pain or death. It is irrelevance: losing the one thing that made them worth taking.

**Blessing: Lifebound.** The Elemental is bound to the living world even while in the Dreaming. They gain one additional health level, **Battered**, at −0 penalty, placed at the top of the track: eight levels rather than seven. They also gain +1 die on all Stamina-based rolls, including soak. Neither scales, and neither can be lost, sold, or bought a second time.

**Curse: Inhuman.** The depth of the Elemental's conditioning has cost them ordinary emotional range. −2 dice on all Charisma-based rolls. This is not coldness. They genuinely struggle to feel and express things the way mortals do. They try. The gap between trying and succeeding is always visible.

## Choosing Your Element

Every Elemental names one element at creation, matched to what their Keeper valued in them and approved by their Mentor. The choice is permanent and it governs the whole Contracts of Elements tree.

**The mechanical list is closed:** fire, water, earth and stone, air and wind, wood and green growth, metal, ice, light, shadow. The upper levels of the tree assume something with mass that can fill a room, drown a person, or be called from a mile away, and nothing outside that list does all three.

**The fictional list is open.** A Keeper who reduced someone to music, or grief, or the smell of a kitchen at six in the evening is a better story than one who picked fire, and being flattened into a single trait is the whole premise of the Seeming. If you want an abstract element, name it, then anchor it with your Mentor to one entry on the mechanical list. The Elemental of grief manifests as cold. The Elemental of music as air. The Elemental of memory as water. The abstract thing is what your character is and what every Contract looks like when it fires. The anchored element is what the dice do.

Note that light and shadow on the mechanical list interact directly with the Contracts of Darkness. A light Elemental is a hard counter to a Darkling, and a shadow Elemental overlaps one. Both are allowed. Both should be a deliberate choice at application rather than a surprise in a scene.

**Immunity, and its limit.** An Elemental is immune to harm from their own element at all times. This is part of the Seeming, not a Contract, and it covers mundane harm only. Another being's Art that happens to use fire still burns a fire Elemental.

That limit is worth saying out loud, because the Dreamborn version of this trait works differently and players coming from that side get caught by it. A Dreamborn Phoenix *is* fire; a phoenix does not have a relationship with fire, a phoenix is what fire looks like when a story tells it, and nothing made of fire can touch one. Your element was imposed. A Keeper flattened a person into a single trait, and underneath it you are still a mortal, and mortals burn. A fire Elemental walks through a housefire and is still hurt by a Darkling's conjured flame, because the Darkling's flame is an argument about fire and you are a person who was told they were one.

## Contracts of Elements

**● Element Sense** — *Free*

The Elemental senses the presence and state of their element across a wide radius: how much is there, whether it is moving, whether it has been altered by supernatural means.

*Catch:* The element is actively threatening someone nearby. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Element Shape** — *1 Essence*

The Elemental moves, shapes, intensifies or calms a quantity of their element within sight, roughly a room's worth. This is not creation; the element must already be present. One scene.

*Catch:* Using the element to protect rather than to attack.

**●●● Element Call** — *2 Essence*

The Elemental draws a significant quantity of their element out of the surrounding environment and into the space they occupy. Protective or lethal, as they choose: a room filling with water, stone rising out of a floor. Where it causes harm it deals Contract damage — 1 level on a success, +1 per net success beyond the first, unsoaked, of the type appropriate to the element. Fire is aggravated; water, earth, air and the rest are lethal.

*Catch:* You are somewhere your element is scarce or absent, and the reach is what costs you.

**●●●● Element Dominion** — *3 Essence*

The Elemental commands their element across a whole building or city block with complete authority for the scene, without needing to concentrate. It answers intent rather than instruction: they think the fire back and it pulls back. Beings caught in large-scale movement of the element take Contract damage as above.

Activating it costs the Elemental 2 bashing from the reaching, whether or not the roll succeeds.

*Catch:* Your element is absent from the space and you are calling it in from a great distance.

**●●●●● Become the Element** — *5 Essence*

The Elemental transforms wholly into their element for one scene. Becoming fire, becoming water, becoming a localised wind, becoming living stone. In this form they are immune to all physical damage, move anywhere their element could naturally go, and deal Contract damage of their element's type to anything they pass through or touch. They cannot speak and cannot activate any Contract while transformed. Other Lost perceive and interact with them normally.

When the scene ends the Elemental reforms and rolls Elements + Glamour at difficulty 8. On a failure they take −2 dice on all rolls using the Attribute most tied to their element for one week, as the re-embodiment charges them for it.

*Catch:* You transform at the moment your body would otherwise have been destroyed. The element takes you before the killing blow lands, and at that instant the transformation is involuntary rather than tactical.

## Contracts of the Hearth

*The space matters because the Elemental is part of it.*

**● Warm Presence** — *Free · passive, no roll*

The Elemental's connection to their element radiates a subtle comfort. Allies within arm's reach gain 1 automatic success on their first Willpower roll each scene. It is not activated. It simply comes off them.

*Catch:* Flavour only.

**●● Elemental Ward** — *1 Essence*

The Elemental pushes a protective resonance through a space they occupy: a room, a small area. For one scene, every attempt to use an Art to harm someone inside is at +1 difficulty, to a maximum of 9.

*Catch:* Protecting a space that somebody calls home.

**●●● Hearth Fire** — *2 Essence*

The Elemental makes a space of genuine safety. Anyone resting there for at least an hour heals 1 additional bashing level beyond their normal rate and regains 1 Essence, or 1 Willpower for a mortal. One scene, up to five people.

*Catch:* The people resting explicitly asked you for shelter.

**●●●● Sanctuary** — *3 Essence, Held*

The Elemental consecrates a location as a true sanctuary: a space that enforces peace and need-meeting inside its boundaries, up to a city block. While it stands, all healing inside runs at double rate, nobody in genuine need who reaches it can be turned away by those inside, and hostile supernatural activity inside is at +2 difficulty, to a maximum of 9.

It stands as long as it is kept. The Elemental must set foot inside at least once per cycle. The first cycle they do not, it lapses entirely. Not by degrees, not one clause at a time. It simply stops being a sanctuary, and consecrating it again costs the full 3 Essence.

*Catch:* You establish it at the moment of your own greatest need. Exhausted, hurt, with nothing left. You make the space safe for others before you rest in it yourself.

**●●●●● Heart of the Home** — *5 Essence*

The Elemental becomes the living heart of a home: a building or established community space they have occupied for at least a month. For one scene they perceive everything happening anywhere inside it, act through any instance of their element present in it at full power without being physically there, and cannot be removed from it by any supernatural force.

Every person harmed inside the space while the Elemental is in this state costs the Elemental 1 bashing, regardless of scale, and they feel it as the person felt it.

At the end of the scene the Elemental is bound to the space until the following dawn and cannot leave.

*Catch:* The home is under genuine threat at the moment of activation. You are not fortifying it in advance. You are pouring yourself into it because it is already in danger.

## Contracts of the Clean Flame

*Purification flowing from nature. The element cleanses because that is what it does.*

**● Sense Taint** — *Free*

The Elemental senses spiritual contamination, Wyrd corruption or supernatural pollution in a person, place or object, and roughly how severe it is. Not its source. Not yet.

*Catch:* Touching something you are already deeply suspicious of. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Remove Taint** — *1 Essence*

The Elemental draws minor contamination out of a person or object and lets it go harmlessly: light Wyrd corruption, lingering Bane influence, supernatural emotional residue. It cannot touch deep curses, active Art effects, or real possession.

*Catch:* Cleansing someone who asked for help without knowing what it would cost.

**●●● Purify Space** — *2 Essence*

The Elemental consecrates an area, purging supernatural contamination and making it resistant to more. A purified space resists Wyrd corruption and Bane infiltration for one week and reads to spiritual sight as a faint clean glow.

*Catch:* Cleansing a place where somebody died violently or unjustly.

**●●●● Purge the Deep Taint** — *3 Essence*

The Elemental removes deep supernatural corruption from a person, place or object: significant Wyrd corruption, advanced Bane attachment, moderate possession, substantial Chi or Pathos contamination. The removal is permanent.

The Elemental takes the taint into themselves. For one full day they suffer its mechanical effect — whatever dice penalty, visions or physical wrongness it carried — and cannot use any Clean Flame Contract during that day.

*Catch:* The affected person asked for it. They named what they needed and accepted what it would cost. You did not choose the target; they chose to be cleansed, and that consent is what makes the deep work possible.

**●●●●● The Clean Slate** — *5 Essence*

A complete spiritual cleansing of an entire location: a building, a city block, a significant natural space. Every supernatural taint, Bane attachment, residue of dark working and accumulated spiritual damage is removed, however old. The space is spiritually new.

Beings attached to that taint — Banes living there, spirits feeding on the corruption, supernaturals drawing power from the damage — are expelled permanently, and must beat the Elemental's activation successes in a contested roll to return at all.

The ritual takes a full hour. Afterward the Elemental is bedridden for 24 hours. **Cost: 1 current Stability, direct, no roll.**

*Catch:* The place genuinely matters to you. Your home, somewhere you committed to protecting, somewhere a community you care for depends on. Not for payment and not for obligation. That care is what gives the cleansing its full force.
