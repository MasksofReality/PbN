## Who They Are

Mirror, mirror. Welcome to the Wicked Queens and Kings of the Lost. The Fairest were often beautiful before the Dreaming took them, and the Dreaming took that and twisted it into something that serves the Fae's appetite for adoration. They are more thorn than rose now, and they will make you forget that purity matters long before you realise what you traded away.

The Fairest crave adoration. Not to be liked — to be cherished, coveted, held as the most worthwhile thing in any room. This is not vanity in the simple sense. It is the wound the Dreaming left: the absolute certainty that the only safety is being too valuable to discard. They are the most cutthroat and manipulative of the Lost because they learned early that beauty without ruthlessness gets thrown away. They survived. That means something.

**Blessing: Of Them All.** The Fairest is the most compelling presence in any room, effortlessly. +1 die on all Charisma-based rolls and +1 die on all Appearance-based rolls. Mundane rolls only; neither adds anything to a Contract, and neither scales.

**Curse: Twisted Views.** The Fairest's certainty in their own read of a situation is absolute, and absolute certainty is wrong as often as it is right. −2 dice on all Intelligence-based rolls. This is not stupidity; a Fairest can be very clever. It is that they decide what is happening before they have the information, and evidence does not move them quickly.

## Contracts of Vainglory

**● Aura of Presence** — *Free*

The Fairest's magnetism radiates outward. For one scene, all mundane social first impressions are automatically positive unless the target has a specific reason to distrust them. This is first-impression management, not compulsion, and it does not survive contact with a person's actual judgement.

*Catch:* Entering a space where you are genuinely unknown. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Compelling Performance** — *1 Essence, Contested*

The Fairest performs — sings, speaks, dances, or simply stands — and one chosen target cannot look away or think about anything else for the scene. The target is unharmed and keeps full agency. They simply find the Fairest more compelling than anything else in the room, including things that are on fire.

*Catch:* Performing for someone who genuinely needs to be distracted from pain.

**●●● Legendary Aspect** — *2 Essence*

The Fairest's Mien blazes through their Mask for one scene, visible to everyone. Mortals who see it are struck with awe and cannot act against the Fairest for the duration unless attacked. Supernaturals contest on Grit + highest Art, and on a failure take −2 dice on all rolls directed at the Fairest for the scene.

This is a BRD breach in front of every mortal witness. Under the Pax Nocturnorum that means an Order of Vigiles investigation and, on a confirmed finding, a writ of execution. Use it when subtlety has already failed and you are prepared to spend the next month of play on the consequences.

*Catch:* You are genuinely threatened with death.

**●●●● The Undeniable** — *3 Essence*

The Mien blazes at full intensity, not merely visible through the Mask but overwhelming it. Every being who can see the Fairest perceives their complete supernatural presence with no filter. Mortals are struck with awe and cannot take hostile action for the scene unless attacked first. Supernaturals contest on Grit + highest Art to act against the Fairest or to look away.

The Mask is comprehensively broken for the duration, in front of every mortal present, all of whom will remember seeing something impossible. The Fairest deals with that afterward. There is no version of this that goes unreported in a Basilica.

*Catch:* You reveal yourself at the moment of your own greatest vulnerability. Wounded, afraid, or having just failed at something that mattered. The glory is strongest when it comes out of the truth of what you are rather than the performance of it.

**●●●●● Living Legend** — *5 Essence*

For one full day the Fairest is a living story. Every being who encounters them folds them into their personal mythology. Mortals remember them as the most significant person they have ever met. Supernaturals find them impossible to dismiss or minimise in any context. Anyone who watches the Fairest do something remembers that action as legendary in scale, whatever its actual size.

This is memory rather than impression. An Art attempting to edit or erase it is contested at +2 difficulty. Not impossible, but very hard, and the story tends to grow back.

When the day ends the Fairest is spent and cannot use any Vainglory Contract for one week. **Cost: 1 current Stability, direct, no roll.**

*Catch:* Someone who matters to you has genuinely given up on you. Your reputation in their eyes is at its lowest point, and that is the moment you become legendary to everyone else.

## Contracts of Mirror

**● Reflection Read** — *Free*

Meeting someone's eyes for a full turn, the Fairest reads their surface desires and insecurities. Not thoughts — emotional impressions. What this person wants most, and what they are afraid of losing. It cannot be switched off.

*Catch:* First meeting with someone you find genuinely interesting. (−1 difficulty.)

**●● Flattering Glass** — *1 Essence*

The Fairest alters their appearance within their Seeming: specific features, apparent age by up to a decade, hair and eye colour. A real physical change, not an illusion, and it does not touch the Mask's fundamentals. One scene.

*Catch:* Changing your appearance specifically to comfort or reassure someone rather than to deceive them.

**●●● Mirror's Truth** — *2 Essence, Contested*

The Fairest shows someone their own reflection as it truly is. Not their face, but their emotional truth as the Fairest reads it: their real fears, desires and self-image, made visible for one moment. It is profoundly disorienting. On a success the target takes −2 dice on all Social rolls for the rest of the scene while they put themselves back together.

*Catch:* The person genuinely needs to see themselves clearly, even though it will hurt.

**●●●● The Perfect Reflection** — *3 Essence*

The Fairest becomes a flawless mirror of one target. Not their appearance, but their entire self as they most wish to be seen, reflected back at the target and at everyone around them.

For the scene the target gains +3 dice on all Social rolls, as others perceive them through their own best self, and cannot act contrary to the values they privately hold most dear; the mirror holds them honest. Meanwhile the Fairest can express no personality or preference of their own. They are only the reflection.

*Catch:* You show someone their ideal self at the moment they have most completely failed to be it. After a mistake, a betrayal, a collapse. You do not comfort them. You hold up the mirror.

**●●●●● Mirror Maze** — *5 Essence*

The Fairest creates a zone of reflective reality in which every being sees only what they most need to see rather than what is there. Each perceives their own version of the space, shaped by their own psychology. It covers a large room or a small building and lasts one full scene.

Nobody navigates it effectively except the Fairest, and any being with current Stability 8 or higher or an equivalent supernatural resistance at staff's call. When the scene ends, everyone in the maze gets a moment of complete clarity: they see themselves and each other with no filter at all. It is jarring. Willpower at difficulty 7 to act normally for the following turn.

*Catch:* You build the maze while genuinely uncertain of your own reflection. You do not know, in this moment, what you would see if you looked honestly. The power requires that vulnerability, and certainty collapses it before it forms.

## Contracts of Feeling

**● Emotional Read** — *Free · requires physical contact*

By touch, the Fairest senses a being's genuine underlying emotion: the feeling beneath the performed feeling. Accurate, and not blocked by mundane composure. A supernatural effect concealing emotion is contested.

*Catch:* Flavour only. It fires automatically on contact.

**●● Stir Emotion** — *1 Essence, Contested*

The Fairest amplifies an emotion that already exists in a target, making it louder and harder to ignore. It cannot create a feeling from nothing. The emotion must genuinely be there, however faintly.

*Catch:* Stirring an emotion in someone who is suppressing it at real cost to themselves.

**●●● Emotional Anchor** — *2 Essence, Contested, Conditional*

The Fairest builds a genuine emotional bond between themselves and a target. This is not a Blood Bond and not compulsion. It feels completely authentic because the Fairest has actually understood what the target needs and given it to them. The target feels real affection or trust, shaped by what they actually want.

The bond has no timer. It lasts until it breaks, and it breaks the moment the target either learns that the Fairest acted against their genuine interests, or is shown clear evidence that the affection was engineered. Then it is gone, permanently, and the Fairest cannot re-anchor that person for a year.

While it holds, the target is disinclined to act against the Fairest's interests without strong motivation.

A bonded target does not count as a willing donor for feeding purposes. Their consent is supernaturally influenced, and the reduced-damage rate does not apply.

*Catch:* You genuinely like the target and want them as an ally rather than a tool.

**●●●● Emotional Architecture** — *3 Essence · requires a willing target*

The Fairest reaches into a target's emotional landscape and reshapes a long-standing pattern. Not a passing feeling — a deep-rooted response. An ingrained fear is made smaller. A source of shame is recontextualised. A grief that calcified into bitterness reopens to its original tenderness.

The change is real and lasting, made collaboratively with the target's own capacity for change. The target must be genuinely willing and the change must serve them. This cannot install a fear, a loyalty, or a shame. The Fairest absorbs the weight that comes loose and feels the original wound in full for one scene.

*Catch:* You are carrying the same pattern, in a different form, unresolved. You are helping them with what you have not yet helped yourself with, and that parallel is what makes the depth possible.

**●●●●● The Heart's Truth** — *5 Essence*

The Fairest creates a space — a moment, a scene — in which nothing but genuine emotional truth can be spoken or felt. All performance drops. All masks fail, the Fairest's own included. Everyone present experiences their authentic emotional reality unmediated.

Revelations come out that were not intended. Connections form between people who had been performing distance. Conflicts that needed to happen, happen, cleanly and with full honesty. The Fairest cannot control what emerges. They only make the conditions.

Afterward the Fairest loses all Social dice pools for the following scene, overwhelmed by the volume of real feeling that went through them.

<div class="callout callout--rare">
<strong>Table safety.</strong> This Contract reaches into other players' characters and pulls out things they may not have planned to reveal. Like a Cathartic Vice, it needs the agreement of every player whose character is in the scene, every time.
</div>

*Catch:* You have something true you have been avoiding saying, and you say it as part of the scene. The Heart's Truth demands your honesty too. You cannot open this door for other people and stand outside it yourself.
