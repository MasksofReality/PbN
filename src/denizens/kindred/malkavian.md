## Who They Are

The moment the world ended, the Malkavians went from laughing stock to the sanest people in the room. In the new world the broken are the wise. They speak truths nobody is ready for, they saw the real horrors first, and they carry that knowledge differently than anyone else does.

They are also the only bloodline with a native theory about the Interstice, and the fact that no two of their theories agree is, they will tell you, the finding rather than the failure.

## Your Starting Derangement

Chosen with staff at application (and with your Mentor in the fiction, if you have one) and written into your background. It is Stigma-class: it costs nothing, provides nothing, cannot be bought off, and is not a Flaw, since this system has none. Treat it as your character's truth rather than their obstacle. Options that work well here include Obsessive-Compulsive, Paranoia, Synesthesia, Fugue, and Prophetic Dreaming.

## Promise: The Web of Many and One

Something older than Auspex and deeper than Dementation. Two or more Malkavians in the same scene sense each other's presence and general emotional state without effort, and may pass impressions, though not words, reflexively. They feel each other's triumphs. No roll, no cost, no dice.

## Bane (Stigma): Webbed In

They feel the bad ones too, in the body. **In any scene containing two or more Malkavians, when one takes damage the others present take the same number of levels of the same type, simultaneously and unsoaked.** The same sharing applies between a Malkavian and anyone they are currently Tethered to who is present in the scene, which is a reason to be careful whose peers you eat twice. Damage transferred this way does not itself transfer onward; the chain stops at one link.
