## Who They Are

The Nosferatu went from the dregs of the line to its kingmakers. The Sundering disfigured a great deal of the world's population and wyrd-taint disfigures more of it every year, so a Nosferatu can walk the Mangrove stilt-hamlets and the outer wards among the altered and the ichor-burned without anyone looking twice. They turned their love of information into an open business, and after twenty-seven years of the Ascendancy teaching everyone what a registry can do, they built better ones than the Vigiles have.

Now they sit at tables no Ventrue can reach, hold paper the Directorate would like back, and know what is in every cellar on the City-Isle. It feels good to be the king.

## Presence Works Differently in Their Hands

See the [Presence entry](/denizens/kindred/blood-arts/#presence-ventrue-toreador-nosferatu): every tier reads as dread rather than glory, and Awe applies to Intimidation and information-gathering rather than to charm.

## Promise: Tech Bros

You take +2 dice on all mundane Technology rolls: Glyphcode notation, paratech maintenance and bypass, salvaged Old World scitech, leystone grading. In a world that runs on enchantment nobody fully documented and salvage nobody has a manual for, this makes them indispensable to every faction that needs something fixed, opened, or understood. It stacks with nothing on an Art roll, because nothing ever does.

## Bane (Stigma): Monstrous

All Nosferatu are hideous: diseased, disfigured, wrong to look at. Appearance is 1 at creation and cannot be raised by any Merit, Art, freebie, XP purchase or supernatural means. Obfuscate's Mask of a Thousand Faces conceals it; it does not cure it. In lit public space they conceal themselves with coverings, a mask or Obfuscate, or they are a registration incident that has not happened yet.
