## WHAT A SHIFTER IS

Gaia keeps a blueprint of what the living world is supposed to be, and the Conceptual Domains keep a reflection of everything mankind built on top of it. Both need hands in the Middle Realm. Shifters are those hands: mortal-born and beast-born lineages bound by pact to a spirit court, given a second body and told to go and be useful.

The pact is the whole of it. A shifter is not cursed and is not infected. A shifter is a person whose family signed something, generations back, and who inherited the terms along with the teeth. The court supplies the shape and the Gnosis. The line supplies the work. Neither side can dissolve the arrangement on its own, which is why so much shifter politics reads as labour dispute conducted with claws.

**The Sundering broke the labour market.** Before it, courts were stable, territories were assigned, and a line knew its patron. Then the Wyrd flooded back, the Pattern Web collapsed, and half the Conceptual Domains dissolved or reformed around things that had not existed a century earlier. Lines lost their patrons. New courts formed around foundries, landfills, waygates and quarantine wards, and recruited whoever answered. A shifter in 200 TE is as likely to owe fealty to something that lives in a Johannesburg pump-house as to anything with a name Kathmandu would recognise.

**The social problem, stated plainly.** A shifter in beast form is indistinguishable from an aberrant to any sane observer, and the Third Era pays professionals to kill aberrants. Registration is what stands between you and a legally uncontroversial death. Nothing about your nature makes humans forget what they saw, and no shifter power in this document does it for free.

**What shifters are not.** They are not aberrants, though a few Wyrdtouched lines are close enough that the distinction is a court matter rather than a biological one. They are not Awakened, and it matters enormously to shifters that nobody confuses the two, because the Awakened Censure Act is still in force. They are not Fae, though the Bitterthorn briarfolk and several Isle beast-lines have blurred that boundary badly enough that the Triune have opinions.

Choose one of each. Frame and Court are independent: a Stalker in the Iron Legion and a Stalker in the Made Courts share a body and share nothing else. That independence is the point of the kit.

Everything else comes from core, unmodified. Attributes at 7/5/3, Abilities at 13/9/5, 7 Psyche dots with a minimum of 1 in each, Willpower 3, 5 Backgrounds, 21 freebies. Nature and Demeanour are mandatory. No Flaws exist.

## COMPONENT A: KINSHIP FRAMES

A Frame is a body plan, not a species. Eight of them cover the terrestrial spread. A ninth exists and is staff-gated.

Your species must be a predatory terrestrial mammal, an avian, a reptile or a serpent. Grazing animals, insects, cephalopods and the various things people ask about every few months are not shifter stock and never have been. If your concept does not fit one of the eight below, it is a concept for a different splat.

**How Frame bonuses work.** Every bonus in the table is **bonus dice on mundane rolls only**. Core bars any splat trait from adding dice to an Art roll, and that bar holds here without exception. Frame bonuses never raise an Attribute either: the cap is 5 for everyone, and a Bruiser with Strength 4 has Strength 4 in every shape she wears. Wound penalties apply normally throughout.

## COMPONENT B: SPECIES AND SIGNATURE

Name the actual animal. That is flavour, and it sets your Kinship taboo. Then take exactly one **Signature**, the trait that separates a viper from a python inside the same Frame.

Signature effects are mundane-only, on the same terms as Frame bonuses.

| Signature | Effect |
| :---: | :---- |
| **Venom** | A successful natural attack also inflicts 1 lethal per turn for 3 turns unless treated. Soakable. |
| **Quills or Spines** | Anyone who grapples you, or whom you grapple, takes 1 lethal per turn of contact. |
| **Nightsight** | Darkness is never worse than \+1 difficulty for you, and effective darkness is never impossible. |
| **Echolocation** | You perceive shape and movement without light or line of sight, out to roughly 50m. Hush and similar concealment still work against it. |
| **Ambush** | \+2 dice to initiative and \+1 damage die on the first attack of any scene you began hidden. |
| **Burrower** | You can dig through soil, sand, rubble or rotten flooring at walking pace and leave no usable entrance behind. |
| **Armoured** | \+2 soak in Beast form, whether shell, plate or matted hide. Stacks with Frame. |
| **Amphibious** | Water is never an obstacle, in any form, and you breathe it. |
| **Tireless** | You ignore fatigue, exposure and sleeplessness for up to three days. |
| **Screamer** | A roar, shriek or call that carries for miles and that other shifters can read as language. \+2 dice on mundane Intimidation. |
| **Scentmark** | You can lay a territorial mark that any animal or shifter reads instantly. Mundane animals will not cross it. |
| **Mimic** | You reproduce any voice or sound you have heard, exactly, in any form that has a throat. |
| **Prehensile** | Hands, a gripping tail, or feet and beak dextrous enough to work. You open doors, carry, and use simple tools in Beast form. It does not give you speech. |
| **Gorge** | You can eat anything organic and most things that are not, without harm, and you can survive on very little for very long. |

Two of these are more popular than their descriptions suggest, and for the same reason. **Mimic** is the only Signature that lets you produce clear human speech in War or Beast form, which turns a shape that can only growl into one that can negotiate. **Prehensile** is the only one that lets a Beast form work a latch. Between them, they cover the two things new players always assume their animal can do and then discover it cannot.

## COMPONENT C: FORM SETS

| Set | Shapes | Notes |
| :---: | :---: | :---- |
| **Twinned** | Skin, Beast | No War form. Mandatory for Winged. Compensation: \+1 die to every entry in your Frame's Beast package, and you are far harder to prosecute, because you never wear a shape that looks like a monster with hands. |
| **Triad** (default) | Skin, War, Beast | The standard. |
| **Quartet** | Skin, Near, War, Beast | Requires the Half-Step Merit, 2 points. |
| **Dire** \[RESTRICTED\] | Adds a fifth, larger shape | Staff only, never at creation, story-gated. |

**Skin.** Your human shape. No bonuses. Passes as human automatically, including under casual paratech scrutiny.

**War.** The hybrid, and the shape that gets shifters killed. Your Frame weapon applies. You take \+2 dice on Brawl attacks and \+2 difficulty on every Social roll with any human who can see you, whether or not they know what you are. You cannot pass as human at all, you cannot speak clearly without Mimic, and you cannot operate most paratech, because the interface assumes hands of a certain size.

**Beast.** The animal. Your Frame package applies. You cannot speak human language, you cannot manipulate ordinary objects without Prehensile, and anyone who does not already know you will read you as an aberrant.

**Near.** A subtle shape: taller, wrong eyes, too many teeth. \+2 dice on mundane Intimidation and on Perception, and you can still wear a coat and hold a conversation.

**Changing form** is one action. No roll, no Gnosis, no successes to accumulate. You may act normally on the turn you change. Gear and clothing do not come with you and are destroyed or left behind unless you have Wildskin 1\.

## COMPONENT D: COURTS

A Court is your line's patron and its employer. It sets three Aligned Arts, your Attribute priority at creation, what counts as Replenishment for you specifically, and the ideal your line is expected to serve.

| Court | Patron | Aligned Arts | Attributes 7/5/3 | The work |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: | :---- |
| **The Quicksilver Road** | Mitanu | Hush, Parley, Palewalking | Phys / Soc / Ment | Carry things. Messages, contraband, people, secrets. |
| **The Veiled Grove** | Tambiyah | Beast-Call, Hush, Palewalking | Ment / Phys / Soc | Hide what is still alive. Sanctuaries, seed stock, last populations, and groves that appear on no survey. |
| **The Green Keepers** | Gaia | Ferocity, Beast-Call, Palewalking | Phys / Ment / Soc | Maintain the blueprint. Kill what should not be here, protect what should, and keep the cycle of birth, death and renewal turning where it still can. |
| **The Iron Legion** | Nerigal | Ferocity, Dread, Beast-Call | Phys / Soc / Ment | Garrison and campaign. War and strategy as sacred disciplines. |
| **The Storm Lords** | Zarok | Ferocity, Dread, Parley | Phys / Soc / Ment | Ride the fronts. Weather and wrath, sieges broken, droughts broken, and sentences carried out in the open. |
| **The Silent Guard** | Lu-Bat | Dread, Palewalking, Parley | Phys / Ment / Soc | Hold boundaries. Seal what is open, guard what is sealed, and enforce limits nobody else remembers agreeing to. Mostly silent. |
| **The Deep Seers** | Shantar | Hush, Palewalking, Dread | Ment / Phys / Soc | Dissolve boundaries and see what is behind them. Prophecy, forbidden knowledge, and places still uncharted. |
| **The Azure College** | Ruatma | Parley, Beast-Call, Hush | Ment / Soc / Phys | Record and teach. Keep the registers, translate between courts, and train the young of lines whose patrons died in the Sundering. |
| **The Dominion Courts** | Regional spirit lords | Parley, Beast-Call, Dread | Phys / Ment / Soc | Work the domain. Run the machine, tend the ground, feed the process, keep it going. |
| **The Silent Moon** \[RARE\] | Luna | Ferocity, Hush, Palewalking | Phys / Ment / Soc | Nothing. Luna's domain is void and haunting music from the dark. The lines bound to her take no orders because none come. |
| **The Golden Sun** \[RARE\] | Helios | Ferocity, Dread, Palewalking | Soc / Phys / Ment | Be seen doing it. Heralds, judgement at noon, and the burning of what ought to be burned. |

**The Dominion Courts** are the Sundering's children and the youngest thing in this world. A Dominion Court has no ancient name, no lineage myth, and frequently no fixed identity: it is whatever accreted around a working domain when the old order came apart. Foundry floors, tram substations, quarantine wards, the drainage works under a city-isle. They pay well, in the sense that the work is constant and the Replenishment is easy to reach, and they are regarded by older courts as roughly one step above employment by a landlord.

**The Silent Moon** requires staff approval. A Silent Moon shifter's patron cannot answer, which makes their Replenishment the hardest in the splat and removes their exception to presumption failure entirely. In exchange, they are the only shifters no court can order, renounce, or hold to an oath. Play it as an inheritance nobody wanted rather than as a licence.

**The Golden Sun** requires staff approval and admits only Winged, Basker, Serpentine, and some Anachronistic frames. Everything about it is public: the work, the sentence, the shape you wear while carrying it out. Golden Sun lines have the shortest average lifespan of any court and the most complete records, which their rivals consider related.

**Arts nobody's court claims.** Hunt and Mending are Aligned to no Court and are therefore Nonaligned for every shifter. Every shifter can hunt, and every shifter can heal. Nobody's patron gets to charge rent on it.

**Fetishcraft is Restricted.** It takes a teacher and staff permission to learn at all, is never available at creation, and advances on the Nonaligned track once learned.

**Discordant** means another Court's Aligned Art that is not on your list, at 39 XP to 5, and it needs an in-fiction route established in play before any XP applies. A Green Keeper buying Hush is not a purchase. It is a plot.

## GNOSIS: REPLENISHMENT, THE SUSTAINING CONDITION, AND ATTRITION

Gnosis is the Resource Trait and Essence pool for the whole splat. It equals your highest Art rating, and your pool is that plus 5\. Shifters do not feed and never generate a Tether as predator. They can be fed on, and a shifter drained to 0 loses Gnosis directly.

**Replenishment** runs at core rate: 1 Gnosis per 2 post rounds, roleplayed, never assumed. Core already states it for shifters as fulfilling a spirit pact or acting meaningfully on the patron's ideals. In practice, that means something different for each court.

| Court | Replenishment |
| :---: | ----- |
| Quicksilver Road | Completing a delivery, a crossing, or a bargain that moved something |
| Veiled Grove | Concealing, sheltering, or restoring something living that was about to be found or lost |
| Green Keepers | Tending, defending, or restoring a living system; killing something that should not be alive |
| Iron Legion | Combat honestly entered against a worthy opponent; drilling, teaching, or fortifying |
| Storm Lords | Coming through weather or violence that should have killed you; passing a sentence and carrying it out yourself |
| Silent Guard | Sealing, warding, or enforcing a boundary; keeping a vigil |
| Deep Seers | Immersion in deep water, deep dark, or a real mystery; scrying |
| Azure College | Teaching what you know to somebody who needed it; recovering a record, a name, or a rite thought lost |
| Dominion Courts | Working the domain: running the machine, tending the ground, feeding the process |
| Golden Sun | Being seen doing it. Public defence, public execution of a sentence, or standing somewhere visible and not moving |
| Silent Moon | Nothing reliable. Adjudicated per scene, and assume less than any other court gets |

**Ambient recovery** is core's: 5 Gnosis and a full Willpower refresh at the midnight reset, no roleplay needed.

**The sustaining condition** is the splat's one exception to presumption failure:

**Four unbroken hours of the cycle spent in Beast form, in direct contact with the raw material of your Court's domain, with nothing worked between you and it.**

* **Quicksilver Road:** a road, rail, or open crossing.  
* **Veiled Grove:** living wood, in a place no map records.  
* **Green Keepers:** unturned earth, living water, or a living tree.  
* **Iron Legion:** the stone of a fortification, a rampart, or a drill ground packed hard by use.  
* **Storm Lords:** open sky in weather, on high ground, unsheltered.  
* **Silent Guard:** a threshold, wall, or boundary marker.  
* **Deep Seers:** water deeper than you are tall, or dark with no light source in it.  
* **Azure College:** the raw stock of a library, paper and vellum and ink in bulk, against your skin.  
* **Dominion Courts:** the working floor of a live machine.  
* **Golden Sun:** direct unfiltered sunlight, outdoors.   
* **Silent Moon:** none.

The condition exempts you from nothing else. You can still be drained to 0, you still enter Attrition, and you still cannot activate a costed Art at 0\.

The practical consequence is the one the Order of Vigiles worked out about forty years ago. A silver-lined holding cell with a paratech floor denies a shifter the sustaining condition, denies them Replenishment, and denies them bane-free flesh, all in one purchase. It is now standard fitting in eleven Basilicas. Shifters call it the dry room.

**Attrition** begins at 0 Gnosis. Core gives shifters as feral, hyper-aggressive and territorial, with the Beast form taking over involuntarily and staying. On top of that:

* Regeneration stops entirely at 0 Gnosis unless you hold Mending 1\.  
* You cannot return to Skin form until you are above 0\.  
* If a scene ends with you still at 0, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6\. A botch is total loss of control until the next midnight reset, not merely until the end of the scene.

## COMPONENT E: BANE, AND THE REST OF WHAT YOU CARRY

The following are Stigmata: imposed by the fiction, costing nothing, removable by nothing. Every shifter carries all of them. None are optional and none are priced.

### Bane materials

You have two banes. The first comes from your Kinship, the second from your species' actual waking cycle, which you already decided when you named the animal.

Sky covers the Winged Frame and avian Anachronistics. Waters covers the Waterborn Frame and any serpent that lives in water. Land covers everything else.

|  | Nocturnal (Night) | Diurnal (Day) | Crepuscular (Twilight) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Child of the Sky** | Lead / Fine Silver | Lead / Gold (24K) | Lead / Green Gold |
| **Child of the Land** | Iron (not steel) / Fine Silver | Iron (not steel) / Gold (24K) | Iron (not steel) / Green Gold |
| **Child of the Waters** | Copper / Fine Silver | Copper / Gold (24K) | Copper / Green Gold |

Both banes bite equally. The base metal is the one you meet constantly, in rooflines and pipework and cell doors, and the noble metal is the one somebody buys on purpose to use on you.

* A weapon made of, plated with, or loaded with either bane deals **aggravated** damage to you in any form. That damage is unsoakable, and it is the one thing your regeneration cannot outrun.  
* Either bane in prolonged contact with your skin inflicts 1 bashing per turn and **suspends all regeneration** while it remains.  
* Being inside a space substantially built of either bane is \+1 difficulty on all rolls, mundane and Art alike.  
* You know both on sight and by smell. You cannot willingly touch either.

### The Read

Mundane animals know. Dogs bark, horses shy, birds go quiet, cats either flee or refuse to leave you alone. In any populated place this is a standing stealth and social liability, and in Calgary it amounts to disclosure. Mechanically: \+1 difficulty on mundane Stealth anywhere animals are present, and any handler-grade observer gets a free Perception \+ Animal Ken roll to notice something is wrong with you.

### Kinship taboo

One per Frame, set the moment you choose it. Breaking it marks a Stability trigger, and other shifters of your Frame will know, because they carry the same prohibition and they can smell what you did.

| Frame | Taboo |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Pack Hunter** | Never leave one of yours behind. Not a body, not a wounded packmate, not anyone you brought with you. |
| **Stalker** | Never kill where you can be seen doing it, and never take prey you did not stalk first. |
| **Bruiser** | Never strike first at something smaller than you, and never break a truce you accepted. |
| **Winged** | Never take what cannot leave the ground. No nestling, no fledgling, nothing penned, tethered or already caught. |
| **Waterborn** | Never foul living water. No body left in it, no kill abandoned in it, no waste in a source anything drinks from. |
| **Skulker** | Never inform. You do not give up a bolthole, a name or a route, and you do not refuse shelter to anything smaller than you. |
| **Serpentine** | Never strike a thinking creature without warning it first. Hood, rattle, hiss, coil, something. Prey is exempt; people are not. |
| **Basker** | Never take a kill from something already feeding, and never leave meat to rot that you could have finished. |
| **Anachronistic** | Never let yourself be recorded. No glyph-capture, no sketch, no cast taken of your print, no entry in any register that describes what you actually are. |

### The Frame frailty

Yours, permanently, as listed in Kinship Frames.

## STABILITY, THE LONG QUIET, AND THE LAW

### Additional triggers

A splat may add Stability triggers and may never remove or soften a core one. Shifters mark all core triggers, plus these, once per scene each:

* Broke an oath sworn to a spirit.  
* Broke your Kinship taboo.  
* Killed, maimed, or handed over one of your own Kin.  
* Took a life you did not need and did not use.

The core trigger for using an Art on the helpless or unaware fires constantly for Hush and Parley users, and mortals always count as helpless before an Art. That is intended. Hush is the splat's tool for repairing exposure, and it is priced in composure.

### Moving your current Stability

Both directions, both priced.

**Give In**, a loss. Once per scene, at your own call, you surrender the decision to the beast and automatically succeed at any single mundane feat of strength, speed, ferocity or endurance a beast could plausibly manage, with no roll. It costs 1 current Stability, immediately. It is not a trigger and does not roll against the trigger list; it moves the pool directly. It also does whatever the beast would do, which the storyteller narrates and you do not.

**Running**, a restoration. One full cycle in Beast form, in true wilderness, outside every Hearthstone field, with no human contact and no work performed. Restores 1 current Stability, once per lunar month. It cannot be done inside a Basilica, cannot be done in summary, and the wilds are where the aberrants are.

Both are deliberately poor trades, and you should know why before you reach for either. Running restores the same single point Catharsis does, at roughly a quarter of the frequency, for the price of a cycle spent offscreen or in solo play and a journey past the walls. It exists for the character whose Vice is currently unusable, not as a better option. Give In buys a guaranteed success in exactly the situations where you probably had the dice to make it anyway, which is the point: the moments it looks attractive are the moments you have already lost your grip on the decision.

### The Long Quiet

Core permits a splat one mechanic standing in for the **first Breaking roll of a lunar month**. This is ours. It stands in for that roll only, does not stack, and does not touch any later Breaking in the same month.

Instead of rolling Conscience at 0 current Stability, you may declare the Long Quiet: go to ground for a full cycle, in Beast form, alone.

* No Conscience roll, and no permanent Stability lost.  
* Current Stability returns at 2, not at your permanent rating.  
* You lose all current Gnosis and cannot replenish or take ambient recovery during that cycle.  
* You are out of play for the cycle, offscreen or roleplayed alone with no other character present.  
* Anything you had running, guarding, waiting or promised for that cycle does not happen.

Old shifters describe it as the only mercy the pact ever offered, and they do not say it warmly.

### The law

This is the part most likely to kill your character.

* Registered BRD status covers you in Skin form without qualification.  
* Beast form in public is legal in eleven Basilicas, restricted in five, and prosecuted in Salamanca. Purgatory does not care.  
* **War form in public is the problem.** It is the shape that looks like an aberrant with hands, and the Order of Vigiles treats any War-form incident involving a human casualty as a presumptive breach. A confirmed breach revokes BRD status and produces a writ of execution to the Aberrant Hunters, who are then acting entirely within the law.  
* **Unregistered, in Beast form, outside the walls, you are an aberrant at law.** No writ is required. A Nairobi guild hunter who kills you has filed correctly and will be paid.  
* There is no Delirium. Humans remember exactly what they saw, they can describe it accurately, and paratech recording glyphs hold the image. This is the Third Era after the Week of Torches. Nobody here has the luxury of a Masquerade.

## HEALTH, DAMAGE, AND DEATH

There is no second health track. Core's seven levels are the live track, with wound penalties as written, applying to Art rolls as well as mundane ones.

**Soak** is Stamina plus armour, plus Frame and Signature bonuses where stated. Aggravated is unsoakable, and no shifter trait, Art or Merit in this document grants aggravated soak.

**Firearms.** Core states that nothing makes bullets less effective against the supernatural, and that any splat claiming otherwise must say so explicitly. Shifters do not. Bullets deal lethal to a shifter exactly as they deal lethal to anyone else. What shifters have is not resistance. It is recovery.

**Regeneration.**

| Damage | Rate |
| :---- | :---- |
| Bashing | 1 level per post round |
| Lethal | 1 level per 5 post rounds |
| Aggravated | Core's mortal lethal rates, unmodified, and no faster |

A combat post round is three seconds of game time, so a shifter closes bashing about as fast as she takes it and spends a quarter of a minute on each lethal level.

Regeneration is free and automatic. It **stops completely** while either bane is in contact with you, and while you are at 0 Gnosis unless you hold Mending 1\. Aggravated cannot be accelerated by any Art here except Mending 5, which names aggravated explicitly because core requires it to.

**Past Incapacitated, lethal.** A shifter does not die. She drops involuntarily into Beast form and enters **Deadfall**: unconscious, unable to act, regenerating normally. Deadfall absorbs a further 3 levels of lethal. A fourth kills. A shifter left alone in Deadfall wakes up, and a shifter finished off in Deadfall does not. Everybody in this setting knows that, which is why hunters carry a second weapon.

**Past Incapacitated, aggravated.** No Deadfall. A single further level of aggravated kills outright. This is what bane weapons are for, and it is the whole reason silver is a strategic commodity in shifter politics.

**When the anchor breaks.** For a shifter the anchor is the pact, not the body. If the Court renounces the line, the patron is destroyed, or the shifter breaks an oath badly enough to be cast out, she becomes **Riven**:

* The sustaining condition stops working. Presumption failure applies in full and permanently.  
* All Court-Aligned Arts operate at \+1 difficulty and advance thereafter on the Discordant track.  
* Immediate loss of 2 current Stability, and a Stability trigger marked every cycle until a new pact is struck.  
* Forms, Frame, Signature and Bane are unchanged. The body was never the court's to take back.

Riven status reverses only by pacting with another Court, which takes play and staff approval, and which almost always costs more than the first pact did.

**Death.** A dead shifter's soul goes down the soul rivers like anybody's. Nerigal's spirits are documented to intercept accomplished warriors, and Iron Legion lines regard this as a retirement plan rather than a hazard. Whether a shifter can rise as one of the Restless Dead is that document's business, not this one's.

## SPIRIT ARTS

Ten Arts, rated 1 to 5, rolled as **Art \+ Gnosis, difficulty 6, 1s cancel**. Dot 1 is free, dot 2 costs 1 Gnosis, dot 3 costs 2, dot 4 costs 3, dot 5 costs 5\.

Every Art below states whether it is contested, and every damaging effect states its type and whether it can be dodged.

---

### WILDSKIN: the shape itself

**Affinity Art for every shifter. Aligned to no Court, granted by Kinship, mandatory at creation.**

Shifting between the forms in your Form Set is free and needs no Art at all. Wildskin is everything beyond your set.

**●○○○○** **Steady Shape** *(free)* — Your worn clothing and carried gear change with you and return intact. You may change form while grappled, falling, submerged, or at Crippled. You do not pay the Greater Hearthstone surcharge.

**●●○○○** **Borrowed Part** *(1)* — Graft one feature onto whatever form you are wearing, for a scene: claws, jaws, hide, eyes, nose, gills, a tail. One at a time. Claws taken in Skin form deal Str+1 lethal. Uncontested; roll only when concealing the change matters.

**●●●○○** **Wear Another** *(2)* — Take the appearance of a specific creature or person you have studied, within your band. 1 net success gives a convincing generic member of the type. 2 to 3 gives a specific individual, proof against casual inspection. 4 or more holds up to people who know them. Appearance only. You gain none of their traits, memories, or voice without Mimic.

**●●●●○** **Unmake the Frame** *(3)* — For a scene, change size band up or down by one, or build a War form to specification with features your Frame does not have. Your Frame package is reassigned to the borrowed band for the duration.

**●●●●●** **The Shape You Choose** *(5)* — Decisive. For a scene you are whatever you declare yourself to be, redeclarable once per turn, including the body plans of other Frames and non-animal shapes of comparable mass. Once during the scene you may **shed a wound**: discard all bashing and up to 2 lethal by leaving the injured shape behind. Aggravated does not shed.

---

### FEROCITY: the killing shape

*Aligned: Green Keepers, Iron Legion, Storm Lords, Silent Moon, Golden Sun.*

**●○○○○ Bared** *(free)* — Your natural weapons ignore 1 point of armour and never suffer the improvised-weapon penalty. You act normally on the turn you change form.

**●●○○○ Sundering Blow** *(1)* — Art damage: 1 level on a successful roll, \+1 per net success beyond the first. **Lethal** against living beings and physical vessels, **aggravated** against spirits, corpus, and the animate dead. Not contested. **Dodge allowed** on Dex \+ Athletics, because it crosses the space between you. Unsoaked.

**●●●○○ Unstoppable** *(2)* — For a scene, ignore up to −2 of wound penalty **on mundane rolls only**, since Art rolls take the full penalty as always, and you cannot be knocked down or grappled by anything of a smaller band.

**●●●●○ Blood Frenzy** *(3)* — For a scene, take one extra attack action per turn without splitting your pool. If anyone you harmed during it was not a real threat, mark the core trigger for acting on a desire during loss of control.

**●●●●● The Killing Shape** *(5)* — Decisive. Costs 5 Gnosis and 1 Willpower. For a scene your natural attacks deal **aggravated** to living beings and spirits alike, ignore all armour, and your lethal regeneration runs at the bashing rate. Automatically marks a Stability trigger on use, no roll, no exceptions. What you put down stays down.

---

### PALEWALKING: the Realm of Doors

*Aligned: Quicksilver Road, Veiled Grove, Green Keepers, Silent Guard, Deep Seers, Silent Moon, Golden Sun.*

**●○○○○ Threshold Sense** *(free)* — You see doors, crossings and thresholds for what they are, know roughly what lies on the far side, and know what toll each charges. You know when something is watching you from the Pale.

**●●○○○ Step Aside** *(1)* — Cross into the Pale Between at any threshold without paying the aggravated toll, carrying what you wear. Perception inside is muted, as the cosmology has it: the Middle Realm stays visible, drained and hazy, sound arriving hollow or not at all.

**●●●○○ Open the Way** *(2)* — Make a threshold where none exists, for yourself and up to one companion per net success, lasting one scene. Companions pay their own toll unless you spend a further Gnosis each.

**●●●●○ Deeper Roads** *(3)* — Cross from the Pale into an adjacent domain of your own Court, or travel the Pale to arrive elsewhere in the Middle Realm. 1 net success reaches within the same settlement, 2 to 3 the same landmass, 4 or more anywhere your Court's current runs. You cannot enter Arcadia, Everflux, Keystone, Repose, the Ten-Thousand Hells, the Labyrinth, or the Outside, and attempting it is how people stop existing.

**●●●●● Carry the Pack** *(5)* — Decisive. Take a full group across in one crossing, or pull something out of the Pale into the world. Once per scene, step out of reality entirely for a turn: untargetable, unreachable, and back where you left.  
>   
> **What the fourth dot buys, and what it costs you.** Deeper Roads moves cargo between Basilicas with no waygate, no Hearthstone and no manifest. The Council of Six would find that interesting. The Quicksilver Road already knows. Anyone reaching this dot should expect the plot to arrive rather than the other way round.

---

### PARLEY: dealing with spirits

*Aligned: Quicksilver Road, Storm Lords, Silent Guard, Azure College, Made Courts.*

**●○○○○ Spirit Tongue** *(free)* — Speak with and understand any spirit. You know its domain and roughly its standing on sight.

**●●○○○Petition** *(1)* — Ask a small service and be taken seriously. Contested against a spirit's permanent Willpower \+ highest Art, or the tier subtraction core uses for NPC spirits.

**●●●○○Oathbind** *(2)* — Bind a spirit and yourself to a stated bargain. Both sides are held. If you break it, mark a Stability trigger and expect the Court to hear about it. Left standing as a ward, the successes bank as a static threshold.

**●●●●○Chain** *(3)* — Compel service without consent, for one hour per net success. Contested. Using it on a spirit that was not actively resisting marks the core trigger for the helpless and unaware, every time.

**●●●●●Summon the Court** *(5)* — Decisive. Call your Court's local power to attention and speak to it directly. A real favour is on the table. So is a real debt, and it will be collected.

---

### HUNT: the pursuit

*Nonaligned for every shifter.*

**●○○○○ Nose** *(free)* — Track by scent and sign. You identify individuals by scent permanently thereafter, and you can read from a trail how many, how long ago, and what they were afraid of.

**●●○○○ The Long Chase** *(1)* — Ignore fatigue for a full day of pursuit. Your quarry cannot lose you by ordinary means, only supernatural ones.

**●●●○○ Mark of the Quarry** *(2)* — Name a quarry you have seen. For a lunar month you know its direction and rough distance, and you take \+2 dice on mundane rolls to find, pursue or ambush it.

**●●●●○ The Ground Tells** *(3)* — Read a place's last day: what happened, who was present, in what order, and what frightened them.

**●●●●● Nowhere Left** *(5)* — Decisive. Contested once at the outset. For a lunar month the quarry cannot cross a threshold, use a waygate, enter the Pale, or be concealed from you by any means, and you know when it sleeps.

---

### MENDING: the knitting

*Nonaligned for every shifter.*

**●○○○○ Knit** *(free)* — Your own regeneration continues at 0 Gnosis. You can close another's wound by hand: stop bleeding, stabilise, no levels healed.

**●●○○○ Lay On** *(1)* — Heal another: 1 bashing per net success, or 1 lethal per 2 net successes.

**●●●○○ Draw Out** *(2)* — Purge poison, disease, drugs or radiation from a subject, or suppress an anomaly's effects on them for a scene.

**●●●●○ Share the Wound** *(3)* — Take another's damage onto yourself, level for level and type for type, up to your net successes. It is real when it arrives.

**●●●●● Green Blood** *(5)* — Decisive. This effect names aggravated explicitly, as core requires. Heal 1 aggravated per 2 net successes, on yourself or another, once per lunar month per subject. It cannot touch aggravated dealt by your own bane, and no amount of Gnosis changes that.

---

### BEAST-CALL: the wild's ear

*Aligned: Veiled Grove, Green Keepers, Iron Legion, Azure College, Made Courts.*

**●○○○○ Kinship** *(free)* — Mundane animals do not fear you, will not raise an alarm, and will do simple things if asked. This does not suppress the Read. It only means the animal is calm while it stares at you.

**●●○○○ Speak With** *(1)* — Full conversation with any mundane animal, within its understanding, which is often more precise than a human's and always differently organised.

**●●●○○ The Gathering** *(2)* — Call every suitable animal within one mile per net success. They arrive and act as a body for a scene.

**●●●●○ Ride the Skin** *(3)* — Sense through an animal at any distance, or steer it precisely. Your own body is inert and helpless while you do.

**●●●●● The Wild Answers** *(5)* — Decisive. Call and command a host for a scene, aberrant beasts included, though aberrants contest. Alternatively, bind a single aberrant beast into willing service for a lunar month. Worth knowing that Calgary's beast-tamers achieve a lasting version of this with rope and patience and no Gnosis at all, which some shifters find humbling and others find insulting.

---

### DREAD: the thing in the dark

*Aligned: Iron Legion, Storm Lords, Silent Guard, Deep Seers, Made Courts, Golden Sun.*

Every contested effect here is a fear effect and is therefore resisted on **Grit**: Grit \+ highest Art for supernaturals, Grit alone for mortals, NPC Grit or the tier subtraction for NPCs.

**●○○○○ The Look** *(free)* — Anything that meets your eyes understands at the animal level that you are dangerous. \+2 dice on mundane Intimidation.

**●●○○○ Hackles** *(1)* — Contested, one target. 1 net success and it cannot approach you this turn. 2 to 3 and it flees or freezes for the scene. 4 or more and it leaves the area and will not willingly return this cycle.

**●●●○○ The Killing Field** *(2)* — As Hackles, against every hostile who can see you. Contested individually.

**●●●●○ Terror Made Flesh** *(3)* — The target's own worst fear takes shape and hunts it for a scene. Contested. Art damage, **bashing**, **no dodge**, because it originates at the target and always has.

**●●●●● The Thing In The Dark** *(5)* — Decisive. For a scene, everything within earshot that can feel fear contests each turn or acts only to get away from you. One target of your choosing who **botches** its defence is left with a lasting derangement, staff approval required.

---

### HUSH: going unfound

*Aligned: Quicksilver Road, Veiled Grove, Deep Seers, Azure College, Silent Moon.*

**●○○○○ Softfoot** *(free)* — You make no sound you did not intend and leave no scent trail. \+2 dice on mundane Stealth in any form.  
**●●○○○ Unnoticed** *(1)* — Ordinary attention slides off you for a scene. Uncontested against anyone unaware of you, contested against anyone actively looking.  
**●●●○○ Nothing Here** *(2)* — Extend it to a group, a room, a vehicle or a den. Left standing as a ward, the successes bank as a static threshold.  
**●●●●○ Forget the Fur** *(3)* — Erase the last minute per net success of what witnesses saw **of you specifically**. Marks the core helpless-and-unaware trigger every single time, without exception, and mortals always count as helpless.  
**●●●●● Never Was** *(5)* — Decisive. For a lunar month, records, scent, recordings, glyph-logs and memory all fail to hold you. Your BRD registry entry blurs, which is a felony in every Basilica that keeps a registry, and the Vigiles hold a standing file on the Arts that can do it.

---

### FETISHCRAFT: binding the willing and the unwilling

**Restricted.** Requires a teacher and staff permission to learn at all. Never at creation. Advances on the Nonaligned track once learned.

**●○○○○ Read the Made Thing** *(free)* — Know what any fetish, talen, glyph-work or paratech device does, what is bound into it, and whether the binding is sound.  
**●●○○○ Talen** *(1)* — Bind a spirit into a single-use object producing one effect equal to a 1- or 2-dot Art you know. Takes a scene to make. Expires within a lunar month whether used or not.  
**●●●○○ Fetish** *(2)* — A permanent, reusable bound object producing an effect equal to a 2-dot Art you know. The spirit must be bargained with or coerced first, which is Parley's business. Anyone can use the finished item.  
**●●●●○ Klaive-work** *(3)* — Bind into a weapon or armour: \+2 to its damage rating or \+2 armour dice, and the item may be made to deal aggravated to one named category, whether spirits or one kind of bane-bearer.  
**●●●●● Seat the Spirit** *(5)* — Decisive. Bind a powerful spirit into a place or a vehicle. A den that wards itself with a banked threshold, a boat that steers itself, a door that opens only to named blood. Staff-gated per instance.  
>   
> **Why this Art is Restricted, and what it will do to your character's life.** A fetish reproduces a paratech effect with no leystone, no Hearthstone draw, no Glyphcode compliance and no Artificer's guild seal. Bengaluru would call it uncertifiable, Johannesburg would call it a threat, and roughly four factions in Purgatory would call it an opportunity. Every dot of this Art is a political event, and the politics find you whether or not you sell anything.

## KIN, VITAE, AND OTHER AWKWARD QUESTIONS

**Kinfolk.** Most of a shifter line never changes. Those relatives are Mundane Humans with a Background, not a splat: no Gnosis, no forms, no pact, and no obligation beyond the family one. They can see what you are without flinching and they can hold property in a Basilica that would never register you, which makes them both the safest and the most exposed people in your life. Killing, maiming or handing over one of your Kin is a Stability trigger, and the courts do not accept excuses about it.

**A shifter fed Vitae.** Gnosis and Vitae conflict about as violently as Mana and Vitae do. This is not a table ruling and there is no chart for it. It is a scene, run by a storyteller, and it can end with a dead shifter. Anybody planning it should raise it before it happens rather than after.

**Wyrdtouched lines** exist, are close enough to aberrant that the difference is a court's opinion rather than a biologist's, and are not available at creation.

## FOR STORYTELLERS: EXTENDING THE KIT

A new were-line does not need a document. It needs a row.

**To add a Frame,** name the band, the War-form weapon and its damage type, three Beast-form bonuses totalling no more than 7 dice across them, and one Frailty that bites in ordinary play rather than in a scenario nobody will run. Compare it against the eight existing Frames before approving. If it is strictly better than any of them, it is not finished.

**To add a Court,** name the patron, pick exactly three Aligned Arts from Ferocity, Palewalking, Parley, Beast-Call, Dread and Hush, set the 7/5/3 priority, write the Replenishment act, and write the sustaining condition as a physical, checkable thing that the court's own members can actually touch. Never put Hunt or Mending on a Court's Aligned list. They are Nonaligned to everyone by design, and claiming one makes it Discordant for the rest of the splat.

**To add a Signature,** one line, mundane-only, roughly equal to Nightsight in value.

**Never** add an Art without checking core's damage formula and its bar on bonus dice, never grant aggravated soak, never grant a second pool, and never let a Frame bonus raise an Attribute past 5\.

**On balance levers.** The eight standard Frames run between 5 and 7 dice of Beast-form package. If Beast form proves dominant in play, trim the packages. Do not touch the Hearthstone surcharge or the action economy of changing form, because both are load-bearing elsewhere.

**On the Silent Moon and the Golden Sun.** Both are deliberately underpowered and deliberately available. The Silent Moon trades every support a court provides for the fact that no court can give it orders; the Golden Sun trades concealment for standing. Treat anyone pitching either as a power build with appropriate suspicion, and check what they think they are buying. If nobody plays them, that is an acceptable outcome. If several people suddenly do, find out why.

**On Fetishcraft pricing.** The setting has no equipment document yet, and Fetishcraft 3 and 4 produce items the economy has no price list for. Price them case by case, as the Artificer guidance already recommends, and treat the price as a plot rather than a number.
