Fifteen Arts: Discernment, which every Imbued holds; twelve Calling Arts, two to a Calling; Steadfast, which belongs to nobody; and Reckoning, which is restricted.

All are rated 1 to 5. Dots cost 1 free, then 1, 2, 3 and 5. Your Art roll is Art + Pneuma at difficulty 6, and effects grade in bands of 1, then 2 to 3, then 4 or more net successes. You may hold four Arts on your tree.

## Discernment *(every Calling)*

The Sight, trained. Reading rather than doing.

**Read the Room** *(free)*. You know the number and rough direction of all magically made items in the room you occupy. No detail, no identification. If you are outside, it will work within a 20 meter radius of yourself.

**Residue** *(1)*. Read what was worked here within the last cycle, its shape, its scale, and roughly when. Not who.

**Provenance** *(2)*. Of an object, a wound, a corpse or a leystone sample, learn what made it and whether the maker was something that should not exist. Uncontested against objects.

**The Long Look** *(3)*. Contested, and contested even against a target who is unaware or helpless. At 1 success, their kind and rough power tier. At 2 to 3, every Art effect currently running on them as well. At 4 or more, the state of their Resource pool and whether they are in Attrition.

**Attested Witness** *(5)*. See one event that happened in this place, as it happened, up to a lunar month back. Sight only, no sound, no interaction. The Sight cannot be lied to and can absolutely be misread. Mark the Stability trigger if what it shows you qualifies.

## Coursing *(Hunt)*

The finding. Nothing in this Art does damage of any kind, and that is deliberate.

**Cast** *(free)*. Stand where a supernatural being has been within the cycle and you know which way it left and how long ago, to the hour. Not what it was, not why. The trail reads through mundane cleanup and does not read through a Hearthstone field.

**Stay On It** *(1)*. For the scene you cannot lose a trail you have Cast, or a being you have Marked, to any mundane means. Crowds, water, doubling back, a vehicle, a change of clothes, a mile of open ground. Supernatural concealment works on you exactly as well as it ever did.

**Close** *(2)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes the subject cannot break your line of sight by moving, and any Art it uses to try is at +2 difficulty.

**Bay** *(3)*. Contested. For hours equal to net successes the subject knows it is being hunted and knows by whom, and takes +1 difficulty on everything while it is within a mile of you. A fair number of hunts end here, with the thing leaving the city and nobody touching anybody.

**Nowhere Left** *(5)*. Name a being you have Marked. For a lunar month you know where it is at any distance, on any layer of reality it can reach without dying, the Shadow and the Hedge and the far bank included. No Art conceals it from this. It knows you have it from the moment you cast, and it knows for the whole month.

## Anathema *(Hunt)*

**Mark** *(free)*. Designate one being you can see. You know its direction and rough distance while it stays within a mile, until midnight.

**Sear** *(1)*. Art damage, lethal, 1 level on a success and 1 per net success after that. It originates at the target, so there is no dodge, and it is unsoaked. It does nothing to anything the Sight does not read as supernatural.

**Cold Iron** *(2)*. For the scene, one held weapon deals aggravated to Marked supernaturals. Mundane damage rules apply in full, so you roll a pool and it can be soaked by anything that soaks aggravated, which is almost nothing.

**Hobble** *(3)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes the target cannot leave by any supernatural means: no mist, no step sideways, no shadow-walk, no crossing, no teleport. It can still run.

**Anathema** *(5)*. Art damage, aggravated, 1 level on a success and 1 per net success after. Originates at the target, no dodge, unsoaked. Damage from this Art cannot be healed supernaturally by anything and heals only at the victim's mortal aggravated rate.

## Bulwark *(Hold)*

**Stand Fast** *(free)*. Declare a spot you are holding. Nothing short of physical force moves you off it, and you cannot be surprised while you hold it.

**Interpose** *(1)*. A declared action. Take a blow aimed at anyone within arm's reach. The damage lands on you and you soak it normally. Subject to pool splitting.

**Ward** *(2)*. A banked effect. Successes bank as a static threshold on a door, window, gate or drawn line and hold until midnight. Anything supernatural that wants to cross rolls its own defence pool and must equal or beat the banked number or be barred. The banked figure is never rerolled.

**Sanctuary** *(3)*. Ward extended to a room or a small building, every threshold at once. You know the moment anything tests it and what direction it came from.

**Keystone** *(5)*. For the scene, within twenty metres of you, no Art may alter, move, conceal, transform or unmake terrain, objects or bodies, and every other Art activation inside the area takes +2 difficulty. Mind the difficulty cap: stacked with other modifiers this makes some workings outright impossible, which is the point.

## Custody *(Hold)*

Bulwark holds ground. This one holds people. Charges do not have to consent and are frequently annoyed.

**Take Charge** *(free)*. Name up to Pneuma people aloud. Until midnight you know the direction and rough distance of each while they are within a mile, and whether each is hurt.

**Cover** *(1)*. For the scene, any attack against a charge in your line of sight takes +1 difficulty. It does not stack with itself and no Hold has ever stopped trying.

**Bar the Way** *(2)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes the subject cannot decrease the distance between itself and any of your charges, by any means at all. It may attack at range. It may leave.

**Stand In** *(3)*. For the scene, any Art targeting a charge in your line of sight can be redirected to you as it is cast. You then resist it the way you resist everything, actively and knowingly, with the contest you always get. Nobody marks a Stability trigger for what is done to you afterward, because nobody ever does.

**Nothing Gets Past** *(5)*. For the scene, no charge within twenty metres of you dies. Damage that would take one past Incapacitated stops there instead, and you take 1 aggravated per instance, unsoaked, and it keeps coming as long as they keep getting hit. Ending the Art early is not an action you have.

## Testimony *(Witness)*

**Recall** *(free)*. Perfect, uncontestable memory of anything you personally witnessed, recitable word for word, indefinitely.

**Show** *(1)*. A touched, consenting person sees one thing you witnessed, exactly as you saw it, for a moment. If it qualifies, mark their Stability trigger rather than yours.

**Compel Answer** *(2)*. Contested. For a number of questions equal to net successes the subject cannot tell you a deliberate lie. It does not compel them to speak.

**Public Record** *(3)*. Show, to everyone present who can see you, at once, without consent. Mark every qualifying witness. This is the reason the Codicil has an evidentiary clause, and the reason the Shadow Directorate wants the Codicil repealed.

**The Ledger** *(5)*. For the scene, everything within earshot is recorded and can be replayed to any audience afterward. The record cannot be altered and cannot be reached by anything that eats memory: not the Attention backfire, not the Hedge's fog, not Oblivion's touch.

## Attendance *(Witness)*

Testimony is the saying so. This is the being there.

**Unremarkable** *(free)*. Mortals do not question your presence, do not ask your business, and do not recall you unprompted. It ends the instant you act on anything. It has never once worked on a supernatural.

**Clear Sight** *(1)*. For the scene, mundane obstruction imposes no penalty on your Perception rolls. Darkness, smoke, glare, distance, a crowd, a bad angle. Supernatural concealment is untouched and always was.

**Standing** *(2)*. Contested. For hours equal to net successes the subject cannot order you out, bar your entry, or have you removed from anywhere it controls. It can still have you killed. Witness Imbued who have not thought carefully about that distinction tend to find out which sort of host they are dealing with.

**Left Behind** *(3)*. An object you have handled this scene becomes an eye. For the rest of the scene, at any distance up to a mile, you see the room around it. Sight only, no sound, no interaction. Finding the object is an ordinary search, and people do find it.

**Nothing Hidden** *(5)*. For the scene, no Art conceals anything from you within line of sight. Illusion, obfuscation, invisibility, glamour, a stolen face, a working laid over a room: you see the thing and you see the working laid over the thing. It lasts one scene and it does nothing for whoever is standing next to you, which is what Show and Public Record are for.

## Assize *(Weigh)*

The deciding. Sentence is what follows, and it follows less often than the rest of the splat assumes.

**Convene** *(free)*. Declare a hearing aloud. Everyone present knows it has begun, knows you began it, and knows what is being weighed, in their own language. Nothing is compelled and nobody has to stay.

**Weigh** *(1)*. Name an act aloud, precisely. Contested. You learn whether the subject did it within the last lunar month. Yes or no, no circumstances, no intent, no detail. The precision is your problem. Name it badly and you get a truthful answer to a question you did not mean to ask.

**Hear Both** *(2)*. Two or more subjects speak in turn, and of each you know whether they believe what they are saying. Belief, not truth. Compel Answer stops the lie and this only finds the liar. Both together would be worth something, and no Calling holds both.

**Distraint** *(3)*. Contested. For days equal to net successes, one named thing the subject holds does not answer to them. A relic goes inert, a title opens no doors, a pact yields nothing, a name they took stops being theirs. You must be able to name the thing aloud, and vaguely named things are not affected.

**Outlawry** *(5)*. Contested, once per subject ever, spoken in front of witnesses. For a lunar month no Art cast by anyone else benefits the subject: no healing, no ward, no blessing, no shelter, no boon. Harm lands normally. If the subject did not do the thing you named at Convene, you find out before the month is out, because you always do, and you mark the Stability trigger. Being answerable is not conditional on having been wrong on purpose.

## Sentence *(Weigh)*

**Terms** *(free)*. State a condition aloud to a party who accepts it. Both of you thereafter always know whether it has been kept. No enforcement of any kind.

**Bind Word** *(1)*. Contested. A subject who accepted Terms takes +2 difficulty on any roll that would break them, for a lunar month.

**Stay** *(2)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes the subject cannot take violent action against you or against anyone you named aloud when you spoke.

**Deny** *(3)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes, one named Art of the subject's does not function. You must have seen it used, or read it with the Long Look.

**Sentence** *(5)*. Contested. Either the subject uses no Art at all for the rest of the scene, or one named Art of theirs does not function for a lunar month. You choose at casting, you say which aloud, and you cannot take it back.

## Balm *(Mend)*

Nothing in this Art works on an Imbued, the caster included.

**Steady** *(free)*. An Incapacitated mortal stops dying. They do not heal and they do not wake. They simply stop getting worse until they are moved or harmed again.

**Knit** *(1)*. Heal 1 bashing or lethal Health level per net success.

**Quiet** *(2)*. Contested. A subject in Attrition, in frenzy, or in any other loss of control comes out of it, or is held out of it for the scene. It restores no Essence and does not stop them dropping straight back in at the next end of scene.

**Draw Off** *(3)*. Transfer up to net successes in Health levels of lethal or aggravated from a subject to yourself. The type does not change. You do not soak it.

**Unmake the Wound** *(5)*. Heal aggravated, 1 level per net success. This Art names aggravated explicitly, which makes it one of the few things in the setting that speeds aggravated healing up. It does not work on Imbued and it does not work on the caster.

<div class="callout">
<strong>For the Storyteller:</strong> the most valuable healing in the setting sits in the hands of the only people it cannot be used on. A Mend Imbued is worth a fortune to every other kind of being alive and cannot buy a single day of their own recovery with it. Do not soften this.
</div>

## Remembrance *(Mend)*

The other reading of the word, given mechanics. Remembrance is not barred from Imbued the way Balm is. It simply finds nothing to grip.

**Who You Were** *(free)*. By touch, learn what the subject was before it was this, and roughly when it stopped being that. Anything that was never mortal reads as never mortal and gives you nothing else.

**Hold the Thread** *(1)*. For the scene, a subject losing itself — whether the sheet calls it frenzy or rage or hollowing or riding the wave — will remember afterward everything it did. It does not stop and it is not held out of anything. Mend Imbued argue loudly about whether this is a kindness. It is cast on request more often than not.

**Give It Back** *(2)*. Restore one memory per net success taken from a subject by any means at all: the Attention backfire, the Hedge's fog, Oblivion's touch, ordinary tampering. Contested if unwilling. You do not choose which memories come back, and neither do they.

**The Face You Had** *(3)*. Contested. For rounds equal to net successes the subject wears the body it was born in and cannot change shape by any means. Damage carries over unchanged and the shape does not heal it.

**Put It Back** *(5)*. Contested, once per subject ever. For a lunar month the subject is the mortal it used to be. No Arts, no Resource Trait, no supernatural trait of any kind, and no supernatural weakness either. It ages that month, it heals at mortal rates, and it remembers every hour of it afterward, because there is an afterward. Nothing that was never mortal is affected. Nothing happens to an Imbued.

## Alarum *(Warn)*

**Carry** *(free)*. Your voice reaches every ear in a defined space, a street or a hall, and is understood in the hearer's own language.

**Rouse** *(1)*. Everyone within earshot wakes, and any ongoing effect compelling them to sleep, linger or stay ends.

**Muster** *(2)*. Every Imbued within a mile knows where you are and the one thing you named. No contest, no consent, no reply.

**Clear the Ground** *(3)*. Mortals leave by the nearest exit, in order, without panic, and remember afterward that they chose to. Contested against each supernatural present individually.

**Hue and Cry** *(5)*. Name one being aloud. For a lunar month everyone who heard you knows its face and its nature, cannot be made to forget it by any means, and cannot be deceived about its identity by any Art. Used inside a Basilica this is a Week of Torches in miniature, and every Imbued alive knows it.

## Portent *(Warn)*

Alarum tells them. This is knowing first.

**Bad Air** *(free)*. In the moment before it acts, you know when a supernatural being in your line of sight has settled on violence. Not who it means to hurt, and not how.

**Look Out** *(1)*. A declared reaction. For the scene, nobody in earshot who can hear you is surprised or ambushed. That includes you, which changes nothing for you.

**Read the Weather** *(2)*. One question to the Storyteller, answerable yes or no, about the next hour and this place. Is the thing in the building. Is the door trapped. Will it come tonight. The answer is true, it is never elaborated, and you get one per scene.

**The Hour** *(3)*. Name a person you can see. For a lunar month you know whenever they are in danger of dying, and roughly where they are while it is happening. You will usually be somewhere else. Warn Imbued buy this dot and then wish they had not, and they do not stop buying it.

**Fair Warning** *(5)*. Name aloud a thing you have foreseen — an attack, a working, a collapse, a betrayal. Everyone who hears and understands you is proof against it once within the lunar month, and the first time that named thing comes for them it does not land. One instance each. The warning has to be specific enough to be wrong.

## Steadfast *(open to anyone)*

No Calling's property. Anyone may learn it on the Nonaligned track, 31 XP to 5.

**Watch** *(free)*. Go without sleep for days equal to Stamina with no penalty of any kind.

**Brace** *(1)*. For the scene, 2 extra dice on all soak rolls. Soak is not an Art roll, so this is legal.

**Endure** *(2)*. For the scene you do not fall unconscious at Incapacitated. You act at −5. Further lethal or aggravated still kills you.

**Unyielding** *(3)*. For the scene, 3 extra dice on contested defence and on all Grit rolls. Contested defence is not an Art roll, and a splat trait may add dice to a defence where it says so. This says so.

**Finish It** *(5)*. Once. When you would die, you do not. You continue at Incapacitated, acting at −5, until the scene ends or the thing you named aloud is done, whichever comes first. Then you are dying, you have minutes, and only Steady, Knit or mortal medicine saves you.

## Reckoning *(restricted)*

You need a teacher who holds it and Storyteller permission to learn it at all. Never at creation, under any circumstance. It advances on the Nonaligned track once learned.

Every dot, the free one included, costs 1 current Stability per use, paid on activation whether or not the roll succeeds. Permanent Stability is untouched, nothing restores what you spent, and the asymmetry is deliberate.

**Attend** *(free)*. You know whether the thing in front of you falls within the God-Machine's remit. Most things do not.

**Rescind** *(1)*. End one ongoing Art effect outright. Against a banked effect, equal or beat the banked threshold. Against a live effect, contested against its creator.

**Efface** *(2)*. Art damage, aggravated, 1 level on a success and 1 per net success after, against a being of pure aether only: spirit, shade, hob, plasmic thing, anything whose body is essence. Originates at the target, no dodge. Cannot be healed supernaturally.

**Seal** *(3)*. Close a door, gate, crossing or waygate for a lunar month. Banked. It closes waygates too, which is a commercial and political act, and the Council of Six treats it as one.

**Reckoning** *(5)*. Art damage, aggravated, 1 level on a success and 1 per net success after, doubled against anything that entered the Middle Realm from outside it. Originates at the target, no dodge, unsoaked. Every use is logged toward Requisition.

<div class="callout callout--rare">
<strong>For the Storyteller:</strong> Reckoning is the God-Machine's own hand, lent. It is the only Sacred Art that costs composure to use and the only one that writes to your log. A character who learns it has chosen an ending. Make sure the player understands that before you approve the teacher.
</div>
