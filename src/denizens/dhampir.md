## Those Born Caught Between Life and Death

## Who They Are

A Dhampir is the living child of an undead sire and a mundane human mother. They breathe, eat, bleed, sleep, and age. They also carry a corruption that should not be in a living body, and everything about their existence follows from that one contradiction.

Most descend from the Hungry Dead, the only undead lineage that can reliably sire life at all. A smaller number descend from the Restless Dead, and specifically from body-usurpers: shades who wear stolen living flesh. Both groups produce Dhampir often enough that most Basilicas have a handful.

### Growing Up Half Dead

*There is no healthy version of this childhood.*

The Hungry Dead have used their children as daytime servants for centuries, filling the role Ghouls filled for the Cainites of old and still fill for some Lilum today. A Dhampir is an investment expected to be useful above all else, and knows it early. They run the errands the sun makes difficult. They hold the leases, sign the papers, answer the door. Some are treated well, the way a valuable tool is treated well. Most find out eventually, at the first hint of defiance, how cruel someone who was twisted by hell's tortures can be to their own children.

Those sired by the Restless Dead get one of two extremes with almost nothing in between. Either the shade is a smothering presence that has decided this child will live the ideal life they never got — every failure is an insult, every success insufficient, and the obsessive nature of shades means they never give up, ever — or the shade loses interest, wanders off after an obsession, and never comes back. Both produce adults with a particular relationship to being watched.

Jaded and resentful Dhampir become Aberrant Hunters at a rate no other splat comes close to. There are countless theories about why: biology, immunities, the fact that they can work daylight hours. Every Dhampir hunter has heard all of them and has their own answer, which is usually shorter and about their parent.

### What They Know About Themselves

Every Dhampir learns the two facts that define them, and there is no gentle way to hear either.

The first is that they age slowly. That sounds like a gift for about a decade.

The second is that it does not matter. A Dhampir who dies always rises, and rises as the same kind of undead that sired them. However virtuous they try to be with the life they have, the ending is the same.

## Dhampir in the Third Era

Dhampir register as BRDs (Benign Reality Deviants), and whether they should is a running argument. They are alive, they read as human to every mundane test, and most Basilicas register them anyway, on the reasonable grounds that a Writ of Execution is a bad thing to argue about after the fact. A minority never register and pass as human for decades, which works right up until it doesn't.

Purgatory has the highest per-capita Dhampir population in the world, for two reasons. Everyone here came from somewhere else, and nobody's stated origin is challenged. Purgatory is also full of the *sires*, so a Dhampir here is never more than a few streets from the thing they are running from. Some of them did not come here to run.
