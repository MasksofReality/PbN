## Who You Are

You were born with it. No decade of schooling capped by seeing things you were never meant to and perhaps surviving. Your powers were there before you had a word for it, it came down through your family, and if you have children it will go to them.

Nobody can explain how it works. Get comfortable with that now, because it sits underneath every legal and social problem you'll experience.

The Basilican academies teach that Metahumans were created by Wyrd infusion, which is the closest thing to a consensus anyone has managed. During the Sundering or the Interstice, raw paracausal energy was bound into a genetic inheritance, and what came out the other side was more than human. The evidence for it is genealogical: Metahuman ability runs true along family lines, generation after generation. A Sorcerer watching you through Mana Sight sees none of the structured equations or planar conduits they would read off a working. They see an erratic, blinding cascade.

### What People Call You

| Term | Usage |
|---|---|
| **Metahuman** | The clinical and legal designation. This is the word in BRD files, Vigiles reports, and civic records. |
| **The Gifted** | Older and warmer. Still common in Purgatory, in the Circle of Bone & Ivy, and among Metahumans talking to each other. |
| **Mutant / Mutie** | A slur, carrying two centuries of pogrom history. In play it is treated strictly as hostile characterization. |

### Your First Manifestation

The gift stays latent until puberty. Then it arrives, usually in a moment of intense emotional outburst or intense emotional repression, and it arrives without asking. Almost nobody's first manifestation is deliberate. It is an accident, and often a bad one.

You cannot become a Metahuman during play. If your character is one, they have known for at least five years, and they have had that long to hide it or build a life around it.

## Where You Stand

| | Unregistered* | Registered (BRD) | Misregistered* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Status** | Passes as Mundane Mortal | Full Legal Citizen Papers | Falsified as Kin/Lost/Etc. |
| **Access** | +2 diff: Border/Work/Medical | Subject to Registry and Files | Valid Until Scrutinized |
| **Risk** | Uncontrolled Use = Exposure | Loss of Control = Incident Track | Discovery = Immediate Fraud Writ |

*Unregistered and Misregistered are restricted statuses. Both need staff approval at character creation.*

### You Are Not a Mage

The difference matters to you far more than it matters to anyone watching. The Awakened Censure Act was theoretically repealed in 180 TE under the Pax Nocturnorum, but the Awakened still disappear off the street, are widely known to suffer terrible mental health problems, and public fear of reality-warping humans has not cooled since. You carry no Sorcerous Stigmata and use no ritual formulas, and you draw on Psi rather than Mana.

None of that is visible to a frightened crowd. To them, an uncontrolled telekinetic surge looks exactly like rogue magecraft.

### Registering With the BRD

Under the Pax Nocturnorum you are Nightfolk, and Nightfolk register. Your classification is **Benign Reality Deviant**.

**The Registry:** the Order of Vigiles keeps genealogical archives tracing Metahuman Inheritances. An archivist with a registered bloodline in front of them can name your primary Inheritance Art and estimate how strong you will eventually get.

**Intent is not part of the law.** Your abilities fire on their own under extreme duress (see [Psi, Essence & Backlash](/denizens/metahumans/psi-and-backlash/)). A reflexive Art in public is an objective Pax violation, and nobody has to prove you meant it.

### Passing (Choose This at Creation)

Pick one of these three. It shapes how the setting treats you far more than your Arts do.

- **Registered:** a documented citizen with full civil protections. You are on file, and every witnessed loss of control goes on your Incident Track.
- **Unregistered** *(restricted)*: you pass as a baseline mortal and hold no BRD papers. Take +2 difficulty on any roll to obtain licensed employment, clear a Basilica border checkpoint, or receive institutional medical care. If you are seen manifesting, that is an Exposure event and the Vigiles start a manhunt.
- **Misregistered** *(restricted)*: forged or altered papers naming you as some other kind of Nightfolk — Kinfolk, Dhampir, Lost. You get everything registration gives you, right up until somebody runs a thaumaturgical inspection. Discovery means document fraud and breach charges together.

### The Incident Track

If you are Registered, every public loss of control that reaches the authorities accrues an Incident under the Vigiles' accidental breach protocols. They stack.

| Incidents | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **1 (Noted)** | A Vigiles officer interrogates you and opens an active monitoring file. |
| **2 (Supervision)** | A named handler, mandatory check-ins every two weeks, inter-district transit logged. +1 difficulty on any roll to act covertly inside the Basilica. |
| **3 (Judicial Review)** | A formal Pax tribunal. Can end in a **Glyphwork Seal** (an Artificer caps your Art ratings at 1, permanently), civic relocation, or BRD revocation with an Aberrant Hunter writ issued in your name. |

*Incidents clear at one per clean year of active play.*

## Metahumans and Other Nightfolk

Sooner or later somebody at the table will want to Ghoul you, Embrace you, or wire you with paratech. Here is what happens.

- **Sorcery** is barred entirely. You cannot hold Occult Arts, channel Mana, or learn Sorcerous paths.
- **Cybernetics.** Paratech implants interfere with biological Wyrd conduits. Installing augmentations permanently caps every current and future Psychic Art at the rating you held on the day of surgery, and caps Psi with them.
- **Ghouling.** See the Lilum [Vitae & the Cost of Immortality](/denizens/kindred/vitaeandcost/) page for details.
- **Embrace, Lost, Hungry Dead.** Undergo the Embrace, get taken into the Dreaming as a Lost, or rise as a Hungry Dead, and you lose every Psychic Art and all Psi permanently. The character converts wholly to the new splat's traits.
- **Being fed upon.** A predator draining you takes Psi directly. Drained to 0, you enter Attrition on the spot and risk Backlashing continuously against the thing that is feeding on you.
- **Death.** Your biology is mortal biology. Damage past Incapacitated inflicts terminal trauma, and further lethal or aggravated damage means clinical death.

## What You Gain and What It Costs

Every Metahuman carries these four traits. Two of them are the reason people want you on their crew, and two of them are the reason people are afraid of you.

**Strength — Attuned to the Paracausal.** +1 die on all mundane Perception or Arcane rolls to notice, identify, or track supernatural phenomena, active workings, anomalies, or aberrants. Nobody can gaslight or talk you out of what you sensed.

**Weakness — Paracausal Resonance.** To any active supernatural perception, whether Auspex, Mana Sight, Chi Sight, or Spirit Sense, you register unmistakably as an unclassified supernatural entity. Only the Common Art Aegis ●●● (Blank) masks it.

**Strength — Inviolable Source.** Your power cannot be severed, pact-broken, counter-magicked, or stripped. Any supernatural attempt to suppress or forcibly shut down one of your Arts takes a difficulty increase equal to your rating in that Art.

**Weakness — Autonomic Backlash.** Your powers are hardwired into your stress responses. Under duress, trauma, or Attrition they activate on their own through Backlash, with all the Exposure and legal risk that carries.

## The Six Inheritances

Every Inheritance runs in three Expressions. Each Expression is its own Art track, bought and raised separately, with its own costs. None of it was chosen. It came down the family line to you.

| Inheritance | Primary Attribute | Expressions | Role and Capability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Seer](/denizens/metahumans/seer/)** | Mental | Residue / Trajectory / Vantage | Psychometric reconnaissance and trajectory |
| **[Empath](/denizens/metahumans/empath/)** | Social | Living / Dead / Choir | Telepathic communication and mediumship |
| **[Kinetic](/denizens/metahumans/kinetic/)** | Physical | Force / Element / Vector | Unsoaked telekinetic and elemental force |
| **[Phantom](/denizens/metahumans/phantom/)** | Social | Unseen / Hunger / Oblivion | Infiltration and predatory psychic drain |
| **[Vitalist](/denizens/metahumans/vitalist/)** | Physical | Self / Other / Flesh | Somatic enhancement and injury transfer |
| **[Wildspeaker](/denizens/metahumans/wildspeaker/)** | Physical | Beast / Ground / Green | Animal coordination and wilderness navigation |

**Common Arts**, carried by every bloodline regardless of Inheritance: **[Wyrdsense](/denizens/metahumans/common-arts/#wyrdsense)** (paracausal perception) and **[Aegis](/denizens/metahumans/common-arts/#aegis)** (mental shielding and Backlash abort).
