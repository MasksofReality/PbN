## The Imbued

Imbued are best known as monster hunters, those that slay what goes bump in the night. They are celebrated as larger-than-life heroes of humanity in the same way as Sorcerers, though where Sorcerers are celebrated builders of the Third Age, the Imbued are celebrated as its guardians. Nightfolk opinions tend to be a bit more clear-eyed. Imbued are humans, bound to an unknown force that drives them to hunt and destroy the supernatural. Nothing prevents an Imbued from becoming jaded, nor from developing grudges and hatreds. Most of the Nightfolk who perished during the Week of Torches were at the hands of the Imbued, and every Nightfolk has a story about someone they knew running afoul of a rogue Imbued with a chip on their shoulder.

## The Harrowing

You died. You are sure of it. The aberrant killed you, and like every other mundane human in your situation you would have died helpless. Then you woke up, your body restored despite the painful evisceration you endured. Perhaps you prayed to someone, anyone, for the power to fight back. Perhaps you were too preoccupied with your insides being on your outside. Either way *something* etched a nearly unbreakable edict into your very soul, took your eyes, and in return put your broken body back together and imbued it with power and sight-beyond-sight. The Imbued know this experience as the **Harrowing**, and it is what makes them what they are.

Only mundane mortals can undergo the Harrowing, and it seems to happen at random to any outside observer.

When the Imbued awake, they find three things awaiting them:

* An Edict, etched into their very soul.
* The Sight, which cost them their mundane eyes.
* A pattern, woven into their very being, which gives them a unique durability and weaknesses no one else shares.
* A body that ages slowly, resisting mundane disease and the decay of time. An Imbued who somehow lives to 80 looks like they are in their 50s.

Nothing else about you changes. You bleed. You die of the things that kill people; you get no further second chances.

### Who Gave the Edicts

No Imbued knows, and the Edict remains just the same. Three answers circulate commonly:

* Most adherents of the New Church, and most who grew up near the Society of Leopold, hold that it was God's final miracle before He left, still unveiling itself. Followers of other faiths have similar beliefs, though the names and reasons change.
* Most who have some education in planar studies think it was likely either Lu-Bat, worldsoul of Saturn, or the Weaver herself.
* Most who have spoken with the Sorcerers about their Great Mechanism, which they seem to almost worship, see enough similarities in their Edict and the vast Ineffable Truth that empowers Sorcery to suspect both come from the same source.

## The Sight

Permanent, free, and always on. It is not an Art, and it never adds a single die to anything. Warrant paperwork calls it *attested perception*. Nobody says that aloud.

**What it gives you:**

* You see in your mind's eye in 360 degrees at roughly the acuity a healthy human eye could manage, and for you even the dim starlight of night is as bright as noon.
* You are never helpless and never unaware. You always count as actively and knowingly resisting, so the uncontested-target rule never applies to you and the subtraction for a resisting target always does.
* Supernatural beings, anomalies and aberrants stand out in an outline glow like a halo, making them easy to notice. No roll. It does not tell you age, type, power, or threat level — that is what Discernment and hard Eldritch lore are for. Telling a Nightfolk from an aberrant from an anomaly is an ordinary Perception + Awareness roll at difficulty 6, modified freely for circumstance.

**What it costs you:**

* Your mundane eyes are **gone**. Missing. Only empty sockets. As most people would not respond well to such a sight, most Imbued cover them with opaque glasses or goggles, a blindfold, or some other covering.
* Mundane objects are colorless before the Sight, robbing you of simple joys like a rainbow.
* It never shuts off. Not in sleep, not in company, not in a room where being seen to see would get you killed. Add 1 difficulty to any Social roll where you are trying to feign ignorance of a supernatural presence the Sight has already shown you.
* Nobody ever marks the Stability trigger for using an Art on a helpless or unaware victim when the victim was you. Working on an Imbued troubles no conscience anywhere.

## The Anchored Pattern

Your body actively resists forces attempting to change it.

**The Benefit.** You can soak physical aggravated damage on Stamina alone. Armour never applies to aggravated damage as normal. Roll it at difficulty 6 like any soak: never penalised by wounds, never an action. This covers mundane, physical damage only — claws, fangs, fire, a paratech weapon that names aggravated, a Garou's jaws. It does nothing against Art damage, which stays unsoaked unless the Art itself grants a soak, and nothing against the aggravated paid to Push, since that is a cost rather than damage.

**Nothing transforms you.** You cannot be Embraced, ghouled, pacted, taken by the Gentry, raised as Restless Dead, or awakened as a sorcerer or a psychic. The attempt does not partially work. Vitae will not sit in you, a pact will not close, and an Embrace kills the body without producing anything. There is no resistance roll here. It simply does not happen.

**The Cost.** No supernatural healing takes. Not your own kind's, not anyone else's, not a ritual, not a relic, not a faerie fruit. You heal at the mortal rates, by mortal medicine, and there is no second route. Balm does not work on an Imbued, including the Imbued casting it, which means you cannot even patch each other up.

**Being fed on.** Nothing special happens. Your Pneuma drains like any Nightfolk pool, converts at full value, and generates Tether normally — no mechanical penalty. What there is instead: every other Imbued within reach of the Sight can see what was done, on the predator, for the rest of the cycle. That is a social fact, not a die roll, and it has ended more careers than the drain ever has.

## Pneuma

Your Resource Trait is Pneuma. Your Essence Pool is Pneuma + 5, and Pneuma comes off your highest Sacred Art. A starting character with an Affinity Art at 2 has Pneuma 2 and a pool of 7.

**Refilling it:** you keep a declared watch, in the open, over a person, a threshold or a place you have named as yours. Or you do the work that your Edict demands, somewhere it can be seen. Standard rate: 1 Pneuma per 2 rounds of play, deliberate, visible and roleplayed. The phrase *in the open* is doing real work there. You cannot refuel by privately feeling strongly about something. The watch has to be a watch somebody could walk past.

**When there is nothing to watch:** the presumption-failure rule applies to you with no exception. An Imbued locked in a cell with nothing to hold and nobody to hold it for receives nothing at midnight.

**Attrition:** the imperative speaks with the filter off. You pursue your Calling on the nearest available subject and you cannot weigh innocence, proportion or consequence while it lasts. Hunt attacks. Hold refuses to let anyone pass, including the people it was holding the door for. Weigh sentences. Mend treats whoever is in front of it, consent or no consent. Attrition is a social catastrophe first and a violence problem second. It is how Warrants get pulled.

**Pushing:** spend 1 Willpower and 1 aggravated to substitute the base Essence cost, or 1 extra die at a strict one-for-one. The aggravated is unsoakable as a cost, and it heals at the mortal lethal rate. A capstone Imbued who Pushes twice in one night is Wounded for a week.

## The Callings

Your Calling is the word you heard. It does not repeat and it is not negotiable. Six are attested. The Calling sets your Attribute priority at creation and names the Arts you learn on the Aligned track.

| Calling | The Edict | Aligned Arts | Affinity (mandatory) | Attributes (7/5/3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hunt** | Find it and end it | Coursing, Anathema, Discernment | Coursing | Physical / Mental / Social |
| **Hold** | Allow nothing past you | Bulwark, Custody, Discernment | Bulwark | Physical / Social / Mental |
| **Witness** | See it, and say so | Testimony, Attendance, Discernment | Testimony | Mental / Social / Physical |
| **Weigh** | Decide, and be answerable | Assize, Sentence, Discernment | Assize | Mental / Social / Physical |
| **Mend** | Put it back | Balm, Remembrance, Discernment | Balm | Mental / Physical / Social |
| **Warn** | Tell them | Alarum, Portent, Discernment | Alarum | Social / Mental / Physical |

Where a Calling's word has two halves, the mandatory dot goes to the first half. Find it, then end it. Decide, then be answerable. No starting character opens play holding the coercive or lethal half of their own Calling.

Every Imbued of every Calling also holds Discernment on the Aligned track. It belongs to the trade rather than to any one Calling, the way Glyphwork and Scrying belong to every Sorcerer.

**Learning outside your Calling:** another Calling's Aligned Art counts as Discordant, which costs 39 XP and a route in the fiction. The route is narrow for Imbued, because there are no teachers who are not also Imbued, and most of them think cross-Calling instruction is a category error. A Hold who wants Anathema needs to find a Hunt willing to break something to teach it.

**The argument about Mend:** *Put it back* has been interpreted as *heal the wounded* and as *restore the person the monster used to be* by people bearing the same Edict, and the splat has been arguing about it for a century. Both readings are attested and neither is confirmed. Both are now purchasable, as Balm and as Remembrance.
