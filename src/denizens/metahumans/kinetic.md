## Who They Are

Kinetics move things: objects, an element, momentum itself. Of the six this is the one that leaves the most wreckage behind, and Collateral Exposure is the price of that.

**Attribute priority:** Physical primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Social tertiary (3)
**Element choice:** select one element at creation (Fire, Water, Earth, or Air)
**Expressions:** Force (Telekinesis) · Element (Elemental Control) · Vector (Inertial Control)

## Force (Telekinesis)

**●○○○○ Minor Telekinesis.** Free. Move or levitate small objects under 5 lbs within line of sight, with fine motor control.

**●●○○○ Object Control.** 1 Essence. Lift, hold, or manipulate up to 200 lbs within line of sight. Hurling an object uses Dexterity + Athletics and inflicts standard blunt damage, soaked normally.

**●●●○○ Kinetic Blast.** 2 Essence. Unleash a concussive shockwave. Ranged stroke, Dodgeable. Inflicts 1 level of bashing damage + 1 per net success, unsoaked.

**●●●●○ Sustained Multi-Control.** 3 Essence. Independently manipulate up to 5 heavy objects at once for a scene, or maintain a kinetic barrier granting +3 soak against physical attacks.

**●●●●● Force Shatter.** 5 Essence. Focused structural implosion originating directly at the target, Undodgeable. Inflicts 1 level of lethal damage + 1 per net success, unsoaked, and obliterates mundane reinforced barriers.

## Element (Chosen Element)

**●○○○○ Element Awareness.** Free. Sense the location, volume, purity, and manipulation of your chosen element across a wide area.

**●●○○○ Element Shape.** 1 Essence. Redirect and reshape existing quantities of your element: bend flames, shape water currents, part air, sculpt loose earth.

**●●●○○ Element Surge.** 2 Essence. Project a blast of elemental energy. Ranged stroke, Dodgeable. Inflicts 1 level of lethal damage (aggravated if Fire) + 1 per net success, unsoaked.

**●●●●○ Element Domain.** 3 Essence. Command large volumes of your element for a scene: a wall of earth granting full cover, suppression of a burning room, gale-force winds.

**●●●●● Element Mastery.** 5 Essence. Unleash building-scale elemental control: divert a river, extinguish a massive inferno, rupture a city plaza with tremors. Once per session.

## Vector (Inertial Control)

**●○○○○ Sure Footing.** Free. Passive. You cannot be made to lose your footing, you ignore all penalties from unstable or slick terrain, and you take no damage from falls of ten feet or less.

**●●○○○ Redirect.** 1 Essence. Reflexive, once per turn. Shed the momentum of a single incoming physical attack, declared before damage is rolled, granting +3 soak against it. Or halve the damage of any fall.

**●●●○○ Leap the Gap.** 2 Essence. Kinetic propulsion for one scene: leaps of up to thirty yards, wall-running, and controlled descent from any height. Grants +3 dice on mundane Athletics rolls for movement.

**●●●●○ Locked Frame.** 3 Essence. For one scene, anchor your own inertia. You cannot be moved, thrown, knocked prone, grappled, or disarmed by anything short of structural force, and you gain +2 automatic soak against bashing. May be inverted at activation to shed inertia instead, slipping any mundane restraint or grapple automatically.

**●●●●● Free Flight.** 5 Essence. True flight at running speed for one scene, carrying one passenger. Once per scene, convert flight into a full-body impact against a single target for 1 level of lethal damage + 1 per net success, unsoaked, delivered as a discrete stroke (Dodgeable).

## Inheritance Strength: Living Weapon

You are never unarmed, you cannot be stripped of your power, and you can use Force abilities while physically restrained.

## Inheritance Weakness: Collateral Exposure

Your abilities are loud and destructive, and they leave obvious physical signatures. An uncontrolled Kinetic Backlash scrambles paratech leystone circuits throughout the scene.

## Backlash: The Pressing Hand, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Force** | Everything unsecured in the scene is hurled at the threat at maximum rating, and the structural implosion follows. | A concussive burst clears a path through walls, windows, and anyone standing in the gap. | A kinetic shell seals around the body, immobile, crushing whatever reaches into it. |
| **Element** | The chosen element floods outward at full intensity with no discrimination. Fire inflicts aggravated damage on everyone in the room. | The element obscures and blocks pursuit: steam, dust, blinding smoke, upheaved ground. | The element wraps the body, encased in earth or sheathed in water or standing inside a corona, while the Metahuman is catatonic. |
| **Vector** | A full-body impact charge at the threat, undodgeable, with no regard for what is behind it. | Uncontrolled propulsion toward open air, through whatever is in the way. | Locked Frame at maximum: immovable, silent, non-responsive. |
