## Who They Are

Empaths work in feeling: one mind, the local dead, or a whole crowd at once. The door opens inward as well, which is what Bleed-Through is about.

**Attribute priority:** Social primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Physical tertiary (3)
**Expressions:** Living (Telepathy) · Dead (Mediumship) · Choir (Mass Empathy)

## Living (Telepathy)

**●○○○○ Surface Feeling.** Free. Know the dominant emotion of anyone in line of sight, and whether they are performing an emotion they do not hold. Feeling only, never facts.

**●●○○○ Mind to Mind.** 1 Essence. Silent two-way telepathic conversation for one scene with one subject you can see, who is either willing or touched by you. Meaning transmits rather than words, so language is no barrier.

**●●●○○ Read the Surface.** 2 Essence. Perceive a target's active surface thoughts for one scene. Contested against permanent Willpower + highest Art; unaware or unresisting mortals are uncontested. Grants +2 dice on mundane Social rolls against that target while active. Reaches present thought only, never memory.

**●●●●○ Impress.** 3 Essence. Push one emotion or single imperative impulse into a target's mind for a scene: trust, dread, doubt, a simple instruction. Contested. The target may resist again immediately if the impulse endangers them or contradicts a core conviction. **Coercive mental influence is a Pax breach if witnessed or proven.**

**●●●●● Deep Communion.** 5 Essence. Enter a mind entirely: buried memory, honest motive, the things the target does not admit to themselves. Or link up to (Psi rating) willing minds into one shared awareness for a scene. Involuntary use is contested and triggers a Stability roll for both parties, and is prosecuted as mind-invasion under the Pax Nocturnorum.

## Dead (Mediumship)

**●○○○○ Cold Presence.** Free. Sense whether the dead are present, roughly how many, and their emotional weather. You know where in a room a person died.

**●●○○○ Speak with the Dead.** 1 Essence. Converse with a ghost or wraith present in the scene. They perceive you clearly and can answer. There is no compulsion in it, so the dead may lie, bargain, or refuse.

**●●●○○ Summon the Lingering.** 2 Essence. Call the nearest dead bound to this place, object, or bloodline. An unwilling spirit contests on Willpower + its own rating. This compels presence and attention. It does not compel honesty.

**●●●●○ Lend the Voice.** 3 Essence. Invite a spirit to speak and act through your body for one scene. You may substitute the spirit's rating for one of your Abilities, and you do not control your body while it rides you. Mandatory Stability roll when it ends.

**●●●●● Open the Door.** 5 Essence. For one scene, manifest the local dead to everyone present as visible, audible, half-solid figures. Or contest a single ghost to lay it permanently to rest. Mandatory Stability roll. An unmissable Exposure event.

## Choir (Mass Empathy)

**●○○○○ Take the Room.** Free. Read the aggregate emotional state of any crowd: where the tension sits, who is about to break, whether the mood is about to turn. Grants +2 dice on mundane Empathy or Alertness rolls to anticipate a riot, an ambush, or a walkout.

**●●○○○ Set the Tone.** 1 Essence. Shift a room's ambient mood one step for a scene: cool a tense barroom, sour a celebration, steady a panicking queue. Individuals who actively resist roll permanent Willpower to exempt themselves.

**●●●○○ Sway the Many.** 2 Essence. Impose one specific emotion — fear, fervor, apathy, trust — on an entire crowd for a scene. Enough to disperse a forming mob, or to assemble one. Supernaturals and mortals with permanent Willpower 7+ contest individually.

**●●●●○ Broadcast.** 3 Essence. Project a single wordless imperative — *scatter*, *kneel*, *be silent*, *run* — to every mind within a city block. Mundane crowds comply for the scene unless compliance directly endangers them. Supernaturals contest on permanent Willpower + highest Art.

**●●●●● The Common Heart.** 5 Essence. For one scene, bind a district's crowds into one emotional organism with you at its center. You sense through all of them at once and move them as a single body, and you feel every injury and death among them as it happens. Mandatory Stability roll. Once per story arc, with staff confirmation.

## Inheritance Strength: Nothing Performs Past You

No mundane performance can fool you about what another person actually feels, and you gain +1 die on all mundane Empathy or Subterfuge rolls to detect deception. Facts can still be hidden from you. Feelings cannot.

## Inheritance Weakness: Bleed-Through

The door opens inward too. In crowded, grieving, panicked, or violent environments you take −1 die on all mundane Mental rolls. On top of that, witnessing traumatic grief or terror in someone else counts as a Trauma trigger for you (roll permanent Willpower, difficulty 6), whether or not you were ever in danger yourself.

## Backlash: The Open Door, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Living** | Raw pain and rage forced straight into the threat's mind. Mortals seize, vomit, or collapse. | A crude imperative, *look elsewhere*, shoved into every mind in reach. Allies included. | Every nearby mind floods inward at once. Catatonia, and a Stability roll on emergence. |
| **Dead** | The local dead are flung at the threat, manifest and screaming, consent not required. | The door opens wide and the Empath hides among the dead. The living lose track of them entirely. | The Empath is ridden. A lingering ghost takes the body until the scene ends. |
| **Choir** | Terror or fury broadcast into every mind in range. Crowds riot, stampede, or turn on each other. | A compulsion to disperse and forget the exits, pushed across a city block. | The Empath absorbs the room instead of projecting it. Catatonic and weeping, feeling all of it. |
