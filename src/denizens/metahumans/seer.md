## Who They Are

Seers read what has already happened, what is happening somewhere else, and what is about to happen.

**Attribute priority:** Mental primary (7) · Social secondary (5) · Physical tertiary (3)
**Expressions:** Residue (Psychometry) · Trajectory (Precognition) · Vantage (Clairvoyance)

## Residue (Psychometry)

**●○○○○ Surface Read.** Free. Physical contact with an object or person reveals the dominant emotional impression left within the last 24 hours.

**●●○○○ Object History.** 1 Essence. Touch an object for one minute and receive clear sensory impressions of its significant past owners and any traumatic events.

**●●●○○ Deep Reading.** 2 Essence. Full sequence scan of a location or item. Three net successes give you an exact chronological playback of past events.

**●●●●○ Psychic Echo.** 3 Essence. Relive a past traumatic event at your current location in real time. May trigger a Stability check if the event involves horror.

**●●●●● True History.** 5 Essence. Perceive the complete, unbroken history of a person, place, or relic from creation to the present. Sensory exhaustion imposes −2 dice on all mundane rolls for 24 hours.

## Trajectory (Precognition)

**●○○○○ Weathervane.** Free. Once per scene, before making a significant choice, sense whether a course of action brings immediate personal harm or benefit.

**●●○○○ Likely Outcome.** 1 Essence. Ask the Storyteller one specific question about the probable trajectory of events over the next 24 hours, based on current conditions.

**●●●○○ Vision of the Path.** 2 Essence. Receive a vision detailing the likeliest outcome of a major decision over the coming week.

**●●●●○ Cascade Read.** 3 Essence. Observe multiple probability branches from a single decision point and identify the currently dominant timeline.

**●●●●● The Fixed Point.** 5 Essence. Identify a fixed, inevitable future outcome that cannot be averted under current conditions. Once per story arc, with staff confirmation.

## Vantage (Clairvoyance)

**●○○○○ Cold Spot.** Free. Sense the number, rough position, and general disposition of living minds through walls, doors, and floors within roughly twenty yards. You know whether a room is occupied, and whether it is being watched.

**●●○○○ Cast the Eye.** 1 Essence. Project sight and hearing to a location you have physically stood in within the last month, for a few minutes. Your body stays upright but distracted, taking −2 dice on all mundane rolls while the eye is cast.

**●●●○○ Anchored Sight.** 2 Essence. Remote-view any location in the Basilica you hold a personal anchor to: a person you have touched, an object you have handled, a threshold you have crossed. Lasts one scene, and your body is catatonic. Warded sites contest with the ward's rating as difficulty.

**●●●●○ Split Focus.** 3 Essence. Sustain remote viewing while awake, mobile, and able to act, or hold two separate vantages at once. Either mode imposes −1 die on mundane rolls for the duration.

**●●●●● The Standing Eye.** 5 Essence. Perceive any person or place you can name on the continent, anchor or no anchor, for one scene. Pierces mundane and paratech observation-denial. Active supernatural wards may contest at difficulty 9. Sensory exhaustion imposes −2 dice on all mundane rolls for 24 hours. Once per session.

## Inheritance Strength: Passive Psychometry

Free passive psychometric impressions from any room or object you touch for more than one continuous minute.

## Inheritance Weakness: Sensory Flooding

−1 die on all Social rolls in unfamiliar environments.

## Backlash: The Long Sight, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residue** | Every violent memory the room holds replays at once, forced outward into the threat's senses. Witnesses may need Stability rolls of their own. | Awareness sinks into the past and the body follows it, walking routes dead occupants used, toward exits that may no longer exist. | Locked into the site's worst memory and reliving it in real time. Unresponsive, and often screaming in someone else's voice. |
| **Trajectory** | The Seer narrates the next minute aloud, involuntarily and accurately, including what everyone present intends to do. Allies' secrets, plans, and positions all spill out. | The body moves on precognitive autopilot down the one survivable path, abandoning everyone not on it. | Fixed on a single inevitable outcome, frequently their own, standing motionless and watching it arrive. |
| **Vantage** | The senses lunge at the threat's location and lock. The Seer describes aloud, involuntarily, everything they can now see of it. | Consciousness flees the body for the last safe place they stood, leaving the body upright and vacant. | Full remote-viewing lock. The body is catatonic until contact is broken. |
