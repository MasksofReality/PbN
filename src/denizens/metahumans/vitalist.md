## Who They Are

Vitalists work on bodies: their own, someone else's, and the shape of both. Every level of healing you hand out lands on your own health track.

**Attribute priority:** Physical primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Social tertiary (3)
**Expressions:** Self (Bio-Control) · Other (Psychic Healing) · Flesh (Somatic Shaping)

## Self (Bio-Control)

**●○○○○ Pain Regulation.** Free. Permanently ignore 1 point of wound penalties at all times. Passive.

**●●○○○ Enhanced Physiology.** 1 Essence. Hold your breath for 20 minutes, resist environmental temperature extremes, and neutralize mundane toxins.

**●●●○○ Body Mastery.** 2 Essence. Gain +2 dice on all mundane Physical rolls or +2 Stamina soak for one scene.

**●●●●○ Override.** 3 Essence. Ignore all wound penalties for a scene, eliminate the need for sleep for 48 hours, or purge supernatural pathogens.

**●●●●● Transcendence.** 5 Essence. For one scene, gain +2 to all Physical Attributes, complete immunity to wound penalties, and immunity to bashing knockout. You take 3 bashing damage when the scene ends.

## Other (Psychic Healing)

**●○○○○ Soothing Touch.** Free. Touch a target to suppress 1 die of wound penalties for the scene and reduce Frenzy, Rage, or Torment risk by 1.

**●●○○○ Knit.** 1 Essence. Heal 1 level of bashing damage per net success on touch. You take 1 level of bashing damage per level healed.

**●●●○○ Close the Wound.** 2 Essence. Over one minute of contact, heal 1 level of lethal damage per 2 net successes. You take 1 level of lethal damage per level healed.

**●●●●○ Emergency Care.** 3 Essence. Over ten minutes, heal up to 3 lethal or 5 bashing levels. You absorb half the healed amount, rounded up, onto your own health track.

**●●●●● Miraculous Healing.** 5 Essence. Over one hour of deep trance, heal all non-aggravated damage on a target. You absorb all lethal damage directly onto your own health track.

## Flesh (Somatic Shaping)

**●○○○○ Erase the Mark.** Free. Over a minute of concentration, alter your own minor identifying features: fingerprints, scars, tattoos, eye color, hair color and length, vocal pitch. Changes persist until you change them again.

**●●○○○ Shape the Self.** 1 Essence. Over ten minutes, substantially rebuild your own appearance: facial structure, build, apparent age, apparent sex, height within a few inches. If you have studied a specific person closely you may take their likeness, gaining +3 dice on mundane Subterfuge or Performance rolls to hold the physical impersonation. Persists until changed.

**●●●○○ Shape Another.** 2 Essence. Twenty minutes of contact performs any of the above on another subject. A willing subject is uncontested. An unwilling one contests on permanent Willpower + highest Art, and forced shaping inflicts 1 level of bashing damage + 1 per net success from tissue trauma, unsoaked, delivered by touch and Undodgeable.

**●●●●○ Adaptive Form.** 3 Essence. For one scene, restructure yourself or a touched subject for an environment: sealed respiration for smoke, gas, or water for hours; tolerance of pressure or cold extremes; dark-adapted eyes that ignore all darkness penalties; or a climbing form granting +3 dice on mundane Athletics rolls to climb. One adaptation per activation. Grants no soak.

**●●●●● Rewrite.** 5 Essence. One hour of deep trance permanently rebuilds a body: a wholly new face, frame, voice, and biometric set that survives physical Vigiles inspection while still failing any resonance scan, correction of congenital conditions and chronic disease, and restoration of lost function over subsequent weeks of natural growth. You take 3 bashing damage on completion. It cannot alter Wyrd, Psi, Inheritance, or resonance, it cannot make a Metahuman read as mundane, and it cannot defeat the Registry's genealogical archives. A rebuilt body with a documented bloodline is still a documented bloodline.

## Important Limits

**Healing limitation.** Vitalist Arts cannot heal aggravated damage on any target.

**No healing from Flesh.** *Flesh* restores no health levels of any type and cannot stand in for *Other*. It shapes living tissue. It does not mend injury.

**Debt exception.** Because it heals nothing, *Flesh* never triggers the Healer's Debt. Nothing transfers to your health track.

**Somatic tax.** Shaping another body is still work done with your own. *Flesh* ●●● and ●●●● inflict 1 bashing damage on you per subject shaped, and *Rewrite* inflicts 3. This damage heals at your normal doubled bashing rate.

## Inheritance Strength: Accelerated Recovery

You heal mundane bashing damage at twice the normal rate, 1 level per 30 minutes, without spending Essence.

## Inheritance Weakness: The Healer's Debt

Healing someone else transfers their physical injury onto your own health track, where it heals at standard human rates.

## Backlash: The Kind Flesh, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self** | *Transcendence* fires and the Vitalist attacks with an enhanced body and no awareness of injury. They have to be physically stopped. | The body is driven past its limits to flee, sprinting through wounds, ignoring pain and judgment alike. | Full override lock. Catatonic, wounds ignored, extremely difficult to kill and impossible to move. |
| **Other** | The Art seizes the nearest injured body and heals it violently, dumping the whole injury onto the Vitalist at once. If the wounded party is the threat, it heals the threat. | Heals whoever is blocking the exit and absorbs their injuries to buy passage. | Grips the nearest wounded body and will not let go, absorbing everything until the scene ends. This can end in clinical death. |
| **Flesh** | The nearest body warps against its owner's will. Joints invert, features slide, tissue rebels. | The Vitalist's own body contorts to pour through any gap it can reach. | The body locks into a hardened, unresponsive form until the scene ends. |
