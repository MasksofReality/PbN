## Who They Are

Wildspeakers talk to animals, to terrain, and to green growing things. Most of that is worth little inside the Basilica walls and a great deal outside them.

**Attribute priority:** Physical primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Social tertiary (3)
**Expressions:** Beast (Animal Communion) · Ground (Survival Attunement) · Green (Verdant Communion)

## Beast (Animal Communion)

**●○○○○ Animal Speech.** Free. Communicate telepathically with any mundane animal within sensory range. What you learn is limited by the creature's intellect.

**●●○○○ Calm the Beast.** 1 Essence. Soothe an agitated or hostile animal of any size, removing its aggression for one scene.

**●●●○○ Command.** 2 Essence. Issue a complex directive to an animal, such as scout, track, defend, or retrieve. The creature carries it out faithfully.

**●●●●○ Familiar Bond.** 3 Essence. Form a permanent psychic bond with one animal. You share sensory awareness and emotional state at any distance. One familiar at a time.

**●●●●● Apex Command.** 5 Essence. Command any beast regardless of temperament. Works on beast-form aberrants at +2 difficulty. It cannot touch spirits or paracausal entities wearing animal shapes.

## Ground (Survival Attunement)

**●○○○○ Survival Sense.** Free. Passive awareness of natural hazards, toxic terrain, weather shifts, and stalking predators. Grants +2 dice on mundane Survival rolls.

**●●○○○ Wayfinding.** 1 Essence. Navigate trackless terrain supernaturally well, locate fresh water and shelter, and bypass wilderness hazards.

**●●●○○ Borrowed Eyes.** 2 Essence. Project your senses through any animal you have spoken with, for up to an hour. Your body stays catatonic.

**●●●●○ Pack Speech.** 3 Essence. Broadcast thoughts and commands to every animal of your Familiar's species across a wide radius at once.

**●●●●● Wearing the Skin.** 5 Essence. Project your consciousness fully into your Familiar for up to 24 hours, controlling its body while your own lies comatose.

## Green (Verdant Communion)

**●○○○○ Green Sense.** Free. By touch or proximity, know a plant's health and age, and what has passed through, fed on, or been buried near it within the last season. Edible, medicinal, and toxic flora are identified automatically.

**●●○○○ Quickening.** 1 Essence. Accelerate or arrest growth across a small area: sprout and ripen a plot, wither a hedge, coax roots to grip a foundation, lace a doorway with vine, cover a trail in moss. Minutes, not hours.

**●●●○○ Thornhold.** 2 Essence. Existing vegetation in the scene goes active: difficult terrain, full concealment, or entanglement (contested against Strength or Dexterity + Athletics; an entangled target loses its action). It needs flora already present. Bare stone or a paved interior gives you nothing.

**●●●●○ The Long Root.** 3 Essence. Sink your senses into a contiguous root or fungal network and perceive everything in contact with it across a wide radius for up to an hour, including traces hours old. Your body stays still and vulnerable.

**●●●●● Rise, Wildwood.** 5 Essence. Once per session. Scene-scale reclamation: pavement ruptures, a street walls off, a structure drowns in growth over minutes rather than instantly. Anyone caught in the surge takes 1 level of lethal damage + 1 per net success, unsoaked, Dodgeable by breaking for open ground (Dexterity + Athletics, difficulty 6). It produces an unmissable Pax breach and Exposure event.

## Inheritance Strength: Wilderness Mastery

You cannot become lost, you cannot be surprised by natural fauna, and there is no better scout in the wastes.

## Inheritance Weakness: Primitive Logic

Take −1 die on mundane rolls that turn on complex political maneuvering or layered social deception. Your Backlash also works as a telepathic distress call to predatory wildlife.

## Backlash: The Old Tongue, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beast** | *Apex Command* at maximum. Every animal in range converges on the threat, compounding with the telepathic distress call from the Inheritance Weakness. | Animals swarm to obstruct pursuit and shepherd the Wildspeaker out. | The mind flees into the nearest animal and wears it. The body drops comatose. |
| **Ground** | Every hazard the Wildspeaker can sense activates at once, and they drive the threat into it with uncanny precision. | Perfect wayfinding. They vanish into the terrain along the ideal route and do not stop, sometimes for a day. | They take on the stillness of the ground itself. Undetectable, immobile, and missing for hours. |
| **Green** | Vegetation in the scene attacks the perceived threat. | Growth walls off pursuit and covers the retreat. | The Wildspeaker roots in place, and growth encases and protects the body. |
