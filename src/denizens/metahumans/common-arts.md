These two Arts turn up across all six Inheritances. You cannot start with either one unless you spend freebie points on it.

<h2 id="wyrdsense">Wyrdsense</h2>

*Attunement to the paracausal and to reality distortions.*

**●○○○○ Taste the Air.** Free. Detect active supernatural phenomena within sensory range and identify the broad category: an Art in use, a paratech leystone, a ward, an aberrant, an anomaly.

**●●○○○ Read the Working.** 1 Essence. Discern the precise nature, relative age, and strength of an active supernatural effect or paratech enchantment.

**●●●○○ Storm Sense.** 2 Essence. Detect an approaching wyrdstorm hours before mechanical sensors register it, and evaluate the structural integrity of a Hearthstone barrier by touch.

**●●●●○ Mark the Seam.** 3 Essence. Perceive dimensional thin spots, spirit crossings, waygate conduits, and the edges of the Governor's Toll. Grants +3 dice to track or navigate spatial anomalies.

**●●●●● The Shape of It.** 5 Essence. For one scene, perceive the entire paracausal architecture of an area: every active ward, spirit, leyline, and flaw in it. Triggers a mandatory Stability roll for witnessing eldritch reality.

<h2 id="aegis">Aegis</h2>

*Psychic shielding and mental fortitude.*

**●○○○○ Shutters.** Free. A passive mental barrier. Inflicts +1 difficulty on all hostile Arts or mental intrusions aimed at your mind.

**●●○○○ Hold.** 1 Essence. Spend before rolling to reduce the difficulty of an incoming Backlash or Attrition roll by 1.

**●●●○○ Blank.** 2 Essence. For one scene, suppress your paracausal resonance completely. You register as an ordinary baseline mortal to all passive supernatural perception, including Auspex, Mana Sight, and Chi Sight. This is the only thing in the setting that masks the Paracausal Resonance weakness every Metahuman carries.

**●●●●○ Cage.** 3 Essence. Project a protective psychic sphere over a room-sized area. Every ally inside gains +2 difficulty against mental intrusion, fear, or emotional manipulation.

**●●●●● Sever.** 5 Essence. Instantly terminate one active mental domination, psychic link, or compulsion on any target, contested against the original activation's successes. Alternatively, spend it once per scene to abort a Backlash in progress, on yourself or on any Metahuman within reach.

<div class="callout">
<strong>Aegis and Backlash.</strong> Where Aegis ●●●●● is your highest-rated Art, its own abort clause can cancel the Backlash that summoned it, resolving as no external effect. You still spend the Essence, and the visible distortion still counts as a manifestation for registration purposes. See <a href="/denizens/metahumans/psi-and-backlash/">Psi, Essence & Backlash</a> for the full mechanics.
</div>
