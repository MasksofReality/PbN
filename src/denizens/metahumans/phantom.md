## Who They Are

Phantoms take themselves out of the picture, take other people out of the picture, and feed.

**Attribute priority:** Social primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Physical tertiary (3)
**Expressions:** Unseen (Psychic Invisibility) · Hunger (Psychic Vampirism) · Oblivion (Imposed Absence)

**Predation rules:** all Hunger Arts run under the standard feeding rules. Points drained transfer 1:1 into Essence. Victims take 1 lethal damage per 2 points drained across a 24-hour cycle. Repeated feeding on Nightfolk generates Essence Tether.

## Unseen (Psychic Invisibility)

**●○○○○ Overlooked.** Free. Passive mental misdirection. Witnesses take +1 difficulty to notice you and +2 difficulty to recall identifying details.

**●●○○○ Social Shadow.** 1 Essence. Active misdirection for a scene. Observers must score net successes on Perception + Alertness (difficulty 6) to perceive you at all.

**●●●○○ Unpersoned.** 2 Essence. Total erasure from general awareness and from mundane and paratech surveillance cameras for one scene.

**●●●●○ Crowd Fade.** 3 Essence. Complete invisibility to every observer in a room-sized area for up to an hour. It degrades if you interact directly with a target.

**●●●●● True Absence.** 5 Essence. Complete cloaking against all passive supernatural senses for one hour. Active supernatural searches roll at difficulty 9.

## Hunger (Psychic Vampirism)

**●○○○○ Ambient Feed.** Free. Harvest emotional exhaust from casual social crowds. Siphon 1 point (a safe sip, 0 damage) from up to (Psi rating) mortals per scene.

**●●○○○ Focused Drain.** 1 Essence. Establish direct eye contact and siphon 2 points, dealing 1 lethal damage to the victim.

**●●●○○ Deep Drain.** 2 Essence. Siphon up to 4 points (2 lethal). Contested against Willpower + Art. The victim takes −2 dice on mundane Social rolls for 24 hours.

**●●●●○ Soul Leech.** 3 Essence. Siphon up to 6 points (3 lethal). Contested. The victim takes −2 dice on all mundane rolls for 1 week, and you heal 2 bashing wounds.

**●●●●● Hollowing.** 5 Essence. Drain a victim past empty, inflicting lethal damage and permanently reducing their permanent Willpower rating by 1. Automatic Stability trigger, and a capital crime under the Pax Nocturnorum.

## Oblivion (Imposed Absence)

**●○○○○ Overlook It.** Free. At will, one small object or single detail escapes casual notice: a scar, a satchel, a shape under a tarp. Witnesses take +1 difficulty to notice it and +2 difficulty to recall it.

**●●○○○ Hush.** 1 Essence. Extend active misdirection to one other person or object for a scene, working as Social Shadow. The subject must stay within your line of sight.

**●●●○○ Cut the Thread.** 2 Essence. Contested against permanent Willpower + highest Art. Remove roughly the last five minutes of the target's memory of you specifically. They keep the events, but not your presence, your face, or your words. Unaware or unresisting mortals are uncontested.

**●●●●○ Scrub the Scene.** 3 Essence. Room-sized. For one scene, every mundane witness fails to register a chosen event, person, or object, and any paratech surveillance that captured it degrades to noise on playback. Supernatural witnesses contest individually.

**●●●●● True Erasure.** 5 Essence. Contested. For one scene a single target is erased to everyone but you. As far as the world is concerned they never existed, and they are utterly imperceptible. Automatic Stability trigger. Making the condition permanent is a story-level action requiring downtime and staff approval, and it is prosecuted as a capital crime under the Pax Nocturnorum, alongside Hollowing.

## Phantom Replenishment Exception

Deep rest restores Essence only up to half your maximum pool. The rest has to be taken through Hunger feeding.

## Inheritance Strength: Undetectable Infiltration

You bypass security, paratech surveillance, and social scrutiny without leaving physical or magical residue behind you.

## Inheritance Weakness: Predatory Leash

Draining Nightfolk builds Utkinov Tether. At 3 Tether points toward one victim, Essence drawn from every other target yields half value, and all actions against that victim take +2 difficulty.

## Backlash: The Hollow, Out of Control

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unseen** | The Phantom goes wholly absent and the scene becomes a hunt they are not consciously running. The threat perceives an empty room and their own injuries appearing from nowhere. | Instant total absence. The Phantom exits, cannot be tracked, and frequently does not remember leaving. | Erasure so complete it takes their own self-perception. Unresponsive, and allies cannot locate or move them. |
| **Hunger** | A feeding surge on the nearest living being, friend or foe. At ●●●●● that means committing *Hollowing*, a capital crime, involuntarily. | Indiscriminate ambient drain from everyone present to fuel the exit. The crowd goes down behind them. | Locked and feeding on whoever touches them, unable to stop until contact is forcibly broken. |
| **Oblivion** | Erases the threat's allies, their weapons, and the floor under their feet from their own awareness. | Scrubs the scene wholesale. Every witness loses the encounter. | The Phantom erases themselves from themselves. Dissociative catatonia. |
