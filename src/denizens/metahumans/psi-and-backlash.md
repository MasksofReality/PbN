## Psi, Essence, and Running Dry

Your powers are Psychic Arts, and they run on the Resource Trait **Psi**. Psi measures how strong the gift is. Essence is the fuel you spend using it, and it runs out.

**Essence Pool:** equals Psi + 5, so 7 at creation, 10 at maximum.

**Ambient Recovery:** you regain 5 Essence and refresh current Willpower at midnight server time, provided you had the chance for quiet rest.

**Replenishment:** Deep Rest means sleep, meditative trance, or sensory quiet. It restores 1 Essence per two post rounds of active, uninterrupted roleplay somewhere secure. Phantoms are the exception and follow the Feeding rules instead — see the [Phantom](/denizens/metahumans/phantom/) page.

**Pushing:** permitted. Spend 1 Willpower and take 1 aggravated damage to fire an Art in place of its Essence cost, or to buy +1 die on an Art roll, one for one.

**Attrition (0 Essence):** at zero you enter Attrition and cannot activate any costed Art. At the end of each scene you spend there, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6. Fail, and you lose the reins: your abilities Backlash continuously for the rest of the scene, firing at whatever you perceive as a threat and paying for it through mandatory Pushing. Botch, and it holds until the next midnight reset.

## Backlash

Your ability is wired into your nervous system, which means it answers to your nervous system. Frighten you badly enough or hurt you badly enough and it fires, whether or not you wanted it to. Read this section before your first scene.

### The Dual-Axis Trigger Check

| Trigger Type | Roll | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Panic / Fear / Horror | Grit | 6 |
| Trauma / Pain / Attrition | Permanent Willpower | 6 |

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Success** | You hold. Nothing activates. |
| **Failure** | Your highest Art fires on its own. Instinct picks the target. Cost comes out of your Essence Pool, or out of you via Pushing if the pool is short. |

### When to Roll

Roll for Backlash when your character:

- Takes damage that marks a health level while surprised or unprepared.
- Is cornered by a lethal threat, or targeted by a supernatural display of horror.
- Suffers acute psychological terror, extreme pain, or sudden traumatic grief.
- Is in Attrition (0 Essence) or at Unmoored Stability (2–3) at the end of a violent or stressful scene.

### The Roll

- **Fear, horror, or panic triggers:** roll Grit, difficulty 6.
- **Physical trauma, Attrition, or pain triggers:** roll permanent Willpower, difficulty 6.

Modifiers: +1 difficulty for every Backlash roll you have already made this scene. +1 difficulty at Fraying Stability (6–7) or lower — the standard passive modifier, which does not stack with itself.

### Outcomes

- **Success:** you keep hold of it. Nothing activates.
- **Failure:** the gift goes off. Your highest-rated Art fires at its maximum rating, shaped by your chosen Instinct, aimed at whatever your subconscious has decided is the threat. You do not get to modulate scale, target, or rating. Essence comes off immediately, and if the pool cannot cover it you pay through mandatory Pushing: 1 Willpower and 1 aggravated damage per activation.
- **Botch:** as a failure, but it manifests in the worst way available — allies, structural supports, bystanders. Then roll on the Backfire Table (d10).

### Instincts (Permanent, Chosen at Creation)

- **Fight:** the Art turns outward at full intensity against the threat, and it does not account for bystanders or collateral damage.
- **Flight:** the Art works to get you out — barriers, kinetic propulsion, blinding effects, obscurement. Anyone standing between you and the exit is treated as an obstacle.
- **Freeze:** the Art turns inward. You go catatonic and locked in place while protective, bio-control, or personal sensory Arts run on your own body, until the scene ends or somebody forcibly breaks contact.

### What Backlash Looks Like

Backlash fires your highest-rated Art at maximum rating through your Instinct, with no modulation of scale, target, or rating. That means any Art available to a Metahuman can be the one that goes off, including the ones that look harmless written down. See your [Inheritance's page](/denizens/metahumans/) for its own Fight/Flight/Freeze Backlash table.

**Resolving ties:** when two or more Arts share the highest rating, work down this list: the Art most directly responsive to the trigger (Trauma favors physical and somatic Arts, Fear favors perceptual and mental ones); then the Art you have already used this scene; then an Inheritance Expression over a General Art; then the Storyteller's call.

**Harmless is not free.** A Backlash that fires something quiet and undramatic hurts nobody, and it is still a public involuntary manifestation. That is an Exposure event if you are Unregistered, an Incident if you are Registered, and a fraud risk if you are Misregistered.

**Freeze is not safe.** A catatonic Metahuman in a violent scene is a body on the floor. Freeze ends when the scene ends or when somebody forcibly breaks contact, and nothing in between is under your control.

**Costs:** Essence comes off immediately. Any shortfall is paid by mandatory Pushing, at 1 Willpower and 1 aggravated damage per activation.

## The Common Arts, In Backlash

| Expression | Fight | Flight | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wyrdsense** | Their senses tear open and broadcast. Everyone present perceives the full paracausal architecture. Mandatory Stability rolls for all witnesses, masked resonances revealed, other Nightfolk in the room outed. | They perceive every seam and thin spot at once and bolt for the nearest, whether that is a waygate, a spirit crossing, or a wyrdstorm, with the danger disregarded. | Total perceptual immersion. Catatonic, eyes open, seeing all of it. Stability roll on emergence. |
| **Aegis*** | *Sever* fires indiscriminately. Every active mental effect, psychic link, compulsion, and bond within reach is cut, including a Familiar Bond, an ally's telepathy, or a blood bond keeping someone alive. | *Blank* and *Cage* together. The Metahuman goes resonance-silent and psychically sealed, and supernatural senses lose them completely. | The mind slams shut. Catatonic, immune to all mental contact, unreachable by telepathy or *Soothing Touch*. The scene has to be waited out. |
| **Cascade** | Every Art the Metahuman holds fires simultaneously at maximum rating. Gain 1 Eldritch. | The same, shaped toward escape. They leave through a cratered path of their own making. Gain 1 Eldritch. | Total conduit lock. All Arts fire inward and the Metahuman becomes a standing paracausal event. Immediate Attrition, Stability roll at difficulty 8, gain 1 Eldritch. |

*A note on Aegis, which works as intended: where Aegis ●●●●● is your highest-rated Art, its own abort clause can cancel the Backlash that summoned it, resolving as no external effect. You still spend the Essence, and the visible distortion still counts as a manifestation for registration purposes. Aegis-heavy characters really are safer to stand next to. They paid four Art slots and a lot of XP for that, and they should feel it.
