*The open conduit.*

Every Metahuman carries Neurological Volatility, the wiring that puts your power inside your stress response and lets it fire without permission. Cascade is what happened in the handful of bloodlines where somebody stopped treating that as a defect.

It is not control in the way most people mean the word. The conduit does not narrow. It widens, on purpose, and you learn to stand in the current and aim what you can.

## Getting Access

Cascade cannot be taken at creation and cannot be bought with freebie points. Picking it up in play requires all four of the following:

1. One Inheritance Expression at ●●● or higher.
2. At least one Backlash **botch** survived in play, meaning a rolled result on the Backfire Table. You do not learn to ride the surge without having been thrown by it first.
3. Staff approval of a **bloodline strain**. Cascade runs in families, which means the Registry's genealogical archives can predict it. A Registered character found to carry the strain should expect handler attention whether or not they have ever used it.
4. An open Art slot. Cascade counts against your four-Art cap like any other track.

## The Ladder

**●○○○○ Take the Reins.** Free, +1 Willpower. Reflexive, when Backlash triggers. You pick the target instead of letting Instinct pick it. Scale, rating, and Essence cost stay entirely out of your hands.

**●●○○○ Bleed the Surge.** 1 Essence. Reflexive, on a failed Backlash roll. Convert the involuntary firing into a controlled activation of that same Art at any rating up to your own, paying that rung's normal cost rather than the maximum-rating cost. It does nothing on a botch.

**●●●○○ Deliberate Cascade.** 2 Essence, gain 1 Eldritch. Trigger your own Backlash as a declared action. Your highest-rated Art fires at maximum rating with +2 dice, fully targeted and modulated. If anyone sees you do it, that is a willful Pax violation.

**●●●●○ Wider Conduit.** 3 Essence, +1 Willpower and 1 aggravated damage (mandatory Pushing), gain 1 Eldritch. For a single activation, run one Art you hold at one dot above your rating, to a maximum effective rating of 5. You get the higher rung's effect and pay the higher rung's Essence cost on top of everything above.

**●●●●● Second Manifestation.** 5 Essence, gain 1 Eldritch. Once per story arc, staff confirmation required. For one scene, every Art you hold operates at maximum rating and every activation cost is waived. When the scene ends, all of the following happen: immediate Attrition (Essence to 0 regardless of your pool), 3 aggravated damage, a mandatory Stability roll at difficulty 8, and a roll on the Backfire Table (d10).

## Things to Know Before You Take It

**Aegis still works.** Aegis ●●●●● (Sever) aborts a Deliberate Cascade or a Second Manifestation exactly the way it aborts any other Backlash. That is intentional. It is the only reliable brake another character has on you, which makes an Aegis-holding ally the single most valuable person in a Cascade practitioner's life.

**Eldritch is permanent.** Eldritch gained through Cascade does not clear with the midnight reset, with clean play years, or with Stability recovery. This is the price of the track and the whole reason it is gated.

**No stacking.** Wider Conduit and Second Manifestation do not combine. Nothing raises an Art above effective rating 5.

**It is not a cure.** Cascade gives you no protection from involuntary Backlash, does not lower Backlash difficulty, and does not stack with Aegis ●● (Hold) beyond that Art's normal −1.
