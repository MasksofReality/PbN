## What You Are

You died, went to the Hells, escaped, and reanimated your rotting corpse. You remember little of what you endured in the hells; your mind locked it away in a haze of disassociative amnesia to keep you sane. It still sometimes haunts your daysleep as horrific visions you don't truly remember when you awaken.

Your soul returned fractured into four aspects, bound only by an unpaid debt to a Demon King whose ledger still holds your name. To survive, you must drain vitality from the living. Over the centuries, your kind built Fellowships, families, and philosophies around their predicament and the best way to overcome it — or if it should be overcome at all.

You are neither Lilum nor Shade. Unlike Lilum, your origin is your own death, your diet is broader, your feeding inflicts conscious agony, and walking into spirit realms is effortless. Unlike Shades — who reached the Underworld rivers and chose to stay — your soul was dragged off its natural course into the hells. You spend eternity seeking the passage they rejected.

To cut the chain holding you from the underworld's Rivers of Eternity, every Hungry Dead walks a spiritual path: a **Dharma**. Stray from it, and you risk tumbling back into the Hells, with no second chance at escape.

## How You Got There

Most of the dead go the way the dead are supposed to go. Their souls come loose, are drawn to the headwaters of one of the Rivers of Eternity, and travel down it into the Shadowlands and on toward whatever waits past its end. You did not, and there are only three roads that end where yours did. Every Hungry Dead in the world walked one of them, and the first question asked of a newcomer is which they came down.

**You made a bargain.** An agent of the Hells offered you power; you took it, and the damnation it came with. It does not matter what you wanted it for. A cure, a child saved, a city fed one more winter. The nobility of the purpose has never once altered the outcome. The pact taints your soul at the moment of signing, and when you die they come to collect. Hungry Dead who were dragged into the hells this way are the ones who will tell you, at length, that they would do it again. Some of them are even telling the truth.

**You inherited it.** Someone in your line sold you before you were ever born. A Demon King's ledger accepts futures, and an ancestor vile enough to trade off some or all of their descendants gets exactly the power they asked for, though most discover too late doing so does not mean they escaped hell's chains. Children sired by a Hungry Dead go into the same column, which is why the Fellowships have opinions about that. A soul tainted this way may have been blameless. It may have even been a true saint in life. It did not matter and it was never going to. You were doomed from birth by a generations-old pact, and the Hells always collect.

**You earned it.** You lived a life depraved enough that one of Hell's collectors noticed you and on its own initiative came to mark your soul. This road is the rarest of the three — it is reserved for serial killers and their ilk — and the one most often claimed falsely, by people who would rather be feared than pitied, and most often denied by the ones it actually applies to.

Some Hungry Dead want to finally pass on cleanly, others to simply be free of the threat of falling back into a place they may never escape if they wind up there again. All agree the only way forward is to liberate themselves.

## Where You Stand in the Third Era

You register as a Benign Reality Deviant like everyone else, and the Pax Nocturnorum covers you exactly as far as it covers a Lilum or a Fae, which is to say completely right up until an Order of Vigiles investigator confirms an accusation. Then a writ goes to the Aberrant Hunters and the protection evaporates. Your feeding leaves witnesses who stay conscious and stay angry, so you are, statistically, the easiest Nightfolk in the League to build a case against.

## The Hellbound Fellowships

Six paths are open at character creation. The Hellbound Fellowships are bound together by their belief in a Dharmic path and a belief that working together lessens the weight of one's footsteps along the way to liberation. Each Fellowship sets your Attribute priority, your Affinity Art, and the soul your path is keyed to.

| Fellowship | Objective | Keyed Soul | Affinity Art |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Bone Flowers](/denizens/hellion/bone-flowers/)** | Tend the dead, keep the records, remember everything | Denial | Godbody of Bone |
| **[Cerulean Veils](/denizens/hellion/cerulean-veils/)** | Serve the spirits, defend the places, obstruct the developers | Vitality and Denial | The Spirit Mantle |
| **[Devil-Tigers](/denizens/hellion/devil-tigers/)** | Provide the corrective suffering the Hells failed to provide you | Wickedness | The Red Panoply |

| **[Resplendent Cranes](/denizens/hellion/resplendent-cranes/)** | Bring order to a corrupt world, by force where necessary | Righteousness | The Mandate |
| **[Thrashing Dragons](/denizens/hellion/thrashing-dragons/)** | Be more alive than the living, because you had to earn it | Vitality | Black Wind |
