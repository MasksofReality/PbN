## Who They Are

The environmental and biological systems branch. Druids sustain, protect and maintain. They are not dramatic. Every settlement outside a Basilica wall would like one, and most cannot get one.

**Attribute priority:** Social primary (7) · Physical secondary (5) · Mental tertiary (3)

**Strength.** They are essential. This is the only reliable healing in the setting that does not cost the healer blood, because a Vitalist absorbs what they heal and a Druid does not. Add wyrdstorm warning and weather control, and a Druid is the difference between a caravan arriving and a caravan being a story. Every faction wants one and none of them want to spend one.

**Weakness.** They are slow, and blamed. Component-hungry, ritual-slow, and close to useless in a fight, since the four-dot Working takes an hour. And a practitioner who can move the weather will be blamed for the weather, by farmers, by stormchasers, and eventually by somebody with a writ.

### Healer

**●○○○○** **Herbal Compound**: accelerates natural healing by 50% for 24 hours and grants +1 die on Stamina rolls for the same period.

**●●○○○** Diagnose precisely: what is wrong, how long it has been wrong, whether it was done on purpose, and whether it is contagious.

**●●●○○** Heal 1 lethal or 3 bashing on a willing target. What the Druid does not pay is blood: unlike a Vitalist, nothing is absorbed and nothing is taken into the healer.

**●●●●○** Heal 3 lethal, or bring a body back from Incapacitated to functional, or break a chronic condition over weeks of care. Aggravated is untouched.

### Purifier

**●○○○○** Neutralises one mundane poison, venom or contaminant in a body, or makes a barrel of foul water safe to drink. It does not heal the damage already done. Against anything supernatural it buys hours, not a cure.

**●●○○○** Read the contamination rather than the patient: what is in it, how much, how far it has spread, where it entered, and whether it is ordinary poison, wyrdstorm residue, or something still alive and still doing it.

**●●●○○** Draw a contamination out of one body, or one small site: a well, a spring-head, a cellar, a few paces of ground. It removes the cause, never the damage. What comes out has to go somewhere, and it leaves in a stoppered vessel you are now carrying.

**●●●●○** Clear a site outright — a field, a well-system, a caravanserai, a floor of a building — or break a poisoning, wasting or blight in a person that Restoration only slowed. Contested where the contamination is a being's deliberate work. Damage already dealt is untouched, aggravated included.

### Greenworker

**●○○○○** A fodder, feed or soil compound: an ailing beast eats, a sickening crop holds, a wound on an animal closes.

**●●○○○** Read a herd, a field, or a wood: what it needs, what is eating it, and whether the thing eating it is natural.

**●●●○○** Restore a herd or a stand of crops that was dying: one season's yield saved outright, which in this setting is a political act.

**●●●●○** Wake a wood, a hedge, or a herd to hostility or to guardianship for a day. Things with teeth and no opinions acquire an opinion about a named person.
