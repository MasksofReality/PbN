## Who They Are

The statistics branch. Augurs read the load on the system and, at the top of the tree, adjust it. They are rarely the most dramatic practitioners in a room and frequently the only one anybody actually needed.

**Attribute priority:** Mental primary (7) · Social secondary (5) · Physical tertiary (3)

**Strength.** Other Sorcerers hire them. An Augur's opinion moves money, caravans, and the agenda of the Council of Six. No other School produces a deliverable that powerful people want this badly and that fits in an envelope.

**Weakness.** They are believed, and being believed makes you responsible. One wrong Reading ends a career, and a right Reading somebody did not want ends something else.

<div class="callout">
<strong>Naming an inevitability</strong> — stating a Reading's outcome as fact to the person it will happen to — is a Stability trigger for Augurs. It counts once per scene like any other. Storytellers should award Augurs Eldritch at the fast end of the range. You cannot look at the structure of causality repeatedly and stay comfortable.
</div>

Choose a divination method at creation — cards, runes, bone-casting, ledgers of figures, tea, or a pendulum over a chart — and stay consistent with it. The method is not the Tradition. A card-reader can work in any of the three.

## The Path

| Rank | All Traditions |
|---|---|
| ●○○○○ | **Reading.** A general sense of the likeliest outcome of one specific event within 24 hours. *One action.* Free |
| ●●○○○ | **Probability Sight.** The actual weights of a situation: what is likely, what is not, and roughly why. *One minute.* 1 Mana |
| ●●●○○ | **Working of Weights.** A minor blessing (target's next significant roll at −1 difficulty) or a minor curse (+1 difficulty). Curses are Contested. *Five minutes.* 2 Mana |
| ●●●●○ | **Extended Reading.** Likeliest outcomes across the next month. 4+ net successes yields detailed, branching information. *One hour of reading.* 3 Mana |
| ●●●●● | **Fate Working.** A month-long alteration to a target's overall luck. Contested, and Nightfolk resist at +2 difficulty to the Augur. Resolved as a static threshold. *One hour or more; a significant material cost.* 5 Mana |

### Diviner

**●○○○○** Asked as a question and answered symbolically. Cheap, constant, and the bread and butter of the trade.

**●●○○○** Full spread. Names the two or three live branches and what distinguishes them.

**●●●○○** Worked on a person you have read: you must have a rank-1 Reading of them from this cycle.

**●●●●○** The League standard, and the deliverable that fits in an envelope. Auditable, citable, and admissible.

### Precognate

**●○○○○** Arrives unasked as a flash of the moment itself. You see the thing, not a symbol of it, and you cannot choose the subject.

**●●○○○** Names the branch that is *closest*, not the likeliest, which is a different and often more useful fact.

**●●●○○** Worked on a moment rather than a person: the next significant roll *in this scene*, whoever makes it, chosen as it happens.

**●●●●○** Not a report but a residency: you live the month's worst hour in advance, in detail, and know it when it arrives. Mark *witnessed something that should not exist*.

### Fortune-Teller

**●○○○○** Read as a weight rather than an event: whether this person is currently lucky, and roughly how much.

**●●○○○** Names who in the situation is carrying good or bad fortune, including whether it was put there on purpose.

**●●●○○** Worked freely and at will, and lasts until spent rather than expiring with the scene. Cursing a mortal is a Pax matter.

**●●●●○** Reads the month's *load* rather than its events: where fortune is already pooling, and who is standing in it.
