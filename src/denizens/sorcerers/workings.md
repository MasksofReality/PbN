## Mana, Essence and Attrition

Your trees are **Occult Arts**, an individual tree is a **Path**, and your Resource Trait is **Mana**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Essence Pool | Mana + 5. Rises automatically as your best Path rises |
| Ambient recovery | 5 Essence at midnight server time, assuming you had the ordinary opportunity to think. A Sorcerer denied quiet, denied light to read by, kept in pain, or kept in a room full of screaming gets nothing. Interrogation starves a Sorcerer specifically |
| Replenishment | Meditation on the fundamental theorems of reality as you understand them, as likely a lotus position as a chalkboard. One Essence per two post rounds, roleplayed and visible. It looks either extremely holy or extremely tedious depending on the practitioner |
| Pushing | Permitted. One Willpower and one aggravated in place of the base Essence cost, or per additional die. For a Sorcerer this is the difference between having a five-dot Path and being able to use it in an emergency |
| Attrition | At 0 Mana you cannot activate any costed Path. At the end of each scene you remain there, roll Willpower at difficulty 6. On a failure you turn sociopathic and obsessive for the rest of the scene, pursuing the secrets of the Works without regard for cost or casualty. A Sorcerer in Attrition does not attack the room; they start an experiment in it |

You claim no exception to the ambient-recovery presumption beyond what is written above, and you hold no second pool of any kind. Mana is the only pool on your sheet.

## Workings

A Sorcerer effect is a **Working**: notation, components, time and Mana. Nothing you do is fast or private by default, and everything about how this splat plays follows from that.

### The Standard Working

| Path | Mana | Ritual Time | Components |
|---|---|---|---|
| ●○○○○ | Free | One action | Chalk, ink, and a surface |
| ●●○○○ | 1 | One minute | Prepared reagents |
| ●●●○○ | 2 | Five minutes | Reagents and leystone dust |
| ●●●●○ | 3 | Fifteen minutes | Etched green leystone |
| ●●●●● | 5 | One hour or more | A significant material cost specific to the School: refined leystone, heartglass, or worse |

Where a Path entry gives its own ritual time, that entry overrides the table. The overrides are intentional. A brewed tincture is slower than chalk on a floor and a bolt of fire is faster than either, so the entries say so.

Your dice pool is **Mana + Path rating at difficulty 6**, unless the entry or the circumstance says otherwise. Contested effects are opposed by the target's permanent Willpower + highest Art, or by Grit + highest Art where the effect is fear, horror, or a compulsion to flee or yield. Mortals defend on permanent Willpower alone, or on Grit alone against the same. **Damaging Workings are not contested.**

Mundane craft is still mundane. The Path produces the effect; ordinary Attribute + Ability rolls still cover the work wrapped around it. An Artificer rolls Mana + Path to enchant a blade and Intelligence + Crafts to have made a blade worth enchanting.

### Prepared Workings

You may inscribe a Working in advance, on a scroll, a plate, a chalked floor or a tattoo, paying the Mana and the ritual time at inscription and triggering it later as a single action.

**The dice are rolled at the trigger, not at the inscription.** Inscribing stores the Mana and the ritual time; the roll, and any contest, resolves at the moment of use. An inscription is raw potential rather than finished product, so another Sorcerer reading one can identify its effect and its Path rating but never its quality, because its quality does not exist yet.

Anyone holding at least one Art rating and Eldritch 1 can trigger a prepared Working. The trigger roll is the standard shape of any Art roll, the Sorcerer's Resource Trait + highest Art, **capped at twice the Working's own Path rating**. A rank-3 inscription is never fired on more than six dice, by anyone, ever. A foreign hand cannot fire a Working better than its author could have, because the author's price bought the author's ceiling.

Effect, rating and parameters stay the inscriber's. Only the dice belong to the hand that fires it. The cost was paid at inscription, so triggering costs nothing, and a botch rolls Backfire on the triggerer.

Triggering is not reading. The notation stays unreadable without Mana Sight. A qualified outsider completes the circuit by touch and will rather than by comprehension, and whether they know what they are holding is a separate question that Intelligence + Eldritch answers.

Your cap counts every inscription still in existence. The most prepared Workings you can have out at once equals the Path's rating, wherever they are and whoever holds them. Handing one off does not free the slot. Only use or degradation does.

On paper or skin, a prepared Working degrades in 24 hours. A tattooed Working is skin, and degrades like skin: inked the morning it will be needed, gone by the next. Etched into green leystone it holds indefinitely until used, because green leystone is the only known substance that will hold multiple active enchantments at once.

Prepared Workings are physical objects. They can be stolen, ruined, read by another Sorcerer, found during a search, and fired by anyone qualified.

### Openness, Closure, and What Paratech Is

An inscription is **open**. It is aimed at the trigger, contested at the trigger, rolled at the trigger, and its quality stays undetermined until a qualified will resolves it. That openness is why Eldritch 1 is required: the triggerer supplies the judgment the working was inscribed without, and only a mind that has seen beneath the world can hold a working's shape while choosing for it.

Paratech is the same potential **closed**. Parameters fixed, choices engineered out, the circuit completed in advance behind a mundane interface. Anyone can flip its switch because there is nothing left to decide, which is also why paratech never rolls.

Closure comes in two kinds, and only one of them is an industry.

**Consumed and single-purpose closure** needs no permanence and no high rank. A substance has no aim. A tincture does what it does to whoever drinks it, and a coat that shrugs off weather has no target to contest. Products like these are closed at any Path rating, work in a stranger's hands, and are the reason Artificers can sell things. They cannot be aimed, cannot be re-aimed, and cannot do anything their maker did not fix in advance.

**Interfaced closure** is a switch, a dial, a device that produces a chosen effect on a chosen target on demand. It requires permanence, and permanence is Artificer four- and five-dot territory, a project in its own right. It is never bolted onto a prepared Working. There is no one-shot scroll-gun for the unqualified, and 115 TE is the year somebody first substituted engineering for having-seen.

<div class="callout">
<strong>Who marks the trigger.</strong> Where your product does something to a person, the Stability trigger falls on <strong>the hand that administered or used it</strong>, not the hand that made it. The Artificer who brews the sedative marks nothing. Whoever poured it into a stranger's cup marks <em>used an Art on a victim who is helpless or unaware</em>, and mortals always count as helpless. That is the mechanical shape of the line above: work on materials and you are a tradesman, and it is your buyer who becomes the case file. Selling knowingly into an obvious crime is a Vigiles matter and a staff matter, but it is not this trigger.

### Rushed Workings

Casting a Working you already know with no notation, no components and no ritual time, on will and memory alone, as a single action.

**It costs +2 difficulty and double the Mana.** A five-dot Rushed Working therefore costs 10 Mana, which for most Sorcerers is the entire pool. Difficulty still caps at 10, and a Rushed Working that would exceed the cap is impossible rather than merely desperate, so it is not attempted and not paid for.

Rushing is not inventing. Altering an existing Working, or improvising one that does not exist, needs Eldritch equal to or above the Path rating involved, whatever speed you cast at. A Rushed Working is a known effect cast badly fast. A new effect is a different and rarer thing, and no amount of Mana buys the difference.

Pushing pays half. Pushing substitutes Willpower and aggravated damage for the base cost only, and the Rushing surcharge has to be paid in Mana. A drained Sorcerer can still Push a standard or prepared Working. They cannot Push their way into a free Rushed capstone.

A Rushed Working leaves no inscription. Prepared Workings need the full ritual, and there is no rushing the front-loading of time, because the front-loading is what the time is.

Botches resolve as Backfire, like any other Art.

### Backfire

Backfire governs every splat's botched activations, and yours works like everyone else's with one exception: a **Bleed** result vents through the Stigmata, which glows, ticks or smokes, visibly and publicly and memorably wrong.

## What You Can and Cannot Buy

- **Your School Path** is your **Affinity** Path. 20 XP to 5.
- **The Common Paths**, Glyphwork and Scrying, are **Aligned-track** for every Sorcerer. 20 XP to 5.
- **Another School's Path** is **Discordant**. 39 XP to 5, and the in-fiction route is a teacher willing to break their own traditions. It is expensive, slow and socially costly. A Sorcerer known to hold another School's Path is a Sorcerer other practitioners stop leaving alone in their workshop.
- **The Restricted Paths**, Chronomancy and Invocation, need a teacher and staff permission before you can learn them at all, are never available at creation, and advance on the **Nonaligned** track once learned (31 XP to 5).
- **Psi Arts** are barred. Not expensive. Barred.
- **Four trees, maximum.** Your slots almost always end up as your School Path, both Commons, and one Discordant or Restricted Path earned over a long campaign. That is the whole purchase space, and it is meant to be legible at a glance.

## The Eldritch Gate

There is one gate, and it governs invention. No Occult Art functions at all without Eldritch 1, and **altering an existing Working, or improvising one that does not exist, requires Eldritch equal to or above the Path rating involved.**

There is no separate advancement gate. A Sorcerer with Eldritch 1 can buy a Path to 5, cast everything on it, prepare it and rush it. What they can never do is write anything new above rating 1, and that is the whole difference between a superbly credentialed practitioner and the people who move the discipline forward.
