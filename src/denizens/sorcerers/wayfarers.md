## Who They Are

The transit-and-interface branch. Wayfarers move between places and between states of awareness, and they were on every team that built the waygates a decade ago, which everyone knows and which they are unbearable about. Their Fascination practice makes them the School best suited to intelligence work and to the managed influence Purgatory's politics rewards.

**Attribute priority:** Social primary (7) · Mental secondary (5) · Physical tertiary (3)

**Strength.** Logistics and access: speed, reach, doors that were not doors, and a practice that turns objects into informants. For intelligence work, smuggling and expedition support there is no better School, and the Promethean Institute cannot run an expedition without one.

**Weakness.** Fascination is a tripwire. It is the fastest route to a Pax violation in this book, because mortals always count as helpless before an Art, so every use on one is a Stability trigger and, if witnessed, a case file. Translation's limits mean the Isle is still a cage. And awakened objects talk, including to other people, including about you.

<div class="callout">
<strong>The Toll is not defeated by this Path.</strong> No rank, no Tradition and no number of successes pays the Governor's Toll or crosses the coastal warp of the Wandering Isle. Nor does any Conveyance effect cross a Hearthstone barrier or a Glyphwork Keystone. The waygates were institutional projects built by teams over years. A practitioner cannot reproduce one, and the Isle remains what it is.
</div>

### Conveyor

**●○○○○** Read as distance and obstruction.

**●●○○○** Passage rather than person: the door, gate or checkpoint simply processes you.

**●●●○○** Wakes a vehicle, a road-marker, a gate: things whose business is passage.

**●●●●○** **Translation**: move yourself, plus one passenger per net success beyond the first, to a marked terminus you have physically stood in, within roughly ten miles. Cannot enter sealed space.

### Fascinator

**●○○○○** Read as *people*: who is watching, and what they want.

**●●○○○** Delivered by presence and voice. Voice-delivered, for the purposes of anything that resists such things.

**●●●○○** Wakes what was *present at a conversation*: cups, chairs, the coat on the hook.

**●●●●○** **Suggestion**: install one course of action in a target for a day; they will pursue it and will believe it was theirs. Contested. Voice-delivered. Against a mortal, uncontested, a trigger, and squarely a case file.

### Spirit Wakener

**●○○○○** Read from the door itself, which remembers being opened.

**●●○○○** Vouched for by the building's own fittings, which is unnerving to watch and impossible to argue with.

**●●●○○** Wakes anything, and can put it back to sleep, which is the only version of this that is not a liability.

**●●●●○** **Compact**: bind an awakened object or minor spirit to a standing duty for a month, to watch this door, follow this person, report to me. This is a Binding and requires a writ.
