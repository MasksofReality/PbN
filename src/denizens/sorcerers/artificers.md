## Who They Are

The materials-and-manufacture branch, and the most consequential School in history. The Artificers of 115 TE invented paratech, which means they invented the modern world. Their output persists after the ritual is over, which makes them the wealthiest, the most employable, and the most closely watched by people with money.

**Attribute priority:** Mental primary (7) · Physical secondary (5) · Social tertiary (3)

**Strength.** Their work outlives the scene. Artificers are the only School whose output can be sold, inherited or shipped. An Artificer solves other people's problems permanently, which is the most reliable source of allies, income and legitimate cover in the game.

**Weakness.** Everything they make is evidence. Enchantment carries a maker's hand, and another Sorcerer can read it. Every object you have ever made is a signed statement in someone else's custody, and both the Sovereign Club and the Promethean Institute keep lists of who made what. Artificers are also the School most likely to be kept rather than killed.

<div class="callout">
<strong>A word on what Leycraft covers.</strong> Leycraft works on unaspected ambient aether, leystone, and installed workings. It never touches a person's pool. It cannot drain, tap or transfer Essence from any living or undead subject, at any rating, by any route. That keeps the feeding rules, the tally, the damage and the Utkinov Effect out of your business entirely, and it is why a Leycrafter can work a reactor undercroft without being a predator.
</div>

## The Path

| Rank | All Traditions |
|---|---|
| ●○○○○ | **Preparation.** One minor effect. *Thirty minutes, specific components.* Free |
| ●●○○○ | **Minor Work.** One minor property for one week. *One minute, prepared reagents.* 1 Mana |
| ●●●○○ | **Compound Work.** Potent effects. *One hour, rarer components.* 2 Mana |
| ●●●●○ | **Permanent Work.** A lasting property, indefinitely. *Fifteen minutes plus crafting time; requires etched green leystone.* 3 Mana |

### Alchemist

**●○○○○** A single-use tincture or coating: mild sedation, one scene of sensory enhancement, accelerated bashing recovery, a reagent that reveals recent glyphwork. Works in any hand.

**●●○○○** A batch of three doses of a rank-1 preparation, shelf-stable for the week.

**●●●○○** An elixir. It works in hands you did not choose, for anyone who gets hold of it.

**●●●●○** A regimen rather than a dose: continuous benefit for as long as it keeps being taken, and a real problem when it stops.

**●●●●●** Part of the Great Work — see above.

### Enchanter

**●○○○○** A charm or coating on a crafted object: one minor property for one scene.

**●●○○○** A knife that never dulls, a container that preserves indefinitely, a coat that shrugs off weather, a lamp that needs no leystone.

**●●●○○** A worn or carried piece, its property active a full day and re-primeable while the Working holds.

**●●●●○** A blade that deals lethal to Nightfolk, armour granting +1 soak, a lens that defeats mundane disguise, a lock only you can open.

**●●●●●** Part of the Great Work — see above.

### Leycrafter

**●○○○○** A tap: draws light, heat or motion off the local grid, metered by nothing and billed to nobody.

**●●○○○** A patch on failing paratech; a glyph line rerouted around a dead cell. Holds a week, then fails as it was going to.

**●●●○○** **Refit paratech beyond its rated tolerance**, the trick every stormchaser crew wants on payroll, and the reason a Leycrafter never buys their own drinks in Ulaanbaatar.

**●●●●○** An **installation**: a room, a vehicle, a workshop. Interfaced closure, so it has a switch, anyone can work it, and it never rolls. This entry is what paratech is.

**●●●●●** Part of the Great Work — see above.
