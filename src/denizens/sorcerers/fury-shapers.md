## Who They Are

The energy-release branch: what happens when you stop reading the Mechanism's waste heat and start letting it out on purpose. The most feared School, the most useful in a real emergency, and the one every Basilica's Vigiles keeps a current list of.

**Attribute priority:** Physical primary (7) · Social secondary (5) · Mental tertiary (3)

**Strength.** This is the School that ends fights. It is the only Path in this book that reliably resolves a physical crisis, and the one every aberrant-hunter guildhall in Nairobi will hire on sight without asking a second question. In the wilds, against an aberrant, nobody fears a Fury Shaper. They are the reason the caravan is alive.

**Weakness.** They are watched. Inside a wall it is a different profession. There is no version of a Cataclysm in a city that does not become a Vigiles investigation, Salamanca will not admit a Fury Shaper at all, and Purgatory's Council reads the residency list. Fury Shapers accumulate Stability loss faster than any other School, because the triggers are written almost exactly around what this Path does.

<div class="callout callout--rare">
<strong>Ritual timing on this Path is different, and it is the largest exception in the book.</strong> Ranks ●–●●● resolve in one action; ●●●● takes one minute; ●●●●● takes five minutes. Without this the School cannot function, since a five-minute bolt is not a bolt. It is a real dent in "nothing you do is fast."
</div>

**Damage type.** All Fury Shaper damage is **lethal against the living**, unsoaked, except where an entry produces an ordinary physical effect instead. Where the target is a spirit, corpus, or the animate dead, the damage is **aggravated**.

### Pyromancer

**●○○○○** Heat and flame in the palm: ignites, boils, welds, and burns a lock out of a door given a minute.

**●●○○○** A blade sheathed in flame. Ignites what it cuts.

**●●●○○** A bolt. Dodgeable. Sets flammables alight on any success.

**●●●●○** Firestorm. The ambient heat alone is lethal to anyone caught without cover for a full scene.

**●●●●●** Hellstorm. Structures burn, and the burning does not stop when you do.

### Shadow Caster

**●○○○○** Deepens the shadow in a room and puts unease into it: +1 difficulty on rolls to see or find you here.

**●●○○○** Hardened shadow. Silent, and invisible in poor light.

**●●●○○** A lash from the target's own shadow. No dodge, no line of sight required if you can see their shadow.

**●●●●○** Total dark. Paratech recording fails outright, and a mortal caught in it rolls Grit or leaves.

**●●●●●** The shadows go solid and take hold: leaving the area costs a Strength contest per round.

### Stormcaller

**●○○○○** Local air turns: a gust that slams a door, a chill you can see your breath in, static enough to spoil an instrument reading.

**●●○○○** A haft of compressed air and charge. Reaches a pace further than it looks.

**●●●○○** A strike. No dodge. Conducts: anyone in contact with the target, or the same conductive mass, takes 1 level.

**●●●●○** Cloudburst: rain, wind and lightning, and the only one of the three that reads to a bystander as weather rather than as a crime.

**●●●●●** Tempest. The roof comes off. Ulaanbaatar's stormchasers can tell it from real weather; nobody else can.
