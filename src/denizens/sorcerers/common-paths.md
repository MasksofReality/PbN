Two, and only two. They belong to the trade rather than to any School, every Sorcerer may learn both, and they advance on the **Aligned track** (20 XP to 5), with one available at rating 1 at creation.

They are deliberately opposed. **Glyphwork keeps things out. Scrying gets in.** A Sorcerer who holds both at high rating is the person every faction on the Council of Six wants on retainer and nobody wants in the building.

Neither has Traditions. There is nothing to declare and nothing to choose.

## Glyphwork: The Path of the Written Ward

Wards, seals, and the notation trade proper. The Bengaluru guilds standardised it, Tabriz weaves it into carpet, Sana'a burns it as incense, and Salamanca will hang you for it.

**●○○○○ Mark** *(free)*. A glyph that records. It registers when crossed, opened or handled, and roughly by what. *One action.*

**●●○○○ Seal** *(1 Mana)*. Hold a door, lid or container shut against mundane force: +2 difficulty to force. Obvious to Mana Sight, and to anyone who knows what a seal looks like. *One minute.*

**●●●○○ Ward** *(2 Mana)*. A threshold that one named category of thing must beat to cross: spirits, aberrants, one splat, or one named person. Lasts one week. Resolves as a static threshold: the crosser rolls their own defence pool at difficulty 6 and must equal or exceed your banked net successes. The ward is never rerolled, and you are never called on to roll for it in your absence. *Five minutes.*

**●●●●○ Veil-Glyph** *(3 Mana)*. Etched or woven, it makes an area or object unremarkable: paratech surveillance does not retain it and passive supernatural senses skim past. Active, directed searching beats it as a static threshold. This is what a very expensive Tabrizi carpet actually is. *Fifteen minutes; etched green leystone.*

**●●●●● Keystone** *(5 Mana)*. Raise a barrier over a single building for 24 hours that turns aberrant beasts and deflects the fringe of a wyrdstorm. *One hour; etched green leystone.*

<div class="callout">
<strong>Keystone stops short of Hearthbinding on purpose.</strong> The Hearthbind Ritual founds Basilicas and is 75 TE institutional history, not a five-dot purchase. Keystone is the individual practitioner's version: one building, one day, no follow-on infrastructure. This is not a Hearthstone: it does not moderate weather, does not power paratech, does not persist, and will not stop a determined siege. It is what a stormchaser crew caught in the open pays a fortune for, and it is the reason some of them come back.
</div>

## Scrying: The Path of the Far Window

The looking trade. Every faction on the Council of Six retains one, none of them admit it, and the Sana'a Star-Chandlers have spent two centuries proving it cannot find the Isle.

**●○○○○ Cast the Glass** *(free)*. See and hear one place you have physically stood, within roughly a mile, for one minute per net success. The view cannot be moved. *One action; a reflective surface.*

**●●○○○ Sympathy** *(1 Mana)*. Anchor the window to a *person* rather than a place, given something of theirs; the view follows them. Contested where the subject has any supernatural means of resistance. A mortal is helpless before it, and this is a Stability trigger every time. *One minute.*

**●●●○○ Penetration** *(2 Mana)*. The window passes walls, floors and natural rock, and range extends across a Basilica. It does not pass a Glyphwork Ward or Veil-Glyph without beating the banked threshold. *Five minutes.*

**●●●●○ The Turned Window** *(3 Mana)*. See through wards, sealed rooms and paratech surveillance blackspots, and detect whether someone else's window is open on you. Beating another practitioner's watch tells you where they are sitting. *Fifteen minutes; etched green leystone.*

**●●●●● Violation** *(5 Mana)*. Anywhere on Earth that a living mind you have named currently is: all five senses, the view movable, for one hour per net success. Contested at +2 difficulty to you against a Nightfolk subject. This is the most litigated act in Purgatory and the reason the Sovereign Club's vaults are lined in etched leystone. *One hour or more; refined leystone.*

<div class="callout">
<strong>The limits here are firm, and they hold a lot of the setting up.</strong> Scrying reaches no further than the present moment, because Divination owns time. It cannot be used to aim a Translation or a Burst at a place you have not stood. And it returns nothing at all from the Inner Reaches, the Governor's Manor, or the Isle in transit. That last one is the Isle's answer rather than a rule about Scrying, and it is precisely why Sana'a's charts are worth what they are worth.
</div>
