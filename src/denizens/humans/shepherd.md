## Who They Are

Every other Basilica has a cultural core. Purgatory does not, and that is the point. It has an exile creole instead of a heritage: elaborate courtesy over total mutual suspicion, obligation tracked like currency, and an allergy to questions about where anyone is from. That leaves the Shepherd holding the one thing the city itself lacks. A person who can build a real community out of dockside wards, Mangrove stilt-hamlets and Grenvalden-descended families has made something no faction can purchase and no Art can counterfeit.

Shepherds here come from everywhere: Salamanca-trained clergy on a long posting, ward representatives who have been fighting the Council since before the waygates, spirit-mediators trained on the Kathmandu model, and people who arrived and gradually became who everyone went to. Some sell threshold wards on the Mangrove road, and an honest Shepherd knows which of their stock is functional, which came from an Artificer, and which is comfort. A mundane human works no magic, and pretending otherwise gets parishioners killed.

Then there is the Grenveld, where no settlement may hold more than two hundred and the two hundred and first drops dead, and where much of the City-Isle's native human stock comes from. Every Grenvalden family has done the arithmetic on a birth. If you shepherd those people, you have been asked questions no doctrine answers.

**Attribute priority:** Social 7, Mental 5, Physical 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Allies (community members)

## Practice: The Voice the People Hear

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and call on your community for one specific collective action. A watch on the ward tonight. Forty people at a Council session. A door that stays closed to someone. A crowd that disperses instead of forming. They act. Scale grows with your Allies rating, your reputation, and which groups you have actually stood up for.

## Stigma: Held to the Standard

Your community Background dots exist only as long as their trust does. Break it by your own choice, whether you lied to them, traded on them, or cut a deal that cost them, and the dots are gone with no XP credited back, because a Background lost to your own decision is not a Background lost to plot. Rebuilding is staff-gated and slow.

## Added Trigger: Spent Their Trust on Something That Was Not for Them

Mark it.

## Shepherd-Specific Merits

**Recognised Standing** (2) — licensed clergy, a ward representative, or simply known, so your word carries where the community can hear it.

**The Room Quiets** (2) — difficulty drops by 1 on Leadership rolls addressing ten or more people.

## Faction Fit

Every faction wants you and none of them will say why plainly. The Sovereign Club offers funding for your hall, the Ashen Court offers protection you did not ask for, the Vigiles want you to keep your people calm, and the Circle wants to know which of your congregation is talking to something. Playing a Shepherd means deciding, repeatedly, what your community's trust is worth while being courted by people whose interest you cannot account for.
