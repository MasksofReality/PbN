## Who They Are

Purgatory is a freeport where every Basilica's goods, agents and secrets cross the same crowded streets, and the Broker is what makes that legible. They trade goods, information, favours and access, sometimes all four before lunch. The waygates opened about ten years ago, and an older Broker remembers the before, when getting anything off the Isle meant paying the Governor's Toll in time or magic and being here was mostly a sentence.

The stock in trade is specific to this city: restricted heartglass off the Ruby Coast, goblin fruit out of the Bitterthorn, raptor ichor rendering into unusually pure leystone, Old World salvage, League currency and Basilica futures, and the one commodity Purgatory produces natively, which is obligation. Favours here are recorded, sometimes literally. The Ashen Court supplies and the Sovereign Club launders, they loathe each other publicly and cannot function apart, and the Broker stands in the gap taking a percentage of both.

A Broker's asset is not inventory but reputation. Building it takes a career. Losing it takes an afternoon. Most are not criminals in any meaningful sense. They are people who understand that rules are for those who cannot afford exceptions.

**Attribute priority:** Social 7, Mental 5, Physical 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Resources

## Practice: Everything Has a Price

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and name a specific item, service or piece of information reachable from the City-Isle. It can be found. Staff set the price and the delivery time, and you decide whether to pay. The question is never whether, only what it costs.

## Stigma: The Ledger Runs Both Ways

Every use of the Practice creates one Obligation you owe somebody. While you carry three or more outstanding, any roll to secure new credit or a new favour takes a difficulty increase of 1. Staff call them due at their own convenience, and this is Purgatory, so at least one of your creditors is not a person, does not forget, and treats "nothing is free, least of all the things that claim to be" as a statement of law rather than a proverb.

## Added Trigger: Sold Something You Said You Would Not Sell

Write your lines down at creation. The trigger is worthless if nobody knows where they were.

## Broker-Specific Merits

**Good Paper** (2) — once per scene, produce credible documentation for one claim. Whether it is forged or merely convenient is your business.

**The Long Memory** (1) — difficulty drops by 1 on any roll concerning who owes what to whom, which in Purgatory is most rolls.

## Faction Fit

The Sovereign Club if you want to be respectable, the Ashen Court if you want to be useful. The Promethean Institute is a reliable customer with grant money and no sense of price.
