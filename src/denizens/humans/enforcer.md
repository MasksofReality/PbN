## Who They Are

Enforcers keep order in the City-Isle: licensed watch, harbour security, faction muscle, private contractors, Vigiles auxiliaries, and the informal authority that keeps the crowded wards from boiling over. They are not Hunters. Aberrant Hunters are a splat of their own and their business is writs and the wilds. An Enforcer's business is this street, tonight.

An Enforcer knows Purgatory's rhythms: which wards are quiet, who the players are in a dispute, when to step in and when to let something burn itself out. In a city where five factions each hold their own definition of order and the Council of Six has to be woken up to arbitrate, that judgement is the job. The law is twenty years old. Nobody agrees on what the Pax means on the ground. The Enforcer decides, one call at a time, and lives with it.

They are also the humans most likely to be first at something that is not a registered anything: a body that was not killed by a person, a face nobody can hold in memory, a door in a wall that has no door. What they do next is the most character-defining question this Profession offers.

**Attribute priority:** Physical 7, Social 5, Mental 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Contacts (watch, Vigiles, or faction security)

## Practice: Known Quantity

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and the system works for you. Enter a sealed location, pull a Vigiles record or a harbour manifest, get a patrol to attend. No roll. It works because you are a recognised part of the apparatus, which means it also works on the record.

## Stigma: On the Roster

Your shift pattern, your address, your colleagues and your file all exist, and in Purgatory a file is a tradeable good. Staff may always establish where you were and when. Any roll to conceal your identity or whereabouts from an institution takes a difficulty increase of 1.

## Added Trigger: Wore the Authority for It

Mark if you used your standing to do something to a person that you would have refused to do without it.

## Enforcer-Specific Merits

**Warranted** (2) — a recognised watch or Vigiles-auxiliary credential, so official cooperation is other people's default assumption.

**Read the Room** (2) — once per scene, ask staff one factual question about imminent violence in the scene, such as who is armed or who is about to move.

## Faction Fit

The Order of Vigiles is the obvious home. The Sovereign Club pays better and asks worse. The Promethean Institute needs expedition security. An Enforcer with Ashen Court contacts is either compromised or very good at their job, and telling the difference is the campaign.
