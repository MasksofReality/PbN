*Doctors, surgeons, and the people who keep everyone alive*

## Who They Are

Physicians are indispensable anywhere. In Purgatory they are something more. The Isle produces injuries that nothing in a textbook covers: ichor exposure from the refineries, goblin fruit in a patient who did not know what they were eating, whatever the Ruby Coast does to living tissue that lingers near it, and the slow effects of an ambient aetheric field that no Hearthstone is flattening. Every other Basilica's clinical baseline assumes a stasis field. Yours does not, and the difference shows up in your patients.

The pharmacopoeia is alchemical, which means Antananarivo reagents at Antananarivo prices, and half your clinical equipment is paratech that fails when the weather turns strange.

Post-Pax, your patients include BRDs, and their physiology is not in the textbook either. Some of them will not survive an honest chart. That is the whole of this Profession's moral problem. A wound recorded as inflicted by a Nightfolk starts a Vigiles inquiry, an inquiry can end in a writ of execution, and the Hunters do not investigate twice. What you write down decides who lives. Every faction knows it, and every faction keeps a quiet relationship with a Physician.

**Attribute priority:** Mental 7, Social 5, Physical 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Resources (supplies, instruments, reagent access)

## Practice: Everyone Needs a Doctor

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and reach a person or their file through clinical channels without a roll. A consultation with someone in Vigiles custody. A restricted record. Unchallenged passage through a ward or a quarantine line. Medicine opens doors that warrants do not.

## Stigma: Everyone Knows What You Know

You hold information about people, and people know you hold it. Expect to be regularly brought someone who wants it: a Vigiles investigator with a form, a faction with a favour, a patient's family with a grievance. Refusing has consequences that have nothing to do with medicine. Disclosing costs you the thing the Profession runs on, and may cost your patient their head.

## Added Trigger: Broke a Confidence

Mark if you disclosed what a patient told you, or what you found in them, to someone with no right to it. Whatever the pressure, and whatever it bought.

## Physician-Specific Merits

**Licensed Practitioner** (2) — clinical standing, so you are admitted anywhere medicine is happening, including a Vigiles holding cell.

**Steady Hands** (2) — difficulty drops by 1 on Medicine rolls made under fire, in the dark, or on a boat.

## Faction Fit

The Circle of Bone and Ivy will make a grudging exception for a useful skillset, and the Order of Vigiles will not stop asking. The Promethean Institute funds research and will want the interesting cases; whether the interesting cases consented is your problem rather than theirs.
