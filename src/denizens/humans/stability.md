## Stability, Triggers and Eldritch

The core trigger list applies in full, the end-of-scene roll is unchanged, and a trigger counts once per scene however often it fires. This document adds triggers. It softens nothing.

Mark one of these if your character, in this scene:

**Denied what they saw.** Talked themselves, or someone else, out of the truth in order to keep their life intact.

**Used their standing against someone who had none.** Turned a record, a credential, a complaint or a uniform on a person with no way to answer it. In Purgatory that includes the Pax itself: informing on a BRD you knew was innocent, or letting a writ proceed on evidence you knew was thin. The machinery that protects you kills people, and using it is a choice.

**Broke the specific faith their Profession runs on.** Each Profession names its own — see each [Profession's page](/denizens/humans/) for its added trigger. Where an added trigger and a core trigger describe the same act, mark one.

**Witnessed something that should not be seen.** Because Nightfolk are publicly known, much of their existence no longer shocks. A registered Lilum drinking from a filed donor at the next table is a regular sight. A Garou changing shape in the street is a public-order matter, not an existential one. A Lost telling stories in a park about their Keeper is good entertainment. The trigger fires when humanity's "new normal" fails to contain the horror you have actually witnessed. Working examples:

* The Mistcrown noticing you, or anything the Inner Reaches do with distance and time.
* The bana'Shee's attention.
* Someone grown into the Ruby Coast reef, red and smiling and not quite alive or dead.
* A Grenveld village that just lost its two hundred and first resident, on schedule, to nothing.
* Anything the Dreadmire coven says about the Governor while refusing to say what "It" is.
* Standing next to a Working that goes badly wrong — the kind that draws Attention — and watching the caster come back from wherever they went with a hole in their memory they can feel but not open.

## Eldritch, for Humans

Eldritch is what a mind pays to see the inner workings of the cosmos. For a human, the trade buys exactly one thing: the Intelligence plus Eldritch roll to read a Working by sight. Every dot also charges a permanent point of Stability that never comes back.

Eldritch 1 is awarded only after repeated, undeniable contact the character chose not to look away from. One impossible night is a trigger, not a dot. A season on the thread is a dot. A second dot is awarded only for method: an Archivist reconstructing the Interstice from primary sources, a Physician cataloguing what heartglass does to tissue, a Ghostwright following a reactor conduit further than the schematic goes.

The human roads to Eldritch in this setting are all survivable and none of them free. The Interstice. The Inner Reaches. The quintessence reactors. Heartglass and its appetite. The Governor. What aberrants actually are, as against what every respectable text says they are. What a waygate is really cutting through, and where its passengers go for the second they are inside it. And where the Awakened went.
