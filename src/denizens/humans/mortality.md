## Draining, Death and the Two Ways Out

**Health.** Seven levels, one track, wound penalties as written. No second track.

**Soak.** Stamina plus armour at difficulty 6, against bashing and lethal. Aggravated is unsoakable, and no Merit here grants aggravated soak.

**Healing.** The mortal table, of which this splat is the baseline. Aggravated heals at the lethal rate and nothing accelerates it.

**Being fed upon.** Feeding takes Vitality at one point per Essence gained. The loss carries no wound penalty. Damage is 1 lethal per 2 points taken, or 1 per 3 if you are freely willing, which under the Pax usually means filed. The tally belongs to you rather than to the predator and resets at midnight, so two predators in one night share one count. Vitality returns at midnight equal to your unmarked Health boxes: the uninjured refill fast, the battered barely at all, the Incapacitated not at all.

**Humans generate no Tether.** The Utkinov Effect is a property of aspected essence, and mortals do not produce it, however regular the arrangement. A human herd is safe to keep, which is exactly why humans are farmed rather than hunted, and why a filed donor arrangement is more secure for the Nightfolk than for the human.

**At 0 Vitality.** Pallid, freezing, barely lucid. Further feeding takes Health levels one for one as lethal, and that damage must begin to heal before Vitality starts refilling.

**Attrition.** A human drained to nothing enters Attrition like anything else. At the end of each scene you remain at 0 Vitality, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6. On a failure you lose control for the rest of the scene, and for a human that means a classical reality break. On a botch you do not come back until the next midnight reset. This is the only route by which a Human reaches Attrition at all, and it always takes somebody putting you there.

**Past Incapacitated.** Further lethal or aggravated damage kills you. No corpus, no anchor, no vessel, no clause that turns it around. A dead human is dead, and in Purgatory a body is a commodity: the Vigiles look the other way where Amaia is concerned, the Bitterthorn does not return what it swallows, and the reef keeps what grows into it. Death is not automatic, not owed, and not a build plan.

## The First Way Out: Seeing the Ineffable Truth

A mortal who sees something they should not have may gain a permanent Eldritch 1 and become a Sorcerer, taking Occult Arts on the Nonaligned track. This requires staff approval and an in-fiction scene.

As a Sorcerer you lose everything you had on your prior sheet and transition to the [Sorcerer ruleset](/denizens/sorcerers/).

## The Second Way Out You Didn't Want to Take: Abduction by the Gentry

The Gentry are mercurial beings of such godlike power that human law helplessly looks the other way at whatever they do. Selfish in the extreme, and lacking the spark of creativity that makes humans what they are, they sometimes steal a Mundane away to suit their own whims, with little interest in consent. Those who escape and claim sanctuary under the sacred law of guest right before any of the Triune are off-limits for recapture, and count thereafter as one of the Lost.

As a Lost you lose everything you had on your prior sheet and transition to the [Lost ruleset](/denizens/lost/).

## The Third Way Out That Isn't: Ghouling

A living human regularly fed Vitae becomes a Ghoul. Ghouling is a state rather than a change of splat. The character stays a Human, with all that entails. They gain no Vitae pool; instead they pay for their domitor's Arts out of Vitality, at twice whatever the Art normally charges, and they may learn only those Arts, capped at the second dot.

A Ghoul fed within the last lunar month stops aging and heals lethal damage at the bashing rate — one level an hour. Aggravated is unchanged. They also acquire the domitor's leash: an unbuyable physical dependency that raises the difficulty by 2 on any roll to act against the domitor or to refuse them. It is chemical, not telepathic. There are no commands and no mental override.

What ghouling reliably provides beyond that is a domitor and a Vigiles inquiry waiting to happen, because the line between a filed donor arrangement and a fed thrall is exactly the line the Pax was written to police.

Sorcerers and Metahumans cannot take this road quietly. Mana and Psi both reject Vitae, and the attempt is an agonising internal conflict that requires a storyteller scene and can end in failure or death.
