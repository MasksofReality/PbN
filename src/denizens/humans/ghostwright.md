## Who They Are

Paratech runs the world, and the Glyphcode Standards mean a device etched in Bogotá can be repaired in Chino. A Ghostwright is who repairs it. They are the mechanics, salvagers, hackers and improvisers who keep the grid alive, bring Old World scitech back from the dead, and jury-rig things from leystone offcuts that the guilds would price at a year's wages.

Know the boundary, because it defines the Profession. A Ghostwright works no magic. Novel enchantment is the trade of Artificers and Sorcerers. What Glyphcode literacy buys is everything else: reading a formula, repairing it, rerouting it, bypassing it, cannibalising one device into another, and diagnosing why a conduit that passes inspection is quietly wrong. In a world where the qualified practitioners are few, expensive, and mostly employed already, that is not a lesser skill. It is why the lights stay on in the wards nobody bills for.

It also runs straight into the illegal trades. Registration-evasion work — a paratech piece built to hide an inhuman feature from a Vigiles inspection — is the most in-demand and most dangerous product a Ghostwright can offer, because the Pax is built on being able to see what someone is. Add Glyphcode forgery, unlicensed grid taps, salvage that was never declared, and red leystone, which pound for pound holds ten to twelve times what purple does and wants to merge with living things, so nobody sane builds with it and somebody always does.

The parties who notice are the Promethean Institute, which owns discovery and licensing and is on cordial terms with everyone; the Order of Vigiles, whose remit is paracausal crime; and the Sovereign Club, which funds the Institute and dislikes competition. Their attention starts friendly. That is the frightening part.

**Attribute priority:** Mental 7, Physical 5, Social 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Contacts (salvage, grid crews, or the unlicensed trade)

## Practice: If It Runs, I Can Run It

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and roll Intelligence plus Technology at difficulty 7 to get into a specific paratech or scitech system without the credentials or physical access it requires: a grid node, a Vigiles ward-lock, an Institute vault seal, a salvaged Old World device that has not run in two centuries. Success means you are in. It does not mean nobody noticed. This is an ordinary roll, wound penalties and all, and it can fail like one.

## Stigma: The Institute Takes an Interest

Track a counter from 0 to 5, rising by 1 in any cycle in which you used the Practice or did significant unlicensed work. At 1 or 2, somebody has a file and a very pleasant recruiter knows your name. At 3 or 4 the interest is active: an Institute contact who is neither hostile nor going away, and a Vigiles inspection that is technically routine. At 5 it converts. The Institute makes an offer that is not really one, or the Vigiles make a case, or the Sovereign Club decides your workshop is a competitor. The counter fades by 1 per full month of clean work.

## Added Trigger: Gave Someone Up to Keep Your Own Name Clear

Mark if you handed over a person, a workshop, or a location to Institute or Vigiles attention in order to stay out of it yourself.

## Ghostwright-Specific Merits

**Grafted** (2) — a physiology that accepts modification, and the Glyphcode literacy to keep salvaged Old World cyberware running on paratech life support. Required before any implant. It includes one cosmetic or utility implant that confers no dice.

**Salvage Eye** (1) — difficulty drops by 1 to identify Old World hardware or a leystone grade by sight, and to guess what a device was for.

## Faction Fit

The Promethean Institute is the legitimate path, well-funded and careless with other people's lives. The Ashen Court pays cash for the evasion work. The Circle of Bone and Ivy will keep you on retainer for when something they found starts humming, ticking, or otherwise acting odd.
