## Who They Are

Most pre-Sundering knowledge was destroyed. The people who preserve and transmit what remains are not clerks. They are performing an act of survival. Archivists are historians, scholars, librarians and researchers: the people who know where to find what is known, and increasingly what the gaps mean.

The gaps are enormous. The Interstice has no record at all — ten years in which evidence of human activity simply ceases — and survivors came out the other side with a decade unaccounted for. The Itinerant century exists mostly in caravan oral accounts. And Purgatory has the worst record of any League city. It is barely eight decades old, its founding population was exiles and fugitives, and its whole civic manner holds that a stated origin is never challenged and never believed. Your archive rests on false names, and you know it.

What is here instead is volume: League correspondence passing through the freeport, faction ledgers, harbour manifests, Institute expedition reports, and the ruins on the Ghostlit Coast that match no known architecture and never have. There is also the thing every Archivist eventually notices, which is that the record contains people who appear across generations without ageing. That used to be a scandal and is now simply BRD registration, except for the ones whose names predate any registry.

**Attribute priority:** Mental 7, Social 5, Physical 3
**Required Background:** at least 1 dot of Contacts (scholarly, Institute, or archival)

## Practice: The Record Remembers

Spend 1 Willpower, once per scene, and locate a specific piece of historical, legal or administrative information held anywhere in the human record reachable from the City-Isle: manifests, ledgers, League correspondence, Institute holdings, technically restricted material. Legitimate access and professional method, no roll. It does not reach supernatural secret-keeping. The Directorate's archives are not yours and the Triune keep no files.

## Stigma: The Gaps Call

When you find an inconsistency in the record, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6 to set it aside and go back to your other work. Fail and you pull the thread. You do not get to decide whether the anomalies interest you, only what you do once they have.

## Added Trigger: Falsified or Suppressed the Record

Mark if you altered, hid or destroyed a true record, or certified a false one. Even for a very good reason, and especially for a very good reason.

## Archivist-Specific Merits

**Registered Reader** (2) — standing access to restricted stacks by legitimate channel, covering Institute holdings, harbour archives and League correspondence.

**Total Recall** (3) — once per scene, ask staff whether your character has read something relevant. If it is plausible that they have, they have.

## Faction Fit

The Promethean Institute is the natural patron and the natural hazard: they will fund the expedition and they will not read your caveats. Lhasa's archive is the dream, and "most die waiting" is a policy rather than an accident.
