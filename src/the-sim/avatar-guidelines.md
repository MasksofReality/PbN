## Why We Have These Guidelines

Purgatory by Night is set on the Wandering Isle of Purgatory within the only civilization: an aetherpunk city touched by the Dreaming, where humanity and the Nightfolk live in a tense community dynamic. The build carries a steampunk and art deco and art nouveau character, layered with modern touches and small, strange signs that something otherworldly has warped the area. Your avatar is often the first thing another player sees, so we ask that everyone's look supports the tone and atmosphere we are building together. These guidelines exist to keep the sim feeling cohesive, immersive, and welcoming to everyone who steps into our story.

## What We're Looking For

- Human-presenting avatars, or the appropriate supernatural variant for your splat
- Realistic proportions, with well-fitted mesh bodies and Bento hands
- Apparel and styling that reflects an aetherpunk, steampunk, or modern urban aesthetic, with art deco and art nouveau touches
- Looks that feel lived-in and grounded, even when touched by the strange or the supernatural

## What We're Not Able to Allow

To keep the sim's tone consistent for everyone, the following avatar types are not permitted at Purgatory by Night:

- Furry avatars or anthro mesh bodies
- Dinkies, toddleedoo, or similarly scaled avatars
- Space alien or other non-human sci-fi avatars
- Anime-styled avatar body meshes, including chibi or waifu-style bodies
- Any avatar that is, or could reasonably be read as, underaged

## Setting & Theme Restrictions

Beyond body type, we also ask that looks and roleplay styles stay within our setting. Purgatory by Night carries fantastical elements, strange plantlife, animal life, and crystal formations touched by the Dreaming, but it is an urban fantasy setting, not a high or medieval fantasy one. The following are lovely in other communities, but they fall outside what we're building here, so we ask that they be left at the door:

- Medieval looks or apparel, including armor, doublets, and fantasy-court wear
- Space-oriented looks or sci-fi fashion
- Gorean looks or Gorean-style roleplay
- 2D paper doll avatars

## A Quick Gut Check

If you're ever unsure whether a look fits, a simple question can help: "Could this exist in an aetherpunk city touched by the Dreaming, somewhere between steampunk elegance and modern street life?" If the honest answer is no, it likely isn't the right fit for Purgatory by Night. Our staff team is always happy to help with avatar troubleshooting or point you toward stores and looks that suit the setting.

## Approved Style Inspiration

For a sense of direction, here are several examples of styles and motifs that will fit into Purgatory. Please note these are **examples**, and these are not the only styles accepted. They are just meant to help give inspiration!

**Gothic Elegance**

- Dark, tailored formalwear blending gothic foundations with art nouveau flourishes and art deco geometry, faded or antique in feel
- Structured coats, corsetry, or cravats finished with brass and clockwork detailing, in rich, muted fabrics like velvet or brocade
- A subtle mechanical accent, such as a pocket watch chain or filigree cuff, ties the refined and the mechanized together

**Feral & Primal**

- Earthy, primal styling; fur, hide, and bone accents worked into modern or ritual dress
- Battle-worn, tribal, or ritual markings, fantastical elements included. Two centuries of change and the touch of the Dreaming have made plenty "normal" here that would look strange to us today

**Steamwork & Ingenuity**

- Layered vests, pocketed coats, and brass or copper fittings that suggest a life spent building, repairing, and moving through the district
- Small personal touches like pinned gears, strapped goggles, a signature tool at the hip, or a well-worn duster and satchel for those who travel between the Island's corners

**Punk & Rebellion**

- Studded leather, band tees, safety pins, DIY patches, and deconstructed layering pulled from decades of punk subculture
- Bold, unapologetic styling that reads as defiance

**Modern Streetwear**

- Contemporary casual pieces: fitted jackets, hoodies, sneakers, and everyday looks reminiscent of the early 2000s through 2010s
- Grounded, familiar silhouettes that read as "regular person" against the sim's more stylized looks

**Aether-Touched**

- Faceted, crystalline accents worked into clothing or accessories, hinting at a brush with aether energy
- Subtle luminescence, glass-like textures, or geometric crystal shapes incorporated into otherwise grounded outfits

**Dreaming-Touched**

- Whimsical, otherworldly details worked into clothing that still holds up to everyday wear, practical rather than costume
- Fantastical shapes, colors, or textures balanced with grounded cuts and materials, so the look feels touched by wonder without tipping into fantasy cosplay
