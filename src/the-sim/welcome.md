Welcome to Purgatory by Night.

This is a collaborative roleplay experience set within the World of Darkness, a homebrew setting built from the 20th Anniversary editions of the classic game lines, woven together into something entirely our own. We are glad you found us.

Purgatory by Night takes place in the year 200 TE / 2278 CE by the old calendar, two centuries after the catastrophic events that reshaped the world and nearly ended it entirely. You find yourself in Basilica Purgatory: a city-state fused with a piece of fallen Hedge Gate to become the Wandering Isle. It is one of many Basilicas within the Basilican League and one of the only marginally safe places that society has begun to rebuild itself.

<div class="callout callout--rare">
<strong>A note for staff:</strong> this description of Basilica Purgatory's origin — fused with a fallen Hedge Gate fragment to become "the Wandering Isle" — is new and differs from what's currently written on the <a href="/the-world/world-bible/">World Bible</a> and <a href="/the-world/glossary/">Glossary</a> pages (built on the sunken ruins of San Francisco, with a separate nearby "Isle of Purgatory"). This page reflects the most recently provided document, but the World Bible, Glossary, and Timeline haven't been updated to match yet. Worth a canon pass to reconcile these before Grand Opening.
</div>

Make no mistake, though. There is no truly safe place left in all the world. There are simply places that hide the darkness better.

Inside these walls, you will find vampires who chose rebirth over oblivion, werewolves remade by a new goddess, the fae-touched navigating a Dreaming that will not stop bleeding into reality, and ordinary people trying to find an existence worth living for in the aftermath of the Sundering among other denizens of Nightfolk.

This is not a game about winning. It is a game about surviving, and about what surviving costs, and what it makes you.

*We are currently in soft open, with parts of the sim and website still under construction. Please forgive our dust as we work toward the grand opening. We are building this world with care, and we are glad you are here to be part of it.*
