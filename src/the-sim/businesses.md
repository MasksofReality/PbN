*Storefronts, lounges, and gathering spots woven into the fabric of the Basilica.*

Purgatory Island is home to a wide range of storefronts, lounges, and gathering spots woven into the fabric of the Basilica. These businesses are more than backdrops. They are living pieces of the sim, meant to host roleplay, build community, and give characters a reason to cross paths. Below you will find how our businesses work, along with the current directory of what is open, claimed, or available for a new owner.

## How Sim Businesses Work

1. **All sim businesses are permanent establishments of the sim.** The build, location, and concept do not change, though the player who 'owns' and runs a business may change over time due to real life circumstances or inactivity.
2. **Players may make minor changes to their business build** to decorate for events and IC holidays, up to 15 LI. This keeps businesses feeling festive and alive without altering the sim's permanent look.
3. **To run a business, a player must be an active member of the community.** This means the character has a completed character sheet, and the owner has been present at the business for at least one hour a week so the space stays available for roleplay.
4. **Business owners may request that certain characters not be allowed inside their establishment.** Owners are not permitted to ban an entire splat or faction from their business unless doing so is part of a story set by Staff.
5. **If a business owner goes inactive** and does not meet the weekly hour requirement for an extended period (30 days), the business becomes available for another player to apply for, so the space does not sit unused. If a player knows they are going on an RL hiatus, please let the Staff know so that they can keep players aware of the character's presence even if RL is being cruel.
6. **Owners are encouraged to post their business's regular open hours or standing roleplay times** on the sim's Discord in the #yellow-pages, so other players know when to stop by to see the owner. Also on the Discord owners can create their own advertisements for our sim radio, or request one made in the Discord category for Fang FM.
7. **Out of character disagreements between players should never affect in-character access to a business.** Only in character actions and story should determine who is welcome. If there is a player that is causing you, as an owner, grief, please let the Staff know first, so we can help handle the situation.
8. **Any major IC event at a business,** such as a takeover, raid, or destruction, should be discussed with Staff ahead of time so it can be woven into the wider story.
9. **New or reopened businesses are announced to the community** once unclaimed, so players know where fresh roleplay opportunities are starting up.
