Every Lost begins from the same numbers. Your Seeming sets your Attribute priority and your Contract list; it does not give you more of anything.

| | |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3 across Physical, Social, Mental. Priority order is set by your Seeming, not chosen. |
| Abilities | 13 Talents / 9 Skills / 5 Knowledges |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control and Grit, minimum 1 in each |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Contracts | One Aligned Contract at 2, another Aligned Contract at 1. One of the two must be your Seeming's Affinity Contract. |
| Glamour | 2. Derived from your highest Contract. Never purchased. |
| Essence Pool | 7 |
| Stability | Conscience + Self-Control. Lost begin with Eldritch 0, and nobody but a Sorcerer takes Eldritch at creation. |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots. Allies, Contacts, Mentor, Resources and Safe Place are the usual picks. |
| Freebie points | 21 |
| Eldritch | 0, no exceptions. It is awarded by staff, through play. |

**Freebie costs:** Attribute 5, Ability 2, Willpower 2, Psyche 2, Background 1, Merit 1 per point, Contract dot for 11 with a maximum of one bought this way.

**Discordant and Restricted Contracts cannot be bought with freebies under any circumstance.**

Attributes, Abilities, Psyche and Contracts all cap at 5. Willpower caps at 10.

**Four Arts, total.** No character on the game holds more than four Arts. For a Lost that means your three Seeming Contracts, plus at most one more, and the fourth requires a story to get there.

## Your Affinity Contract

Every supernatural character designates one starting Art as their Affinity: the one tied closest to what they are. For the Lost, it is not chosen. Your Seeming decides it, because the Seeming is what your Keeper decided you were, and the Affinity Contract is the part of that decision that stuck.

| Seeming | Affinity Contract |
|---|---|
| Beast | Contracts of Fang and Talon |
| Darkling | Contracts of Darkness |
| Elemental | Contracts of Elements |
| Fairest | Contracts of Vainglory |
| Ogre | Contracts of Stone |
| Wizened | Contracts of Artifice |

You start with exactly two Contracts and one of them is this one. So the real decisions at creation are which of your other two Aligned Contracts joins it, and whether the Affinity sits at 2 or at 1.

A Beast is a thing with teeth before it is anything else. A Wizened is a pair of hands. A Fairest is a face in a room. You may become other things later. You started as that one.

## Learning More

Contracts come in three flavours, and what you pay depends on which.

**Aligned** are the three Contracts of your own Seeming, including your Affinity. Twenty XP takes one from nothing to five, and the first dot of an Aligned Contract is free. A Lost who opens with two of their three Seeming trees picks up the third for nothing as soon as play shows them growing into it. Nearly every Lost ends up holding all three. The interesting question is which two they had when it mattered.

**Discordant** means any Contract Aligned to a different Seeming. Thirty-nine XP to five, which is roughly thirteen months of income. This is scenery for a long-running character, not a build plan.

**Restricted** means the [Contracts of the Hedge](/denizens/lost/hedge-contracts/), and it needs both a teacher and staff permission. Never at creation. Once you have it, it advances on the middle track: thirty-one XP to five.

Money is not the obstacle for a Discordant Contract. A route is. Somebody has to be willing to teach you a thing your Seeming was never given, and in practice that somebody is a Wyrdborn with a House and an agenda. See [The Wyrdborn and the Houses](/denizens/lost/wyrdborn-and-houses/).

**Mind the slot count.** Three Seeming Contracts fill three of your four Art slots. The fourth can be a Discordant tree from another Seeming, or it can be the Hedge. Never both. A Lost who wants to be a Hedge-walker is giving up the possibility of ever holding another Seeming's tree, and that is the single most consequential long-term choice on the sheet.

No Lost holds an Art belonging to another splat. There is no exception and there is no register entry to point at.

## When You Were Taken

In 2041 CE the Dreaming was sealed, and the seal cut both ways. Every Fae still walking the Middle Realm died, and every Changeling with them. Everything inside survived, sealed in but intact. Twenty-seven years later the Sundering ruptured the vessel and the Wyrd flooded back out as the Great Wyrdstorm.

Every Lost declares one of two brackets at creation.

### Post-Sundering Abductee

*Default. Open to anyone.*

You were taken at some point in the last two centuries, since the Dreaming reopened. The Gentry have had two hundred years to resume an old habit and have wasted none of it. Durances run from months to decades, and time in Arcadia does not keep faith with a calendar.

Your Fetch works as described on the [Lost overview](/denizens/lost/). A construct left in your place, carrying your memories and your name, being you ever since. It is not a copy and not a twin. It is a thing that has been living your life for years and has opinions about how you were doing it.

### Pre-Seal Survivor *[Rare]*

*Mentor-gated.*

You were in your Keeper's hands when the Dreaming closed. You have been trapped in Arcadia for over two hundred years — though it may feel as if it were far longer or shorter than this, depending on your Keeper's desires. You are one of a handful of people alive who remember the Old World with their own eyes — and nobody agrees how many of you there are, including you.

Everyone you knew is dust. The country you were taken from is under several hundred metres of water or several metres of ash. There are eighteen functioning cities on the planet and you have heard of none of them.

Your Fetch did not simply die. It should have. It was left in a world that suffered a nuclear war, a decade-long unmaking of reality, and ten years no record survives from at all. What is walking around now wearing your name is not what your Keeper made. It has been reanimated by the Great Wyrdstorm into something aberrant, something utterly inhuman and single-minded, and what it wants is not your life back. It wants you, specifically, dead, because it has worked out — or been told, or simply always known — that it cannot be truly alive while the original is still breathing.

It has had a very long time to think about this. It is patient, it is not confused about who it is looking for, and it has spent two centuries being the only one of you the world has met.

**This bracket needs Mentor approval and a staff conversation.** It is not a background flourish. The Fetch is an active antagonist attached to your character from creation, and staff will use it. Pre-Seal survivors are genuinely rare.

| | |
|---|---|
| Resource Trait | Glamour, derived from your highest Contract, never purchased |

| Feeding, gently | Harvesting. 1 Essence per 2 post rounds. No Vitality, no damage, no tally, no Tether. |
| Feeding, otherwise | Draining. Full feeding rules — rate, damage, tally, Tether. Always a Stability trigger against a mortal. |
| At 0 Essence | Conduit of the Dreaming. Fears leak out as waking nightmares. |
| Contract roll | Contract rating + Glamour, difficulty 6 |
| Costs | Free / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 by rating |
| Catch | Waives the Essence cost. At one dot, −1 difficulty instead. |
| Affinity Contract | Fixed by Seeming. Must be one of your two starting Contracts. |
| Aligned | Your Seeming's three Contracts. 20 XP to five; first dot free. |
| Discordant | Another Seeming's Contracts. Needs a House. 39 XP to five. |
| Restricted | Contracts of the Hedge. Wyrdborn teachers only, never at creation. 31 XP to five. |
| Tree limit | Four Arts. Three Seeming Contracts, plus either one Discordant tree or the Hedge. Never both. |
| Kith | Flavour only. No dice, no difficulty, no Contract access. |
| Health | 7 levels; 8 for Elementals |
| Aggravated healing | 3 Essence + one week's rest per level. Cold iron aggravated cannot be healed with Essence at all. |
| Cold iron | Aggravated and unsoakable; 1 unsoakable bashing per round of contact; bound in it, no Essence and no Contracts |
| Seeming ledger | Blessing +2 dice against Curse −2 dice, net zero — except Beast, which pays in control, and Darkling, which pays in situation |
| Extra Stability triggers | A scene wholly in the Hedge · seen in Mien by an uninformed mortal · broke a freely struck bargain · in the presence of your Fetch or Keeper |
| Direct Stability loss | 1 per scene in an active Wyrdstorm · 1 for each of the six Contracts that say so · 1 **permanent** for breaking a Hedge-oath |
| Stability restoration | Catharsis, or the Honoured Bargain. One point per cycle, from one or the other, never both. |
| Breaking stand-in | The Hollow Night. Once per lunar month, lose yourself in the Hedge until dawn instead of rolling Conscience. |
| End state | Permanent Stability 1. Taken Back. |
