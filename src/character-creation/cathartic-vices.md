Basilica Purgatory is a hard place to live. Your character's **Stability** measures how steady their mind stays in a world full of horrors, and sooner or later, the horrors take their toll.

That's where your **Cathartic Vice** comes in. It's the thing your character does to feel like themselves again. Every character has one, and fulfilling it helps them **recover lost Stability**.

### How It Works

- **Pick one Cathartic Vice** when you create your character. It goes on your sheet right under your Nature and Demeanor.
- **Browse by mood.** We've grouped the vices to help you find one that fits.
- **Read the "What fulfills it" column.** It tells you what your character needs to do to recover Stability.
- **Some vices are limited to certain characters.** The table marks them.

***Quick refresher:*** *Stability = Conscience + Self-Control − Eldritch dots. Not sure what that means yet? The [Character Creation Guide](/character-creation/) walks you through it.*

### Choosing Yours

- **Pick one that makes a good story.** A vice isn't about being a bad person. It's about being a person, flaws and all.
- **Pick one you'll enjoy playing.** You'll be acting it out, so choose something that sounds fun, not just something that sounds dark.
- **Not sure?** Ask a Mentor on Discord or in-world. They'd love to help you find a vice that fits.

***A friendly reminder:*** *These are things your character does in the story, and real-life safety always comes first. Check in with your scene partners before anything intense, and use the sim's boundary and consent guidelines.*

## Indulgence

*Giving in to appetites, a little too far.*

| Vice | What fulfills it |
|---|---|
| **All Night Bender** | Lose a night to drink, drugs or revelry in company, and stay with it until it ends somewhere you didn't plan to be. |
| **Compulsive Gambling** | Stake something you'd genuinely miss (money, a favor, an object, a secret) on chance against others, and keep playing past the point you should have stopped. |
| **One Night Stands** | Seduce a stranger, spend the night, and be gone before they wake, leaving no real name and no promises. |
| **Over Indulgence** | Gorge on a single appetite (food, drink, a drug, luxury) well past enjoyment and into impairment, where others can see it. |
| **Reckless Spending** | Spend money or resources you can't spare on something you don't need, in front of someone, enough that it will be missed. |

## Thrill-Seeking

*Chasing the rush of almost getting caught.*

| Vice | What fulfills it |
|---|---|
| **Hunting in Plain Sight** *(predators only)* | Feed somewhere you could easily be seen, like a crowded club or a lit street, when somewhere safe was available. |
| **Shadowing Strangers** | Follow a stranger through their night unseen, as close as you dare, risking discovery the whole way. |
| **Tempting Death** | Put yourself in real, unnecessary danger, such as a ledge, a train line, or something far bigger than you, where a failed roll would actually hurt. |
| **Theft** | Take something you don't need from someone who will miss it, with a real chance of being caught. |
| **Trespassing** | Go somewhere you're forbidden to be, purely because it's forbidden, and stay long enough to risk being found. |

## Violence and Pain

*Hurting, breaking, and destroying.*

***Heads up:*** *These are some of the most intense vices. Check in with your scene partners before playing them out.*

| Vice | What fulfills it |
|---|---|
| **Back Alley Fighting** | Start or accept a fight that serves no purpose but the fight, against someone able to hurt you, and see it through. |
| **Casual Masochism** | Seek out pain for its own sake and take at least one real Health level that bought you nothing. |
| **Cruelty to Those Who Deserve It** | Inflict pain or humiliation on someone you've judged guilty, past anything justice or necessity required. |
| **Desecration** | Defile something another person or tradition holds sacred, where its keepers could find out. |
| **Watching It Burn** | Destroy something of real value (a building, a car, someone's life's work) and stay to watch it go. |

## Drama and Defiance

*Stirring things up where everyone can see.*

| Vice | What fulfills it |
|---|---|
| **Burning Bridges** | Deliberately wreck a relationship, deal or standing that was going well, in front of the person on the other side of it. |
| **Defying Authority** | Openly defy or insult someone with real power over you, like an elder, a boss, or a cop, to their face. |
| **Flaunting It** | Show what you are or what you can do to someone who shouldn't know, for the thrill of their reaction. |
| **Lying for Sport** | Feed someone an elaborate lie for no gain but the game, and keep building on it until getting caught would cost you. |
| **Making a Scene** | Cause a deliberate public spectacle, such as a screaming row, a tantrum, or a performance, that everyone in the room will remember. |
| **Malicious Gossip** | Spread a damaging story about someone, true or not, through people who could trace it back to you. |

## Secrets and Sacrifice

*The quieter ones, close to the heart.*

| Vice | What fulfills it |
|---|---|
| **Haunting Your Old Life** | Return to a person or place from before you changed and linger close enough to be seen, recognized, or drawn back in. |
| **Taking the Fall** | Claim the blame for something you didn't do and accept whatever punishment comes with it. |
| **Unwanted Confessions** | Burden someone who didn't ask with a true secret that damages you, leaving them holding something they could use against you. |
