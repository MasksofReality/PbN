Garou use the standard creation spread with nothing added and nothing taken away. There is no special Garou version of this table.

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3 across Physical, Social and Mental, with the priority set by your **Patron court** |
| Abilities | 13 Talents, 9 Skills, 5 Knowledges, from the permitted V20 list |
| Spirit Arts | Your Lineage path, plus **one of your other two Aligned paths** (Tribe or Patron, your choice). One starts at 1 dot, the other at 2. |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control and Grit, minimum 1 each |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots, and your tribe wants one of them spent somewhere specific |
| Freebie points | 21. Attribute 5, Ability 2, Willpower 2, Psyche 2, Background 1, Merit 1 per point, and **one Art dot at 11, no more than one** |
| Rite | One **Minor Rite**, chosen with your Mentor. A Rite is not an Art. |
| Rank | Rank 1, Cliath, however old your character is. No lost-cub applications. Wildborn finish their Rite of Passage before play starts. |
| Renown | 0 in Glory, Honor and Wisdom. Standing, not dice. |

A few numbers fall out of the rest of your sheet instead of being bought:

* **Gnosis is your highest Art rating**, so you start at Gnosis 2. You never buy it. It climbs on its own as your Arts do.
* **Your Gnosis pool is Gnosis plus 5**, so 7 to start.
* **Stability is Conscience plus Self-Control**, since your Eldritch is 0 at creation.
* **Health** is seven levels on a single track.

**Rolling a Spirit Art** is Art rating plus Gnosis, difficulty 6, with ones cancelling. Attributes never contribute. No Tribal Gift, Merit, Stigma, fetish, totem, or form bonus adds dice to an Art roll, ever. Anything that helps an Art helps from the difficulty side, or it does not exist. Wound penalties do apply.

## Where to Put Your 21 Freebies

**Willpower** is your universal defence. It is what you roll against Attrition every scene you spend at 0 Gnosis, it is half of Pushing, and it is what most Tribal Costs will ask you to spend. Take it to 5 if you have the points.

**Grit** matters more for us than for most. You have a war form that terrifies people and a bane material that scars you permanently, so you will spend your career on both sides of fear rolls. Grit caps at 5 where Willpower caps at 10, and ten dice is the best fear defence anyone will ever hold. Yours starts at one.

**Self-Control** deserves a look before you finalise Psyche. Four of the six tribes will ask you for a Self-Control roll on an emotional trigger your Storyteller controls, so read your Tribal Cost first and buy accordingly.

## Abilities

There is no Primal-Urge, Rituals, Security, or Occult in this system. If you were reaching for one of those, here is where it went:

| You mean | Buy |
|---|---|
| Instinct, reading a scene, sensing wrongness | **Awareness** |
| Wilderness competence, tracking, weather, terrain | **Survival** |
| Handling animals, wolf-to-wolf social work | **Animal Ken** |
| Performing a Rite | **Intelligence + Arcane**, a mundane roll |
| Spirit lore, taxonomies, the Litany as a text | **Arcane** |
| Glyphcode, paratech, salvage | **Technology**, a Knowledge |
| Lockpicking, bypassing physical security | **Larceny** |

Eldritch is closed to everyone but Sorcerers at creation, and only staff move it afterwards. Arcane cannot start above 3.

## Backgrounds

Garou may take Allies, Ancestors, Contacts, Fetish, Mentor, Resources, Rites, Totem, and Waystone.

Fetish, Totem, Rites, and Waystone are story-gated in both directions and staff price them individually. No fetish or totem boon adds dice to an Art roll. They work from the difficulty side, or they do something an Art roll never does.

## Two Worked Builds

Both of these come out to exactly 7/5/3, 13/9/5, five Backgrounds, and 21 freebies. Copy one outright if you want to get into play quickly, or take them apart to see where the points go.

**Ahroun, Get of Fenris, Cityborn.** Attributes: Str 4, Dex 3, Sta 3. Cha 3, Man 2, App 3. Per 2, Int 2, Wits 2. Arts: Nerigal's Path 2, Pact of Stone 1. Gnosis 2, pool 7. Talents (13): Brawl 3, Alertness 3, Athletics 2, Intimidation 2, Awareness 2, Leadership 1. Skills (9): Melee 3, Firearms 2, Survival 2, Drive 1, Stealth 1. Knowledges (5): Arcane 2, Investigation 1, Politics 1, Medicine 1. Backgrounds (5): Allies 2, Kinfolk 1, Contacts 1, Mentor 1. Freebies (21): Strength to 5 (5), Willpower to 5 (4), Grit +1 (2), Self-Control +1 (2), Brawl to 4 (2), Steady Under the Moon (2), Pactbound (2), Allies to 3 (1), Kinfolk to 2 (1).

**Theurge, Silent Strider, Traveler.** Attributes: Per 4, Int 3, Wits 3. Cha 3, Man 2, App 3. Str 2, Dex 2, Sta 2. Arts: Lu-Bat's Path 2, Pact of Wind 1. Gnosis 2, pool 7. Talents (13): Awareness 3, Alertness 3, Empathy 2, Expression 2, Athletics 2, Streetwise 1. Skills (9): Survival 3, Stealth 2, Etiquette 2, Animal Ken 1, Drive 1. Knowledges (5): Arcane 3, Academics 1, Investigation 1. Backgrounds (5): Waystone 1, Contacts 2, Ancestors 1, Mentor 1. Freebies (21): Perception to 5 (5), Willpower to 5 (4), Grit +1 (2), Arcane to 4 (2), Awareness to 4 (2), Pactbound (2), Waystone-Keeper (2), Contacts to 3 (1), Ancestors to 2 (1).

## Everything on One Page

| | |
|---|---|
| Resource trait | Gnosis. One pool. There is no Rage. |
| At creation | Gnosis 2, pool 7. Willpower 3. One Aligned path at 2, one Pact path at 1. |
| Four paths, which is the cap | Lineage (Aligned), Tribe (Aligned), Patron (Aligned), one Pact (Nonaligned) |
| Art roll | Art + Gnosis, difficulty 6, bands of 1, then 2–3, then 4+ |
| Shifting | Free, no roll, one change per round |
| Crinos | A fear effect defended on Grit, and a registration incident |
| Regeneration | Bashing 1 per round. Lethal 1 per minute of rest or 1 Gnosis. Aggravated at the mortal rate and nothing speeds it up. |
| Bane | Obsidian, fire, and your court's material. Aggravated, unsoakable, permanent scars. |
| Frenzy | Attrition at 0 Gnosis, or a failed Self-Control roll from a Tribal Cost or bane-shock |
| Harano | Stability. A number on your sheet, not a mood. |
| Beyond the sheet | Renown, Rank, Rites, fetishes, totems. Social and story-gated, never dice on an Art roll. |
