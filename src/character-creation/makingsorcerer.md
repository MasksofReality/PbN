| Stigmata | One, rolled or chosen |

| Freebie Points | 21, at the standard creation costs |

<div class="callout callout--rare">
<strong>Read this before you spend your freebies, because it decides how your first month plays.</strong> Seven Psyche dots with a minimum of 1 in Grit caps Conscience + Self-Control at 6, so a Sorcerer who spends nothing on Psyche opens at permanent Stability <strong>5</strong>. That is inside the <strong>Slipping</strong> band from your first scene, with the once-per-scene Self-Control roll and the one-Willpower-override limit already live. It is legal and it makes a real character. It is not a default. Psyche traits cost 2 freebies each, so 4 points buys you out to 7 (<strong>Fraying</strong>) and 6 points to 8 (<strong>Composed</strong>), and most concepts should spend it. That freebie tax is the Sorcerer's true creation cost, nobody else in the setting pays it, and you should hear the number when you apply rather than discover it in play.
</div>

## Questions to Answer in Your Application

- Where did you study as a Sorcerer, and who administered your Rite?
- What did the Mechanism take?
- Which tradition do you work in, and who taught you the notation? Do they still speak to you?
- Who holds your licence, and what do they expect for it?

## Stigmata

Roll or choose one at creation, with staff approval. All Stigmata are permanent. They cannot be healed, regenerated, replaced with mundane prosthetics, or removed. The Mechanism's part does not come out.

Stigmata bonuses and penalties apply to mundane rolls and to contested defences, and nowhere else. No Stigmata ever adds dice to an Art activation. The Hand improves the craft of inscription; it never improves the trigger.

Nobody can fake being a sorcerer, and no sorcerer can pass as a mortal. The Stigmata is your receipt. The Basilicas trust the profession because every practitioner is visibly someone who already paid, and the old Psychic trick of masking as a hedge-magician died the same way. Metahumans have no Stigmata, and everybody knows to look.

| d10 | Stigmata | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Hand** | A hand or forearm replaced by articulated brass, silver pathway and seated leystone. +1 die on inscription, etching, and fine glyphwork. −1 die on anything needing a light touch on living flesh: surgery, pickpocketing, tenderness. |
| 2 | **The Eye** | One or both eyes replaced by a lens assembly. Mana Sight cannot be closed even by shutting your eyes. +1 die on any roll Mana Sight already improves (total +2 on those rolls). Any aetheric flare — wyrdstorm lightning, a capstone working, a hearthstone surge — blinds you for a turn. |
| 3 | **The Jaw** | Lower jaw or voicebox replaced. Speech is a flat mechanical drone. −1 die on Social rolls with strangers. +2 dice to defend against any Art whose entry states it is delivered by voice or spoken command. |
| 4 | **The Leg** | Leg and hip replaced. You never tire from walking and ignore fatigue on a march. −1 die on Dexterity rolls involving footwork; you cannot really run. |
| 5 | **The Heart** | Heart replaced by a ticking assembly. Immune to shock; you need no more than two hours' sleep. Audible at ten feet in a quiet room, and anything that hunts by mana finds you without rolling. |
| 6 | **The Spine** | Vertebrae replaced by a segmented column. +1 soak against bashing. Nothing supernatural heals you — no Art, ritual, Vitalist, alchemical preparation or Druidic Working, of any splat or School. You heal at the ordinary mundane rate, always. |
| 7 | **The Lung** | One lung replaced by a bellows. Immune to airborne toxin and smoke; you cannot hold your breath, and you will always have slightly visible breath upon exhale, in some cases in abnormal colors like purple or orange. |
| 8 | **The Rift to Somewhere** | You have a hole somewhere on your body which is always absolutely black, and anything pressed into it never returns. If you lean close to it, you can hear distant clockwork. The older a sorcerer with the stigmata is, the larger it gets, and no one over 50 exists with it because it simply eats them. |
| 9 | **The Seam** | A large line of glyph-scar across the body where something opened you up, rummaged around, then closed you back up. Cosmetically unmistakable. +1 die on any roll to prove your credentials to another practitioner. It cannot be concealed to scans and glows when near paratech even through light clothing. |
| 10 | **Candidate's Choice** | Propose one. Staff will hold it to the pattern above: one real benefit, one real cost, permanently visible to someone. |
