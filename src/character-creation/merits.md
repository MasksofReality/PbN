Merits are the qualities that set your character apart. Here is how they work:

- **At creation,** Merits cost 1 freebie per point.
- **After creation,** the only Merits you can purchase are those in the [Approvals and Post Character Creation Merits](#approvals-and-post-character-creation-merits) section. Each one is marked with its XP worth.
- **Once play begins,** Merits are story-gated and are not refunded if you lose them. When a Merit becomes untrue, staff may permit reassignment rather than credit.
- **No Flaws.** There are none in this system, at creation or ever. There are Stigmata in all splats, and you never pay for those.

Three rules shape everything below:

- **No Merit carries a cost.** A paid advantage with a paid drawback is a Flaw, and this system has none. If a Merit reads as a trade, it is written wrong.
- **No Merit adds dice to an Art roll.** Where a Merit touches an Art at all, it does so from the difficulty side, or it applies to a contested defence, which, as core is explicit, is not an Art roll. Everything else on this list is mundane.
- **Merits are not Backgrounds.** Resources, Contacts, Allies, Influence, Status, Fame and Alternate Identity are bought as [Backgrounds](/character-creation/backgrounds/), are story-gated in both directions, and are credited back when plot takes them. A Merit describes you. Several Merits below sit close to that line because a character's advantages are so often arrangements rather than qualities; where one is lost to plot rather than choice, staff should lean toward reassignment.

<h2 id="approvals-and-post-character-creation-merits">Approvals and Post Character Creation Merits</h2>

These are the only Merits that can be purchased after Character Creation, and every cost here is listed in XP. Weapon Merits can be purchased multiple times, once for each weapon you want access to. Augmentations and Specializations apply to a single weapon you already have through a Weapon Type Merit.

### Approvals

| Name | Cost | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Destiny** | 5 XP | You have a destiny (pre-chosen by the Storytellers) that becomes clearer to you and your character as your story continues, granting resolve against setbacks related to it. **Requires Storyteller Approval.** *You might not like your destiny.* |
| **True Love** | 5 XP | A true love gives you the strength to persevere; you gain automatic successes on Willpower rolls made in their defense. Requires another player (or more, you do you) on the other end of your romance, and all parties must take it at the same time. Note that if your true love dies, this Merit can have unfortunate side effects. If the romance breaks, the Merit becomes Love's Ashes (**needs Storyteller approval** to do so). |
| **Love's Ashes** | 5 XP | True hatred is born out of a love that has fallen. Your pain is only mended by seeing the one you once loved suffer the most; you gain a +3 on all attack rolls, and your difficulty to be harmed by them will decrease by 3. The pain goes both ways. Requires another player (or more; you do you) on the other end of this hate train, and all parties must take it at the same time. If the fire of love is rekindled, the Merit becomes True Love (**needs Storyteller approval** to do so). |

### Weapon Customization

| Name | Cost | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Weapon Augmentation** | 3 XP | Fit one of your weapons with one magic or tech based augmentation, such as a laser scope or a rune carved into the blade. One augmentation per purchase, and the Merit can be purchased multiple times. No weapon can carry more than two augmentations at a time. |
| **Weapon Specialization** | 5 XP | Define what one of your weapons is made of, coated with, or loaded with. One specialization per purchase, and the Merit can be purchased multiple times. Each weapon can hold **one internal** specialization (what the weapon is made of) and **one external** specialization (a coating, or the ammo it uses). You must have a specialization to use a rare type of metal, or any type of metal that can harm a supernatural. |
