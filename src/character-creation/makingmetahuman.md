Creation follows the core creation standards with no deviation. Everything below sits on top of them.

| Trait Category | Allocation / Standard |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3 across Physical, Social, Mental, priority set by your Inheritance |
| Abilities | 13 Talents / 9 Skills / 5 Knowledges (caps at 5) |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control, and Grit (minimum 1 each) |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Arts | One Inheritance Expression at 1 (Affinity) and one Inheritance Expression at 2 (Aligned) |
| Psi (Resource Trait) | 2 at creation (derived from your highest Art rating) |
| Essence Pool | 7 at creation (Psi + 5) |
| Vitality | 0 (consumed at Manifestation) |
| Eldritch | 0 (never available at creation; gained only through play) |
| Stability | Conscience + Self-Control (permanent pool; ranges 4–8) |
| Instinct | Choose Fight, Flight, or Freeze (see [Psi, Essence & Backlash](/denizens/metahumans/psi-and-backlash/)) |
| Cathartic Vice | One specific, inconvenient, staff-approved Vice |
| Passing | Choose Unregistered, Registered, or Misregistered — see [the overview](/denizens/metahumans/) |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots (Contacts, Allies, and Mentor are standard; Resources is restricted if you are Unregistered) |
| Freebie Points | 21 |

## Using Your Arts

**Standard Art roll:** Art Rating + Psi, difficulty 6. Wound penalties apply to Art activation. No Stigmata, Merit, or splat trait may add dice to an Art roll.

**Contested defense:** the defender rolls permanent Willpower + highest Art, or Grit + highest Art against fear and compulsion effects. Mortals defend on permanent Willpower alone, or Grit alone. Mortals who are unaware or unresisting are uncontested.

**Standard costs:** 1-dot Arts are free. 2-dot costs 1 Essence, 3-dot costs 2 Essence, 4-dot costs 3 Essence, and 5-dot costs 5 Essence.

**Damaging Arts** deal 1 level of damage on a success, plus 1 more per net success beyond the first. Art damage is unsoaked unless the write-up specifies a physical object. If it comes in as a ranged stroke, the target may declare a Dodge (Dexterity + Athletics, difficulty 6).

**What you may hold:** a maximum of 4 Arts. Occult Arts are barred outright. Discordant Psychic Arts need a staff-approved bloodline discovery in play and advance on the Discordant track (39 XP to 5). Common Arts advance on the Aligned track (20 XP to 5).
