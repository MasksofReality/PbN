Backgrounds are the people, resources, and special gifts your character has picked up along the way, like a trusted ally, a healthy bank account, or a one-of-a-kind magical item. They add depth to your story and give you more to roleplay. Here is everything you need to know.

## Backgrounds Overview

**Name your type.** On your Character Sheet, each Background is listed with a set of brackets () behind it. When you choose a Background, you MUST name its type inside the brackets. For example: Allies for 1 Dot (Dock Worker).

**More dots, more detail.** The more Dots you buy, the more in-depth your description needs to be.

**Sim NPCs.** Sim NPCs can only be used as a Background at 3 Dots or higher.

**PCs do not count.** Other player characters (PCs) cannot be taken as Backgrounds.

**Storyteller approval.** Artifact (Lilum), Fetish (Garou), Treasure (Dreaming), and Wonder (Mortals) are all Storyteller Approval Needed Backgrounds.

**Buying more later.** After Character Creation, every Dot costs 3 XP.

***Please note that Backgrounds must be justified in your backstory; the more dots you request, the higher the bar of proof will become.***

## General Backgrounds

| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| **Allies** | Loyal associates who help you when called on, without needing to be coerced or paid. |
| **Alternate Identity** | A separate, fully documented identity you maintain apart from your true one. How convincing it is scales on its dots. |
| **Certification** | Official credentials granting access and authority within a bureaucratic or scientific structure. |
| **Contacts** | A network of people who trade favors and information, usually for something in return. |
| **Cult** | A body of followers devoted to you or your cause. Kindred call this their *Herd* and rely on them for safe and legal blood. |
| **Fame** | How widely and favorably you're recognized in wider society. |
| **Mentor** | An experienced NPC guide who offers advice, aid, or protection. Once per month, you can request aid via ST ticket; note it might not be what you wanted. |
| **Patron** | An NPC benefactor with their own agenda who backs you. Once per month, you can request aid via ST ticket; it will come with strings. |
| **Requisitions** | Your ability to draw equipment and resources through an organization's official channels. |
| **Resources** | The wealth, property, and material assets you can readily draw on. |
| **Status** | Your standing and rank within a specific group. Must be bought per social circle/organization. |

## Supernatural Backgrounds

| Name | Cost | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Artifact (Lilum)** | 1–5 dots | A magical item you own outright; its power scales with your rating. **This is a Storyteller Approval Needed Background.** |
| **Augmentation** | 1–5 dots | A paratech augmentation grafted into you. Limbs that mimic normal human capacity do not need this background. |
| **Blessed** | 1–5 dots | A supernatural boon granted to you by an outside power. |
| **Companion / Familiar** | 1–5 dots | A bonded spirit that assists you, often at the cost of magical energy or blood. |

| **Spirit Network** | 1–5 dots | A web of Spiritual informants who feed you information. Once per plot, you can request information via ST ticket. |
