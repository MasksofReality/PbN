Every character has two sides: the person they really are, and the person they show the world. At the top of your character sheet, you'll pick one of each. It's one of the most fun choices in character creation.

### The Two Sides of Your Character

| | What it is | Does it affect Willpower? |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Your true self, the core that drives you when no one is watching | **Yes.** Acting in line with your Nature helps you regain Willpower |
| **Demeanor** | The face you show the world, your social mask | **No.** It can be completely different from your Nature |

**Willpower** is the stat you use to withstand a supernatural or mental attack. It measures how well your character stands against one. You lose a point of Willpower when you fail such a challenge, and acting in line with your Nature is how you win it back.

***Good to know:*** *Every archetype below can be used as either a Nature or a Demeanor. Only your Nature affects Willpower.*

### Choosing Yours

1. **Start with your Nature.** Ask yourself: what does my character truly want, and what makes them feel most alive? Each archetype lists what helps your character regain Willpower, so pick one you'll enjoy acting out.
2. **Then pick a Demeanor.** How does your character want the world to see them? It can match your Nature for a straightforward character, or it can clash for something more interesting.
3. **Not sure?** Ask a Mentor on Discord or in-world. They'd love to help.

***Example:*** *A character with the Nature **Caregiver** and the Demeanor **Bravo** is intimidating and gruff on the outside, but fiercely protective underneath.*

<h2 id="protectors-and-helpers">Protectors and Helpers</h2>

*Looking after other people.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Benefactor** | Shows up with exactly the help you need, right when you need it. | Regain Willpower when you provide help that someone else desperately needs. |
| **Caregiver** | Heals, feeds, and comforts, and feels most useful when someone is leaning on them. | Regain Willpower whenever you successfully protect or nurture someone else. |
| **Caretaker** | Keeps a whole community safe and takes responsibility when things go wrong. | Regain Willpower when you avert disaster for those in your care. |
| **Defender** | Sworn to one person, place, or thing, and will stand in front of it against anything. | Regain Willpower when you successfully defend your charge. |
| **Guardian** | A shield for the helpless, standing between the weak and whatever threatens them. | Regain Willpower when your actions directly save a weaker group or character from assured destruction. |
| **Guru** | A spiritual guide whose wisdom leads others, and sometimes themselves, toward enlightenment. | Regain Willpower when your spiritual guidance moves another to enlightened action, or when you achieve personal epiphany. |
| **Meddler** | Can't resist stepping into other people's problems, asked or not. | Regain Willpower when your interference turns out to help, even over objections. |
| **Mentor** | Measures success by how far a student goes beyond what they could do before. | Regain Willpower when your guidance helps your student accomplish something beyond their previous reach. |
| **Pedagogue** | A natural teacher for anyone who will listen, driven to pass knowledge on. | Regain Willpower whenever someone benefits from the wisdom you shared with them. |

<h2 id="leaders-and-followers">Leaders and Followers</h2>

*Taking charge, backing someone up, or taking orders.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpha** | Leads by presence and strength, and expects the pack to follow. | Regain Willpower when you achieve control over a group or force a rival to submit to your authority. |
| **Autocrat** | Wants the throne itself: control is the prize, not the tool. | Regain Willpower when you achieve control over a group or organization. |
| **Beta** | The trusted second who advises the leader and keeps the group working. | Regain Willpower when your counsel benefits the group; lose Willpower if the leader you support proves incompetent. |
| **Conformist** | Finds belonging in fitting in and strengthening what the group is trying to do. | Regain Willpower whenever the group achieves a goal due to your support. |
| **Director** | Works behind the scenes, steering people toward success without taking the spotlight. | Regain Willpower when you influence or aid a group or individual in completing a difficult task. |
| **Follower** | Gives loyalty completely, to a person or cause greater than themselves. | Regain Willpower every time you demonstrate absolute loyalty under trying circumstances. |
| **Omega** | Takes the lowest place in the group and waits quietly for a chance to earn respect. | Regain Willpower when you accomplish something that earns real recognition. |
| **Soldier** | Lives by orders and takes pride in finishing the mission. | Regain Willpower when you achieve your orders' objectives, especially difficult ones. |
| **Visionary** | Paints a future so compelling that others choose to chase it. | Regain Willpower each time you convince others to have faith in your dreams and follow the course of action your vision dictates. |

<h2 id="champions-of-a-cause">Champions of a Cause</h2>

*Fighting for something bigger than yourself.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Activist** | Fights injustice head-on, from confronting abuse to exposing conspiracies. | Regain Willpower when you successfully confront abuse, right wrongs, or reveal and bring down a conspiracy. |
| **Adherent** | Stays faithful to one cause through every hardship. | Regain Willpower when you stay loyal to your cause despite hardship. |
| **Celebrant** | Burns with one all-consuming passion and wants everyone else to feel it too. | Regain Willpower when you pursue your cause or convert another to your passion; lose Willpower when denied it. |
| **Crusader** | Sets a higher goal before the story begins and holds to it even when it hurts. | Regain Willpower when you accomplish a great deed in the name of a higher goal, defined before play and followed even when it hurts. |
| **Fanatic** | So devoted to a cause that every task is measured by whether it serves it. | Regain Willpower whenever you accomplish a task that directly relates to your cause. |
| **Idealist** | Lives by a set of principles and works patiently to make them real. | Regain Willpower any time an action in pursuit of your ideals furthers your goals and brings them closer to fruition. |
| **Prophet** | Tells powerful people the truths they don't want to hear, hoping to spark real change. | Regain Willpower when you speak truth to power and inspire successful change. |
| **Vigilante** | Wages a one-person war against an organization far too big to beat. | Regain Willpower when you inflict major suffering on a target organization that consumes your entire life, one large enough to potentially end it. |
| **Zealot** | Holds beliefs so firmly that every success feels like proof they were right. | Regain Willpower whenever your deeply held beliefs are proved right through your behavior. |

<h2 id="thinkers-and-seekers">Thinkers and Seekers</h2>

*Curious minds and clever solutions.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Adjudicator** | Steps in to sort out a problem and delivers a quick, workable fix. | Regain Willpower when your proposed solution produces immediate results. |
| **Dabbler** | Chases one enthusiasm after another, dropping each old one completely. | Regain Willpower whenever you find a new enthusiasm and drop your old one completely. |
| **Explorer** | Can't rest until they've found what no one else has seen. | Regain Willpower every time you discover something previously unknown. |
| **Futurist** | Embraces the next big idea before anyone else, and trusts progress over tradition. | Regain Willpower when you're the first to embrace a new, progressive idea or invention. |
| **Innovator** | Turns sparks of inspiration into real, useful breakthroughs. | Regain Willpower when your inspiration leads to a helpful new breakthrough. |
| **Judge** | Hears every side, weighs the evidence, and delivers a fair verdict. | Regain Willpower whenever you correctly solve a problem by weighing evidence, or when your argument unites dissenting parties. |
| **Mediator** | Finds the middle ground between opposing sides and gets them to meet in it. | Regain Willpower every time you convince others to compromise on a high-stakes disagreement. |
| **Pragmatist** | Keeps their eyes on the goal and has no patience for sentiment. | Regain Willpower every time you succeed by putting the goal first. |
| **Quastor** | A seeker devoted to answering life's deeper questions. | Regain Willpower when you distill an experience into a lasting insight or guiding principle. |
| **Scientist** | Solves problems with logic, method, and careful observation. | Regain Willpower any time a logical, systematic approach solves a problem, or gathered information proves useful later. |

<h2 id="builders-and-dealmakers">Builders and Dealmakers</h2>

*Creating things and making things happen.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Architect** | Builds things meant to outlast them: institutions, structures, and legacies. | Regain Willpower whenever you establish something of importance or lasting value. |
| **Artist** | Pours truth and feeling into creative work that moves an audience. | Regain Willpower whenever you create a work of art that communicates sublime truth to your audience. |
| **Barterer** | Treats every deal as an art form and loves landing something valuable at the right price. | Regain Willpower whenever you gain a significant commodity (a favor, fetish, or something of genuine power) at a good price. |
| **Capitalist** | Sees profit in everything and knows how to close a sale. | Regain Willpower whenever you make a successful "sale" of any commodity (physical, informational, or otherwise). |
| **Perfectionist** | Holds every result to a flawless standard and leads by example. | Regain Willpower when you accomplish a goal without demonstrable flaw, and when you motivate others by your shining example. |
| **Tycoon** | Plays the long game with big, ambitious schemes that can change everything. | Regain Willpower when a long-shot plan pays off grandly in influence, love, or achievement, not merely money. |

*A fetish is a specific type of magic item. See the [Backgrounds page](/character-creation/backgrounds/) for details.*

<h2 id="steady-and-principled">Steady and Principled</h2>

*Holding firm, whatever comes.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Fatalist** | Believes everything is already written, and loves sharing how they read the signs. | Regain Willpower when you share your personal interpretation of fate with someone else. |
| **Loner** | Prefers to go it alone, but still does it for the good of the group. | Regain Willpower when you accomplish something by yourself that still benefits the group. |
| **Machine** | Drives past the limits of the body through relentless discipline. | Regain Willpower when you push through and exceed your body's normal limits. |
| **Optimist** | Finds the silver lining in every disaster and champions it out loud. | Regain Willpower every time you discover and champion the bright side of an otherwise grim event. |
| **Paragon** | Says what they mean, plays it straight, and wins on honesty. | Regain Willpower when your straightforward approach succeeds where deception would not. |
| **Stoic** | Stays calm through loss and setbacks, no matter how hard they hit. | Regain Willpower when you weather a serious setback or loss without giving in to strong emotion. |
| **Survivor** | Refuses to go down, and helps others refuse too. | Regain Willpower whenever you survive a threatening situation through tenacity, or when your counsel causes someone else to persist in spite of opposition. |
| **Traditionalist** | Trusts the old ways because they have already been proven. | Regain Willpower when proven ways turn out best, or when adhering to previous positions proves wise. |
| **Wanderer** | Never stays long, and leaves every place with its business finished. | Regain Willpower when you finish your purpose in a place and move on, leaving no loose ends behind. |

<h2 id="charmers-and-performers">Charmers and Performers</h2>

*Winning people over, one way or another.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Bon Vivant** | Savors every pleasure life offers, loudly and without shame. | Regain Willpower whenever you truly enjoy yourself and can fully express your exultation. |
| **Chameleon** | Becomes whoever the moment needs, and uses it to get ahead. | Regain Willpower whenever you fool someone into thinking you're someone else for your own benefit. |
| **Conniver** | Gets what they want by talking people into it, or into trouble. | Regain Willpower whenever you trick someone into doing something for you or against their own interests. |
| **Enigma** | Does baffling things that look like mistakes until the results come in. | Regain Willpower whenever someone is baffled by your actions that later turn out to be fruitful. |
| **Gallant** | Dazzles with style, charm, and flair. | Regain Willpower whenever you successfully impress another person. |
| **Masquerader** | Blends in so well that outsiders never suspect there's more to them. | Regain Willpower when you resolve a situation without anyone realizing you're more than you appear. |
| **Recognition Seeker** | Wants the world to see who they really are, not just the surface. | Regain Willpower the first time someone recognizes you as more than ordinary. |
| **Romantic** | Sees beauty in the ruins and chases love and meaning amid the wreckage. | Regain Willpower when you throw yourself into a gloriously ruinous affair, or reveal life's howling beauty to a hesitant soul. |
| **Trickster** | Uses humor to lift everyone's spirits, especially when they're hurting. | Regain Willpower any time you manage to lift others' spirits, especially while assuaging your own pain. |

<h2 id="thrill-seekers-and-competitors">Thrill-Seekers and Competitors</h2>

*Chasing danger, victory, and the rush.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Bravo** | Gets their way by looming over people, with force or with threats. | Regain Willpower when you achieve your agenda through brutishness or intimidation, physical or social. |
| **Competitor** | Has to win, and measures themselves against every rival. | Regain Willpower whenever you succeed at a dramatic test or challenge. |
| **Eye of the Storm** | Trouble follows them everywhere, and they're calm at the center of it. | Regain Willpower whenever chaos or riot erupts around you. |
| **Gambler** | Pushes every chip to the center of the table, especially when the odds are terrible. | Regain Willpower every time you face unwinnable odds and somehow emerge victorious. |
| **Hunter** | Stalks a chosen rival or quarry with patience and cunning. | Regain Willpower when you outwit or defeat a rival or quarry through patience and cunning. |
| **Thrill-Seeker** | Courts danger on purpose, because the rush is the point. | Regain Willpower any time you succeed at a dangerous task you deliberately undertook. |

<h2 id="rebels-and-rule-breakers">Rebels and Rule-Breakers</h2>

*Pushing back against the way things are.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Anarchist** | Rejects imposed rules and builds their own order from scratch. | Regain Willpower whenever you reject unnecessary rules or social norms and succeed because of it. |
| **Avant-Garde** | Chases the new and the shocking, and pushes convention to its breaking point. | Regain Willpower every time you experience something new and controversial. |
| **Barbarian** | Distrusts civilization and trusts older, rougher, more primal ways. | Regain Willpower when your unconventional methods prove superior to civilized ones. |
| **Deviant** | Refuses to play by social expectations, and flouts them openly. | Regain Willpower any time you flout social mores without retribution. |
| **Devil's Advocate** | Challenges every plan and authority to find the crack in it. | Regain Willpower when your questioning exposes a real flaw in a plan or structure. |
| **Heretic** | Attacks beliefs everyone takes for granted and tries to change minds. | Regain Willpower whenever you challenge a commonly held conviction and manage to change people's minds. |
| **Rebel** | Stands against authority and the status quo, and strikes at their chosen opposition. | Regain Willpower whenever your actions adversely affect your chosen opposition. |
| **Rogue** | Looks out for number one, and sometimes for their own crew at others' expense. | Regain Willpower when self-centered disposition leads to profit, or when benefiting your group at another group's expense. |

<h2 id="wounded-and-wavering">Wounded and Wavering</h2>

*Carrying something heavy.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Addict** | Fixated on one passion or vice, and chases it above all else. | Regain Willpower when you fully indulge your chosen passion. |
| **Child** | Never fully grew up, and looks to others to keep them safe. | Regain Willpower when you get someone to look after you or your problems with no benefit to themselves. |
| **Coward** | Hides behind a mask for fear of being rejected as they truly are. | Regain Willpower when you reveal something genuine about yourself without being rejected for it. |
| **Curmudgeon** | Expects the worst from people and takes grim satisfaction in being right. | Regain Willpower when someone does something specific and negative, just as you predicted aloud. |
| **Dependent** | Has lost control to an indulgence or attachment, and it rules their choices. | Regain Willpower when you indulge your chosen dependency to excess. |
| **Martyr** | Takes on suffering as a sacrifice, trusting that the pain will serve their purpose. | Regain one Willpower when your suffering leads to tangible gain; two when you experience pain in a truly unique way. |
| **Melancholic** | Dwells in grief for what was lost, and their sorrow stirs others to question their path. | Regain Willpower when your sorrow leads someone else to question their own path. |
| **Outsider** | Stands apart from the group on purpose, and learns who they are from what others decide. | Regain Willpower when decisions made by others teach you something about yourself. |
| **Penitent** | Carries guilt for past wrongs and seeks to earn redemption. | Regain Willpower whenever you feel you have achieved absolution for a given grievance (redemption proportional to the transgression). |

<h2 id="dark-natures">Dark Natures</h2>

*The harshest archetypes, and the hardest to play.*

***Heads up:*** *These are great for villains and deeply complicated characters, but they're intense. Check in with your scene partners before playing them out, and keep real-life comfort first.*

| Archetype | In a sentence | How you regain Willpower |
|---|---|---|
| **Creepshow** | Delights in unsettling people, and measures success by the shudders. | Regain Willpower when your actions genuinely shock or disgust someone. |
| **Maniac** | Driven by inner voices that demand action, whatever the cost. | Regain Willpower when you carry out what an inner compulsion demands, even against your own interests. |
| **Masochist** | Enjoys pain for the pleasure and release it brings them. | Regain one Willpower when suffering leads to tangible gain; two when experiencing pain in a truly unique way. |
| **Monster** | Feeds on one defined atrocity, and indulges it to feel strong. | Regain Willpower by indulging a specific atrocity defined at character creation. |
| **Nihilist** | Believes in nothing, and tears things down, themselves included. | Regain Willpower whenever you engage in self-destructive behavior. |
| **Sadist** | Takes pleasure in hurting others, for no reason but enjoyment. | Regain Willpower when you inflict pain for no reason other than your own pleasure. |
| **Sociopath** | Feels nothing for other people's suffering or lives. | Regain Willpower when you are the greatest contributor to a body count after a conflict. |
