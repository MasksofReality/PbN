# **Making a Lilum**

Lilum use the universal creation standard, unmodified. Nothing on this list is a bloodline's to change.

| Element | Standard |
| :---- | :---- |
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3 across Physical, Social, Mental, with priority set by your bloodline |
| Abilities | 13 Talents / 9 Skills / 5 Knowledges, from the permitted V20 list |
| Blood Arts | Two of your bloodline's three Affinity Arts: one at **2**, one at **1**. The Art taken at 2 is your **designated Affinity Art** |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control, Grit (minimum 1 each) |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots |
| Freebie points | 21\. Attribute 5 · Ability 2 · Willpower 2 · Psyche 2 · Background 1 · Merit 1 per point · Art dot 11, maximum one |
| Nature and Demeanour | Both mandatory |
| Cathartic Vice | One, mandatory, staff-approved |

Attributes and Abilities cap at 5\. Psyche caps at 5\. Willpower caps at 10\. No character holds more than four Arts in total. Restricted Arts are closed to you at creation under any circumstance, and freebies never buy them at any point.

Write your **Demeanour** sharply: once per cycle, when acting squarely within it, you may reroll one failed Social roll. Your **Nature** has no roll attached, and betraying it marks a Stability trigger.

### Your derived values

Your **Vitae rating** equals your highest Art rating, so you start at **Vitae 2**. You never buy it, and it rises on its own as your best Art does. Where an Art roll says "+ Vitae," it means this rating, not the pool.

Your **Vitae pool** is that rating plus 5, so **7** at creation.

**Permanent Stability \= Conscience \+ Self-Control − Eldritch.** Every Lilum begins with **Eldritch 0**, without exception. Nobody but a Sorcerer starts play with Eldritch, and the only registered exception in core is the Lich. An elder who walked through the Interstice and came out knowing something is an NPC or a very long campaign, not a character sheet.

Health is the standard seven levels on one track. You do not track Vitality. That pool belongs to mortals.

### Rolling an Art

Roll Art rating \+ Vitae rating, difficulty 6, ones cancelling. Attributes never contribute. No Promise, Merit, Stigma or bloodline trait ever adds dice to an Art roll: anything that helps an Art does so from the difficulty side, or it does not exist. Wound penalties do apply to Art rolls. The difficulty cap is 10\. Where accumulated modifiers push past it, the effect is impossible, not attempted and not paid for.

A **contested defence is not an Art roll**, and splat traits, Merits and Stigmata may add dice or shift difficulty there where they say so. Your contested defence is permanent Willpower \+ your highest Art, or **Grit \+ your highest Art against anything that is fear, horror, or a supernatural compulsion to flee or yield**.

### Spending your 21 freebies

An Art dot costs 11 of your 21\. The same dot costs 4 XP a month into play. That gap is an impatience tax, and it is a legitimate thing to pay if you mean to open as the Affinity-3 prodigy of your line. Otherwise it is a poor trade. Three better buys:

**Willpower** is the universal defence. You defend a contested Art on permanent Willpower \+ your highest Art, you roll it against Attrition every scene you spend at 0 Vitae, and it is half the price of Pushing. Take it to 5\.

**Grit** answers fear, and fear is the soft target in this system by design. You are a creature with a hard-coded terror trigger, so this one is not optional. Grit is what you roll against Rotschreck. It caps at 5 where Willpower caps at 10, which makes ten dice the best fear defence anyone in the setting will ever hold, and yours starts at one or two.

**Psyche** in general repays attention. Stability is Conscience \+ Self-Control, and the 7-dot Psyche budget caps you at 6 of it during creation. Freebies push you toward 8\. You will spend Stability faster than any mortal, because the rules charge you for the way you eat and for the way you use your Arts. It also decides, night to night, whether you can still sit at a table and pretend to eat.

## **Vitae**

Vitae is Essence. Every rule that governs Essence governs it unchanged. The word is a courtesy your line pays itself.

### What Vitae buys outside an Art

| Effect | Cost |
| :---- | :---- |
| Heal 1 level of bashing | **Free**, 1 level per post round |
| Heal 1 level of lethal | 2 Vitae, instant |
| Heal 1 level of aggravated | 5 Vitae plus one full cycle of unbroken daysleep, per level |
| Appear living: flushed skin, warmth, a pulse, breath | 1 Vitae, one scene |
| Rise from torpor | 5 Vitae |

The aggravated line is the only accelerant your line has, and it is deliberately expensive: core forbids any Art, ritual or regeneration from touching aggravated unless it names aggravated explicitly, and nothing else in this guide does. Do the arithmetic before you need it, because a Vitae 2 Lilum with a pool of 7 cannot close two aggravated levels without feeding first.

### Replenishment and the cycle

Feeding on living blood is your Replenishment. Away from a body (a filed donor's drawn cup, a slow night with a willing partner, whatever your fiction is), you gain 1 Vitae per two post rounds, roleplayed and visible. Latched onto a body, the escalation table below governs instead. At midnight server time you regain 5 Vitae, and your current Willpower refreshes fully.

That midnight refresh can fail. A Lilum shut in a cellar with nothing living to drink gets nothing at all. Captivity starves, and a captor who wants to keep milking you has to keep feeding you.

There is one exception, and it is narrow. **A Lilum at rest in their own prepared resting place**, in torpor or in daysleep, laid there with their body intact, recovers ambiently even where the ordinary presumption fails. It exempts them from nothing else: they can still be drained to 0 by someone else, and they still enter Attrition when they are. A cellar is not a prepared resting place. A coffin you were shoved into by someone else is not a prepared resting place.

Beneath all of that sits one floor, and it is the slowest clock in the system. **A Lilum receiving nothing from any source still accrues 1 Vitae per full lunar month.** Five months under a cellar floor is a resurrection rather than a retirement. Nobody should plan a recovery around it. It exists so that being shelved stays a story instead of a deletion.

### Pushing

You may pay 1 Willpower and 1 level of aggravated either to substitute for an Art's entire base Essence cost, or to buy a single die at 1:1. Buying dice at that price is almost never worth it. Substituting the cost outright is how a drained Lilum still acts at the moment it matters, and because the price is flat it is overwhelmingly a tool for capstone Arts. Where a mechanic surcharges an Art's cost, Pushing covers only the base; the surcharge is paid in Vitae as written.

## **What you gain, and what it costs**

### Strengths

**You do not age, sicken or scar.** Injury is Vitae and time rather than months of convalescence.

**Bashing is halved**, rounding down, before soak. This is a stated splat exception to §10's damage rules. Note the consequence honestly: a single level of ordinary bashing is halved to nothing. Fists are an inconvenience. Beatings are not. Sunlight is exempt from this and is covered below.

**You soak like anyone else,** on Stamina plus armour against bashing and lethal, difficulty 6\. Fortitude adds its rating on top of that, and at tier 2 it reaches aggravated. Art damage remains unsoaked regardless, unless the Art in question produces an ordinary physical effect.

**You have legal personhood.** A registered BRD holds the rights of any citizen: property, contract, standing before the Council, a complaint the Vigiles must investigate. Unsanctioned hunts are prosecuted, and rogue hunters tend to go missing. This is worth more than any Art on the list, and it is twenty years old.

**Foreign essence converts at full value.** A Garou is as nourishing as a docker, and core states plainly that there is no mechanical penalty for it. The deterrent is social, and it is stronger than any modifier. See Tether below, and remember that predation on a peer is an offence the whole community answers. *(The Ventrue Bane is a registered exception to this, and the only one.)*

**A prepared resting place recovers you** even when the world has decided you should starve.

### Costs

**Sunlight.** Direct sunlight does **1 bashing per round. It cannot be soaked and it is not halved.** It escalates: once bashing has filled your track, further exposure is taken as lethal, and once lethal has filled it, as aggravated, which past Incapacitated is Final Death. Indirect sunlight, through glass or under heavy overcast, does **1 bashing per round, soakable but likewise not halved**. Dawn starts a three-stage clock, and a Lilum caught in the open at sunrise is watching it run.

**Fire.** 1 aggravated per round while burning.

**What sleep reveals.** You are effectively dead through the eight hours of your daysleep, anatomically indistinguishable from a common corpse. You cannot wake during that time, and no Merit, Art or ally changes that. Whatever is done to you in those hours is done to a helpless target, including by Arts. While asleep, your soul becomes dormant within your corpse and turns inward, having visions of its past life. Lilum with low Stability tend to see their traumas, while those in better mental health tend to see things they actually want to remember.

**Rotschreck.** Fire in your vicinity may trigger terror. Roll Grit at difficulty 6, with staff adding \+1 or \+2 for engulfment or a closing wall of flame, or flee uncontrollably for the scene. Mortal-style bravery is not available to you. Grit is the only trait that answers here, and no Promise adds dice to it.

**Attrition and frenzy.** You cannot activate an Art with a cost at 0 Vitae; your free tier-1 Arts still function. At the end of every scene you remain there, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6\. On a failure you frenzy and attack anyone within reach to feed, for the rest of the scene. On a botch, control does not return until the next midnight reset. At Stability 2–3, the Unmoored state, frenzy can trigger at any Vitae level at all.

**How you feed matters.** There is no feeding roll. One point is one round, and alone in a cycle it does no damage whatsoever. Each rate holds for as many rounds as its own value: 1 for one round, 2 for two, 3 for three, 4 for four. Breaking contact resets the rate to the first row; the victim's cycle tally never resets until midnight. One lethal lands on the victim per 2 points taken, or per 3 if they are freely willing. An average mortal holds Stamina \+ 7 and is empty in the fifth round, carrying 5 lethal and with their nightly recovery cut to their unmarked Health boxes.

A resisting victim is a mundane problem. Chase, grapple and pin on the ordinary sheet; feeding begins once they are held, willing, or unable to interfere, and nothing makes you faster at it. A victim secured beyond any capacity to resist may be drained in summary at staff discretion. Summary compresses time, never cost.

**Mixed willing and unwilling in one cycle.** Where a victim is fed on both willingly and unwillingly inside the same cycle, the **unwilling rate governs the whole cycle** (1 lethal per 2 points). One coerced mouthful poisons the night's arithmetic, and there is no laundering a coerced feed through a willing one.

**Past empty is a body.** At 0, further feeding takes Health levels one for one as lethal, and that damage must begin to heal before their pool refills. A victim at zero is pallid, freezing and barely lucid.

**Tether, and the leash points at you.** Drain the same Nightfolk victim more than once in a lunar month and you gain a Tether point. At 3, essence from anyone else yields half value, and any roll to act against your Tethered victim's interests or to refuse them takes \+2 difficulty. It fades one point per month of complete abstinence. Mortals never generate it, so your herd is safe to keep and a diet of peers is a collar. They need never know. They may work it out.

**The stake.** A wooden stake through the heart leaves a Lilum immobile rather than destroyed. It is a called shot at \+2 difficulty; the stake is a **Strength \+1 lethal** weapon, and it needs at least **3 successes on the damage roll** to seat. Damage is dealt normally in addition. A staked Lilum counts as helpless, including against Arts, until the stake is removed, which takes one uninterrupted turn by anyone with a free hand.

**Your personhood is revocable.** A confirmed case of predation on a human (note that '*but he told me I could*' does not fly with the Vigiles; get it in writing with a witness), or being *perceived* to use inhuman advantage over one, revokes your BRD status and issues a writ to the Aberrant Hunters, who may then legally kill you; they do not investigate twice.

**Blending into humanity is up to your Stability.** For the Lilum, those at Stability rating 6 or higher find that they can ingest food and drink like a regular human, allowing them to blend into society better. With this, they also maintain more natural living colors and can even appear to be breathing. When their Stability level hits 5 or lower, they lose the ability to hide their undead nature and can no longer eat or drink like humans. Regardless of their Stability level, all drink and food will have no taste to them, and even the higher Stability Lilum will eventually need to remove the food and drink from their body, but they will be able to hold it down for the night.

**You are outnumbered, and they remember.** Humans are the majority everywhere except a handful of Purgatory wards, they hold the institutions, and the generation that wrote the Ascendancy laws is not all dead.

### Where you can feed

| Source | Ruling |
| ----- | :---- |
| Mortals | The default. Vitality \= Stamina \+ 7\. Full transfer, full tally, no Tether. |
| Nightfolk | Full value, no mechanical penalty. Generates Tether. Marks the *fed on an unwilling peer* trigger. |
| Animals | Closed to you without the **Iron Stomach** Merit, and half value with it. Staff set an animal's Vitality; a large beast is Stamina \+ 3 as a working default. Animals generate no Tether. |
| Aberrants & Dreamborn | Their ichor is not blood. Feeding on one yields nothing and is a bad idea for reasons the hunter guilds charge money to explain. |
| Stored blood | Blood drawn within the same night yields 1 Vitae per 2 points; older than that, nothing. |

## **Ghouls**

Ghouling is a state, not a change of splat. A Ghoul keeps their own sheet, so a mortal Ghoul is a Human who is being augmented by being fed Vitae: they keep their single pool, and they never hold Vitae as a pool of their own. **Note that Imbued Hunters cannot be ghouled.**

### **Ghouling a Mundane.** 

* A mundane mortal Ghoul using Blood arts spends Vitality where an Art asks for Vitae: nothing at tier 1, one point at tier 2\. 

  * Their Resource Trait derives as everyone else’s does, equalling their highest Art rating, and they roll Art \+ that rating at difficulty 6\. 

  * A Ghoul with one Art at 2 therefore rolls four dice and pays 1 Vitality. 

* Mundane ghouls stop aging. Youth is not restored, aging simply stops, and resumes with a vengeance if the Vitae supply is cut off.

  * For every week past their feeding date, a Ghoul who is cut off will age a decade.

### **Ghouling Metahumans and Sorcerers.** 

* A Metahuman or Sorcerer Ghoul keeps their own splat's Arts and cannot advance those Arts while Ghouled. 

* A Non-mundane Ghoul using Blood arts spends mana or psi instead of vitae.

* Drinking the domitor's Vitae counts as Replenishment. 

* Vitae and your own innate Essence conflict, producing issues as follows:

  * Whereas Mundanes no longer age at all, you still age very slowly. For every three years, you only age one each month you get your supply of Vitae.

  * **Metahumans.** Vampire blood destabilises your neurology: all Backlash rolls take \+1 difficulty, and the Ghoul's Instinct permanently shifts to **Fight**.

  * **Sorcerers.** Your Stigmata aches terribly, as if something on the other end of it is expressing its displeasure.

    * Nothing can cure this pain, which becomes truly excruciating when you drink Vitae.

### **What Vitae grants every Ghoul.** 

While the feeding is current, meaning within the last 30 days, a Ghoul gains the following:

* You may learn your domitor's own Arts, to level 2 and no further.

* You heal lethal at the bashing rate: one level per hour on the mortal table rather than one per day. Aggravated is untouched, as ever.

* A leash. Any roll to act against the domitor's interests, or to refuse them, takes \+2 difficulty, the same figure Tether uses, because it is the same kind of thing pointed the other way. It is a physical dependency rather than a compulsion to love, and it grants the domitor no commands, no telepathy and no override. Nothing lifts it short of ending the arrangement and enduring what follows.

## **Stability, added triggers, and Eldritch**

The general trigger list applies to you in full, and it is not softened. A trigger counts once per scene however often it fired. At the end of the scene, roll permanent Willpower at difficulty 6 and lose 1 current Stability per contributing trigger on a failure; a botch costs one further. Wound penalties never apply to a Stability roll.

Read that general list as a Lilum and notice how much of it describes your ordinary evening. Fed to serious injury, or fed on someone who had no way to refuse. Used an Art on a victim who is helpless or unaware. Acted on a desire that led to serious harm while in frenzy. Killed, or left someone to die who could have been saved. Nothing there needs adding to make your life expensive. Your line adds three anyway, because they are specific to what you are now rather than to what a monster generally is.

Mark one if your character, in this scene:

* **Fed on an unwilling peer:** took Vitae from another Nightfolk. It is no worse than taking it from a docker. It is the one act your entire community has agreed to answer collectively, and doing it tells them what you have decided to be.  
* **Let the Pax fall on someone else:** broke registration terms and arranged for another Nightfolk, a Ghoul or a mortal to carry the writ, the inquiry or the blame.  
* **Made a Ghoul:** fed a living person your Vitae with intent to keep them. Mark it the scene it begins, not the scene it ends.

Your Cathartic Vice restores 1 current Stability, once per cycle, played out in a real scene with real witnesses and real consequences. That is the only routine restoration you have, and it is an order of magnitude slower than the loss. Stability is a condition, not a resource you manage. It is also, at the 5/6 line, the difference between a creature who can sit down to dinner and one who plainly cannot.

### Diablerie

Draining a Lilum past empty and continuing until they are destroyed. It is one of the in-fiction routes by which staff may open a **Restricted** Art to you, and it is gated in both directions: the access is awarded rather than bought, and the XP still applies on top.

Mechanically it is a feed that does not stop, so the ordinary feeding rules govern all of it. The tally, the feeding damage, the escalation rate, and Tether if you had fed on them before. Beyond that, mark *fed on an unwilling peer*, mark *killed*, mark whatever else the scene earned, and understand that the community's answer to diablerie is not a Vigiles inquiry. It is everyone.

### Eldritch, for Lilum

Eldritch is the ledger of what a mind paid to see the world as it actually is. Every dot permanently reduces both your maximum and your current Stability, forever, and no Lilum may take it at creation under any circumstance. It is awarded by staff and never purchased, and the Stability it costs is never recovered while the knowledge remains, which is to say never.

For you it opens exactly one door, and that door is not an Art. A character with Eldritch may roll Intelligence \+ Eldritch to understand what a working they are watching actually is and does. No Arcane roll substitutes. It also makes them a candidate to trigger a prepared Working, which is a strange qualification for a vampire to hold and a good hook to hang a story on. Eldritch never adds dice to an Art roll.

Note that some knowledge comes with a price you might not want to pay: **Arcane above 3 mandates a point of Eldritch.** Arcane 4 needs staff approval and a scene. For your line, Arcane 3 is the ceiling in practice, and pushing at it is a request to be given something you cannot give back.

As Immortals, Vampires can conceivably predate the Sundering. **Those who do are mandated to take one point of Eldritch** to reflect living through reality literally coming unravelled around them before the Great Restoration patched the planes back together.

Storytellers award Eldritch slowly, and never for exposure to the ordinary supernatural. In Purgatory, a Garou changing shape in the street is a public-order matter. The Lilum roads to Eldritch are the Interstice, and what the elders who walked through it will not say; Lilith, pursued past doctrine into whatever the covenant is actually built on; the Governor, and the Inner Reaches that rearrange when observed; the quintessence reactors under the Basilicas, which everyone knows exist and almost nobody understands; the Pale Between, if Auspex 5 ever takes you into it; a botched working of Temporis, and whatever noticed it; and diablerie, which occasionally teaches the diablerist something they did not want.

Do the sums before the first dot lands, and say them out loud to your player. A Lilum at Eldritch 2 with Conscience 3 and Self-Control 3 holds permanent Stability 4 and is one bad month from Slipping, at which point they cannot spend Willpower to override an urge more than once a scene. They have already lost the dinner table.

## **Health, Torpor, and Final Death**

Your track is the standard seven levels, with wound penalties as written. Wound penalties do not apply to soak, to any Willpower or Psyche roll including contested defence and Attrition, or to Stability rolls. They apply to everything else, Art activations included.

Ordinary bashing is halved, rounding down, before soak; sunlight is not. Soak is Stamina plus armour at difficulty 6 against bashing and lethal, plus your Fortitude rating if you have it. Aggravated is unsoakable except under Fortitude 2\.

Bashing closes on its own at 1 level per post round and costs nothing. Lethal costs 2 Vitae per level, instant. Aggravated costs 5 Vitae plus a full cycle of unbroken daysleep per level, and nothing accelerates it further.

**Past Incapacitated on bashing:** further bashing is taken as lethal.

**Past Incapacitated on lethal:** further lethal damage does not kill you. The body shuts down and you enter torpor, where you are helpless, cannot act, cannot be roused by danger, and do not heal. Rising costs 5 Vitae and may be attempted once per cycle after the first full cycle has passed. **A Lilum in a prepared resting place recovers ambiently while down and will rise on their own schedule. A Lilum in torpor anywhere else fails the presumption test and receives nothing at midnight.** The lunar floor still applies, accruing 1 Vitae per full month, so five months under a cellar floor is enough to stand up on. One shoved into a cellar with an empty pool is not gone. They are shelved, and either another character takes them off the shelf or the calendar eventually does. Treat that as a plot, never as an accident.

**Past Incapacitated on aggravated:** further aggravated damage is Final Death. No torpor, no recovery, and no clause that turns it around, save a Fortitude 5 capstone declared before soak.

**What persists:** nothing, by default. Whether anything of a destroyed Lilum wakes up somewhere else belongs to the Restless Dead, and to the Shadowlands, where the soul rivers run downhill and nobody who has gone into the Maw has come back to comment. It is not automatic, not owed, and not a build plan.

# **The six bloodlines**

Every bloodline begins with **two of its three Affinity Arts, one at 2 and one at 1**. Each suggested build below sums exactly to 7/5/3 attributes, 13/9/5 abilities, 7 Psyche, five Backgrounds and 21 freebies. Attribute priority is set by your bloodline, and swapping it needs staff approval at application rather than a note on your sheet.

## **Ventrue: Old Guard**

### *Heavy is the crown that failed the old world*

Today's Ventrue have not forgotten what their arrogance cost. They rise with respect for the past and clear eyes toward the future, and they favour the people who make the Basilicas stronger: not the iron-fisted lords of the old world, but the best at whatever they have chosen to be. If others must be commanded into that, the Ventrue will use every tool available.

In Purgatory this puts them somewhere awkward and profitable. The Sovereign Club is where a Ventrue belongs, being human-majority, money-solves-everything, and full of Nightfolk expected to play at being human, which a Ventrue finds less humiliating than most do. The Council seat is a Lilum seat, and every Ventrue in the city has an opinion about who should be sitting in it.

**Attribute priority:** Social 7 · Mental 5 · Physical 3 · **Affinity Arts:** Dominate, Fortitude, Presence

**Suggested build** Attributes: Cha 4 / Man 4 / App 2 · Per 2 / Int 4 / Wits 2 · Str 2 / Dex 2 / Sta 2 Arts: **Dominate 2, Presence 1** (Vitae 2, pool 7\) Psyche: Conscience 2 / Self-Control 3 / Grit 2 Talents (13): Leadership 3, Subterfuge 3, Intimidation 2, Empathy 2, Expression 2, Alertness 1 Skills (9): Etiquette 3, Drive 2, Performance 2, Firearms 1, Larceny 1 Knowledges (5): Politics 2, Law 1, Finance 1, Academics 1 Backgrounds (5): Resources 2, Influence 1, Contacts 1, Status 1 Freebies (21): Manipulation to 5 (5) · Willpower to 5 (4) · Self-Control to 4 (2) · Grit to 3 (2) · Politics to 3 (2) · Registered Clean (2) · Filed Donor Roll (2) · Resources to 3 (1) · Influence to 2 (1) *Permanent Stability 6, so the dinner table stays available, which for this line is a working asset.*

**Promise: Steel Mind.** Lords of the mental arena for centuries, and Lilith sharpened the edge. You take −1 difficulty on any contested defence against an Art that reaches the mind, covering Dominate, Dementation, Auspex's Telepathy, and any compulsion to yield or obey. A contested defence is not an Art roll, which is precisely why a bloodline trait may legitimately touch it.

**Bane (Stigma): Sensitive Stomachs.** Ventrue feed safely on mundane mortals alone. From any non-mortal source, a Ventrue gains one Vitae fewer than the points taken, so a single point taken from any Nightfolk yields nothing at all. The victim still loses the full amount and still takes the full feeding damage. Nothing circumvents this, and Iron Stomach is unavailable to them. This stands as the single registered exception to the rule that foreign essence converts at full value.

## **Toreador: Hedonists**

### *Tortured seekers of what beauty remains*

The Toreador of the new world are far less the sophisticated elite than the old ones were. They have embraced the hedonism they were once merely accused of, and they cling to their art with a conviction that reads as ravenous because it is. If they cannot make beauty in a broken world, who can?

Purgatory suits them and ruins them. The city has no culture of its own, only an exile creole, which means a Toreador can be the most interesting thing in any room. It also means there is no tradition to measure the work against, which some of them find liberating and some find unbearable.

**Attribute priority:** Social 7 · Physical 5 · Mental 3 · **Affinity Arts:** Auspex, Celerity, Presence

**Suggested build** Attributes: Cha 4 / Man 2 / App 4 · Str 2 / Dex 4 / Sta 2 · Per 2 / Int 2 / Wits 2 Arts: Auspex 2, Presence 1 (Vitae 2, pool 7\) Psyche: Conscience 3 / Self-Control 2 / Grit 2 Talents (13): Expression 3, Empathy 3, Subterfuge 3, Alertness 2, Athletics 2 Skills (9): Performance 3, Crafts 3, Etiquette 2, Drive 1 Knowledges (5): Academics 2, Arcane 1, Politics 1, Investigation 1 Backgrounds (5): Fame 2, Resources 1, Contacts 1, Allies 1 Freebies (21): Appearance to 5 (5) · Willpower to 5 (4) · Self-Control to 3 (2) · Subterfuge to 4 (2) · Performance to 4 (2) · Pale Passing (2) · Filed Donor Roll (2) · Fame to 3 (1) · Resources to 2 (1) *Permanent Stability 6\.*

**Promise: Wearing the Mask.** Mastery of appearing composed while falling apart. You take \+2 dice on mundane Subterfuge rolls made to conceal your emotional state, hold a performance together, or manage your public face under pressure. Mundane rolls only.

**Bane (Stigma): Enamored.** When placed in a situation to reflect or expound upon their chosen creative outlet, a full image of themselves, or someone they are sponsoring or controlling, a Toreador rolls Self-Control at difficulty 6 or becomes **Captivated**: single-mindedly absorbed, taking no actions outside those that further their obsession. **Failure:** one round. **Botch:** the rest of the scene. It ends early if they take damage, or on a permanent Willpower roll at difficulty 7\.

## **Malkavian: Soothsayers**

### *The fools that saw the world end before the end*

The moment the world ended, the Malkavians went from laughing stock to the sanest people in the room. In the new world the broken are the wise. They speak truths nobody is ready for, they saw the real horrors first, and they carry that knowledge differently than anyone else does.

They are also the only bloodline with a native theory about the Interstice, and the fact that no two of their theories agree is, they will tell you, the finding rather than the failure.

**Attribute priority:** Mental 7 · Social 5 · Physical 3 · **Affinity Arts:** Auspex, Dementation, Obfuscate

**Suggested build** Attributes: Per 4 / Int 3 / Wits 3 · Cha 3 / Man 3 / App 2 · Str 2 / Dex 2 / Sta 2 Arts: Dementation 2, Auspex 1 (Vitae 2, pool 7\) Psyche: Conscience 2 / Self-Control 3 / Grit 2 Talents (13): Alertness 3, Empathy 3, Subterfuge 2, Expression 2, Awareness 2, Streetwise 1 Skills (9): Larceny 2, Stealth 2, Etiquette 2, Performance 1, Crafts 1, Drive 1 Knowledges (5): Arcane 2, Investigation 2, Academics 1 Backgrounds (5): Contacts 2, Allies 1, Resources 1, Mentor 1 Freebies (21): Perception to 5 (5) · Willpower to 5 (4) · Grit to 3 (2) · Arcane to 3 (2) · Alertness to 4 (2) · Old Blood (2) · Sleepwatch (1) · Contacts to 3 (1) · Allies to 2 (1) · Mentor to 2 (1) *Permanent Stability 5, one point below the line, so this Malkavian does not eat. Move a Psyche freebie if you want them to.*

**Your starting Derangement** is chosen with staff at application (and with your Mentor in the fiction, if you have one) and written into your background. It is Stigma-class: it costs nothing, provides nothing, cannot be bought off, and is not a Flaw, since this system has none. Treat it as your character's truth rather than their obstacle. Options that work well here include Obsessive-Compulsive, Paranoia, Synesthesia, Fugue, and Prophetic Dreaming.

**Promise: The Web of Many and One.** Something older than Auspex and deeper than Dementation. Two or more Malkavians in the same scene sense each other's presence and general emotional state without effort, and may pass impressions, though not words, reflexively. They feel each other's triumphs. No roll, no cost, no dice.

**Bane (Stigma): Webbed In.** They feel the bad ones too, in the body. **In any scene containing two or more Malkavians, when one takes damage the others present take the same number of levels of the same type, simultaneously and unsoaked.** The same sharing applies between a Malkavian and anyone they are currently Tethered to who is present in the scene, which is a reason to be careful whose peers you eat twice. Damage transferred this way does not itself transfer onward; the chain stops at one link.

## **Brujah: Sage**

### *The vengeance of the faceless voices*

Brujah came back from the end of the world changed. The chaos and independence of the Anarch years gave them the smoothest transition of any line and stripped away most of their elders, who decided their work was done. The ones who stayed came back for people and for purpose. Today's Brujah are less blowhard and more what they always should have been: people who fight for something worth protecting, with something behind the fist.

In Purgatory that makes them the backbone of half the ward-level politics nobody writes down, and the reason the Ashen Court's more predatory members keep their operations off certain streets. It also makes them the only line whose inheritance is watched from above: Temporis is theirs, and the Triune have opinions about who is permitted to hold it.

**Bane (Stigma): Rage with Intent.** The old frenzy has not vanished, it has focused. When the Brujah's domain, chosen family, or anyone they have formally sworn to protect is directly threatened, they roll Self-Control at difficulty 7 or enter frenzy immediately, as §3's loss of control. ## **Gangrel: Survivors**

### *Scions of the ruined world*

Gangrel were always of the wilds, and the end of the world only confirmed what they already believed about the systems of the old one. They endure. They may be the most numerous surviving line simply because so many of them were never counted. They are also the only Lilum who need nothing at all from civilization, which is their great strength and their deep isolation.

They are the line most willing to work outside a Hearthstone's shimmer, which makes them indispensable to stormchaser crews riding the wyrdstorm line, to aberrant-hunter guilds out of Nairobi and Calgary that can stomach the arrangement, and to anyone who needs something carried between Basilicas overland. Everyone else takes the waygate.

At 6 and above you do not cross a lit street without extraordinary measures, and a BRD who cannot pass an inspection is a BRD with an inquiry pending.

## **Nosferatu: New Moneys**

### *New kings of a world as ugly as they*

The Nosferatu went from the dregs of the line to its kingmakers. The Sundering disfigured a great deal of the world's population and wyrd-taint disfigures more of it every year, so a Nosferatu can walk the Mangrove stilt-hamlets and the outer wards among the altered and the ichor-burned without anyone looking twice. They turned their love of information into an open business, and after twenty-seven years of the Ascendancy teaching everyone what a registry can do, they built better ones than the Vigiles have.

Now they sit at tables no Ventrue can reach, hold paper the Directorate would like back, and know what is in every cellar on the City-Isle. It feels good to be the king.
