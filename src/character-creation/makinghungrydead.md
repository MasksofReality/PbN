Everything here is the standard creation budget, which no splat may bend.

| | |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3, priority set by your Fellowship |
| Abilities | 13 Talents, 9 Skills, 5 Knowledges |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control and Grit, minimum 1 each |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots |
| Freebies | 21 |
| Arts | Your Fellowship's Affinity Art at 2, and one more Aligned Art at 1 |
| Souls | Rank the four aspects |
| Dharma | 1 |
| Chi | 2, derived. Pool of 7. |

Freebies buy an Attribute at 5, an Ability at 2, Willpower at 2, Psyche at 2, a Background at 1, a Merit at 1 a point, and an Art dot at 11, and you may buy exactly one Art dot that way no matter how many points you have left. There are no Flaws in this system and there never will be. Where a drawback lands on your sheet, the fiction put it there and nothing removes it.

## The Shared Inheritance

**The Ghost Mantle belongs to every Hungry Dead**, not to any one Fellowship. Speaking with the dead is the first thing anyone teaches you and the last thing anyone can take. It runs on the Aligned track for all of you regardless of path, and it is the usual choice for the second Art at creation.

## What Arts Cost

| Track | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Affinity** (your Fellowship's Art, and the Ghost Mantle) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 20 |
| **Nonaligned** (any open Art) | 1 | 3 | 5 | 9 | 13 | 31 |
| **Discordant** (another Fellowship's Affinity Art) | 2 | 4 | 7 | 11 | 15 | 39 |

You may hold four Arts at most, ever.

Discordant Arts are never bought with XP alone. You need a route in the fiction, established in play and approved by staff: a teacher breaking their own traditions, a pact, an unwise bargain, or Amaranth. The XP applies on top of the story, and 39 XP is roughly thirteen months of income, so treat it as scenery for a long campaign and not a plan.

**Restricted Arts** (The Demon's Eye, Hellweaving) need both a teacher in the fiction and staff permission before you learn them at all. Never at creation. Once learned they advance on the Nonaligned track.

**Arts belonging to another splat are simply unavailable.** Not expensive, not gated, not a matter of the right story. Blood Arts, Spirit Arts, Pact Arts, Shadow Arts, Occult Arts, Psychic Arts, Sacred Arts and Oneiric Arts cannot appear on your sheet at any price.

Amaranth reaches Dharmic Arts and nothing else. It is a route through the register, not around it.

## Backgrounds Worth Taking

**Herd.** Mortals who let you feed, and mean it.

**Fellowship Standing.** Your title and what it entitles you to. The clean feeding options are political and this is the politics.

**Ancestral Records.** Whose bones you keep, and therefore who owes you.

**Spirit Allies.** Enshrined and wild spirits with obligations to you across the veil.

**Contacts, Resources, Allies, Mentor** and the rest of the standard list all work normally.
