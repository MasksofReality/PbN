# Human Creation Guide

Building A Life in the Basilica

## Making a Mundane

Humans use the universal creation standard without modification. There is no human variant of this table.

| Element | Standard |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Attributes | 7 / 5 / 3 across Physical, Social, Mental, priority set by your **Profession** |
| Abilities | 13 Talents / 9 Skills / 5 Knowledges, from the permitted V20 list |
| Blood Arts | **None.** Humans buy no Arts at creation or afterward |
| Psyche | 7 dots across Conscience, Self-Control and Grit, minimum 1 each |
| Willpower | 3 |
| Backgrounds | 5 dots, one of which your Profession requires you to place somewhere specific |
| Freebie points | 21. Attribute 5 · Ability 2 · Willpower 2 · Psyche 2 · Background 1 · Merit 1 per point |
| Nature and Demeanour | Both mandatory |
| Cathartic Vice | One, mandatory, staff-approved |

Attributes and Abilities cap at 5. Psyche caps at 5. Willpower caps at 10.

Write your **Demeanour** sharply: once per cycle, when acting squarely within it, you may reroll one failed Social roll. Your **Nature** has no roll attached, and betraying it marks a Stability trigger.

## Your Derived Values

Only three derived values need recording.

**Permanent Stability = Conscience + Self-Control.** Humans hold no Eldritch at creation, so nothing is subtracted. Nothing about being human erodes it afterward either. See [Stability, Triggers and Eldritch](https://purgatorybynight.com/denizens/humans/stability/).

**Vitality = Stamina + 7.** Humans are the only characters who track it. It is what a predator takes when they feed, and it is why Stamina is a survival stat for a mortal as it never was before. See [Draining, Death and the Ways Out](https://purgatorybynight.com/denizens/humans/mortality/).

**Health** is the standard seven levels on one track. No second track.

You have no Vitae rating, no Vitae pool, no Resource Trait, and no Tether.

## Defending Against an Art

You do not roll an Art, so there is no Art pool on your sheet. What you roll is the defence.

Your contested defence is **permanent Willpower alone**, or **Grit alone against anything that is fear, horror, or a supernatural compulsion to flee or yield**. A Nightfolk defends on Willpower *plus their highest Art*. You do not have the second half of that.

A creation-standard human therefore defends on **3 dice**, or **5** if you follow the freebie advice below. This is said plainly at application; make sure you have heard it.

**Mortal resistance.** Once per scene, spend 1 Willpower to actively resist a single use of an Art, forcing a contest that would otherwise be uncontested, or to reroll a failed defence in a contest already underway. Learn this clause.

## Spending Your 21 Freebies

You will not be spending 11 of them on an Art dot, which gives you the largest effective creation budget in the game. Three purchases are worth more to a human than to anyone else.

**Willpower** is the mortal defence. You defend against Arts on permanent Willpower alone, or Grit alone against fear. It also pays for your Professional Practice and for the mortal resistance clause, out of the same nightly pool. **Willpower 5 at creation is the standard recommendation.**

**Psyche** feeds Stability directly, so every dot raises both your ceiling and your starting pool. With 7 dots and a minimum of 1 in Grit, the highest a human can start unaided is Stability 6. Freebies push that to 8, which is the top of the Composed band and higher than most Nightfolk will hold for long, because Eldritch arrives on their sheets far more readily than on yours.

**Stamina** sets Vitality, and feeding damage lands at 1 lethal per 2 points taken. For a mortal in this setting, Stamina is a survival stat as it never was before.

## Abilities

There is no Security, Research, Counseling, History or Occult in this system, and Technology is a Knowledge rather than a Skill.

| You mean | Buy |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Lockpicking, safecracking, bypassing physical security | Larceny |
| Source work, archival method, historiography | Academics |
| Following a trail of evidence | Investigation |
| Pastoral care, counselling, reading distress | Empathy, or Medicine for clinical work |
| Supernatural scholarship: taxonomies, doctrine, what a Garou is | Arcane |
| Glyphcode notation, paratech maintenance, Old World scitech | Technology |

### Arcane

Arcane is a taught subject now. Post-Pax, the supernatural is a curriculum: registries, splat taxonomies, ward theory, Directorate law. It is learned the way any subject is learned, and **the first three dots can be bought freely**, at creation or after.

Going past the third dot means learning enough about how the cosmos works to attract the attention of things that are bad for your mental health, and it carries a **mandatory point of Eldritch**. Arcane 4 requires staff approval and a dedicated scene, and a Mundane Human must transition to Sorcerer in the process. **You cannot take Arcane above the third dot at creation.**

## Backgrounds

Humans can have Allies, Alternate Identity, Contacts, Fame, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers, Status. A clinic, a workshop or a stilt-house is a use of Resources. Standing in a Grand Faction or with the Vigiles is Influence or Status. In Purgatory, Alternate Identity is close to civic infrastructure, since nobody's stated origin is challenged and nobody's stated origin is believed.

Backgrounds are story-gated in both directions: you buy them where play has established the circumstance, you can lose them in play, and XP is credited back when the loss came from plot rather than your own choice.

## Choose Your Profession

Each Profession's suggested build sums exactly to 7/5/3 attributes, 13/9/5 abilities, five Backgrounds and 21 freebies, and includes its full Practice, Stigma, added Stability trigger and Profession-specific Merits. Attribute priority is set by your Profession, and swapping it needs staff approval at application rather than a note on your sheet.
