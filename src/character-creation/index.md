## Start Here

Welcome! Creating a character is part imagination, part paperwork, and mostly the fun part: deciding who you want to be.

**You can do this on your own.** Everything you need is on this page.

**Or you can bring a friend.** Our Mentors are experienced players who love helping newcomers. Find them on our Discord or in-world in Second Life. Ask as many questions as you like, whenever you like.

**Short on time?** Most people finish their first character in an evening.

### The 5 Steps

| Step | What you'll do | Think of it as... |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Pick a Type** | Choose what kind of character you'll play | "Who am I?" |
| **2. Pick a Path** | Choose a more specific flavor within your type | "What makes me different?" |
| **3. Fill In Your Sheet** | Spend your starting points on strengths | "What am I good at?" |
| **4. Tell Your Story** | Write a short background | "Where did I come from?" |
| **5. Send It In** | A Mentor takes a look (and an Admin, for Rare paths) | "Welcome to the city!" |

<div class="callout">

***First time?*** *Pick a common path. It's the smoothest way to get started. Rare paths are wonderful, but they need extra review and take a bit longer.*

</div>

## Words You'll See

New to roleplay or World of Darkness? Here's the short version of the words you'll run into.

**Roleplay and the sim**

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Mentor** | A friendly experienced player who guides you through character creation |
| **Admin** | The team that gives final approval for Rare paths |
| **Storyteller** | A staff member who runs plots and scenes |
| **IC** | "In Character." Anything your character says or does |
| **PC / NPC** | Player character (played by someone like you) / non-player character (run by staff) |
| **Faction** | A group of like-minded roleplayers who share ideals. There are five to choose from |
| **TE** | The Third Era, the 200 years since the End of Times. We're in Year 200 TE |

**Your character**

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Type** (also called a Splat) | The big category your character belongs to, like Kindred or Human |
| **Path** (also called a Subtype) | The specific flavor within your type, such as a bloodline, tribe, or profession |
| **Rare** | A special path that needs extra background and review |
| **Metahuman** | A person with hidden gifts who disguises them as sorcery |
| **Dhampir** | People born of two worlds, living and undead |

**Your sheet**

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Dot** | One point, shown as a small circle on your sheet |
| **Freebie Points** | 21 bonus points to fine-tune your sheet |
| **Merit** | A special perk you can buy for your character at creation |
| **Stability** | How steady your character's mind stays in a world full of horrors |
| **Willpower** | The stat you use to withstand supernatural or mental attacks. You lose a point when you fail one, and win it back by acting in line with your Nature |
| **Art** | A special ability from your Type, like Blood Art, Spirit Art, or Pact Art |
| **Affinity Art / Aligned Art** | Your two starting Arts: Affinity at 2 dots, Aligned at 1 dot |
| **Rating** | The pool of energy you spend to use your Arts. It equals your highest Art |

**Lost characters will also see**

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Triune** | Three powerful Fae Gentry (godlings of land, sky, and sea) charged with oversight, focused mainly on Purgatory itself |
| **Gentry** | Dreaming House Lords that hold power over all the Lost within the Basilica |

## Fill In Your Sheet

This is where your character gets their strengths. Think of it as a shopping trip: you get a set number of dots for each part of the sheet, and you spend them on what your character is good at. A **dot** is one of the little circles on your sheet. One dot equals one point.

**You can fill this out on your own.** Or ask a Mentor to go through it with you. Either way works.

### At a Glance

| Part of your sheet | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Nature, Demeanor, and Cathartic Vice** | Pick one of each |
| **Attributes** | Spend 7, 5, and 3 dots across Physical, Social, and Mental |
| **Abilities** | Spend 13, 9, and 5 dots across Talents, Skills, and Knowledges |
| **Mental Aspects** | Spend 5 dots across Conscience, Self-Control, and Grit |
| **Backgrounds** | Spend 5 dots |
| **Arts** | Start with 2: an Affinity Art at 2 dots and an Aligned Art at 1 dot |
| **Willpower and Freebie Points** | Willpower starts at 3. Spend 21 Freebie Points to fine-tune |

### Who Are You?

At the top of your sheet, you pick three things that shape how your character acts:

- **Nature:** who your character is deep down
- **Demeanor:** the face they show the world
- **Cathartic Vice:** how your character lets off steam and recovers lost Stability (see below)

Browse the full lists: [Natures & Demeanors](/character-creation/natures-and-demeanors/) · [Cathartic Vices](/character-creation/cathartic-vices/)

### Attributes: What You're Naturally Like

Attributes come in three groups. Every trait starts with 1 free dot, and you choose which group matters most to your character.

| Group | Traits |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | Strength, Dexterity, Stamina |
| **Social** | Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance |
| **Mental** | Perception, Intelligence, Wits |

***Pick your focus:*** *Give one group **7** dots, another **5**, and the last **3**. A brawler might go Physical first. A schemer might go Social first.*

### Abilities: What You've Learned

Abilities also come in three groups, and again you choose which gets the most.

| Group | In short | Traits |
|---|---|---|
| **Talents** | Things you do by instinct | Alertness, Artistry, Athletics, Brawl, Empathy, Expression, Intimidation, Leadership, Streetwise, Subterfuge |
| **Skills** | Things you've trained to do | Animal Ken, Craft, Drive, Etiquette, Firearms, Melee, Performance, Security, Stealth, Survival |
| **Knowledges** | Things you've studied | Arcane, Academics, Awareness, Eldritch\*, Finance, Investigation, Linguistics\*, Medicine, Politics, Technology |

***Pick your focus:*** *Give one group **13** dots, another **9**, and the last **5**.*

**About the two starred Knowledges:**

- **Linguistics\*** covers languages beyond the ones you start with. Every character knows basic English plus one language they grew up with, for free. Any extra language is bought with Linguistics.
- **Eldritch\*** is knowledge of the hidden truths behind how the world was salvaged and what really happened in the Lost Decade. It can help you, but it takes a toll on your Stability (see below). Choose it with care.

### Mental Aspects: How Your Mind Holds Up

These three measure how your character copes in a hard world. Each starts at 1 dot, and you get **5 extra dots** to spread among them however you like.

| Aspect | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Conscience** | Your sense of right and wrong, and how well you perceive it |
| **Self-Control** | How well you stay composed instead of acting on impulse |
| **Grit** | How well you resist fear, horror, and supernatural pressure to flee or yield |

### Stability: Your Mind in a Broken World

At the top of your sheet is your **Stability**, which measures how steady your character stays in a world full of horrors.

***Stability = Conscience + Self-Control − Eldritch dots***

*Example: Conscience 3 + Self-Control 3 = 6. If you also have 1 dot of Eldritch, your Stability is **5**.*

Your Cathartic Vice helps you recover Stability when you lose it.

### Backgrounds: Who and What You Have

Backgrounds are the allies, resources, and connections your character brings to the Basilica. You get **5 dots** to spend. [See all Backgrounds](/character-creation/backgrounds/).

### Arts and Rating: Your Special Powers

Every Type has special abilities, and they all work the same way:

- **Arts** are your special abilities. Each Type names its own, like Blood Art, Spirit Art, or Pact Art.
- **You start with two Arts:** your **Affinity Art** at 2 dots, and one **Aligned Art** at 1 dot.
- **Rating** is the pool of energy you spend to use your Arts. It always equals your highest Art.

Your Mentor can help you sort out the specifics for your Type, and your Type's page lists your Arts too.

### Finishing Touches

**Willpower** starts at 3 for everyone.

**Freebie Points** are 21 bonus points to spend anywhere on your sheet, for fine-tuning. Here's what each one costs:

| What you're buying | Cost per dot |
|---|---|
| **Backgrounds** | 1 Freebie Point |
| **Abilities** | 2 Freebie Points |
| **Willpower** | 2 Freebie Points |
| **Mental Aspects** | 2 Freebie Points |
| **Attributes** | 5 Freebie Points |
| **Arts** | 11 Freebie Points (one dot maximum) |
| **[Merits](/character-creation/merits/)** | 1 Freebie Point per Merit point |

***Heads up on Merits:*** *They can only be bought during character creation, so this is your one chance to pick some up.*

***A friendly tip about Arts:*** *Don't worry about buying extra Art dots now. They cost far less after creation, and the high price here is what we jokingly call the **Impatience Tax**. After about a month of regular roleplay, you'll most likely have the XP for that extra dot anyway.*

***Not sure where to put your dots?*** *Pick two or three things your character is really great at, then spread the rest around. You can always adjust before you submit.*

## Tell Your Story

Your background is how we get to know your character, and it's the most fun part of the process. You don't need to write a novel. **At a minimum, answer the six questions below in full sentences.** You're always welcome to write more.

**Stuck?** Ask a Mentor to brainstorm with you on Discord or in-world. Many great characters started as a rough idea and a good conversation.

**A quick note on time:** We're in Year 200 of the **Third Era (TE)**, the 200 years since the End of Times.

### Answer These Six Questions

1. **Who were they before?** Tell us about their life before the Basilica. Were they alive before the End of Times, or born during the Third Era? *Example: a night-shift nurse, a street musician, a soldier who never talked about the war.*
2. **How is your character surviving in the world throughout their lifetime?** What have they endured, lost, or given up to make it to Year 200 TE? *Example: they left someone behind, or made a deal they still regret.*
3. **Why Basilica Purgatory?** What brought them here, specifically? *Example: a rumor of safety, a debt to repay, someone they're searching for.*
4. **What do they want?** Give us one short-term goal and one long-term goal. *Example: find a safe place to sleep this week, and eventually earn a seat on a Faction's council.*
5. **Who do they know?** Include at least one NPC (a staff-run character) and one possible PC connection (another player's character). Not sure who? A Mentor can help you find a fit. *Example: an old friend who runs a market stall, and a rival who might become an ally.*
6. **What are they hiding?** Every character has something they don't advertise, and it's often the best story hook. *Example: a hidden power, a past crime, a secret loyalty.*

### See What "Good Enough" Looks Like

These two samples answer all six questions in just a few sentences each. That's all it takes.

#### Sample 1: A Human Mechanic

*Being an ordinary human in the Basilica is a great way to play. Your character's strength is their grit, their skills, and the people they know.*

1. **Who were they before?** Dana Reyes was born in Year 171 TE in a small farming settlement outside the Basilica. Her mother was a mechanic and her father kept the settlement's water pumps running, so she grew up with grease on her hands.
2. **How is she surviving?** Dana stays alive by being useful. She repairs generators, vehicles, and anything else people can't afford to lose, and she trades repairs for food and safe places to sleep. She lost her father to a brutal winter when she was nineteen and still carries his old wrench.
3. **Why Basilica Purgatory?** The settlement's equipment finally failed, and word spread that the Basilica always needs skilled hands. She came looking for steady work and walls that hold.
4. **What does she want?** In the short term, she wants to land a regular repair job within the month. In the long term, she wants to open her own shop and train apprentices so no one else has to learn the trade the hard way.
5. **Who does she know?** Her NPC connection is Old Tomas, a scrap dealer who sold her first set of tools and still sends her work. She hopes to meet a PC who needs a good mechanic and is willing to pay in more than favors.
6. **What is she hiding?** Dana is hiding a debt. She borrowed parts from a loan shark in her last settlement and left town without paying.

#### Sample 2: A Toreador Vampire

1. **Who were they before?** Lucien Marchetti was a portrait painter in a coastal city before the End of Times, admired by a few and envied by many. He was Embraced at thirty-four by a Toreador who admired his work.
2. **How is he surviving?** Lucien survives by making himself valuable. He paints, restores, and appraises art for anyone who will pay in protection or blood, and he learned early that beauty opens doors that force can't. He lost his sire in the chaos of the End of Times and has been on his own ever since.
3. **Why Basilica Purgatory?** Lucien heard the Basilica still values culture and craft. After two centuries of wandering, he wants a place where his work might matter again.
4. **What does he want?** In the short term, he wants a studio of his own with room to work. In the long term, he wants to create one masterpiece that outlasts him, something the Basilica will remember.
5. **Who does he know?** His NPC connection is Marisol, a dealer who sells him pigments and gossip. He is hoping to meet a PC who is an artist or collector and might become a patron, a rival, or a muse.
6. **What is he hiding?** Lucien can no longer paint from life without feeling hunger. He tells everyone it's creative block.

### Playing Something Special? Add a Little Extra

Some paths need a few more details so we can fit them into the world.

| If your character is... | Please also explain... |
|---|---|
| **A Rare path** (any Type, including every Imbued) | How this path survived, how it works today, and what it contributes to the Basilica |
| **A Metahuman** | How your character disguises their abilities as Sorcerer-style magic. Be detailed, since this matters in the Basilica |
| **A Dhampir** | Who their parents were, how they've survived despite being so rare, and how they feel about both sides of their heritage |

***Rare path reminder:*** *These take an extra review, so give yourself a little more time. Not sure if your idea qualifies? Reach out to our Storytellers and Admin to talk it through.*

## Send It In

You've done the hard part. Here's how to send it in, what happens next, and who's on your side along the way.

### How to Send It In

There are three easy ways to get started. Pick whichever suits you:

- **On Discord:** Open an application ticket in our Mentor ticket system. A Mentor will be assigned to you.
- **In Second Life:** Ring for a Mentor at the Landing Zone.
- **Away from the sim?** Leave us a message in-world, and we'll find you.

***No Discord yet?*** *You can request an invite in-world.*

### What Happens After You Submit

| Step | What happens | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Your Mentor reads it** | A Mentor looks over your sheet and background and gives you friendly feedback | Within 3 days (72 hours) |
| **2. Small tweaks, if needed** | Most characters need one round of changes at most | Depends on you |
| **3. Welcome to the Basilica!** | You get your character tag, an introduction to a Faction, and a starting scene with your Mentor | |

***Don't worry about revisions.*** *Needing a tweak isn't a rejection. It's a chance to make your character even better, and your Mentor will help you every step of the way.*

***Playing a Rare path?*** *An Admin also gives final approval before you're welcomed in, so plan ahead and give yourself some breathing room.*

### Your Checklist

Before you send it in, make sure you have:

- ☐ Filled in your character sheet
- ☐ Chosen your Nature, Demeanor, and Cathartic Vice
- ☐ Picked your Type and path
- ☐ Answered all six background questions in full sentences
- ☐ Noted your Faction preference (or "Undecided," which is perfectly fine)
- ☐ Added the extra details, if you're a Metahuman, Dhampir, or Rare path
