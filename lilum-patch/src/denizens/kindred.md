## Being Lilum in 200 TE

The old Sects are gone. Camarilla, Sabbat and the Anarch Movement died with the world that could afford them. What survived the Sundering was a scatter of the hungry, and what organised them again was the covenant of Lilith. Every Lilum is a Child of Lilith now, whatever their line remembers about who they used to answer to.

The practical history is shorter, and it matters more.

* **The Interstice (2068–2078 CE):** ten years for which no record exists, human or otherwise. Some elders walked into it and came out the other side with the decade missing from them too. Most will not discuss it. The ones who will, do not agree.
* **The Itinerant Period (0–103 TE):** a century of following caravans across a drowned world, feeding on people who could not afford to lose anyone, tolerated because a predator that fights aberrants is still useful. In that century there was no Masquerade at all. The Nightfolk kept apart as distant shepherds, and the caravans invented guardian spirits to explain them.
* **122 TE, the Second Masquerade:** the Shadow Directorate formed, and its elders decreed that all Nightfolk hide their nature on pain of death. Thirty-one years of it.
* **153 TE, the Directorate ended it themselves:** they announced the Nightfolk to the League at its annual meeting. Roughly 6,000 Nightfolk died in the Week of Torches. Every Lilum awake then lost somebody, and every Lilum awake then watched roughly four times as many humans die doing it.
* **153–180 TE, the Ascendancy of Man:** twenty-seven years of law written to make your existence a category of vermin.
* **177 TE, the Awakened Censure Act:** the Awakened were taken to undisclosed prisons. It is the one Ascendancy law that survived the Pax, and nobody who signed the treaty has ever explained why. Whatever your line thinks it knows about that exception, it is not knowledge you can cite.
* **180 TE, the Pax Nocturnorum:** register as a Benign Reality Deviant, behave, and you hold the rights of any citizen.

## Purgatory

You live on the City-Isle, the strangest member of the League and the only one that has never held a Hearthstone. Before the Waygates it was the League's oubliette, and the Governor's Toll meant residency was a sentence. Now it is a freeport, and the most openly supernatural city on Earth.

That absence of a Hearthstone is the single most important physical fact about your home. There is no stasis field flattening the ambient aetheric density here. Nothing about your nature is being pressed on by a Weaver anchor every hour of the day. Whatever else Purgatory costs you, it is comfortable in a way no Basilica behind a shimmer is, and every Lilum who has travelled knows the difference in their teeth.

Two things about the City-Isle you already know, because every Lilum does.

* **The Council of Six has a permanent Lilum seat:** it is held on a claim over the ground the City-Isle stands on, acknowledged by the Governor themself, and it is the only seat on that Council no faction can vote away.
* **The Ashen Court is by and for Nightfolk:** most of your line's business gets done there, which means most of your line's business is also the Order of Vigiles' caseload.

## What You Know That a Citizen Does Not

A university degree (Arcane 3) and a decent library will get a mortal scholar the taxonomies, the registry law, the ward theory and the public history. It will not get them Lilith, the covenant, what the Directorate's elders were actually voting on in 122 TE, or what happened inside the Interstice. That last one is not knowledge you can buy either. It is Eldritch, it is awarded by staff and never purchased, and what it costs is set out on the [Vitae & the Cost of Immortality](/denizens/kindred/vitaeandcost/) page.

## The Six Bloodlines

Every bloodline begins with two of its three Affinity Arts, one at 2 and one at 1. Attribute priority is set by your bloodline, and swapping it needs staff approval at application rather than a note on your sheet.

| Bloodline | Priority | Affinity Arts | Promise | Bane (Stigma) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[Ventrue](/denizens/kindred/ventrue/)** | Soc / Ment / Phys | Dominate, Fortitude, Presence | Steel Mind: −1 diff defending against mind-reaching Arts | Sensitive Stomachs: one Vitae fewer from any non-mortal |
| **[Toreador](/denizens/kindred/toreador/)** | Soc / Phys / Ment | Auspex, Celerity, Presence | Wearing the Mask: +2 dice, mundane Subterfuge for composure | Enamored: Self-Control diff 6 or Captivated |
| **[Malkavian](/denizens/kindred/malkavian/)** | Ment / Soc / Phys | Auspex, Dementation, Obfuscate | The Web: passive sense and impressions between Malkavians | Webbed In: shared damage, plus a starting Derangement |
| **[Brujah](/denizens/kindred/brujah/)** | Phys / Soc / Ment | Potence, Temporis, Fortitude | Cool and Calculated: +2 dice Self-Control, +1 diff to bait them | Rage with Intent: Self-Control diff 7 on perceived threat |
| **[Gangrel](/denizens/kindred/gangrel/)** | Phys / Ment / Soc | Animalism, Fortitude, Protean | Survivalist: +2 dice mundane Survival | Animal Features: permanent, +1 diff Social per 3 |
| **[Nosferatu](/denizens/kindred/nosferatu/)** | Ment / Phys / Soc | Animalism, Obfuscate, Presence | Tech Bros: +2 dice mundane Technology | Monstrous: Appearance locked at 1 |
