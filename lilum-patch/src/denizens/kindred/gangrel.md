## Who They Are

Gangrel were always of the wilds, and the end of the world only confirmed what they already believed about the systems of the old one. They endure. They may be the most numerous surviving line simply because so many of them were never counted. They are also the only Lilum who need nothing at all from civilization, which is their great strength and their deep isolation.

They are the line most willing to work outside a Hearthstone's shimmer, which makes them indispensable to stormchaser crews riding the wyrdstorm line, to aberrant-hunter guilds out of Nairobi and Calgary that can stomach the arrangement, and to anyone who needs something carried between Basilicas overland. Everyone else takes the waygate.

## Promise: Survivalist

You take +2 dice on all mundane Survival rolls, covering navigation, shelter, enduring exposure, reading terrain, and finding blood in the wild. In a world where nine-tenths of the land is under water and the rest is trying to kill you, this is no small thing. The bloodline grants it rather than selling it, and it applies to mundane rolls, which is the one place a bloodline trait may add dice at all.

## Bane (Stigma): Animal Features

Each time a Gangrel loses control (frenzy from Attrition, a low-Stability episode, Rotschreck, or any other cause), they may take on a permanent animal feature. Claws that do not retract, discoloured or scaled skin, fur, teeth, a changed gait. Eyes of the Beast does not count, since that one switches off. Features never go away.

For every 3 features, all Social rolls in company where the features are visible take +1 difficulty, cumulatively, and concealment becomes a live logistical problem rather than a costume choice. At 0 to 2 you pass as someone the Sundering was unkind to. At 3 to 5, covering up takes planning. At 6 and above you do not cross a lit street without extraordinary measures, and a BRD who cannot pass an inspection is a BRD with an inquiry pending.
