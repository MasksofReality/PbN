## Who They Are

The Toreador of the new world are far less the sophisticated elite than the old ones were. They have embraced the hedonism they were once merely accused of, and they cling to their art with a conviction that reads as ravenous because it is. If they cannot make beauty in a broken world, who can?

Purgatory suits them and ruins them. The city has no culture of its own, only an exile creole, which means a Toreador can be the most interesting thing in any room. It also means there is no tradition to measure the work against, which some of them find liberating and some find unbearable.

## Promise: Wearing the Mask

Mastery of appearing composed while falling apart. You take +2 dice on mundane Subterfuge rolls made to conceal your emotional state, hold a performance together, or manage your public face under pressure. Mundane rolls only.

## Bane (Stigma): Enamored

When placed in a situation to reflect or expound upon their chosen creative outlet, a full image of themselves, or someone they are sponsoring or controlling, a Toreador rolls Self-Control at difficulty 6 or becomes **Captivated**: single-mindedly absorbed, taking no actions outside those that further their obsession. **Failure:** one round. **Botch:** the rest of the scene. It ends early if they take damage, or on a permanent Willpower roll at difficulty 7.
