## Four Paths, and the Price of a Shape Vitae Was Not Meant to Hold

Sorcery runs on Mana. Thaumaturgy does not, because a Lilum has none. What a thaumaturge does is force Vitae through a shape it was never meant to hold, and Vitae resents it. That is the whole of the theory, it is why the working costs more than an Art of the same tier, and it is why blood always ends up on the floor.

The Sorcerer Clans who negotiated the Pax regard it as the counterfeit work of immoral hacks, and say so in public. The Basilica ward-guilds will not certify a Lilum's work at any price. The Vigiles do not have a statute against Thaumaturgy as such, which every registered practitioner on the Isle will tell you is worse than having one, because it means each inquiry starts from nothing and ends wherever the investigating Vigil decides it ends.

## Standing

**Every Path is Restricted, and every Path is its own Art.** You need an NPC teacher and staff permission before you may learn one at all, it is never available at creation, and once learned it advances on the **Nonaligned track: 31 XP to tier 5**. There is no Affinity price for Thaumaturgy and no bloodline holds it.

Each Path you hold occupies one of your four Art slots. A Lilum with two Affinity Arts and two Paths is full, permanently, and has closed the door on ever learning Nonaligned Fortitude or anything else. Say that out loud before the second Path is bought.

Thaumaturgy is a Blood Art and **does not require Eldritch**, because Eldritch gates Occult Arts. A Lilum practising it is not a Sorcerer, and may not learn a Sorcerer's Occult Arts at any price. Your Vitae rating rises with a Path exactly as it rises with any other Art, because it is your highest Art rating and nothing else.

**Only four Paths exist for Lilum.** They are below. Anything else a character claims to practise is an NPC's business, a lie, or a story staff have not written yet.

## The Tithe

**Every Path activation costs 1 Vitae above the tier cost, spilled from your own body and is visible. This costs a point of lethal damage.**

Consequences worth stating plainly:

* **Tier 1 is not free.** A Path's first tier costs 1 Vitae. At 0 Vitae you cannot work Thaumaturgy at all, where every other line still has its free tier available.
* **Pushing does not cover it.** Pushing substitutes for a base cost only; the surcharge is paid in Vitae as written. A drained Lilum cannot Push a capstone Path working, because the tithe still wants a point they do not have.
* **A capstone Path costs 6 Vitae.** Against a starting pool of 7. Nobody opens with this, and nobody works it twice in a night without feeding between.
* **It cannot be done clean.** There is blood, there is a wound, and there is a scene's worth of it on whatever you were standing on. Clean Bite does nothing here. A thaumaturge in a lit room with witnesses has made a decision.

The tithe may instead be paid from **living blood spilled from another body in the same scene, at 2 points per 1 Vitae of tithe.** That costs the victim 1 lethal per 2 points exactly as feeding does, adds to their nightly tally exactly as feeding does, and marks every trigger feeding on them would have marked. It is not feeding, it generates no Tether, and it nourishes you not at all. It is simply cheaper than bleeding yourself, which is the entire reason anyone does it, and it is most of what the Vigiles think Thaumaturgy is.

## Preparation

An hour of uninterrupted work with prepared materials, in a place you control, buys **−1 difficulty** on the activation that follows. That is the cap, it does not stack with itself or with anything else, and it is unavailable in a fight, in transit, or anywhere you are being looked for. There is no separate ritual system. A Path worked slowly is the same Path.

## Notice

**On a botch with any Path, staff may award a point of Eldritch,** on the same terms as Temporis: the working went somewhere, something on the other end of it registered the intrusion, and you caught a glimpse of it. That point costs permanent Stability like every other and it does not leave.

Working Thaumaturgy inside a Hearthstone's field, or within sight of a quintessence reactor housing, is a standing staff hook for the same award. The stasis field does not stop you. It does something else, and the practitioners who have described it do not agree about what.

## The Path of Blood

The oldest of the four and the only one that is unmistakably yours rather than borrowed. It does not touch minds, senses or flesh. It touches the blood, and everything downstream of the blood.

**●○○○○ A Taste for Blood** *(free, plus tithe)*. Taste a sample, from a body or a spill or a cup, and read the blood rather than the person. **1:** whether the donor is mortal, Nightfolk, Ghouled or something else, and how recently it left them. **2–3:** how much Vitae or Vitality the donor was holding when it was taken, whether they had fed that night, and whether it was given willingly. **4+:** whether the donor is Tethered, leashed or under a standing compulsion, and whether they are still alive. It reads the blood. It does not read the mind, the emotional state or the intent, and Aura Sight is still a different power.

**●●○○○ Blood Rage** *(1 Vitae, plus tithe, **Contested**)*. Force another Lilum's Vitae to answer you and burn where it lies. **1:** they lose 1 Vitae. **2–3:** 2. **4+:** 3, and if this empties them they enter Attrition at once, rolling at the end of the scene as normal. It takes nothing for you. It only spends what they had, which against a Lilum who has already worked twice tonight is frequently the whole fight.

**●●●○○ Theft of Vitae** *(2 Vitae, plus tithe, **Contested**)*. Blood leaves the target and crosses open air to you as a ribbon, no contact required, line of sight only. **1:** 1 point. **2–3:** 2 points. **4+:** 3 points. The ordinary feeding rules govern every part of what arrives: the victim's nightly tally, 1 lethal per 2 points taken and per 3 if freely willing, the mixed-cycle rule, Tether if the victim is Nightfolk, the *fed on an unwilling peer* trigger, and the Ventrue Bane where it applies. Aberrants and Dreamborn have no blood to take. What this Art removes is the need to be close enough to be stopped, and the Vigiles are perfectly aware of that.

**●●●●○ The Red Cellar** *(3 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Seal your own Vitae into a vessel where it does not spoil, naming the exception to the rule that stored blood older than a night yields nothing. **1:** up to 3 points, held for a lunar month, drawn only by you. **2–3:** up to 5 points, held indefinitely, drawn by you or by anyone you named at the sealing. **4+:** as above, and the seal reports to you the moment it is opened by anyone else, including who. Drawing from a cellar returns Vitae to your pool at full value and takes one round per point. Understand what you have made: a cache of your own blood, in a place, that somebody else may find. Every thaumaturge who has been ransomed on the Isle was ransomed with one of these.

**●●●●● Cauldron of Blood** *(5 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Boil what is in them where it lies. **Art damage, lethal, unsoaked, no dodge.** Line of sight and a target you can positively identify; +2 difficulty through glass, water or smoke. 1 level on any success, plus 1 per net success beyond the first. Against a Lilum it additionally destroys 1 Vitae per level dealt. Against an Aberrant or a Dreamborn it does nothing at all, because ichor is not blood. This is the least deniable thing on this list, and there is no version of using it in company that ends with your BRD intact.

## The Lure of Flames

You conjure the one thing on Earth that ends you. Practitioners are asked why, constantly, and the honest answer is usually that nothing else in the world makes a room full of Nightfolk stop talking.

**The fire is real.** It is an ordinary physical effect, so it is soaked normally on Stamina plus armour at difficulty 6, and remains unsoakable as aggravated except under Defiance. It burns what fire burns, it spreads if there is anything to spread into, and it does not go out because the scene ended. Adamantine reduces it to lethal for whoever holds that tier. Body of the Mist is no protection whatsoever.

**You are not exempt.** Any Path flame at tier 3 or above triggers Rotschreck in every Lilum present who can see it, **including you**, rolled on Grit at difficulty 6 as normal, with your own working at **−1 difficulty** because it is yours and you know its shape. On a botch with any tier of this Path, you are alight.

**●○○○○ Candle's Breath** *(free, plus tithe)*. A flame no larger than a candle, held in your palm, doing you no harm while it burns there. You may also snuff any fire you can touch, up to a hearth's worth. It sets nothing alight on its own, it lasts a scene, and it is the single most effective piece of theatre available to your line.

**●●○○○ Kindle** *(1 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Set a flammable thing alight at line of sight: a curtain, a spill, a coat somebody is wearing. Against a worn or carried item a dodge is allowed (Dexterity + Athletics, each success cancelling one of yours). Anything burning deals damage by the ordinary rule for fire, 1 aggravated per round, and can be beaten out by anyone who spends their action doing it.

**●●●○○ Flame Lash** *(2 Vitae, plus tithe)*. A whip of fire, one target, medium range. **Art damage, lethal to the living and aggravated to Lilum and anything else fire is aggravated to, soaked normally, dodge allowed.** 1 level on any success, plus 1 per net success beyond the first. It does not set the target alight; it only hits them.

**●●●●○ The Pyre** *(3 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Everything flammable within a few metres ignites at once. **1:** one small area, burning for a round. **2–3:** the area burns for the scene, and anyone in it takes the ordinary fire rule each round until they leave or are put out. **4+:** as above, and the fire is hungry: it spreads to anything adjacent that will take it, and staff should treat the building as a live problem for the rest of the night. Rotschreck as above, for everyone, you included.

**●●●●● The Judgement** *(5 Vitae, plus tithe)*. A standing wall or column of flame you shape and hold for the scene. **1:** a barrier a few metres across that nothing living crosses without burning. **2–3:** a wall long enough to close a street or a hall, and you may move it one increment per round on your action. **4+:** as above, and it burns through what it stands against, breaching an ordinary wall, floor or vehicle in a round. Rotschreck against The Judgement is rolled at **+2 difficulty** by everyone present, and at ordinary difficulty by you. Losing your own capstone to Rotschreck and fleeing a burning street you set is not a hypothetical. It has happened twice on the Isle since the Pax.

## The Sealed Circle

The practitioner's version of what Arcane 3 teaches as theory. A university scholar can tell you what a ward is for. This tells you where its edge is, what it will not admit, and how to draw one out of your own arm.

Every tier from 2 upward **banks** its successes as a static threshold. Anyone attempting to cross, break or open the work rolls their own pool at difficulty 6 and must equal or exceed the banked number. The banked figure is never rerolled.

No circle stops sunlight. Several people have died finding that out.

**●○○○○ Read the Sigil** *(free, plus tithe)*. Touch a ward, circle, threshold or binding and read the fence rather than the physics. **1:** that it is there, and its extent. **2–3:** what category it excludes or contains, and its banked threshold. **4+:** who set it, roughly when, and whether it has been tested since. **This is not a substitute for Eldritch.** It reads wards and bindings only, and it tells you nothing whatever about any other kind of working. Understanding what a working *is* remains Intelligence + Eldritch and nothing else.

**●●○○○ The Drawn Circle** *(1 Vitae, plus tithe)*. A circle of your own blood on a surface, and a named category that may not cross it. **1:** one narrow category (Lilum, mortals, dogs, the man whose name you have) for one scene. **2–3:** a broad category, holding until dawn. **4+:** a broad category, holding until dawn, and the circle also refuses anything that is not what it appears to be. Physically breaking the line ends it, which anyone outside may attempt with a boot and a free hand, and which anyone inside may not.

**●●●○○ Ward the Object** *(2 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Mark a thing so that the named category cannot handle it without cost. **1:** contact deals 1 bashing. **2–3:** 1 lethal, and the holder drops it unless they beat the banked threshold. **4+:** 1 lethal per round held. **Art damage, no dodge, contact only, and it fires no more than once per round per handler.** Wards on a doorframe, a ledger, a coffin lid and a stake are all standard practice, and all four are things somebody has warded on the Isle this year.

**●●●●○ The Sealed Threshold** *(3 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Ward a doorway, a room or a building for a **lunar month**, against a named category, and know the instant somebody tests it. **1:** one doorway. **2–3:** a room or a small building, and you know who tested it as well as when. **4+:** as above, and it may be **inverted to hold something in.** Obfuscate does not walk a cloaked person through it; the threshold does not care whether it can see you. Inversion is how the Isle keeps a torpid elder in a box, and how at least three people currently on the Isle are kept in cellars they cannot leave.

**●●●●● The Iron Round** *(5 Vitae, plus tithe)*. A circle that holds a person or an entity inside it against their will. **1:** one round. **2–3:** the scene. **4+:** until dawn. While it holds, nothing supernatural crosses the boundary in either direction: no Art reaches out, no Art reaches in, no Presence, no Dominate, no Telepathy, no Theft of Vitae. **Mundane things cross freely**, which is the trade and which is the whole reason anyone survives being caught in one. A bullet goes in. A stake goes in. Whoever holds the circle has to decide whether they are here to talk.

## Movement of the Mind

Force at a distance, with nothing between you and it. Temporis stops a thing where it is; this moves it. The two are not the same purchase, and no Brujah has ever been pleased to be told so.

Nothing on this Path adds dice to a mundane roll. Where it performs a task a mundane pool would otherwise cover, the Art performs it on Art + Vitae instead, and you do not get both.

**●○○○○ The Unseen Hand** *(free, plus tithe)*. Fine manipulation at line of sight, up to the weight of a full glass. **1:** slide, tip or drag something. **2–3:** delicate work at range: a latch, a lockpick already seated, a page turned. **4+:** work you could not do with your own fingers if you were standing there: a trigger inside a housing, a key from a pocket, a coin out of a closed hand.

**●●○○○ Grasp** *(1 Vitae, plus tithe. **Contested** when used on a person.)* Lift and hold. **1:** up to a person's weight, held for a round, or a door held shut. **2–3:** held for the scene while you concentrate, or a limb pinned so that the target loses their action. **4+:** the target is held outright, counting as grappled by something they cannot reach, until they win a fresh contest on their action.

**●●●○○ Hurl** *(2 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Throw what is lying about. **Art damage, bashing, soaked normally, dodge allowed** (Dexterity + Athletics, each success cancelling one of yours). Lethal instead where what you threw was a blade, a spar or a mass of masonry, at staff's call. 1 level on any success, plus 1 per net success beyond the first. Nothing here breaches a wall; that is what Potence is for.

**●●●●○ Levitation** *(3 Vitae, plus tithe)*. Move yourself through air, slowly, for the scene. **1:** rise, descend and hold position; walking pace at best. **2–3:** as above, and carry one other person's weight with you. **4+:** as above, at a run, and you may take one other action per round while moving without splitting your pool for the movement itself. It is the least glamorous entry on this Path and the one that has saved the most lives, because a Lilum caught in the open at dawn does not have to find the stairs.

**●●●●● Stillstand** *(5 Vitae, plus tithe, once per scene)*. Hold a volume of air, a room's worth, in which **nothing mundane may fly.** Bullets, thrown weapons, arrows, debris and falling bodies stop where they are and hang there. **1:** one round. **2–3:** the scene, while you remain conscious and within sight of it. **4+:** the scene, and on your action you may release everything held back along any vector you choose, resolved as Hurl at your current successes against targets of your choosing. It stops nothing supernatural, it stops nothing already in contact with you, and it does not stop a person walking. It stops the shooting, which on the Isle is usually the part that was going to kill somebody.

## Path Summary

| Path | Covers | Signature Tier | Does Not Do |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Path of Blood | Reading, burning, stealing and storing Vitae | Theft of Vitae (3): feed at line of sight, under all feeding rules | Minds, senses, emotion. It never touches a thought |
| The Lure of Flames | Real fire, conjured and shaped | Flame Lash (3): lethal, aggravated to Lilum, soaked normally | Deniability. Every tier from 3 up frightens you too |
| The Sealed Circle | Wards, thresholds, containment, banked thresholds | The Sealed Threshold (4): a month, and it inverts | Sunlight, and any working that is not a fence |
| Movement of the Mind | Force and manipulation at a distance | Stillstand (5): nothing mundane flies in this room | Structural destruction, and anything supernatural |
