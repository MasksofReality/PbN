## Who They Are

Today's Ventrue have not forgotten what their arrogance cost. They rise with respect for the past and clear eyes toward the future, and they favour the people who make the Basilicas stronger: not the iron-fisted lords of the old world, but the best at whatever they have chosen to be. If others must be commanded into that, the Ventrue will use every tool available.

In Purgatory this puts them somewhere awkward and profitable. The Sovereign Club is where a Ventrue belongs, being human-majority, money-solves-everything, and full of Nightfolk expected to play at being human, which a Ventrue finds less humiliating than most do. The Council seat is a Lilum seat, and every Ventrue in the city has an opinion about who should be sitting in it.

## Promise: Steel Mind

Lords of the mental arena for centuries, and Lilith sharpened the edge. You take −1 difficulty on any contested defence against an Art that reaches the mind, covering Dominate, Dementation, Auspex's Telepathy, and any compulsion to yield or obey. A contested defence is not an Art roll, which is precisely why a bloodline trait may legitimately touch it.

## Bane (Stigma): Sensitive Stomachs

Ventrue feed safely on mundane mortals alone. From any non-mortal source, a Ventrue gains one Vitae fewer than the points taken, so a single point taken from any Nightfolk yields nothing at all. The victim still loses the full amount and still takes the full feeding damage. Nothing circumvents this, and Iron Stomach is unavailable to them. This stands as the single registered exception to the rule that foreign essence converts at full value.
