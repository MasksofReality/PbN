## The Designations

**Affinity** covers your bloodline's three Arts. 20 XP to tier 5, and the only track open to you at creation. Both of your creation dots come from here.

**Nonaligned** covers every other common Blood Art: the eight that are not your bloodline's three. 31 XP to 5. A Ventrue counts Auspex as Nonaligned; a Gangrel counts Dominate the same way. Learning one still wants a reason and a source in the fiction, but it is a purchase rather than a negotiation, and at roughly ten months of gross income for a capstone it is a slow ambition rather than an impossible one. The four-Art tree limit means you will hold three and choose one.

**Restricted** needs both an NPC teacher and staff permission before you may learn it at all, and it is never available at creation. Once learned, it advances on the Nonaligned track. Only two categories of Blood Art are Restricted, and they are listed below.

*Discordant does not apply to Lilum.* No Blood Art carries that designation for your line.

Some Arts belong to lines you cannot play. **Obtenebration, Chimerstry and Vicissitude** are the Affinities of surviving lines that are not among the six playable bloodlines, and they are Restricted to every one of you. They exist. Their holders are not your peers and mostly not your friends.

**[Thaumaturgy](/denizens/kindred/thaumaturgy/)** works by Path; each Path is its own Art, and every one of those Paths is Restricted. Blood sorcery is politically radioactive in a League whose Pax was negotiated by Sorcerer Clans. One point matters at the table: Thaumaturgy is a Blood Art and does not require Eldritch, because Eldritch gates Occult Arts. A Lilum practising Thaumaturgy is not a sorcerer, is not subject to the Sorcery–Psi bar, and gains no dispensation from the cross-splat bar in either direction. No Lilum may hold an Art belonging to another splat at any price; there is no track to price it on.

## How to Read an Art

Roll Art + Vitae at difficulty 6. Costs are core's: tier 1 free, tier 2 costs 1 Vitae, tier 3 costs 2, tier 4 costs 3, tier 5 costs 5. Effects grade in bands of 1, 2–3, and 4+ net successes, because no acting pool exceeds ten dice and anything keyed at 5+ simply does not happen against a peer.

Where an entry is marked **Contested**, the contest happens even against a helpless or unaware target. A power that reaches into a mind or a fate always gets answered. Everything unmarked behaves normally: a mortal who is unaware or not actively resisting is not contested at all, and you read the effect off your raw net successes. **Any Art used on a mortal marks a Stability trigger**, because mortals always count as helpless before an Art. That is not a Dominate rule. It is every Art on this list.

Defenders roll **permanent Willpower + highest Art**, except where the effect is fear, horror, or an instruction to flee or yield, which is **Grit + highest Art**. Mortals defend on Willpower alone, or Grit alone against fear, and once per scene may spend 1 Willpower to force a contest that would otherwise be uncontested, or to reroll a failed defence. Against NPCs, use the tier subtraction table rather than rolling for them.

Where an Art establishes a lasting concealment, ward or standing condition, its successes are **banked as a static threshold**. The tester rolls their own pool at difficulty 6 and must equal or exceed the banked number. The banked figure is never rerolled.

Art damage is 1 level on any success, plus 1 per net success beyond the first, and it is unsoaked unless the Art produces an ordinary physical effect. A damaging Art is never contested; cover, range, darkness and surprise are difficulty modifiers. Each damaging entry below states its type and whether it may be dodged.

## Animalism *(Gangrel, Nosferatu)*

Aberrant beasts are rewritten Middle Realm animals, not visitors from anywhere, so Animalism reaches them at **+2 difficulty**, and at staff's discretion for anything the Wyrd has changed past recognition.

**●○○○○ Feral Whispers** *(free)*. Speak with animals and instruct them. Simple instructions to a non-hostile animal need no roll; orders against inclination roll Animalism + Vitae, with the bands setting how contrary the order may be.

**●●○○○ Ride the Senses** *(1 Vitae)*. Borrow an animal's senses for a scene, or hold a beast to an instruction it would refuse.

**●●●○○ Quell the Beast** *(2 Vitae, **Contested**)*. Reach past the person to the animal underneath. **1:** the target cannot initiate violence for one round. **2–3:** for the scene, unless attacked. **4+:** as above, and an ongoing frenzy or Attrition loss of control ends immediately.

**●●●●○ Call of the Wild** *(3 Vitae)*. **1:** a handful of small local animals arrive within the scene. **2–3:** a swarm, or several large beasts, arriving within the scene and staying for it. **4+:** every animal within a mile that can reach you comes, and they obey until dawn.

**●●●●● Wear the Beast's Hide** *(5 Vitae)*. Project into an animal; your own body is helpless while you ride. **1:** full control and senses of one animal in line of sight, for the scene. **2–3:** any animal you have fed on or bonded with, at any distance on the Isle, until dawn. **4+:** you may activate your own Arts through the vessel, paying Vitae from your own pool with your dice unchanged, and the animal survives your departure intact.

## Auspex *(Toreador, Malkavian)*

**●○○○○ Heightened Senses** *(free, reflexive)*. −1 difficulty on all mundane Perception rolls while active. Overwhelming stimulus is a liability while it runs, as the Toreador Bane shows.

**●●○○○ Aura Sight** *(1 Vitae)*. Reads present emotional state and whether the subject is living, Nightfolk, or something else, with the bands setting detail. It does not read splat, Stability or intent.

**●●●○○ The Spirit's Touch** *(2 Vitae)*. Psychometry. **1:** the last strong emotional event attached to an object or place. **2–3:** the last several days in sequence, and the identity of the principal actors. **4+:** the single most significant event in the thing's history, whenever it happened, and whether an Art was worked on or near it.

**●●●●○ Telepathy** *(3 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** one surface thought, one exchange. **2–3:** sustained two-way contact for the scene, and you may steer the subject's train of thought. **4+:** you may search for a specific memory the subject is not currently thinking about.

**●●●●● The Far Sight** *(5 Vitae)*. Your senses leave your body, which lies helpless. **1:** disembodied perception roams the scene and its adjacent spaces for the scene. **2–3:** travel anywhere on the Isle you can picture clearly, and you perceive the doors and thresholds of the Pale Between where they overlap the Middle Realm. **4+:** once, you may step into the Pale in truth and re-enter the Middle Realm at another door you can see from within it. Perception inside the Pale is muted: the Middle Realm drained of colour, sound arriving as a hollow echo. *Any use that crosses into the Pale marks the "witnessed or survived something that should not exist" trigger and is a standing staff hook for an Eldritch award.*

## Celerity *(Toreador)*

None of Celerity's tiers roll; you pay and it happens. All extra actions granted here are exempt from pool-splitting.

**●○○○○ Quickness** *(free)*. One additional non-combat action per round.

**●●○○○ Fleetness** *(1 Vitae)*. One additional combat action this round.

**●●●○○ Rapidity** *(2 Vitae)*. Two additional combat actions this round.

**●●●●○ Blur** *(3 Vitae)*. For the scene, anyone attacking you at range or with a called shot takes +2 difficulty, and you may Dodge without splitting your pool.

**●●●●● Cessation** *(5 Vitae, once per scene)*. One round in which you act and nothing else does. Three actions inside it, unsplit. Attacks made in that round are resolved against a helpless target; soak applies as normal, and mortals still count as helpless before an Art, with everything that implies for your Stability at the end of the scene.

## Dementation *(Malkavian)*

**●○○○○ Passion** *(free, **Contested**)*. Amplifies an emotion already present. You cannot create one. The bands set intensity and duration. No effect on anything without functioning mental architecture.

**●●○○○ The Haunting** *(1 Vitae, **Contested**)*. Intrusive sensory wrongness: +1 difficulty on all the target's mundane rolls for the scene at 2–3 net, +2 at 4+.

**●●●○○ Eyes of Chaos** *(2 Vitae, no target)*. Read the pattern in anything: a crowd, a ledger, a ward, a coincidence. **1:** one true, useful, cryptic insight about the scene. **2–3:** a specific hidden connection: who is lying, what the ward is for, whose money it is. **4+:** foreknowledge about events already in motion, delivered as a symbol the player interprets. *Habitual use is a staff hook for Eldritch, and Eldritch is paid for in permanent Stability.*

**●●●●○ Voice of Madness** *(3 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** one round of incoherence. **2–3:** a derangement of your choosing for the scene. **4+:** the derangement holds until the target's next Catharsis, and while it lasts they take +1 difficulty on all Willpower rolls.

**●●●●● Total Insanity** *(5 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** +2 difficulty on everything for the scene. **2–3:** the target acts on their worst available impulse each round, with no Self-Control roll offered. **4+:** the target loses control entirely, as though they had botched an Attrition roll, and does not recover until the next midnight reset.

## Dominate *(Ventrue)*

**●○○○○ Mesmerise** *(free, **Contested**)*. Eye contact and one word naming a single action: *stop, run, drop, sleep*. Where the word is *run*, the target defends on Grit rather than Willpower.

**●●○○○ Compel** *(1 Vitae, **Contested**)*. A sentence-long instruction lasting the scene, provided it is not plainly self-destructive. At 4+ net it may be something the target would ordinarily refuse.

**●●●○○ The Forgetting** *(2 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** remove the last few minutes. **2–3:** remove or rewrite a scene's worth. **4+:** install a specific memory of your choosing; it holds unless directly contradicted by evidence the subject later encounters. The single most useful Masquerade tool on this list, and the reason the Vigiles hate the Ventrue.

**●●●●○ Conditioning** *(3 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** one instruction that holds until performed or until the next sunset. **2–3:** a standing behavioural rule lasting a lunar month. **4+:** for that month, the subject takes +2 difficulty on any roll to act against your interests or to refuse you. This is the same figure as Tether and the Domitor's Leash, deliberately, and it does not stack with either.

**●●●●● Possession** *(5 Vitae, **Contested**, mortals only)*. Your body lies helpless while you wear theirs. **1:** you speak and act through them for one round. **2–3:** for the scene. **4+:** until the next sunrise, with free use of their Abilities. You cannot use your own Arts through a possessed body. A Nightfolk cannot be possessed at any band.

## Fortitude *(Ventrue, Gangrel, Brujah)*

Tiers 1 and 2 are permanent and reflexive respectively; the rest are paid and last the scene unless stated. None of them roll.

**●○○○○ Endurance** *(free)*. Add your Fortitude rating to soak against bashing and lethal, and halve the effect of environmental hardship.

**●●○○○ Defiance** *(1 Vitae)*. Soak aggravated on Stamina + Fortitude for the scene. This is the only aggravated soak anywhere in this guide. It does **not** apply to Art damage, which is unsoaked regardless unless the Art produces an ordinary physical effect.

**●●●○○ Unyielding** *(2 Vitae)*. For the scene, incoming lethal is halved, rounding down, before soak, exactly as ordinary bashing always is.

**●●●●○ Adamantine** *(3 Vitae)*. For the scene, sunlight does you no damage at all at any stage of its escalation, and fire is reduced from aggravated to lethal. Rotschreck rolls are at −1 difficulty while it holds.

**●●●●● Nothing Ends Me** *(5 Vitae, once per scene, declared before soak)*. The next single source of damage, however large and of whatever type, deals nothing. If you are already past Incapacitated when you spend it, you rise to Crippled rather than entering torpor or suffering Final Death. It cannot be paid for with Pushing, because the aggravated level Pushing costs is the thing you are trying to avoid taking.

## Obfuscate *(Malkavian, Nosferatu)*

Every tier from 2 up **banks** its successes as a static threshold; observers roll Perception + Alertness at difficulty 6 and must equal or exceed it.

**●○○○○ Cloak of Shadows** *(free)*. While still and quiet you are overlooked rather than invisible. An observer needs Perception + Alertness at difficulty 8 to register you. Moving or speaking ends it at once.

**●●○○○ Unseen Presence** *(1 Vitae)*. Move while cloaked. Bank your successes as the threshold.

**●●●○○ Mask of a Thousand Faces** *(2 Vitae)*. Wear another face. **1:** a plausible stranger. **2–3:** a specific person you have observed, convincing to strangers and acquaintances. **4+:** convincing to intimates, and it survives the visual portion of a registry inspection. Papers are still papers.

**●●●●○ Vanish from the Mind's Eye** *(3 Vitae)*. Disappear while being watched. Observers who fail against the banked threshold also lose their memory of the preceding round.

**●●●●● Cloak the Gathering** *(5 Vitae)*. Extend any lower Obfuscate power to a number of willing subjects equal to your Obfuscate rating, for the scene, at your banked threshold. They may move and act normally short of attacking, or of speaking to anyone outside the cloak.

## Potence *(Brujah)*

This is muscle, not unmaking. Nothing here deals aggravated at any tier.

**●○○○○ Might** *(free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane Strength-based rolls, and +1 die to unarmed damage pools.

**●●○○○ Prowess** *(1 Vitae)*. +2 dice to mundane Strength-based damage pools for the scene. Soak applies as normal.

**●●●○○ Puissance** *(2 Vitae)*. For the scene, add your Potence rating in dice to all mundane Strength-based damage pools. Replaces Prowess; does not stack with it.

**●●●●○ Earthshock** *(3 Vitae)*. Strike the ground or a structure. **Art damage, bashing, dodge allowed** (Dexterity + Athletics, each success cancelling one of yours). **1:** one target knocked down. **2–3:** everyone within a few metres knocked prone. **4+:** a wall, floor or door gives way as well.

**●●●●● The Blow That Ends It** *(5 Vitae, once per scene)*. One strike. **Art damage, lethal, unsoaked; dodge allowed unless the target is held, pinned or otherwise helpless.** It destroys any mundane object and breaches any ordinary wall automatically as part of the same action.

## Presence *(Ventrue, Toreador, Nosferatu)*

**In Nosferatu hands, every tier reads as dread rather than glory.** The mechanics are identical; the fiction is that this creature knows something about you. A Nosferatu's Awe applies to Intimidation and information-gathering rather than to charm, and it never helps a first impression go *well*.

**●○○○○ Awe** *(free)*. −1 difficulty on mundane first-impression and Charisma-based Social rolls; those present must consciously decide to dismiss or leave you.

**●●○○○ Dread Gaze** *(1 Vitae, **fear**)*. Cows or routs one target. Being fear, it is defended on Grit: Grit + highest Art for Nightfolk, Grit alone for a mortal.

**●●●○○ Entrancement** *(2 Vitae, **Contested**)*. The target treats you as someone they urgently want to please. **1:** the scene, and favours short of real risk. **2–3:** the night, and real risks taken. **4+:** a lunar month, during which refusing you is at +2 difficulty.

**●●●●○ Summon** *(3 Vitae, **Contested**)*. Call one person you have met, from anywhere they can physically reach. **1:** they come if it is convenient. **2–3:** they come, setting other things aside. **4+:** they come by any means available, knowing only that they must.

**●●●●● Majesty** *(5 Vitae)*. For the scene, nobody present may act against you without first winning a contested defence at +2 difficulty. A failed attempt costs them their action, and they may not try again this scene. This is awe rather than terror, so it is defended on Willpower + highest Art, not Grit.

## Protean *(Gangrel)*

**●○○○○ Eyes of the Beast** *(free, always available)*. Perfect night vision, no darkness penalties. The eyes are unmistakably inhuman while active, which is a registration problem in company.

**●●○○○ Feral Claws** *(1 Vitae)*. Claws dealing **lethal at Strength + 2**, resolved as an ordinary weapon on the mundane damage table and therefore soaked normally.

**●●●○○ Earth Melding** *(2 Vitae)*. Sink into unworked earth or stone and rest inside it. A full turn to enter, a full turn to leave. Undisturbed ground counts as a **prepared resting place** for the purposes of ambient recovery, which is the single most valuable thing a Gangrel owns on the road.

**●●●●○ Shape of the Beast** *(3 Vitae)*. Assume one animal form: wolf, great bat, or something the Wyrd made and your line learned to wear. Staff set the physical shift. You keep your Arts, your Vitae and your Stability; clothing and carried gear come with you.

**●●●●● Body of the Mist** *(5 Vitae)*. Become mist for the scene. Immune to mundane physical damage, ungrappleable, able to pass any gap air can pass. **Fire and sunlight still work on you normally.** You cannot attack, activate Arts, or feed while misted.

## Temporis *(Brujah)*

The Governor once wound back time to undo a bargain the bana'Shee had struck, and demoted a Lord of the Wandering Isle for the trouble of it. Temporis is the small, borrowed cousin of that act, and it is not tolerated quietly.

**Sanction.** The Governor requires that the Triune authorise a Lilum's practice of Temporis. A Brujah opening with the Art holds a provisional sanction and owes the scene that confirms it; anyone working Temporis without one is, sooner or later, simply not there any more. Nobody knows where. Nobody has produced a body, a witness, or an explanation, and the Council has stopped asking.

**Notice.** On a **botch** with any Temporis Art, staff may award a point of Eldritch. Something that was not meant to look at you looked back, and you understood a fraction of what it was. That point costs permanent Stability like every other, and it does not leave.

**●○○○○ Slow Time** *(free, once per scene)*. One extra action for any purpose, exempt from pool-splitting, unusable in consecutive rounds.

**●●○○○ Suspend** *(1 Vitae)*. Stop a single object or projectile in motion where it is, for one round.

**●●●○○ Leaden Moment** *(2 Vitae, **Contested**)*. **1:** the target loses their next action. **2–3:** +2 difficulty on all their physical actions for the scene. **4+:** they lose their action in each of the next two rounds.

**●●●●○ Patience of the Norns** *(3 Vitae)*. For the scene, you act first in every round regardless of initiative, and take one extra action per round exempt from splitting.

**●●●●● Cheat the Moment** *(5 Vitae, once per scene)*. Undo the last completed action, yours or anyone's, as though it had not happened. The original actor may not repeat it this round. It cannot undo a Final Death, and it cannot be used to take back a Stability trigger already marked. You remember doing it, which is rather the point.
